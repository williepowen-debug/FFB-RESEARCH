# Finding: Front-seven continuity keeps Seattle D/ST in the premium baseline tier

## Scope

- Team/player: Seahawks front seven and D/ST
- Area: run defense, pass rush, IDP spine
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-09-09

## Finding

Fact: Seattle's 2025 defense allowed 17.2 points per game and 3.7 rushing yards per attempt, per the official Macdonald and Durde profiles. Fact: training-camp reporting says Seattle's defensive front returns Leonard Williams, Byron Murphy II, Jarran Reed, DeMarcus Lawrence, Uchenna Nwosu, and Derick Hall, with Boye Mafe the main notable front departure. Fact: Seattle signed Dante Fowler Jr. and drafted Deven Eastern.

Inference: Seattle D/ST should enter 2026 as a premium baseline unit, with the main uncertainty being how Fowler/Hall/Ivey replace Mafe's edge snaps rather than whether the front is structurally sound.

## Fantasy implication

Seattle D/ST should be a target in drafts and a weekly pressure/run-defense problem for opposing skill players. Ernest Jones IV and Drake Thomas are strong IDP tackle anchors; Leonard Williams and the edge rotation are sack-ceiling plays depending on snap concentration.

- Week 1 status (verified 2026-09-09): Leonard Williams signed a multi-year extension 2026-08-27 (Macdonald: "a foundational player"). The initial 53 (2026-08-30) carries DL Williams, Murphy, Reed, Mills, Morris, Pili; OLB Lawrence, Nwosu, Hall, Fowler, O'Toole; ILB Jones, Thomas, Knight, O'Connell, Surratt. Jared Ivey was waived at cutdown and is not on the practice squad, so the Mafe replacement rotation is Fowler/Hall/O'Toole rather than the Fowler/Hall/Ivey trio named above. Rookie NT Deven Eastern and DE Aidan Hubbard were waived and signed to the practice squad 2026-08-31; Uso Seumalo was released from the practice squad 2026-09-03. No front-seven player carries a Week 1 designation. The core claim is unchanged.
