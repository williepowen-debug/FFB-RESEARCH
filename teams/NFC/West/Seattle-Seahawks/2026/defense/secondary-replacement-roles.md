# Finding: Secondary remains strong but role replacement drives early volatility

## Scope

- Team/player: Seahawks secondary
- Area: safety competition, CB3, dime packages
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Fact: Coby Bryant signed with Chicago and Riq Woolen signed with Philadelphia. Fact: Seattle retained Josh Jobe, added Noah Igbinoghene, D'Anthony Bell, and Rodney Thomas II, and drafted Bud Clark, Julian Neal, Andre Fuller, and Michael Dansby. Fact: the official depth chart lists Ty Okada and Julian Love as first-team safeties and Devon Witherspoon/Josh Jobe as first-team corners.

Inference: Seattle's top-end secondary is still strong, but early-season volatility lives in the open safety role, CB3, and dime usage after the Bryant/Woolen departures.

## Fantasy implication

Seattle D/ST remains viable, but DFS and player-prop decisions should check whether opponents can isolate the new safety/CB3 combinations. Okada, Clark, Neal, and Igbinoghene are IDP/deep-league watch names only if their roles become full-time.

## Evidence

- The free-agency tracker says Bryant's departure opens a safety role, with Okada and Nick Emmanwori among internal options.
- The same tracker says Woolen's departure likely moves Jobe toward a full-time starting role opposite Witherspoon.
- Training-camp storylines identified the open safety job and No. 3 corner job as major camp questions.
- The depth chart shows rookies and veteran additions directly behind the projected starters.
