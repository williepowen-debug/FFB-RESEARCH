# Seattle Seahawks 2026 Coaching Staff

Seattle's 2026 authority model is head-coach continuity with offensive coordinator transition. Mike Macdonald remains the program and defensive identity authority after a Super Bowl LX season. Brian Fleury replaces Klint Kubiak as offensive coordinator, keeping a Shanahan/Kubiak-adjacent wide-zone and West Coast structure while adding his own installation. Aden Durde remains defensive coordinator under Macdonald, with Chris Partridge, Karl Scott, Zach Orr, Josh Bynes, Jeff Howard, and Leslie Frazier supporting the defensive build.

Week 1 status (verified 2026-09-09 against the official coaches page): no coaching changes since the 2026-03-12 staff announcement. The staff directory now also carries the five football assistants listed on the official page but omitted from the 2026-08-17 build (Byrne, Odom, Caprice, Chryst, Fitzsimmons); the March announcement did not name them as new hires, so they are recorded as retained. Strength/performance staff are not tracked. The franchise sale to the Khosla family closed 2026-09-03 (organizational context in the [overview](../overview.md)); no football-operations change was announced with it.
