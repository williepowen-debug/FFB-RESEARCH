# Brian Fleury - Seattle Seahawks

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-09-09

## Responsibilities

Fact: Seattle hired Fleury as offensive coordinator on 2026-02-19. Fact: Fleury replaced Klint Kubiak, who became the Raiders head coach after Seattle's Super Bowl LX season. Fact: the staff announcement lists Justin Outten as run game coordinator, Jake Peetz as offensive passing game coordinator/quarterbacks, Tyson Prince as quarterbacks coach, John Benton as senior offensive assistant/offensive line, and Thomas Hammock as senior offensive assistant/running backs.

Inference: Fleury is the coordinator-level owner of the offense, but Seattle has a distributed support structure around run game, quarterback teaching, line continuity, and backfield development.

## Scheme and tendencies

Fact: Seahawks.com reported Fleury came from San Francisco, where he had been run game coordinator and tight ends coach, and that Macdonald wanted continuity after Seattle finished third in scoring in 2025. Fact: Macdonald described the 2026 offense as a "kinda" new version of last year's offense because both Fleury and Kubiak worked under Kyle Shanahan.

Inference: Baseline expectations should be wide-zone/play-action carryover, tight-end detail, and movement/shift variation rather than a full teardown.

## Personnel usage

Fact: Seattle's official depth chart lists Darnold as QB1, Smith-Njigba and Kupp as primary receivers, Shaheed and Tory Horton in the receiver/return mix, Barner as TE1, and Holani/Price/Wilson ahead of Charbonnet at running back as of the verification date. Fact: Seattle returned all five starting offensive linemen from 2025 and added Beau Stephens plus Bobby Hart.

Inference: Fleury's most fantasy-relevant decisions are whether Price becomes the lead back, how much Shaheed's full offseason expands his route role, and whether Barner becomes more than a blocking/ancillary tight end.

Week 1 status (verified 2026-09-09): Fact: the initial 53 (2026-08-30) carries three running backs (Price, Wilson, Holani) plus FB Brady Russell, five receivers (Smith-Njigba, Kupp, Shaheed, Horton, Foster), and four tight ends (Barner, Saubert, Arroyo, Kallerup); Nick Vannett was released at cutdown. Fact: the official depth chart as of 2026-09-09 lists Price first at running back, then Wilson, then Holani; the 2026-08-17 order (Holani, Price, Wilson) no longer applies. Fact: Charbonnet is on reserve/PUP through at least Week 4. Fact: Wilson missed camp time and all preseason games (hamstring) but was a full participant on the Sunday and Monday Week 1 reports with no designation; Horton (hamstring) is questionable; Bradford was upgraded to full. Fact: Seahawks.com framed Price's debut as sharing the load with Holani and Wilson rather than carrying it alone. Inference: Fleury enters Week 1 with all five linemen and all three healthy backs available, so the first usage sample will be a clean read on his intended rotation rather than an injury-forced one.

## Pace and situational decisions

No regular-season Fleury sample exists in Seattle. Chart first-team preseason and September games for under-center rate, motion/shift volume, tight-end personnel, red-zone back usage, and Darnold's play-action/deep-shot profile.

## Fantasy implications

The offense has continuity protection because Darnold, the receiver core, and the line are intact. Price has the largest upside/uncertainty because Walker left and Charbonnet is recovering. Smith-Njigba remains the clearest fantasy anchor; Shaheed is a best-ball and return-influenced upside play if routes rise.

## Statements and reporting

Official team reporting emphasized continuity as the reason for Fleury's hire. Macdonald's minicamp comments framed the offense as an installation-phase evolution, not a new system, and specifically noted Fleury's detail orientation.

## Reliability cautions

Coordinator intent is not usage. Price's pass protection, Holani/Wilson roles, Charbonnet health, and Shaheed's offensive snap load must be verified with live reps before changing projections materially.
