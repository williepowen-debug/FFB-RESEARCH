# Aden Durde - Seattle Seahawks

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2024
- Last verified: 2026-09-09

## Responsibilities

Fact: Durde joined Seattle as defensive coordinator on 2024-02-13 after three seasons as the Cowboys defensive line coach. Fact: the 2026 staff announcement keeps Durde as defensive coordinator under Macdonald.

Inference: Macdonald remains a major defensive architect, but Durde is a central game-plan and front-development voice. Seahawks.com mailbag analysis reported no expected major change in the Macdonald/Durde defensive setup after the 2025 result.

## Scheme and tendencies

Fact: Seattle's official Durde profile credits the 2025 Seahawks defense with 17.2 points allowed per game, 3.7 rushing yards allowed per attempt, and top-five marks in yards per play, rushing touchdowns allowed, rushing yards per game, and interceptions. Fact: training-camp reporting said Seattle played nickel and dime at high rates in 2025 while still leading the league in yards per carry allowed.

Inference: The defense is built around light-box run integrity, disguised pressure/coverage, and enough front quality to keep sub-packages viable.

## Personnel usage

Fact: Seattle retained Leonard Williams, Byron Murphy II, Jarran Reed, DeMarcus Lawrence, Uchenna Nwosu, Derick Hall, Ernest Jones IV, Drake Thomas, Devon Witherspoon, Josh Jobe, Ty Okada, and Julian Love. Fact: Boye Mafe, Coby Bryant, and Riq Woolen departed; Dante Fowler Jr., Noah Igbinoghene, D'Anthony Bell, Rodney Thomas II, Bud Clark, Julian Neal, Andre Fuller, Michael Dansby, and Deven Eastern were added.

Inference: Durde's preseason usage questions are edge replacement snaps after Mafe, safety alignment after Bryant, and CB3/dime roles after Woolen.

Week 1 status (verified 2026-09-09): Fact: the initial 53 (2026-08-30) carries six defensive linemen, five outside linebackers (Lawrence, Nwosu, Hall, Fowler, O'Toole), five inside linebackers, five corners (Witherspoon, Jobe, Pritchett, Neal, trade acquisition Avery Smith), and three safeties (Love, Okada, Emmanwori). Fact: Igbinoghene, Bell, Thomas II, and Trevon Diggs were released at cutdown; Thomas II, Diggs, and AJ Finley went to the practice squad 2026-08-31 and Bell returned to it 2026-09-05. Fact: rookie safety Bud Clark went to injured reserve 2026-08-25 (broken ankle). Fact: CB Terrion Arnold was signed 2026-08-29 and placed on the Commissioner's Exempt List 2026-08-30 with no availability timetable. Fact: Leonard Williams signed a multi-year extension 2026-08-27 and Devon Witherspoon a four-year extension 2026-08-17. Fact: for Week 1, Okada is out (hamstring), Emmanwori is questionable (July ankle surgery), Finley was signed to the 53, and Thomas II was elevated; Seahawks.com expects Thomas II or Finley to start beside Love. Inference: Durde's Week 1 safety pairing is injury-forced rather than competition-decided, so it should not be read as the season-long answer to the Bryant replacement question.

## Pace and situational decisions

Durde's defense should be projected as a script-shaping unit before opponent-specific weekly adjustments. Weekly matchup files should still chart blitz rate, pressure source, light-box run fits, and whether Seattle keeps playing nickel/dime against heavy personnel.

## Fantasy implications

Seattle D/ST carries a strong season-long baseline because the front and coverage spine largely return. Leonard Williams, Ernest Jones IV, Drake Thomas, Devon Witherspoon, and Derick Hall/Fowler role splits are the IDP watch points. Opposing backs should be treated cautiously until the run-defense standard breaks.

## Statements and reporting

Official and team-site reporting consistently frame the 2025 defense as elite. The unresolved 2026 points are role replacement rather than scheme credibility.

## Reliability cautions

The defense can still create week-to-week fantasy traps if CB3 or safety turnover creates explosive-pass leaks. Treat August depth-chart order as evidence, not final proof.
