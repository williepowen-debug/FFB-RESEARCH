# Finding: Fleury transition is designed as offensive continuity, not a teardown

## Scope

- Team/player: Seattle Seahawks offense
- Area: coordinator transition, offensive line, passing ecosystem
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-09-09

## Finding

Fact: Seattle hired Brian Fleury as offensive coordinator on 2026-02-19 after Klint Kubiak became Raiders head coach. Fact: Seahawks.com reported Macdonald wanted continuity after Seattle finished third in scoring in 2025. Fact: the official depth chart keeps Sam Darnold, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Cooper Kupp, Rashid Shaheed, AJ Barner, and all five offensive-line starters in the front-line structure.

Inference: the 2026 offense should be projected as an evolution of the 2025 attack, with Fleury's details layered onto a stable quarterback, receiver, and line foundation.

## Fantasy implication

Smith-Njigba should remain Seattle's safest weekly volume projection. Darnold's efficiency case is stronger than a typical coordinator-change quarterback because the line and top pass catchers remain intact. Shaheed carries best-ball upside if his first full offseason translates into more routes without sacrificing explosive return value.

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: Staff hire and roster continuity are official, but Fleury's regular-season play-calling sample in Seattle does not exist yet.

- What would invalidate this: Seattle materially changes run/pass structure, Darnold struggles with protection or timing, or Shaheed/Kupp/Barner roles diverge sharply from the current depth chart.
