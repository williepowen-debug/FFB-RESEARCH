# Finding: Post-Walker backfield is Seattle's biggest offensive fantasy variable

## Scope

- Team/player: Seahawks running backs
- Area: rushing workload, passing-down role, injury recovery
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Fact: Kenneth Walker III left Seattle for Kansas City in free agency. Fact: Seattle signed Emanuel Wilson, retained George Holani, drafted Jadarian Price No. 32 overall, and had Zach Charbonnet on the PUP list at the start of camp. Fact: the official depth chart lists Holani, Price, Wilson, Jacardia Wright, Velus Jones Jr., and Charbonnet in the running back group.

Inference: Price has the highest ceiling because of draft capital and explosive profile, but the Week 1 backfield should be treated as unsettled until pass protection, short-yardage, and Charbonnet health are verified.

## Fantasy implication

Price is the upside target and a priority preseason charting subject. Holani and Wilson are contingency plays if Seattle opens with a committee or protects Price from passing-down/goal-line responsibility. Charbonnet's recovery timing can reshape the room and should not be assumed from the depth chart alone.

## Evidence

- The free-agency tracker says Walker's departure created a clear running back need, especially with Charbonnet coming off a late-season knee injury.
- Seattle used first-round capital on Price and later signed him, making the whole draft class under contract.
- The official transaction page placed Charbonnet on PUP on 2026-07-22.
- The training-camp storylines article singled out Price's Week 1 starting-back competition as one of the camp questions.

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: The roster facts are official, but the workload split is unresolved.
- Fact/inference boundary: Walker's departure, Wilson's signing, Price's draft slot, and Charbonnet's PUP transaction are facts; Price's lead-back path is an inference.
- What would invalidate this: Charbonnet returns to full first-team work quickly, Holani or Wilson controls passing downs and goal-line work, or Price misses time/struggles in protection.
