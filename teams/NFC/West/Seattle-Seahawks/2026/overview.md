# Seattle Seahawks - 2026 Fleury Transition and Championship Continuity

- Conference/division: NFC West
- Last verified: 2026-09-09 (Week 1 eve; game not yet played)
- Scope: changes from the 2025 championship roster/staff to the 2026 initial 53-man roster and Week 1 staff
- Build tier: Core

Seattle's 2026 module is a continuity build with one major offensive leadership change. Mike Macdonald and Aden Durde return after a Super Bowl LX season built around elite defense. Brian Fleury replaces Klint Kubiak as offensive coordinator, but official team reporting frames the move as system continuity because both coaches came through Kyle Shanahan's offensive tree. The largest fantasy variables are the post-Kenneth Walker III backfield, Rashid Shaheed's first full Seattle offseason, safety replacement after Coby Bryant, and CB3/dime roles after Riq Woolen.

Organizational context: the sale of the franchise from the Estate of Paul G. Allen to the Khosla family was approved unanimously by NFL owners on 2026-08-26 and closed on 2026-09-03; Vinod Khosla is Chair, Neeru Khosla is Controlling Owner (and President of the Seahawks Charitable Foundation), and Neal Khosla is Vice Chair. The official announcements contain no football-operations changes, and John Schneider and Mike Macdonald remain in place; this is recorded as context only.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Macdonald remains head coach, Durde remains defensive coordinator, Jay Harbaugh remains special teams coordinator, and Fleury replaces Kubiak as offensive coordinator.
- Darnold returns as QB1 with Smith-Njigba, Kupp, Shaheed, Horton, Barner, and a returning starting offensive line.
- Walker left for Kansas City; Seattle added Wilson, retained Holani, drafted Price in Round 1, and moved Charbonnet to reserve/PUP at the 2026-08-30 cutdown (out at least four games). The initial 53 carries three backs; the official depth chart as of 2026-09-09 lists Price, Wilson, Holani.
- Smith-Njigba and Shaheed were retained/extended as central receiver pieces; Jake Bobo (knee, injured reserve 2026-08-22, season-ending) and Irvin Charles (injured reserve/designated for return 2026-08-30) are out, and Montorie Foster Jr. is the fifth receiver.
- Seattle extended Devon Witherspoon (four years, 2026-08-17) and Leonard Williams (multi-year, 2026-08-27).
- The defense returns Williams, Murphy, Reed, Lawrence, Nwosu, Hall, Jones, Thomas, Witherspoon, Jobe, Okada, and Love as the listed first-team spine.
- Bryant, Woolen, and Mafe departed; of the role-replacement additions, Fowler, Neal, and trade acquisition Avery Smith (2026-08-30) made the 53, Terrion Arnold was signed 2026-08-29 but sits on the Commissioner's Exempt List, Clark is on injured reserve (broken ankle, 2026-08-25), and Igbinoghene, Bell, Thomas II, Fuller, Dansby, and Eastern were released at cutdown (Thomas II, Bell, Fuller, Dansby, Eastern, and AJ Finley landed on the practice squad).
- Week 1 designations (official, 2026-09-08): S Ty Okada OUT (hamstring); S Nick Emmanwori QUESTIONABLE (July ankle surgery); WR Tory Horton QUESTIONABLE (hamstring); RB Emanuel Wilson full participant with no designation after missing camp time and all preseason games. On 2026-09-09 Seattle signed Finley to the 53 (G Bryce Cabeldue waived) and elevated Thomas II and RB Velus Jones Jr.; the team site expects Thomas II or Finley to start beside Love.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

- Smith-Njigba remains Seattle's safest target-volume projection.
- Darnold's efficiency floor is supported by offensive-line continuity, but Fleury's regular-season tendencies still need charting.
- Price is the highest-upside running back, but the official Week 1 framing is a shared load with Holani and Wilson; no split is measured yet, and Charbonnet is unavailable until at least Week 5.
- Shaheed's route share is the best upside variable in the receiver room after his first full Seattle offseason.
- Seattle D/ST starts as a premium baseline unit; early volatility is more likely in secondary replacement roles than in the front.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

Fleury replaces Kubiak after Seattle's championship season. Seahawks.com reported the hire was made with continuity in mind after Seattle finished third in scoring in 2025. The line returns Cross, Zabel, Sundell, Bradford, and Lucas as the listed first-team group, with Josh Jones and Bobby Hart as tackle depth and Beau Stephens added in the draft.

The backfield is the largest usage reset. Walker left, Charbonnet is on reserve/PUP through at least Week 4, Holani is retained, Wilson was signed and is cleared for Week 1, and Price was drafted No. 32 overall and is expected to make his NFL debut in the opener after playing no preseason games. Cabeldue was waived 2026-09-09; interior depth is Haynes and Stephens.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Price become the lead early-down back by Week 1?
- Which back handles passing downs and goal-line work?
- Does Shaheed add meaningful route volume while retaining return leverage?
- Does Barner become fantasy-relevant in Fleury's tight-end-friendly background, or stay mostly a real-football starter?
- Does offensive-line continuity keep Darnold's pressure rate low enough to support multiple pass catchers?

The evidence and invalidation tests are maintained in the [offensive research module](offense/README.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Seattle's defensive leadership is stable. The official coach profiles and training-camp reporting frame the 2025 defense as elite against scoring, rushing efficiency, and explosive control. The front returns almost all of its core, with Mafe the notable departure and Fowler the veteran replacement; Leonard Williams was extended 2026-08-27. The secondary lost Bryant and Woolen but retained Love, Witherspoon (extended), and Jobe while adding veteran and rookie competition. As of 2026-09-09 the 53 carries only three safeties, Okada is out for Week 1, Clark is on injured reserve, and the corner group behind Witherspoon and Jobe is Pritchett, Neal, and Avery Smith, with Arnold on the exempt list.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Fowler replace Mafe's edge-rotation snaps cleanly?
- Does Hall's extension signal more reliable pass-rush volume?
- Does Okada hold the open safety job when healthy, or does the Week 1 injury-forced pairing (Thomas II or Finley beside Love) or Emmanwori change the plan? Clark is out of the picture on injured reserve.
- Who wins CB3 behind Witherspoon and Jobe among Pritchett, Neal, and Avery Smith, and when does Arnold become available?
- Does Seattle sustain light-box run defense if opponents test the new secondary roles?

Seattle should be treated as a strong early-season D/ST and a difficult rushing matchup until evidence says otherwise. See the [defensive research module](defense/README.md).
