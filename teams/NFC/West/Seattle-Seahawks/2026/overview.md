# Seattle Seahawks - 2026 Fleury Transition and Championship Continuity

- Conference/division: NFC West
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Scope: changes from the 2025 championship roster/staff to the 2026 preseason roster and staff
- Build tier: Core

Seattle's 2026 module is a continuity build with one major offensive leadership change. Mike Macdonald and Aden Durde return after a Super Bowl LX season built around elite defense. Brian Fleury replaces Klint Kubiak as offensive coordinator, but official team reporting frames the move as system continuity because both coaches came through Kyle Shanahan's offensive tree. The largest fantasy variables are the post-Kenneth Walker III backfield, Rashid Shaheed's first full Seattle offseason, safety replacement after Coby Bryant, and CB3/dime roles after Riq Woolen.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Macdonald remains head coach, Durde remains defensive coordinator, Jay Harbaugh remains special teams coordinator, and Fleury replaces Kubiak as offensive coordinator.
- Darnold returns as QB1 with Smith-Njigba, Kupp, Shaheed, Horton, Barner, and a returning starting offensive line.
- Walker left for Kansas City; Seattle added Wilson, retained Holani, drafted Price in Round 1, and opened camp with Charbonnet on PUP.
- Smith-Njigba and Shaheed were retained/extended as central receiver pieces.
- The defense returns Williams, Murphy, Reed, Lawrence, Nwosu, Hall, Jones, Thomas, Witherspoon, Jobe, Okada, and Love as the listed first-team spine.
- Bryant, Woolen, and Mafe departed; Seattle added Fowler, Igbinoghene, Bell, Thomas, Clark, Neal, Fuller, Dansby, and Eastern as role-replacement options.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

- Smith-Njigba remains Seattle's safest target-volume projection.
- Darnold's efficiency floor is supported by offensive-line continuity, but Fleury's regular-season tendencies still need charting.
- Price is the highest-upside running back, but Holani, Wilson, and Charbonnet health can force a committee.
- Shaheed's route share is the best upside variable in the receiver room after his first full Seattle offseason.
- Seattle D/ST starts as a premium baseline unit; early volatility is more likely in secondary replacement roles than in the front.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

Fleury replaces Kubiak after Seattle's championship season. Seahawks.com reported the hire was made with continuity in mind after Seattle finished third in scoring in 2025. The line returns Cross, Zabel, Sundell, Bradford, and Lucas as the listed first-team group, with Josh Jones and Bobby Hart as tackle depth and Beau Stephens added in the draft.

The backfield is the largest usage reset. Walker left, Charbonnet is in injury recovery, Holani is retained, Wilson was signed, and Price was drafted No. 32 overall.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Price become the lead early-down back by Week 1?
- Which back handles passing downs and goal-line work?
- Does Shaheed add meaningful route volume while retaining return leverage?
- Does Barner become fantasy-relevant in Fleury's tight-end-friendly background, or stay mostly a real-football starter?
- Does offensive-line continuity keep Darnold's pressure rate low enough to support multiple pass catchers?

The evidence and invalidation tests are maintained in the [offensive research module](offense/README.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Seattle's defensive leadership is stable. The official coach profiles and training-camp reporting frame the 2025 defense as elite against scoring, rushing efficiency, and explosive control. The front returns almost all of its core, with Mafe the notable departure and Fowler the veteran replacement. The secondary lost Bryant and Woolen but retained Love, Witherspoon, and Jobe while adding veteran and rookie competition.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Fowler replace Mafe's edge-rotation snaps cleanly?
- Does Hall's extension signal more reliable pass-rush volume?
- Does Okada hold the open safety job, or do Clark and Emmanwori force rotation?
- Who wins CB3 behind Witherspoon and Jobe?
- Does Seattle sustain light-box run defense if opponents test the new secondary roles?

Seattle should be treated as a strong early-season D/ST and a difficult rushing matchup until evidence says otherwise. See the [defensive research module](defense/README.md).

### Analytical projection

Seattle projects as a high-floor fantasy environment for Smith-Njigba, Darnold efficiency, and D/ST. The biggest draft-room edge is correctly pricing uncertainty: Price can become a lead-back value, but the backfield is not settled; Shaheed can gain offense value, but return and route roles must be balanced; Barner can matter only if Fleury's tight-end history turns into route volume.
