# San Francisco 49ers 2026 Roster Spine

Core roster files capture the durable 2026 preseason personnel changes that affect fantasy and matchup analysis. Player-level weekly availability belongs in `weekly/2026/`; this folder is limited to offseason and preseason roster construction.

Files:

- [Departures](departures.csv)
- [Veteran additions](veteran-additions.csv)
- [Retentions and extensions](retentions.csv)
- [2026 draft class](draft-class.csv)
- [Undrafted free agents](undrafted-free-agents.csv)

Primary source preference is official 49ers transaction, roster, depth-chart, and draft pages. NFL.com is used for league-wide departure tracking where the team site does not maintain a consolidated offseason departures page.
