# Finding: Warner and Greenlaw stabilize the middle while the secondary sorts roles

## Scope

- Team/player: 49ers linebackers and secondary
- Area: coverage stability, IDP baseline, and opponent matchup grading
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Facts: the August 12 unofficial depth chart lists Fred Warner at MIKE and Dre Greenlaw at WILL, with Jaden Dugger, Nick Martin, Tatum Bethune, Jalen Graham, and Garret Wallow in reserve roles. It lists Renardo Green and Deommodore Lenoir outside, Upton Stout at nickel, and Ji'Ayir Brown and Malik Mustapha at safety. The 49ers added Nate Hobbs, Jack Jones, Ashtyn Davis, and Ephesians Prysock to the defensive-back competition.

Inference: Warner and Greenlaw give Morris a stable communication and run-fit base if healthy. The bigger weekly variable is coverage personnel, especially nickel/outside distribution among Lenoir, Green, Stout, Hobbs, Jones, and Prysock.

## Fantasy implication

Warner remains the clearest IDP anchor. Greenlaw has IDP relevance if his role is every-down. For offensive start/sit decisions against San Francisco, slot usage and boundary corner assignments should be checked weekly until Morris' preferred secondary grouping is stable.

## Evidence

- The official depth chart identifies first-team linebacker, corner, nickel, and safety alignments.
- The team transaction page lists Greenlaw, Hobbs, Jones, and Davis additions.
- The draft page lists Prysock and Dugger as defensive additions.
- Morris' staff includes Jerry Gray as defensive passing game coordinator and Ray Brown as defensive backs/cornerbacks coach.
