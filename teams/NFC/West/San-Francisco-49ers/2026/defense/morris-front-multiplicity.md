# Finding: Raheem Morris adds front multiplicity without erasing the 49ers line-first identity

## Scope

- Team/player: San Francisco defensive front
- Area: pressure creation, front structure, and D/ST matchup baseline
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Facts: the 49ers hired Raheem Morris as defensive coordinator and added Matt Eberflus as assistant head coach defense. Official offseason reporting says Morris wants to blend player strengths with the existing foundation. Kris Kocurek said the defense should still be a four-down front team while using more multiple-front looks. Day 1 camp coverage noted some five-man fronts. The team added Osa Odighizuwa by trade and drafted Romello Height and Gracen Halton.

Inference: this is a meaningful defensive transition, but not a full philosophical reset. The 49ers should remain line-driven, with Morris adding front variation and coverage communication changes to solve the 2025 pressure problem.

## Fantasy implication

San Francisco D/ST can still be drafted or projected as an upside defense, but early matchup grades should require Bosa/Williams health and pressure-rate confirmation. Opposing quarterbacks and passing games should not be downgraded as aggressively until the new front proves it can create pressure without excessive blitz exposure.

## Evidence

- Official coaching-staff announcement lists Morris as defensive coordinator and Eberflus as assistant head coach defense.
- Team feature reporting says Morris is learning from the existing foundation rather than forcing a personal stamp.
- Kocurek's public comments point to a four-down base with more multiple-front usage.
- Official camp coverage reported five-man front looks during team drills.
- The roster and draft pages show Odighizuwa, Height, and Halton as front-seven additions.
