# San Francisco 49ers - 2026 Core Overview

- Conference/division: NFC West
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Scope: 2026 preseason Core-tier team module
- Build tier: Core

San Francisco remains a Shanahan/Purdy/McCaffrey team, but the 2026 module should be treated as a re-check year rather than a pure continuity year. The offense has a rebuilt receiver room, George Kittle is on active/PUP as of the current roster snapshot, Ricky Pearsall is on injured reserve, and Brandon Aiyuk is on reserve/left squad. The defense transitions to Raheem Morris while preserving major 49ers identity pieces around Fred Warner, Nick Bosa, Dre Greenlaw, and Kris Kocurek's front.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Kyle Shanahan remains head coach and offensive identity source.
- Klay Kubiak is the offensive coordinator; Raheem Morris is the new defensive coordinator.
- Brock Purdy is listed as QB1 and Christian McCaffrey as RB1 on the August 12 unofficial depth chart.
- Mike Evans, Christian Kirk, Deebo Samuel Sr., and rookie De'Zhaun Stribling reshape the receiver room.
- George Kittle is listed on active/PUP, Ricky Pearsall on reserve/injured, and Brandon Aiyuk on reserve/left squad.
- Osa Odighizuwa, Romello Height, Gracen Halton, Nate Hobbs, Jack Jones, Ephesians Prysock, and Jaden Dugger are the key defensive additions.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

- McCaffrey remains the primary fantasy engine if his practice and Week 1 workload normalize.
- Purdy's efficiency projection is strong but more sensitive to Kittle activation, receiver cohesion, and line stability than usual.
- Evans is the clearest new receiver value; Samuel, Kirk, and Stribling need route and touch charting.
- Kittle requires a route-rate and activation checkpoint before aggressive tight end pricing.
- The D/ST ceiling depends on Bosa/Williams health and whether Morris can add front multiplicity without creating coverage leakage.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

San Francisco lost Jauan Jennings, Kendrick Bourne, and Skyy Moore from the receiver group, then added Evans, Kirk, Samuel, and Stribling. Robert Jones is listed as the first left guard on the unofficial depth chart, with rookie Carver Willis behind him. Kittle's active/PUP status and Pearsall/Aiyuk roster statuses create significant pass-game uncertainty.

### Questions to resolve

- Does McCaffrey handle normal first-team, red-zone, and passing-down work?
- Is Kittle activated and running normal routes by Week 1?
- Which three receivers earn full-time routes with Purdy?
- Does Stribling become a real early-season contributor or remain a developmental depth piece?
- Does Robert Jones stabilize left guard, and is Trent Williams' workload normal?

See [receiver room reset](offense/receiver-room-reset.md) and [health and protection checkpoints](offense/health-and-protection-checkpoints.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Morris replaces Robert Saleh as defensive coordinator, while Matt Eberflus and Jerry Gray add senior defensive support. The front added Odighizuwa, Height, and Halton, and the secondary competition added Hobbs, Jones, Davis, and Prysock.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Morris stay primarily four-down while adding five-man and multiple-front looks?
- Is Bosa fully available, and how quickly does Mykel Williams return?
- Do Height and Halton earn pass-rush rotation snaps?
- How do Lenoir, Green, Stout, Hobbs, Jones, and Prysock divide outside and nickel work?
- Does Greenlaw play an every-down role next to Warner?

See [Morris front multiplicity](defense/morris-front-multiplicity.md) and [secondary and linebacker stability](defense/secondary-linebacker-stability.md).

## Coaching and scheme

| Unit | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Head coach | Kyle Shanahan | Kyle Shanahan |
| Offense/play-caller | Kyle Shanahan / Klay Kubiak | Kyle Shanahan / Klay Kubiak |
| Defense/play-caller | Robert Saleh | Raheem Morris |
| Special teams | Brant Boyer | Brant Boyer |

The offensive scheme baseline remains Shanahan. Kubiak's value for fantasy tracking is practical: practice play-calling, coordinator comments, and how the staff allocates receiver and backfield work. Morris is the main scheme transition. Official team reporting frames his approach as blending 49ers strengths with more front flexibility, not replacing the defensive identity wholesale.

### Analytical projection

The 49ers should be projected as a strong but fragile fantasy environment entering late August. McCaffrey and Purdy retain strong contextual value, but Kittle's status and receiver redistribution are mandatory checks. The defense remains dangerous on name value and front talent, but D/ST confidence should trail actual pressure and coverage evidence.

## Special teams

Brant Boyer remains special teams coordinator. Corliss Waitman is listed as punter/holder, Eddy Pineiro as kicker, Jacob Cowing as punt returner, and Jon Weeks as long snapper on the August 12 unofficial depth chart. Special teams remains scaffolded at Core tier unless return roles or kicker conditions become weekly fantasy inputs.
