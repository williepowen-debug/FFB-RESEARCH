# Klay Kubiak - San Francisco 49ers

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2021
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Kubiak is in his fifth season with the 49ers and first as offensive coordinator, per the official coaches roster. His prior 49ers roles included offensive passing game specialist in 2024 and assistant quarterbacks coach in 2022-23. Training camp reporting says he continued handling practice play-calling while Shanahan worked through a limited return.

## Scheme and tendencies

Kubiak is best treated as a continuity coordinator inside Shanahan's structure. The staff added clearer sub-leads: Joe Graves as run game coordinator, Leonard Hankerson as passing game coordinator/wide receivers, Robert Turner Jr. at running backs, Cameron Clemmons at tight ends, and Chris Foerster still anchoring the offensive line.

## Personnel usage

Facts: the 49ers added Mike Evans and Christian Kirk, later signed Deebo Samuel Sr., drafted De'Zhaun Stribling and Kaelon Black, and listed Robert Jones at left guard on the August 12 unofficial depth chart. Kittle and Isaac Guerendo were on active/PUP, while Ricky Pearsall was on injured reserve.

Inference: Kubiak's first fantasy-relevant test is not a new playbook; it is how the staff allocates route volume and backfield relief inside the familiar offense while Kittle/Pearsall/Aiyuk availability is unstable.

## Pace and situational decisions

Kubiak's camp comments about adjusting practice structure to manage workload point to role preservation as an operating theme. That matters for veterans such as McCaffrey, Evans, Samuel, Trent Williams, and Kittle, and for how quickly Stribling and Black can earn high-value work.

## Fantasy implications

The offense should still be projected as efficient, but the market should not assume the 2025 target hierarchy carries forward. Evans can become the primary perimeter touchdown bet, Samuel can regain hybrid-touch value, Kirk can matter in PPR if slot routes consolidate, and Stribling has dynasty and best-ball upside if injuries create early snaps.

## Statements and reporting

Official rookie minicamp coverage quoted Kubiak on Kaelon Black's physical running and Carver Willis' versatility. Camp coverage also described Purdy spreading work to backs, receivers, tight ends, and Juszczyk during team periods.

## Reliability cautions

Kubiak's public role is coordinator, but Shanahan remains the head coach and offensive identity source. Treat any change in play-caller, Kittle activation, Aiyuk status, Pearsall injury timeline, and McCaffrey practice workload as review triggers.
