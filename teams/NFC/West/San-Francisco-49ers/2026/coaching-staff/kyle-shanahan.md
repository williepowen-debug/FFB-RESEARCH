# Kyle Shanahan - San Francisco 49ers

- Role: Head coach and primary offensive authority
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2017
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Shanahan is the 49ers head coach and the durable offensive identity anchor. The official coaches roster says he was named head coach on February 6, 2017, and signed a multi-year extension in September 2023. In 2026 camp reporting, Shanahan was limited while recovering, with Chris Foerster and the staff sharing practice responsibilities, but the team did not expect him to miss the regular-season opener.

## Scheme and tendencies

The offensive baseline remains Shanahan's motion, formation, run-after-catch, play-action, and matchup-space system. The 2026 support structure adds Klay Kubiak as OC, Joe Graves as run game coordinator, Leonard Hankerson as passing game coordinator/wide receivers, and Foerster on the offensive line. That points to continuity with more delegated installation and practice management than a true offensive overhaul.
