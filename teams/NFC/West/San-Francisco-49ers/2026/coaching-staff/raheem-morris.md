# Raheem Morris - San Francisco 49ers

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Morris is the 49ers defensive coordinator after two seasons as Falcons head coach and a prior Rams defensive coordinator stint. San Francisco announced his hire/title in its March 30, 2026 coaching-staff moves. He inherits a defense still led by Fred Warner, Nick Bosa, Dre Greenlaw, and established line coach Kris Kocurek, with Matt Eberflus and Jerry Gray adding senior defensive support.

## Scheme and tendencies

Facts: official offseason reporting says Morris is blending what the 49ers already do well rather than forcing a full identity reset. Kocurek said he expected the unit to remain a four-down front team while using more multiple-front looks, including five-down possibilities. Day 1 camp reporting noted new looks and five-man fronts during team drills.

Inference: expect front multiplicity and coverage teaching changes, not an abandonment of the 49ers defensive-line-first identity.

## Personnel usage

The current depth chart lists Keion White, Osa Odighizuwa, Alfred Collins, and Nick Bosa as first-team defensive linemen, with Romello Height behind Bosa and Gracen Halton behind Collins. Warner and Greenlaw remain first-team linebackers, while the corner/nickel picture includes Renardo Green, Deommodore Lenoir, Upton Stout, Nate Hobbs, Jack Jones, and Ephesians Prysock.

## Pace and situational decisions

Morris' most meaningful fantasy impact is on opponent efficiency and D/ST volatility. If Bosa and Mykel Williams are limited, the defense may need heavier pressure design or more rotational snaps from Height, Halton, Odighizuwa, Okuayinonu, and White. If the front produces pressure without blitzing, San Francisco can remain a difficult passing matchup.

## Fantasy implications

Start/sit grading against San Francisco should remain cautious for opposing quarterbacks, but the early-season rating must be conditional. Confirm Bosa/Williams availability, interior pressure, and corner/nickel assignments before treating the defense as a settled elite matchup avoid.

## Statements and reporting

Official team articles framed Morris as an experienced coach who wants to blend player strengths with his background on both sides of the ball. Rookie-minicamp coverage highlighted his praise for Romello Height's pass-rushing energy and Gracen Halton's interior disruption.

## Reliability cautions

The August depth chart is unofficial. Bosa, Williams, Kittle, Guerendo, and other injury statuses are fast-changing and should be moved to weekly records once the regular season opens.
