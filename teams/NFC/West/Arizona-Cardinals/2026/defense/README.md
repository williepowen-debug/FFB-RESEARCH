# Arizona Cardinals 2026 Defensive Ecosystem

Arizona's defensive module is built around continuity at coordinator and change across the front. Nick Rallis is retained, while the defensive line and linebacker depth chart has new veteran additions, draft additions, and injury losses.

Current watch points:

- Whether Josh Sweat, Zaven Collins, Baron Browning, Darius Robinson, and Walter Nolen III create enough pressure without Kaleb Proctor.
- Whether Mack Wilson Sr. or Cody Simon owns every-down linebacker work.
- Whether Budda Baker's tackle floor remains intact next to Andrew Wingard and the young corner group.
- Whether the young corners turn Arizona into a real matchup downgrade or merely a matchup-dependent D/ST stream.
