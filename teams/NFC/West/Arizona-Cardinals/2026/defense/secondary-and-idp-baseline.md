# Finding: Baker remains the clearest IDP anchor

## Scope

- Team/player: Cardinals secondary and linebackers
- Area: IDP tackle volume, coverage stability, matchup downgrade decisions
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

### Facts

Budda Baker remains a central defensive piece in Rallis' official biography, which cites repeated Pro Bowl selections and consecutive 100-tackle seasons. The current official depth chart lists Andrew Wingard and Baker as first-listed safeties, Cody Simon and Mack Wilson Sr. as first-listed inside linebackers, and Denzel Burke/Max Melton, Will Johnson, and Sean Murphy-Bunting among the cornerback baseline.

### Inference

Baker is the strongest Arizona IDP floor because his role and production history survive staff transition. Mack Wilson Sr. and Cody Simon are the next most important IDP variables: if they consolidate every-down usage, one or both can become tackle-volume starts. Coverage has young talent but still needs regular-season proof before downgrading opposing pass catchers.

## Fantasy implication

Prioritize Baker as the durable IDP assumption. Treat Wilson/Simon as snap-count watches with upside. Do not overreact against wide receivers facing Arizona until the young corner group proves it can limit explosives over regular-season samples.

## Evidence

- Rallis' official bio credits Baker with 371 tackles over the prior three seasons and 100-plus tackles in 2024 and 2025.
- The same bio notes Cody Simon started the final nine games in 2025 after Mack Wilson Sr.'s injury and finished second among rookies in tackles.
- The official depth chart places Baker, Wingard, Simon, Wilson, Denzel Burke, Max Melton, Will Johnson, and Sean Murphy-Bunting in prominent roles.
- Arizona added Andrew Wingard and Isaiah Oliver in free agency while retaining Rallis.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: Baker role and production history are strong; linebacker and corner usage still needs fresh snap evidence.
- Fact/inference boundary: official roles, depth chart, and bio statistics are facts. IDP weekly value and matchup downgrades are inference.
- What would invalidate this: Baker changes role materially, Wilson/Simon rotate more than expected, or the young corners rapidly become a shutdown group.
