# Finding: Skill talent is clearer than touch distribution

## Scope

- Team/player: Cardinals skill-position ecosystem
- Area: running back competition, receiving hierarchy, tight-end usage
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

### Facts

The official depth chart lists Tyler Allgeier first at running back, followed by Jeremiyah Love, James Conner, Trey Benson, Bam Knight, Corey Kiner, and Evan Hull. Love was the No. 3 overall pick and had 11 carries plus three receptions in his preseason debut, then was held out for a week with ankle soreness. The official depth chart lists Marvin Harrison Jr., Kendrick Bourne, and Michael Wilson as the first listed receivers, with Trey McBride as TE1.

### Inference

Love's draft capital and first-half preseason usage create a lead-back ceiling, but Allgeier's depth-chart placement, Conner/Benson health, and Love's ankle soreness keep the early workload unresolved. The passing-game top end is cleaner: Harrison and McBride should be the first reads most likely to carry fantasy starter value, while Bourne/Wilson and the reserve receiver battle shape weekly volatility.

## Fantasy implication

Love is an upside RB with workload and health risk, not a locked-in bellcow as of 2026-08-17. Allgeier is a practical early-season role hedge. Harrison and McBride carry the best season-long target-floor assumptions. Bourne and Wilson are watch-list receivers unless route share and target priority consolidate.

## Evidence

- Arizona drafted Love at No. 3 overall and signed him to a four-year contract with a fifth-year option.
- Love produced 11 carries for 58 yards and three catches for 14 yards against Las Vegas on 2026-08-13.
- Team reporting on 2026-08-15 said Love would sit that week with an ankle injury; the same article noted James Conner had not participated in 11-on-11 work and Trey Benson remained out with knee issues.
- Arizona's receiver-room report identified Harrison and Wilson as the top two receivers, Bourne as elevating the No. 3 role, and Jalen Brooks/Simi Fehoko/Reggie Virgil among the reserve competition.
