# Finding: New offensive leadership resets Arizona's projection

## Scope

- Team/player: Arizona Cardinals offense
- Area: scheme, play calling, quarterback transition
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

### Facts

Arizona moved from Jonathan Gannon/Drew Petzing to Mike LaFleur/Nathaniel Hackett for 2026 while releasing Kyler Murray and listing Jacoby Brissett as QB1. LaFleur's official bio ties him to the Rams/Jets/49ers/Falcons/Browns coaching tree, and Hackett's bio adds Green Bay/Jacksonville/Jets/Buffalo coordinator experience.

### Inference

The Cardinals should be projected as a new offensive environment, not a continuation of the 2025 Murray/Petzing offense. The scheme prior is friendly to play action, backfield receiving, and condensed passing structure, but Brissett's profile points more to operational stability than explosive rushing or off-script creation.

## Fantasy implication

Marvin Harrison Jr. and Trey McBride remain the safest volume bets because elite target earners can survive a transition offense. Brissett is a superflex depth option only until Arizona proves pace or touchdown efficiency. Carson Beck is a dynasty/developmental watch rather than a Week 1 redraft plan.

## Evidence

- Arizona's official transactions show Gannon was relieved of head-coach duties on 2026-01-05, LaFleur agreed to a five-year contract on 2026-02-01, Hackett was hired as offensive coordinator on 2026-02-13, and Murray was released on 2026-03-11.
- The official depth chart lists Brissett first, Gardner Minshew II second, Carson Beck third, and Kedon Slovis fourth.
- LaFleur's official bio says he spent 2023-25 as Rams offensive coordinator and cites the 2025 Rams offense as league-leading in several scoring and yardage categories.
- Hackett's official bio says he has 10 years of offensive coordinator experience and highlights Green Bay's 2020 red-zone and scoring efficiency.
