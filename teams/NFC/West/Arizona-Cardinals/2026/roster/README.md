Key roster assumptions as of 2026-08-17:

- Jacoby Brissett is the current QB1 baseline after Kyler Murray's release and Brissett's new one-year contract.
- Tyler Allgeier is listed ahead of Jeremiyah Love, James Conner, and Trey Benson on the official depth chart, but Love's draft slot and preseason usage keep the lead-back question open.
- Chase Bisontis and Kaleb Proctor are on injured reserve, reducing the immediate impact of the second- and fourth-round picks.
- Marvin Harrison Jr., Kendrick Bourne, and Michael Wilson are the first listed wide receiver trio; Jalen Brooks, Simi Fehoko, Reggie Virgil, Devin Duvernay, Xavier Weaver, Bryson Green, Ihmir Smith-Marsette, and Harrison Wallace III remain relevant to reserve and special-teams decisions.
