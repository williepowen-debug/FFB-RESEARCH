# Nathaniel Hackett - Arizona Cardinals

## Responsibilities

### Facts

Arizona named Hackett offensive coordinator on 2026-02-13. His official Cardinals bio credits him with 17 years of NFL coaching experience, including offensive coordinator stops with Buffalo, Jacksonville, Green Bay, and the Jets, plus one season as Denver head coach.

### Inference

Hackett is the offensive coordinator, but LaFleur's head-coach/offensive background means play design, weekly scripting, and game management may be shared. Do not assume Hackett independently owns the call sheet until Arizona publicly confirms it or regular-season evidence makes it clear.

## Scheme and tendencies

### Facts

The official bio highlights Hackett's Green Bay years, including a 2020 Packers offense that led the league in scoring and red-zone touchdown rate. It also notes that Hackett spent 2025 as a defensive analyst with the Packers, providing offensive perspective to Jeff Hafley's defensive staff.

### Inference

Hackett's best fantasy priors are quarterback-friendly sequencing, red-zone structure, and a willingness to feature a primary receiver/tight end when personnel supports it. Arizona's current roster points toward Trey McBride and Marvin Harrison Jr. as the high-value passing-game anchors, with Kendrick Bourne and Michael Wilson shaping the secondary route tree.

## Personnel usage

The official depth chart places Brissett ahead of Minshew and Beck, Tyler Allgeier ahead of Love/Conner/Benson, and McBride as TE1. That makes the August question practical: whether Hackett and LaFleur use Love as an immediate centerpiece or open with a more distributed backfield while Brissett stabilizes the offense.

No Cardinals regular-season sample exists for Hackett under LaFleur. Track opening-script tempo, under-center rate, two-tight-end usage with McBride/Elijah Higgins, and red-zone touch concentration before importing Green Bay-era assumptions too aggressively.

McBride and Harrison should remain the safest Cardinals fantasy assumptions because their roles are least dependent on early-down game script. Love's ceiling is high if the offense uses him like a first-round feature back, but Allgeier's first depth-chart listing and Love's ankle soreness make Week 1 workload uncertain.
