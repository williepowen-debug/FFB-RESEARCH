# Mike LaFleur - Arizona Cardinals

- Role: Head coach
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026-02-01 agreement; official bio says hired 2026-02-02
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

### Facts

LaFleur is Arizona's head coach after the Cardinals relieved Jonathan Gannon of head-coaching duties on 2026-01-05 and agreed to a five-year contract with LaFleur on 2026-02-01. The official staff announcement framed LaFleur's first Cardinals staff as a blend of retained assistants and new hires.

### Inference

LaFleur is the primary offensive identity setter even with Nathaniel Hackett carrying the offensive coordinator title. The LaFleur/Hackett pairing should be treated as a new offense until regular-season play-calling and personnel usage are charted.

## Scheme and tendencies

### Facts

The Cardinals' official bio states LaFleur arrived after three seasons as Rams offensive coordinator and cites his prior NFL stops with the Rams, Jets, 49ers, Falcons, and Browns. The same bio credits the 2025 Rams offense with league-leading scoring, total-yardage, passing-yardage, offensive-touchdown, passing-touchdown, and total-points marks.

### Inference

The most defensible prior is a Shanahan/McVay-family offense built around condensed formations, motion, play action, outside-zone/duo answers, and high-value touches for backs and tight ends. Personnel reality matters: Jacoby Brissett, Jeremiyah Love/Tyler Allgeier/James Conner/Trey Benson, Marvin Harrison Jr., Michael Wilson, Kendrick Bourne, and Trey McBride will determine how much of that prior translates immediately.

## Personnel usage

Arizona has a transition quarterback room and a crowded backfield. Brissett is listed as QB1, Gardner Minshew II as QB2, and Carson Beck as QB3 on the official depth chart. Tyler Allgeier is listed first at running back, followed by Love, Conner, Benson, Bam Knight, Corey Kiner, and Evan Hull. Love started against Las Vegas in preseason and produced 14 touches in the first half before the team later held him out with ankle soreness.

## Pace and situational decisions

Pace and fourth-down tendencies are not yet stable because 2026 is LaFleur's first head-coaching season. Treat Rams/Jets/49ers history as background only; use the first month of 2026 Cardinals game evidence for hard priors.

## Fantasy implications

LaFleur's arrival raises the ceiling for efficient touches if Arizona can keep the offense on schedule. The key fantasy question is not just scheme quality; it is who earns the valuable work. McBride and Harrison have the clearest route/talent cases, Love has first-round upside but health and depth-chart competition, and Brissett is more stabilizer than fantasy ceiling unless volume or rushing support surprises.

Official Cardinals sources reported LaFleur's contract agreement, staff assembly, and biography. Current preseason reporting from the team confirms Brissett starting preseason work, Love's debut, Love's ankle issue, and the Bisontis injured-reserve move.
