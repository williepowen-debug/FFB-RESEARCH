## Responsibilities

### Facts

Rallis is retained as defensive coordinator under Mike LaFleur. The official staff announcement identifies him as one of the returning coaches, and his Cardinals bio says he is in his fourth season as defensive coordinator after being hired in 2023.

### Inference

Rallis supplies the defensive continuity while the rest of the team changes at head coach and offense. That makes defensive role continuity more reliable than offensive role continuity entering 2026.

## Scheme and tendencies

### Facts

The official bio credits Rallis' recent Arizona defenses with strong individual production from Budda Baker, Josh Sweat, Calais Campbell, Mack Wilson Sr., Cody Simon, Will Johnson, and Denzel Burke. It also cites a 2024 second-half surge in points allowed, sacks, forced fumbles, and pass defense.

### Inference

The durable IDP lens is safety and linebacker tackle volume, plus edge pressure if Sweat/Collins/Browning/Ojulari and the young front stay healthy. The current depth chart still shows Baker, Mack Wilson Sr., Cody Simon, Zaven Collins, Baron Browning, Darius Robinson, Walter Nolen III, and the young corners as key roles.

## Personnel usage

Rallis' 2026 front is a blend of holdovers and additions. Roy Lopez, Andrew Billings, Jonah Williams, Andrew Wingard, Jack Gibbens, and Isaiah Oliver were added, while Dalvin Tomlinson and Bilal Nichols were released. Kaleb Proctor was drafted in Round 4 but later placed on injured reserve, limiting immediate rookie interior depth.
