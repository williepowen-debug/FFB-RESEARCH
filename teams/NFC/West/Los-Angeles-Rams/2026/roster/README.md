# Los Angeles Rams 2026 Roster

Core-tier roster files capture the 2026 preseason baseline for fantasy research. Official Rams transactions, free-agency, draft, and position-reset pages are the factual spine; role interpretation belongs in the offense, defense, and overview records.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): 2025 contributors who left, retired, or remained unsigned as of the verification date.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): external veteran additions and camp-status notes.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): retained Rams and extensions.
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): 2026 draft picks.
- [`undrafted-free-agents.csv`](undrafted-free-agents.csv): 2026 UDFA class and current camp notes where useful.

Last verified: 2026-08-17.
