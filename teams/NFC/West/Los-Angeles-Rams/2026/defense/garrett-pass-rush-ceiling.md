# Finding: Myles Garrett materially raises the Rams D/ST pressure ceiling

## Scope

- Team/player: Los Angeles Rams defensive front
- Area: pass rush, pressure environment, and fantasy matchup effect
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

The Rams' trade agreement to acquire Myles Garrett gives Chris Shula's defense a new elite pass-rush centerpiece. Official Rams materials list Garrett with an NFL single-season record 23 sacks in 2025 and 125.5 career sacks entering Los Angeles. He joins a defense whose 2025 staff context already included 47 team sacks and Pro Bowl edge production from Byron Young and Jared Verse.

## Fantasy implication

The Rams D/ST should be treated as a high-upside pressure play, especially when favored or facing offensive lines that struggle with edge help rules. Opposing quarterbacks, pass catchers, and offensive play-callers may see more volatile efficiency because Garrett can force protection tilts that free Verse, Young, Turner, or Fiske.

## Evidence

### Facts

- The Rams transaction log says the club agreed to terms with the Browns on a trade to acquire Garrett on 2026-06-01.
- Rams reporting said Garrett arrived with 125.5 career sacks, the most among active NFL players.
- The official defensive-line reset listed Garrett's 2025 season as 23 sacks, 60 total tackles, 33 tackles for loss, one pass defensed, and three fumble recoveries.
- The Rams staff announcement said Drew Wilkins' 2025 pass-rush group tied for seventh in the NFL with 47 sacks, and that Byron Young and Jared Verse earned Pro Bowl nods.

### Inferences

- Garrett's presence should change opponent protection plans and create indirect pressure value for the rest of the front.
- The D/ST ceiling is game-script sensitive because the pass rush is most valuable when Stafford's offense creates known passing situations.
