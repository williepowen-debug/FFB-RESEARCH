# Chris Shula - Los Angeles Rams Defensive Coordinator

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2017; promoted to defensive coordinator before 2024
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Shula returns for his third season as Rams defensive coordinator and 10th overall season on the staff. The official staff announcement says his 2025 defense finished 10th in scoring defense and 12th in run defense. His 2026 responsibility is unusual for a continuity coordinator because the Rams added star-level personnel in the front and secondary.

## Scheme and tendencies

The defensive foundation is Shula continuity plus upgraded pass-rush and coverage talent. The Rams added Myles Garrett to a front that already included Kobie Turner, Braden Fiske, Byron Young, Jared Verse, and a pass-rush coordinator in Drew Wilkins. They also reset cornerback with Trent McDuffie and Jaylen Watson while promoting Jimmy Lake to pass game coordinator/defensive backs.

## Personnel usage

Garrett changes protection math and should create more favorable rush looks for Verse, Young, Turner, and Fiske. McDuffie and Watson give the defense a new starting-corner baseline, while Quentin Lake and Kam Curl give the safety/nickel structure continuity after both were retained or extended. The unresolved defensive questions are exact corner rotation, nickel/dime usage, and how Shula balances Garrett with the existing young edge pair.

## Pace and situational decisions

Game script matters. Rams reporting tied Garrett's fit to an offense capable of playing with leads, which can create more obvious pass-rush situations. That is an inference from roster construction and prior scoring environment, not a guaranteed weekly script.

## Fantasy implications

The Rams D/ST profile rises because pass rush and corner play were both materially upgraded. Opposing quarterbacks should be evaluated for sack and turnover risk when game script allows Garrett, Verse, Young, Turner, and Fiske to rush in known passing situations. IDP value is more dispersed across the front, while Lake/Curl remain defensive-back tackle and coverage-snap watches.

## Statements and reporting

Official Rams reporting says Garrett brought 125.5 career sacks to Los Angeles and had set an NFL single-season record with 23 sacks in 2025. The cornerback reset article says McDuffie becomes the star of the cornerback room and Watson adds starting experience.

## Reliability cautions

The staff continuity and personnel acquisitions are official. The D/ST ceiling is a projection until the defense shows early-season pressure rate, turnover rate, and coverage cohesion with the new cornerback group.
