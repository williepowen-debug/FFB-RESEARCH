# Nate Scheelhaase - Los Angeles Rams Offensive Coordinator

- Role: Offensive coordinator under Sean McVay
- Season: 2026

## Responsibilities

Scheelhaase is the Rams' 2026 offensive coordinator after serving as pass game coordinator in 2025. The official staff announcement notes this is his third season on the Rams staff and says he received outside head-coach and offensive-coordinator interview interest before the promotion.

## Scheme and tendencies

The coordinator profile should be read as McVay system continuity with a promoted pass-game voice. The Rams' own materials say the 2025 offense led the NFL in total offense and scoring offense, and the 2026 draft/position-reset articles show the club intentionally sustaining the multiple-tight-end structure that emerged late in 2025.

## Personnel usage

Scheelhaase inherits Stafford as QB1, Nacua and Adams as high-end receiver anchors, Williams and Corum as the primary running-back rotation, and a tight-end room expanded by Max Klare. The key 2026 usage question is whether the Rams keep 13 personnel as a central identity or make it a weekly matchup tool while returning more WR3 routes to Jordan Whittington, Xavier Smith, CJ Daniels, or another receiver.

## Fantasy implications

Scheelhaase's promotion is fantasy-positive if it preserves the 2025 scoring environment while adding enough formation flexibility to keep defenses from overloading Nacua and Adams. It is neutral-to-negative for WR3 speculation if heavy tight-end usage continues to erase the third receiver as a weekly full-time role. Klare is a dynasty/watch-list tight end rather than a confirmed 2026 redraft starter because the veterans are still present.
