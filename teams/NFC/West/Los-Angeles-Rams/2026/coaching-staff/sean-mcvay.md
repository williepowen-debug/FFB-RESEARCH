# Sean McVay - Los Angeles Rams Head Coach

- Role: Head coach and offensive play-calling authority
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: hired as head coach in 2017
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

McVay remains the Rams' head coach entering his 10th season. The official staff announcement frames 2026 as continuity at the top with new offensive support around him: Kliff Kingsbury as assistant head coach, Nate Scheelhaase promoted to offensive coordinator, and Dave Ragone elevated to quarterbacks/associate coordinator. The same official staff article says McVay's 2025 team reached the playoffs and double-digit wins, giving the 2026 build a contention baseline rather than a rebuild posture.

## Scheme and tendencies

The Rams' own 2026 materials describe a 2025 offense that finished No. 1 in total offense and scoring offense, with Matthew Stafford winning MVP after leading the league in passing yards and passing touchdowns. The durable 2026 identity is still McVay's offense, but the personnel evidence points to heavier multiplicity than a static three-receiver label: Los Angeles publicly connected Max Klare's second-round selection to building on 2025's multiple-tight-end foundation.

## Personnel usage

Stafford is the clear QB1. Rams camp preview material says the unresolved quarterback question is the backup job between first-round rookie Ty Simpson and Stetson Bennett IV. The fantasy-relevant skill core is Puka Nacua and Davante Adams at receiver, Kyren Williams and Blake Corum at running back, and a deep tight-end group led by Tyler Higbee, Colby Parkinson, Davis Allen, Terrance Ferguson, and Klare.

## Pace and situational decisions

No 2026 regular-season pace sample exists yet. The current working assumption is that McVay will continue building through Stafford's timing, play action, motion, and formation stress, with 13 personnel remaining part of the weekly menu. The validation point is not whether the Rams use tight ends; it is whether those packages reduce WR3 routes enough to cap non-Nacua/Adams receiver utility.

## Fantasy implications

Nacua, Adams, Stafford, Williams, and Corum should be evaluated inside a high-scoring offensive environment, not a volume-starved one. The key fantasy uncertainty is distribution: if 13 personnel remains a weekly base tendency, tight-end snaps can suppress the third wide receiver while preserving high-value targets for Nacua and Adams. Simpson is dynasty-relevant but not a redraft baseline unless Stafford is unavailable.

## Statements and reporting

Official Rams reporting says McVay emphasized after the Simpson selection that 2026 is still Stafford's team. Rams staff reporting also says Scheelhaase's promotion came after outside interest, while the addition of Kingsbury and Brian Johnson deepens the offensive idea room.

## Reliability cautions

Staff roles, Stafford's QB1 status, and the draft additions are official. Personnel usage assumptions around 13 personnel and target distribution must be checked against preseason first-team formations and early-season route data.
