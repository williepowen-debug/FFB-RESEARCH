# Finding: Stafford remains the QB1 of a high-end scoring ecosystem

## Scope

- Team/player: Los Angeles Rams offense
- Area: quarterback, wide receiver, running back, and primary fantasy environment
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Matthew Stafford is the Rams' confirmed QB1 after a 2025 MVP season, while Ty Simpson's first-round selection is framed by the team as a short- and long-term quarterback-room move rather than an immediate starting competition. The 2026 fantasy baseline should treat Puka Nacua, Davante Adams, Kyren Williams, Blake Corum, and the tight-end group as pieces inside a high-functioning McVay offense.

## Fantasy implication

Stafford is viable in superflex and schedule-driven redraft builds because the Rams preserved a premium scoring environment. Nacua and Adams remain the first passing-game read for fantasy purposes. Williams is still the lead running-back bet, but Corum's 2025 production and the team's public framing of a Williams-Corum-Rivers rotation make a full monopoly workload less certain.

## Evidence

### Facts

- Rams camp preview material called Stafford the unquestioned QB1 and noted his 2025 MVP/First-Team All-Pro season with 4,707 passing yards and 46 passing touchdowns.
- The Rams selected Ty Simpson 13th overall and McVay said Simpson would compete with Stetson Bennett IV for the backup job.
- Rams receiver preview material said Nacua led the NFL with 129 catches in 2025 and Adams led the league with 14 receiving touchdowns.
- Rams running-back preview material listed Williams' 2025 line as 259 carries for 1,252 yards and 10 rushing touchdowns, plus 36 catches for 281 yards and three touchdowns. It listed Corum with 145 carries for 746 yards and six rushing touchdowns.

### Inferences

- The Stafford/Nacua/Adams spine gives the Rams a stronger weekly passing baseline than most teams.
- Corum's 2025 workload makes him more than a pure handcuff, but Williams remains the first back to value unless camp usage says otherwise.
- Simpson is dynasty-relevant and redraft-contingent.
