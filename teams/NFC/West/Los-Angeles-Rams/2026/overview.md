# Los Angeles Rams - 2026 Stafford Core And Defensive Star Additions

- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Scope: 2026 preseason Core-tier team module

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Sean McVay remains head coach; Nate Scheelhaase was promoted to offensive coordinator; Chris Shula remains defensive coordinator.
- Stafford is the 2026 QB1. The Rams drafted Ty Simpson at No. 13 overall, but official team reporting frames him as competing with Stetson Bennett IV for the backup job behind Stafford.
- Nacua and Adams remain the receiver anchors after Nacua led the NFL in receptions and Adams led the NFL in receiving touchdowns in 2025.
- Williams and Corum return atop a running back room that the Rams described as led by Williams, Corum, and Ronnie Rivers.
- The tight-end room remains deep after Tyler Higbee re-signed and Max Klare arrived in Round 2.
- The defense added Garrett, McDuffie, and Jaylen Watson while retaining/renewing safety stability through Quentin Lake and Kam Curl.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

These are projections to test, not established 2026 outcomes:

- Stafford/Nacua/Adams is the safest passing-game spine; Simpson is primarily dynasty and contingency value.
- Williams is the lead running-back projection, but Corum's 2025 workload supports a real complementary role.
- Heavy tight-end usage can keep the offense efficient while making the WR3 role hard to trust weekly.
- Rams D/ST has a higher sack/turnover ceiling after Garrett and McDuffie, especially in positive game scripts.
- Lake and Curl are cleaner IDP watches than the new cornerbacks until sub-package roles are charted.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

The Rams promoted Scheelhaase after he served as pass game coordinator and added Kingsbury and Brian Johnson as senior offensive support. McVay remains the offensive authority. Stafford's 2025 MVP baseline, Nacua/Adams production, and the Williams/Corum backfield give Los Angeles one of the more stable fantasy ecosystems in the league.

The main structural question is personnel grouping. Rams draft reporting tied Klare's selection to a 2025 offense that used 13 personnel at a historic rate, and official receiver/tight-end previews both emphasized how multiple tight ends complicate the WR3 label.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Stafford handle a normal starter workload through camp after the 2025 back-injury context?
- How much high-value work does Corum take from Williams?
- Does 13 personnel remain a core identity or become more matchup-specific?
- Which receiver, if any, separates behind Nacua and Adams?
- Does Klare earn immediate game-day route work in a crowded tight-end room?

The durable offensive baseline and role hypotheses are maintained in the [offensive ecosystem module](offense/README.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

The defensive staff is stable at coordinator but materially different in personnel. Garrett arrives as an elite edge after an official transaction agreement with Cleveland. The cornerback room was reset through McDuffie and Watson after Durant, Witherspoon, McCreary, and Williams left the 2025 baseline. Lake and Curl create safety/nickel continuity.

### Questions to resolve

- How does Shula align Garrett with Verse, Young, Turner, and Fiske?
- Does the pass rush translate immediately into D/ST sack and turnover production?
- Are McDuffie and Watson the stable starting corner structure from Week 1?
- How much nickel/box work do Lake and Curl carry?
- Does new secondary communication create any early explosive-play risk?

The durable defensive baseline and role hypotheses are maintained in the [defensive ecosystem module](defense/README.md).

## Coaching and scheme

Los Angeles' 2026 coordinator structure is:

| Unit | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Head coach | Sean McVay | Sean McVay |
| Offense | Mike LaFleur | Nate Scheelhaase |
| Defense | Chris Shula | Chris Shula |
| Special teams | Chase Blackburn / Ben Kotwica interim | Bubba Ventrone |

The analytical frame is offensive continuity with redistributed staff titles, plus defensive continuity supercharged by personnel additions. McVay and Stafford keep the offensive floor high; Scheelhaase, Kingsbury, and Johnson are offensive support variables, not a wholesale scheme reset. Shula's job is to convert Garrett and the cornerback additions into pressure/coverage synergy without losing the safety stability that Lake and Curl provide.

### Analytical projection

The reasonable preseason prior is a Rams offense that remains fantasy-friendly but condensed around Stafford, Nacua, Adams, Williams, Corum, and the tight ends. The defense should move up D/ST draft boards because star pass rush and corner upgrades create more paths to sacks and turnovers. Verify through preseason personnel grouping, route participation, backfield usage, Garrett alignment, and first-team secondary snaps.

## Special teams

Bubba Ventrone replaces the 2025 special-teams leadership structure. Harrison Mevis was tendered as an exclusive rights free agent after taking over the kicker role in 2025, and Joe Cardona was added at long snapper. At Core tier, no separate special-teams research record is maintained.
