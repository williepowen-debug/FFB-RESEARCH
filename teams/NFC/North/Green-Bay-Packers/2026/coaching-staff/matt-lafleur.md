## Responsibilities

LaFleur remains the franchise-level authority over Green Bay's 2026 staff, with Adam Stenavich retained as offensive coordinator, Jonathan Gannon hired as defensive coordinator, and Cam Achord hired as special teams coordinator. The official coaches roster lists LaFleur as head coach and Stenavich as offensive coordinator, while the team announced Gannon on Feb. 2 and Achord on Feb. 27.

## Scheme and tendencies

The offense remains a LaFleur/Stenavich system centered on Jordan Love, Josh Jacobs, a deep receiver rotation, and tight-end usage that depends heavily on Tucker Kraft's health. The largest 2026 staff change is not offensive philosophy but quarterback-room support: Luke Getsy moved to quarterbacks coach/assistant head coach - offense after Sean Mannion left for Philadelphia.

On defense, LaFleur has delegated a meaningful reset to Gannon. Packers reporting described Gannon as LaFleur's fourth defensive coordinator in eight seasons and emphasized that Gannon is building a player-specific system rather than importing a fixed playbook.
