# Jonathan Gannon - Green Bay Packers

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Gannon was named defensive coordinator on Feb. 2, 2026, replacing Jeff Hafley after Hafley became Miami's head coach. He oversees a defensive staff that includes Bobby Babich as secondary/pass game coordinator, Daniel Bullocks at cornerbacks, DeMarcus Covington as outside linebackers/run game coordinator/assistant head coach - defense, Vince Oghobaase at defensive line, and Sam Siefkes at linebackers.

## Scheme and tendencies

Team reporting says Green Bay is shifting back toward a 3-4 base front, but Gannon has stressed a player-driven system rather than a fixed imported playbook. Packers reporting quoted his focus on a fast, physical defense that takes the ball away and noted he was tailoring usage for Micah Parsons, Edgerrin Cooper, and Xavier McKinney.

## Personnel usage

The major 2026 inputs are Hargrave and Devonte Wyatt inside, Franklin as the veteran linebacker/communicator next to Cooper, Van Ness/Sorrell/Oliver/Dennis-Sutton as edge options while Parsons recovers, and a secondary led by McKinney, Evan Williams, Javon Bullard, Keisean Nixon, plus a cornerback competition involving Valentine, Cisse, and St-Juste.

## Pace and situational decisions

No regular-season Gannon/Packers sample exists. Early charting should prioritize base nickel/safety packages, blitz rate, Parsons' ramp, Franklin/Cooper alignment, and which cornerbacks stay on the field in two-minute and third-down situations.

## Fantasy implications

The D/ST profile has more ceiling than a generic transition defense because Gannon has play-calling experience, Hargrave/Franklin fit his history, McKinney is a high-impact safety, and Parsons can be a premium pressure source when healthy. The early fantasy risk is that Parsons is not expected back until several games into the regular season, leaving young edge players and the cornerback competition to determine the Week 1 floor.

## Statements and reporting

The Packers' Feb. 2 announcement cited Gannon's Philadelphia defensive production and NFL background. A May team feature said the 2026 defense would be built for Green Bay's current personnel. Training-camp reporting identified Franklin's headset role, Hargrave's interior fit, the cornerback battle, and Parsons' delayed return as priority storylines.
