# Green Bay Packers 2026 Coaching Staff

The 2026 Packers are an offensive-continuity and defensive-transition team. Matt LaFleur and Adam Stenavich remain the offensive authority points, while Jonathan Gannon replaces Jeff Hafley on defense and Cam Achord replaces Rich Bisaccia on special teams.

## Authority Model

- Head coach: [Matt LaFleur](matt-lafleur.md)
- Offensive coordinator: [Adam Stenavich](adam-stenavich.md)
- Defensive coordinator: [Jonathan Gannon](jonathan-gannon.md)
- Special teams coordinator: Cam Achord
