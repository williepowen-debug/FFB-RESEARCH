# Green Bay Packers - 2026 Offensive Continuity and Gannon Defensive Transition

- Conference/division: NFC North
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Scope: changes from the 2025 team to the 2026 preseason roster and staff
- Build tier: Core

Green Bay is a strong fantasy environment with continuity on offense and a more meaningful defensive reset. Matt LaFleur and Adam Stenavich remain the offensive authority points for Jordan Love, Josh Jacobs, Christian Watson, Jayden Reed, Matthew Golden, Tucker Kraft, and Luke Musgrave. The defense changes more: Jonathan Gannon replaces Jeff Hafley, Javon Hargrave and Zaire Franklin arrive, Rashan Gary and Colby Wooden are gone, and Micah Parsons' return timeline shapes the early D/ST projection.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- LaFleur remains head coach and Stenavich remains offensive coordinator.
- Gannon is the new defensive coordinator, and Achord is the new special teams coordinator.
- Love remains QB1, with Tyrod Taylor added as the veteran backup.
- Jacobs remains the rushing and touchdown baseline, while Brooks, Lloyd, Strong, Martinez, and rookie Jaden Nixon form the depth competition.
- Watson and Reed were extended, Golden has a larger Year 2 opportunity, and Doubs/Wicks are no longer in the receiver room.
- Kraft was activated off PUP on Jul. 31 after his 2025 ACL injury and remains the highest-upside tight-end variable.
- Hargrave and Franklin were added to the defense, Wyatt and McDuffie were extended, Gary and Wooden were traded, and Parsons' return is not expected until several games into the regular season.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

- Love's efficiency and touchdown environment remain strong, but his pass-catcher distribution requires route evidence.
- Jacobs remains a high-end RB projection unless Green Bay changes his high-value touches.
- Watson/Reed/Golden/Kraft are all draftable profiles; rank order should wait for first-team route, health, and red-zone evidence.
- Lloyd remains an upside backup/dynasty monitor rather than a bankable redraft role.
- Packers D/ST has a higher ceiling under Gannon, but early-season streaming confidence depends on pressure before Parsons returns.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

The offensive coordinator did not change. The support staff did: Luke Getsy moved to quarterbacks coach/assistant head coach - offense after Sean Mannion left, and Noah Pauley is the new wide receivers coach. Personnel changes are more important than staff changes. Doubs departed, Wicks was traded, Watson/Reed were extended, Taylor replaced the backup-quarterback uncertainty, and the line is being reworked after Jenkins' release with Rhyan at center and Morgan at left tackle.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Love keep the 2025 efficiency step with a healthy receiving group?
- Which of Watson, Reed, Golden, and Kraft leads first-team route and target priority?
- How quickly does Kraft move from ACL ramp to full game-plan usage?
- Does Jacobs retain goal-line and passing-down work, or do Brooks/Lloyd siphon specific packages?
- Does the Rhyan/Morgan line configuration stabilize early enough to support deep-shot and play-action efficiency?

The evidence and invalidation tests are maintained in the [offensive research module](offense/README.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Gannon replaces Hafley and is building a Green Bay-specific system. The front is being reshaped around Hargrave, Wyatt, Franklin, Cooper, McKinney, and a young edge group until Parsons returns. The draft added Cisse, McClellan, Dennis-Sutton, and Domani Jackson on defense, while training-camp reporting identified cornerback opposite Keisean Nixon as an open competition.

### Questions to resolve

- Can Van Ness, Sorrell, Oliver, Dennis-Sutton, and Cox create enough early pressure without Parsons?
- Does Franklin become the full-time communication and tackle-volume anchor next to Cooper?
- Which of Valentine, Cisse, and St-Juste wins outside-corner snaps opposite Nixon?
- How often does Gannon deploy the McKinney/Williams/Bullard safety trio as a nickel/base answer?

Green Bay should not be treated as a simple matchup upgrade for opposing offenses, but Week 1 D/ST grades should be tied to pressure evidence and cornerback stability. See the [defensive research module](defense/README.md).

## Coaching and scheme

| Unit | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Head coach | Matt LaFleur | Matt LaFleur |
| Offense/play-caller | Adam Stenavich / Matt LaFleur | Adam Stenavich / Matt LaFleur |
| Defense/play-caller | Jeff Hafley | Jonathan Gannon |
| Special teams | Rich Bisaccia | Cam Achord |

LaFleur and Stenavich give the offense a stronger projection floor than a coordinator-change team. Gannon is the main scheme variable and the main D/ST upside case. Official team reporting frames his defense as player-specific and flexible, not a one-for-one import from Philadelphia or Arizona.

The complete staff and authority model are in the [coaching intelligence module](coaching-staff/README.md).

### Analytical projection

Treat the Packers as a good offense with unresolved target allocation. Love and Jacobs are the baseline fantasy pieces. Watson, Reed, Golden, and Kraft are all meaningful draft decisions, but their preseason route and health data should drive ranking. The defense is more matchup-sensitive: Gannon, Hargrave, Franklin, McKinney, Cooper, and eventually Parsons create ceiling, while Parsons' delayed return and cornerback competition lower early certainty.

## Special teams

Achord replaces Bisaccia as special teams coordinator. Skyy Moore was signed with team reporting identifying him as the full-time returner, and rookie Trey Smack is the kicking monitor after Green Bay released Brandon McManus and Lucas Havrisik. Special teams remains Core-tier background unless return or kicking roles become fantasy-relevant in weekly files.
