# Finding: Safety depth is a strength, while outside corner remains the key coverage question

## Scope

- Team/player: Packers secondary
- Area: coverage personnel and opposing pass-game matchup
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

The Packers secondary has a clear safety strength and a live cornerback competition. Training-camp reporting said Gannon has deployed Xavier McKinney, Evan Williams, and Javon Bullard heavily, while the training-camp preview identified Carrington Valentine, Brandon Cisse, and Benjamin St-Juste as the battle to start opposite Keisean Nixon in the base defense. Bobby Babich and Daniel Bullocks are new 2026 defensive assistants with pass-game and cornerback responsibility.

## Fantasy implication

Opposing pass games should not be blanket-upgraded against Green Bay because McKinney, Williams, Bullard, and Gannon's disguise can create difficult middle-of-field and turnover conditions. The specific weekly angle is outside receiver matchups: until the cornerback order stabilizes, WR1/WR2 projections should account for which corner is on the field and whether Green Bay protects that side with safety help.

## Evidence

- Packers coaching-staff reporting announced Babich as secondary/pass game coordinator and Bullocks as cornerbacks coach on Mar. 19.
- The training-camp preview identified Valentine, Cisse, and St-Juste as the outside-corner competition opposite Nixon.
- July 30 camp reporting said Gannon was using the McKinney/Williams/Bullard safety trio and that nickel packages may effectively be the defensive base.
- The official roster verified McKinney, Williams, Bullard, Nixon, Valentine, Cisse, St-Juste, Domani Jackson, and other defensive backs on the active roster page.
