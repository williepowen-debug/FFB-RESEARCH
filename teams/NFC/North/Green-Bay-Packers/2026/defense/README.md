# Green Bay Packers 2026 Defense

Green Bay's defensive module is built around Jonathan Gannon's coordinator transition, a rebuilt front with Javon Hargrave and Zaire Franklin, Micah Parsons' delayed injury ramp, and a secondary with strong safety depth but an unsettled outside-corner competition.

## Files

- [`gannon-front-transition.md`](gannon-front-transition.md): front-seven and D/ST transition finding.
- [`secondary-competition.md`](secondary-competition.md): safety usage and outside-corner competition finding.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable defensive questions to test in preseason and Week 1.
