## Interpretation Notes

The largest fantasy-relevant offensive changes are DJ Moore leaving the target tree, Garrett Bradbury replacing retired center Drew Dalman, and the left-tackle competition around Braxton Jones, Jedrick Wills Jr., and Ozzy Trapilo's health. The defense reset is heavier in the back seven: Coby Bryant, Devin Bush, Cam Lewis, Dillon Thieneman, Malik Muhammad, and Keyshaun Elliott replace or supplement a unit that lost Tremaine Edmunds, Jaquan Brisker, Kevin Byard, Chauncey Gardner-Johnson, and depth defensive backs.
