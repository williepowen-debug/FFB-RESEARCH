# Chicago Bears 2026 Offense

Chicago enters 2026 with strong system continuity and a reshaped target tree. Ben Johnson remains the offensive authority, Press Taylor was promoted after serving as pass game coordinator, Caleb Williams is in Year 2 of Johnson's system, and DJ Moore's trade moves more opportunity toward Rome Odunze, Luther Burden III, Colston Loveland, and Cole Kmet. The run game returns D'Andre Swift and Kyle Monangai, but center and left tackle are the main stability checkpoints.

## Fantasy Framing

The Bears should be treated as a high-upside offense with enough concentration questions to monitor closely in August. Williams, Odunze, Burden, Loveland, Swift, and Monangai all have plausible fantasy paths; the tiebreakers are first-team route rates, two-tight-end usage, red-zone backfield distribution, and whether Garrett Bradbury plus the left-tackle winner keep the offense on schedule.
