# Chicago Bears 2026 Defense

Chicago's 2026 defense is a continuity-plus-reset unit. Dennis Allen remains defensive coordinator, but the Bears changed the safety and linebacker mix around a front that needs healthier output from Montez Sweat, Austin Booker, Gervon Dexter Sr., Grady Jarrett, Dayo Odeyingbo, and Shemar Turner. Coby Bryant, Devin Bush, Dillon Thieneman, Cam Lewis, Malik Muhammad, and Jordan van den Berg are the key new inputs.

## Fantasy Framing

The Bears are a defensive rebound candidate, not an automatic matchup downgrade. The DST case depends on pressure arriving without excessive blitzing and a new safety group communicating cleanly. IDP monitoring should start with Devin Bush's every-down role, Dillon Thieneman's alignment, and Austin Booker's rush workload.
