# Dennis Allen - Chicago Bears Defensive Coordinator

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2025
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Allen remains Chicago's defensive coordinator on the official staff page. His 2026 remit is to stabilize a defense that changed major personnel at linebacker and safety while getting more from a healthier defensive line.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2026 defense is framed around speed, aggression, and communication. Official team reporting described Coby Bryant as a tone-setter Allen valued for play style, intelligence, and physicality, and described first-round safety Dillon Thieneman as a fast, versatile defender who can play strong safety, free safety, and nickel.

## Personnel usage

The back seven changed materially. Devin Bush replaces some of the linebacker range and production lost with Tremaine Edmunds' departure, while Bryant, Thieneman, Cam Lewis, Xavier Woods, and Malik Muhammad join a secondary still depending on Jaylon Johnson and Kyler Gordon returning from injury-affected 2025 seasons. Up front, Montez Sweat, Austin Booker, Gervon Dexter Sr., Grady Jarrett, Dayo Odeyingbo, Shemar Turner, Neville Gallimore, and Jordan van den Berg give Allen more pass-rush variables if health cooperates.

## Pace and situational decisions

No 2026 game sample exists yet. The preseason read should focus on pressure sources, safety rotation, nickel alignment, and whether Allen can create heat without exposing new defensive backs.

## Fantasy implications

- Bears D/ST has a credible rebound profile because the front's top 2025 sack producers return and the secondary was rebuilt with Bryant and Thieneman.
- Opposing quarterbacks should not be downgraded aggressively until Chicago proves pressure and coverage cohesion.
- IDP watch points are Bush's every-down status, Thieneman's box/nickel role, and whether Booker earns a stable rush workload opposite Sweat.
- Injuries to Bryant, Johnson, Gordon, or the edge group would quickly move the defense back toward a weekly matchup target.

## Statements and reporting

Allen publicly praised Bryant's speed, physicality, intelligence, and instincts at the start of training camp. Official defensive-back preview reporting also said Allen saw Thieneman repeatedly around the ball on college tape.
