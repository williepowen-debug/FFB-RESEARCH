# Ben Johnson - Chicago Bears Head Coach

- Role: Head coach and offensive authority
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: named head coach on 2025-01-21
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Johnson remains the top football authority and the central offensive designer. The official staff page says he was named head coach on 2025-01-21 and credits his first Bears season with an NFC North title, 11 regular-season wins, 12 total wins, and a postseason victory.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2026 baseline is continuity with an adjusted offensive staff. Press Taylor was promoted after serving as pass game coordinator in 2025, while Johnson still frames the offensive development around Caleb Williams' comfort in repeated concepts. Official team reporting said Williams set a franchise single-season passing record with 3,942 yards in 2025, and that the offense scored 441 points, the third-most in Bears history.

## Personnel usage

The highest-value offensive question is how Johnson redistributes DJ Moore's vacated work. Rome Odunze, Luther Burden III, Colston Loveland, Cole Kmet, Kalif Raymond, and rookie Zavion Thomas form a deep but unsettled pass-catching ecosystem. D'Andre Swift and Kyle Monangai return after combining as the only NFL running back duo to each rush for 750-plus yards in 2025.

## Pace and situational decisions

The Bears were exceptional late in games in 2025, with official team reporting crediting Williams and the offense for seven wins after trailing in the final two minutes, but Johnson identified end-of-half offense as a 2026 improvement point after Chicago ranked 27th in points in the final two minutes of the first half.

## Fantasy implications

- Williams starts 2026 with a strong coaching-continuity case and a deeper understanding of Johnson's offense.
- Odunze, Burden, and Loveland are the primary upside bets after Moore's trade, but early route and target concentration must be verified.
- Swift and Monangai can both matter if Johnson keeps a top rushing environment, though split high-value touches cap each player's weekly floor.
- Offensive-line stability, especially left tackle and Bradbury's transition at center, is the main floor risk.

## Statements and reporting

Official Bears reporting says Johnson believes Williams is past the pre-snap survival stage and can now focus more on post-snap play. Training-camp reporting also noted Johnson's emphasis on improving end-of-half execution.

## Reliability cautions

The staff and 2025 production facts are strong. The fantasy distribution is an inference until preseason first-team usage and Week 1 route/target shares are available.
