# Chicago Bears 2026 Coaching Intelligence

Ben Johnson remains the primary offensive authority after a first Bears season that produced an NFC North title, a playoff win, and a 441-point offense. The main 2026 staff change is on offense: Press Taylor was promoted from pass game coordinator to offensive coordinator after Declan Doyle left for Baltimore, and Eric Studesville replaced Eric Bieniemy as running backs coach. Dennis Allen and Richard Hightower remain the defensive and special teams coordinators.

## Fantasy Read

The offensive staff carries more continuity than the roster suggests. Taylor's promotion keeps the 2025 passing-game voice in-house, while Studesville adds a veteran running backs coach to a Swift/Monangai backfield that already fit Johnson's run game. Allen's defense has more personnel churn and should be treated as a rebound candidate, not a proven matchup downgrade, until pressure and secondary roles stabilize.
