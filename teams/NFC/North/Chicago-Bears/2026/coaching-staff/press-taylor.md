# Press Taylor - Chicago Bears Offensive Coordinator

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2025 as pass game coordinator; promoted 2026-02-18
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Taylor was elevated to offensive coordinator after Declan Doyle left for Baltimore. The Bears described Taylor as entering his second season in Chicago after serving as pass game coordinator in 2025.

## Scheme and tendencies

Taylor inherits an offense that already produced with Johnson as the central designer. Team reporting credits the 2025 passing attack with Williams' 3,942-yard franchise record, and credits the 2025 offense with the league's fewest giveaways, the second-most points off takeaways, and 441 total points. That makes the 2026 offensive problem more about role consolidation and counters than installing a new system.

## Personnel usage

Taylor's first fantasy-relevant test is replacing Moore's vacated target volume without flattening the target tree too far. Odunze and Burden are the top wideout bets, Loveland led the 2025 team with 58 catches for 713 yards and six touchdowns as a rookie, and Kmet remains a proven tight end who can keep 12 personnel viable.

## Pace and situational decisions

No independent Taylor play-calling sample exists in Chicago because Johnson remains the head coach and offensive authority. Treat Taylor as an implementation and passing-game influence unless reporting shows he has taken over play-calling.

## Fantasy implications

- Taylor continuity supports Williams' Year 3 projection because the quarterback keeps a familiar passing-game voice.
- Loveland, Odunze, and Burden should be monitored as the three main beneficiaries of Moore leaving.
- Kmet and rookie Sam Roush can affect personnel grouping; 12 personnel would help real football flexibility but can reduce WR3 routes.
- Raymond and Zavion Thomas are best-ball/return-role watchlist names rather than baseline redraft priorities.

## Statements and reporting
