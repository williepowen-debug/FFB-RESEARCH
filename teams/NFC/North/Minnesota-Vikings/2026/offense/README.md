# Minnesota Vikings 2026 Offense

Minnesota's offensive module is built around a quarterback transition inside a familiar Kevin O'Connell and Wes Phillips structure. The core skill group is strong enough for immediate fantasy relevance, but the weekly ceiling depends on Kyler Murray's command of the system, Blake Brandel's center transition, and how the staff allocates work behind Justin Jefferson.

## Baseline

Facts: Murray was signed in March, Jefferson and Addison remain on the roster, Jennings was added, Hockenson returns, and Jones/Mason lead the running back room. The projected offensive line has established tackles in Darrisaw and O'Neill, while Brandel is transitioning to center after Kelly's retirement; multiple inaccurate camp exchanges are now documented. Inference: the offense should remain fantasy-friendly if Murray processes the system quickly, but center execution and the secondary receiving and backfield roles need preseason and early-season confirmation.
