# Finding: Greenard's exit makes Dallas Turner and the rookie interior the front's swing factors

## Scope

- Team/player: Vikings defensive front
- Area: pass rush, interior rotation, and D/ST volatility
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Minnesota traded Jonathan Greenard on 2026-04-24 after he led the 2025 team with 47 pressures in 12 games. The Vikings also released veteran defensive linemen Jonathan Allen and Javon Hargrave, then drafted Caleb Banks at No. 18 and Domonique Orange at No. 82. The 2026 front is still a Brian Flores pressure structure, but its production now depends on Dallas Turner taking more edge responsibility and the rookie interior delivering usable snaps quickly.

## Fantasy implication

The Vikings D/ST should be treated as volatile rather than locked in. Flores keeps weekly pressure upside alive, especially against weaker offensive lines or young quarterbacks, but Greenard's departure lowers the proven pressure floor. In IDP, Turner is the main breakout watch; Banks and Orange are deeper-format or dynasty defensive-line monitors until snap share is clear.

## Evidence

### Facts

- The Vikings traded Greenard and pick No. 244 to Philadelphia for pick No. 98 in 2026 and a 2027 third-round pick.
- Vikings.com said Greenard led Minnesota with 47 pressures in 2025 and that his departure likely means increased responsibility for Turner.
- Minnesota released Allen and Hargrave on 2026-03-11.
- The Vikings selected Banks in Round 1 and Orange in Round 3.
- Vikings.com draft recap quoted O'Connell saying the team wanted to add talented big-body defensive linemen who can do different jobs.

### Inferences

- Turner is the most important pressure-rate variable on the roster.
- Banks and Orange may improve run defense before they become stable fantasy pressure contributors.
- Flores can create pressure schematically, but elite D/ST weeks need individual wins from the new front.
