# Minnesota Vikings 2026 Defense

Minnesota's defense is coordinator continuity with personnel churn. Brian Flores remains the play-caller, but the front lost Jonathan Greenard, Jonathan Allen, and Javon Hargrave, while the secondary moved on from Harrison Smith. The 2026 fantasy profile depends on Dallas Turner's Year 3 jump, rookie defensive-line snaps from Caleb Banks and Domonique Orange, and whether the safety group keeps enough versatility for Flores' disguise package.

- [`secondary-and-versatility.md`](secondary-and-versatility.md): post-Smith coverage and safety/corner depth questions.

## Baseline

Facts: Flores is retained, Greenard was traded, Smith/Allen/Hargrave were released, and Minnesota used premium picks on Banks, Golday, Orange, Jakobe Thomas, and Charles Demmings. Inference: the unit can remain disruptive, but its week-to-week fantasy floor is less bankable until the new front and secondary roles are proven.
