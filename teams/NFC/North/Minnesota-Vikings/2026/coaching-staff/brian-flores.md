# Brian Flores - Minnesota Vikings Defensive Coordinator

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: retained from 2025
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Flores is listed by the Vikings as defensive coordinator and remains the defensive play-calling authority. His 2026 staff adds Ryan Nielsen as defensive running game coordinator and keeps Gerald Alexander, Thad Bogardus, Mike Siravo, and Patrick Hill in key position-support roles.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2026 defense is continuity in coordinator but turnover in personnel. Minnesota traded Jonathan Greenard, released Harrison Smith, Jonathan Allen, and Javon Hargrave, then spent five of its first seven draft selections on defense: Caleb Banks, Jake Golday, Domonique Orange, Jakobe Thomas, and Charles Demmings. Official team draft analysis quoted O'Connell describing Banks and Orange as big-body defensive-line additions who can do multiple jobs.

## Personnel usage

Dallas Turner is the major edge usage watch after the Greenard trade. The official Greenard trade story said the move likely means increased responsibility for Turner and cited Turner's larger 2025 snap load and statistical jumps. Inside, Banks and Orange should be monitored for early-down and pressure-package roles. In the secondary, Harrison Smith's release and Jamal Adams' reported preseason injury make safety versatility a live weekly issue.

## Pace and situational decisions

Defensive pace is opponent-driven, but Flores' unit affects fantasy through pressure, disguise, and turnover creation. The profile should be verified by charting blitz rate, simulated-pressure frequency, rookie defensive-line usage, and nickel/safety roles in the first month.

## Fantasy implications

- Minnesota D/ST has volatility and upside if Turner replaces enough of Greenard's pressure and the rookie interior linemen contribute early.
- Opposing quarterbacks may face a still-aggressive Flores plan, but rebuilt front and safety depth create uncertainty.
- IDP managers should monitor Turner, Ivan Pace Jr., Blake Cashman, Metellus, Banks, and Jakobe Thomas for role consolidation.
- The Greenard exit is a real pass-rush risk until Turner and the new interior rotation prove stable.

## Statements and reporting

Official Vikings reporting directly connected Greenard's departure to increased Turner responsibility. Vikings.com UDFA coverage described Jacob Thomas as a potential Flores fit because of his versatility across box, free safety, slot, line, and perimeter alignments at James Madison.
