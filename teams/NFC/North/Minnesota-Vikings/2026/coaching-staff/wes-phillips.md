# Wes Phillips - Minnesota Vikings Offensive Coordinator

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: retained from O'Connell staff
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Phillips is listed by the Vikings as offensive coordinator. O'Connell remains the head coach and offensive authority, while Phillips supports game-planning, installation, and offensive staff coordination with Josh McCown, Curtis Modkins, Keith Carter, Keenan McCardell, Ryan Cordell, and Chris O'Hara.

## Scheme and tendencies

Minnesota's 2026 offensive work is not a coordinator change; it is a personnel and quarterback adaptation inside the existing O'Connell/Phillips structure. The official quarterback preview put the central issue on Murray or McCarthy mastering a complex system. The offensive-line preview framed Blake Brandel's center work, Donovan Jackson's Year 2 growth, and tackle depth as important supports for whichever quarterback wins.

## Personnel usage

The passing-game hierarchy begins with Jefferson and Addison, with Jennings added as a bigger veteran receiver and Hockenson remaining the most important tight end. The backfield returns Jones and Mason, with rookie Demond Claiborne competing for depth or return value. Phillips' fantasy-relevant impact is whether the staff builds more Murray movement and quick-game answers while preserving Jefferson's downfield and intermediate target volume.

## Pace and situational decisions

No 2026 pace conclusion should be drawn before regular-season play. The first checks should be first-team preseason tempo, shotgun/under-center mix with Murray, and red-zone grouping with Hockenson, Jennings, Jones, Mason, and rookie fullback Max Bredeson.

## Fantasy implications

- If Murray's processing and protection checks stabilize, the offense can support Jefferson as an elite WR1, Addison as a weekly starter, and Hockenson as a top-tier tight end.
- Jennings is a role bet, not a volume lock, until route participation and red-zone usage are charted.
- Jones and Mason may cap each other unless one back controls passing downs or goal-line work.
- Brandel's snaps and center chemistry are understated fantasy variables for Murray, Jefferson, and Addison.

## Statements and reporting

Vikings.com reported that quarterbacks shared reps through spring and that Brandel had first-team center reps throughout OTA and minicamp practices. Daily Norseman training-camp analysis described Murray as ahead because of processing, while noting offensive-line snap precision as a key watchpoint.

## Reliability cautions

Phillips' title is official, but the distribution of play-calling responsibility is inferred from the O'Connell offense. Treat personnel usage as a preseason hypothesis until snap and route data are available.
