# Kevin O'Connell - Minnesota Vikings Head Coach

- Role: Head coach and offensive authority
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: hired 2022; extended in January 2025
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

O'Connell remains Minnesota's head coach and primary offensive authority. The official staff page says he was hired on 2022-02-16 after serving as Rams offensive coordinator and signed a multi-year Vikings extension in January 2025. His 2026 mandate is to stabilize a quarterback room reshaped by Kyler Murray's arrival while keeping Justin Jefferson, Jordan Addison, T.J. Hockenson, Aaron Jones Sr., and Jordan Mason in a high-quality scoring environment.

## Scheme and tendencies

The Vikings' public materials frame the offense as complex and quarterback-dependent. Their July quarterback preview noted that O'Connell's system has been described by current and former Vikings as complex, and that Murray's first task would be mastering it. The team also emphasized a more physical camp and daily work along the line of scrimmage, with run-game changes on offense and an offensive line centered around Blake Brandel, Christian Darrisaw, Brian O'Neill, Will Fries, and Donovan Jackson.
