# Minnesota Vikings 2026 Coaching Staff

Minnesota keeps the core football authority model intact: Kevin O'Connell leads the team and offense, Wes Phillips remains offensive coordinator, Brian Flores remains defensive coordinator, and Matt Daniels remains special-teams coordinator. The notable additions are Frank Smith as assistant head coach and Ryan Nielsen as defensive running game coordinator, while football operations shifted from Kwesi Adofo-Mensah to interim Rob Brzezinski and then Nolan Teasley.

## Authority model

O'Connell is the primary offensive voice, with Phillips coordinating the offensive staff and Josh McCown, Curtis Modkins, Keith Carter, Keenan McCardell, Ryan Cordell, and Chris O'Hara supporting position-level execution. Flores remains the defensive play-caller, with Nielsen, Gerald Alexander, Thad Bogardus, Mike Siravo, and Patrick Hill shaping the front, coverage, and sub-package details.
