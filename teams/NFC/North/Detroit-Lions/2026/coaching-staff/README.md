# Detroit Lions 2026 Coaching Intelligence

Detroit's 2026 coaching module is a continuity-plus-transition file. Dan Campbell remains the authority point, Drew Petzing takes over the offense after three seasons coordinating Arizona, and Kelvin Sheppard remains defensive coordinator with a stated move toward more adaptable nickel and hybrid usage.

## Authority Model

- Campbell owns the program identity, game-management posture, and final roster/situational tone.
- Petzing is the new offensive play-caller and the most important fantasy variable because he inherits Jared Goff, Jahmyr Gibbs, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Jameson Williams, Sam LaPorta, and a rebuilt offensive line.
- Hank Fraley remains run game coordinator/offensive line, which matters because Detroit is changing starters at multiple line spots while trying to keep its run-game identity.
- Sheppard owns defensive play-calling and is entering his second season as coordinator with a more matchup-driven coverage/personnel emphasis.
- Dave Fipp remains special teams coordinator; return jobs matter only if they affect active status or low-volume offensive players.
