## Responsibilities

Campbell remains Detroit's head coach and central authority figure. The official Lions profile says he was named head coach on 2021-01-20 and credits the Campbell/Brad Holmes build with a draft-and-development foundation around Penei Sewell, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Aidan Hutchinson, Kerby Joseph, Jahmyr Gibbs, Sam LaPorta, Jack Campbell, and Brian Branch.

For 2026, Campbell is managing an offense with a new coordinator and a rebuilt offensive line, plus a defense whose two best safety pieces entered camp with injury monitoring.

## Scheme and tendencies

Campbell's durable program identity is physicality, line-of-scrimmage play, and aggression. The 2026 offense changes coordinator from John Morton to Drew Petzing, but Campbell retained Hank Fraley as run game coordinator/offensive line and added Blake Miller, Cade Mays, Larry Borom, Ben Bartch, and Juice Scruggs to manage the line reset.
