## Core Baseline

The facts: Sheppard remains defensive coordinator, Jack Campbell and Aidan Hutchinson are extended through 2030, Detroit added D.J. Wonnum, Payton Turner, Derrick Moore, Chuck Clark, Christian Izien, Roger McCreary, and multiple depth pieces, and Joseph/Branch opened camp with injury monitoring. The inference: Detroit can become a stronger fantasy D/ST if a second rush lane and healthy safety structure arrive together.
