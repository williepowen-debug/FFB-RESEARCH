# Finding: Hutchinson and Jack Campbell provide the floor while complementary rush is rebuilt

## Scope

- Team/player: Lions front seven
- Area: pass rush, linebacker role, IDP/DST projection
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Detroit has two clear defensive anchors: Aidan Hutchinson on the edge and Jack Campbell at linebacker. Hutchinson signed through 2030 in October 2025, and Jack Campbell signed through 2030 in May 2026 after a 2025 All-Pro/Pro Bowl season. The question is whether Detroit's 2026 additions can create enough support around them.

The Lions released Josh Paschal, signed D.J. Wonnum and Payton Turner, drafted Derrick Moore in Round 2, and added multiple developmental front-seven players. Detroit's draft review quoted Holmes describing Moore as a rush-rotation body who can spell Hutchinson, rush opposite him, and move inside in nickel/sub defense. That is a strong role clue, but Moore still has to win snaps from veteran additions.

## Fantasy implication

- Hutchinson remains a premium IDP/pressure-player baseline and the central D/ST pressure assumption.
- Jack Campbell remains a high-floor IDP linebacker unless Sheppard's nickel evolution unexpectedly lowers his full-time role.
- Detroit D/ST upside requires Moore, Wonnum, Turner, or another rusher to prevent protection from tilting fully toward Hutchinson.
- If complementary rush does not arrive, the defense becomes more dependent on turnover variance and safety health.

## Evidence

- Hutchinson's extension through 2030 was announced on 2025-10-29.
- Jack Campbell's extension through 2030 was announced on 2026-05-21; the team cited his 2025 First-Team AP All-Pro and Pro Bowl selections.
- The transaction log records Josh Paschal's release, D.J. Wonnum and Payton Turner signings, and several front-seven depth additions.
- Detroit selected Derrick Moore 44th overall and described him as a nickel/sub-package edge who can spell Hutchinson, rush opposite him, or move inside.
- The Sheppard profile and OTA reporting emphasize coordinator comfort entering Year 2, but not yet a proven 2026 pressure rate.
