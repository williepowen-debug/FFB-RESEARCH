# Finding: Elite skill continuity meets a three-spot offensive-line reset

## Scope

- Team/player: Lions passing game and offensive line
- Area: Goff efficiency, St. Brown/Williams/LaPorta roles, protection stability
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Detroit retains the fantasy-friendly core of Jared Goff, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Jameson Williams, Sam LaPorta, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Penei Sewell, but the offensive line is the main structural risk. The Lions released Graham Glasgow and Taylor Decker in March, acquired Juice Scruggs, signed Cade Mays, Larry Borom and Ben Bartch, and drafted Blake Miller at No. 17 overall. Tim Twentyman's camp preview reported new starters at three offensive-line spots, with Sewell moving from right tackle to left and Mays signed to be the new center.

The passing-game hierarchy is more stable than the blocking front. St. Brown remains Goff's highest-trust target, and Day 1 camp observations said the Goff-St. Brown connection immediately looked sharp. Williams is extended through 2029 and remains the vertical complement. WR3, return work, and depth roles are unsettled among Greg Dortch, Isaac TeSlaa, Cedrick Wilson, Kendrick Law, Tom Kennedy, and others.

## Fantasy implication

- St. Brown remains the safest weekly target projection in this offense.
- Williams keeps best-ball and spike-week value if line play lets Goff access vertical throws.
- Goff's fantasy floor is tied to protection stability because he is more dependent on timing and pocket structure than rushing production.
- Miller/Borom at right tackle and the left-guard rotation are fantasy-relevant line monitors for all Detroit skill players.
- Low-volume receivers should not be aggressively projected until WR3 and return roles settle.

## Evidence

- The transaction log records Glasgow's release on 2026-03-02 and Decker's release on 2026-03-09.
- Detroit acquired Scruggs, signed Mays/Borom/Bartch, and drafted Miller at 17th overall.
- The camp preview said Detroit would have new starters at three line spots and identified Sewell's move to left tackle.
- Day 1 camp observations said Miller and Borom worked with the first-team offense at right tackle, while Bartch, Miles Frazier, and Scruggs rotated at left guard with Christian Mahogany on Active/NFI.
- The same Day 1 report described Goff as sharp and repeatedly connecting with St. Brown.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: Personnel facts and early camp observations are current, but OL competitions and WR3 usage are unresolved.
