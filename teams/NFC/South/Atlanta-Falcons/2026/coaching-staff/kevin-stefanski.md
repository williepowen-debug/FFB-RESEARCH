## Responsibilities

Stefanski is the new head coach and the central architect of Atlanta's 2026 reset. The Falcons announced a finalized 23-coach staff on 2026-03-09, described as the first staff assembled by Stefanski. He retained Jeff Ulbrich on defense, brought Tommy Rees with him as offensive coordinator, hired Bill Callahan for the offensive line, and built a quarterback-development structure around Alex Van Pelt.
