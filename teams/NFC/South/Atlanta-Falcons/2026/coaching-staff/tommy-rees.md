# Tommy Rees - Atlanta Falcons

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026-01-22
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Rees coordinates Atlanta's offense after spending the previous two seasons with Stefanski in Cleveland. The Falcons' announcement said Rees joined Stefanski's Browns staff in 2024 as pass-game specialist/tight ends coach and moved into the Cleveland offensive coordinator role in 2025. In Atlanta, his responsibilities include installing the offense, coordinating the passing-game and run-game staff, and shaping the quarterback competition.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2026 offense should be charted for under-center run action, play-action concepts, quarterback footwork, timing routes, and tight-end usage. The staff's June quarterback piece specifically tied the new system to under-center run game and play-action. That makes Pitts, Hooper, and the offensive line more relevant than in a pure shotgun spread projection.

Rees inherits Bijan Robinson, Drake London, Kyle Pitts, and Michael Penix Jr.; Atlanta added Tua Tagovailoa, Brian Robinson Jr., Jahan Dotson, Olamide Zaccheaus, Austin Hooper, Jawaan Taylor, and Zachariah Branch. The working hypothesis is role-specific complements around the core rather than a target hierarchy reset. Branch is the new slot/YAC variable; Dotson and Zaccheaus add speed and experience; Brian Robinson Jr. adds a heavier backfield profile.
