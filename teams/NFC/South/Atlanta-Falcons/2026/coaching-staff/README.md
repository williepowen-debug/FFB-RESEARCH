# Atlanta Falcons 2026 Coaching Staff

Atlanta's 2026 staff is a mixed reset: Kevin Stefanski and Tommy Rees bring the offensive system over from Cleveland, while Jeff Ulbrich was retained after the 2025 defense set a franchise sack record. The offensive authority model should be treated as Stefanski-led and Rees-coordinated until game evidence clarifies play-calling ownership.

## Authority model

- Head coach: Kevin Stefanski controls whole-team standards, offensive identity, game management, and staff coordination.
- Offensive coordinator: Tommy Rees installs and coordinates the offense after working with Stefanski in Cleveland.
- Quarterback development: Alex Van Pelt is central to footwork, rhythm, and the Tua Tagovailoa/Michael Penix Jr. competition.
- Offensive line: Bill Callahan is a major implementation signal for the run game and protection structure.
- Defense: Jeff Ulbrich retains coordinator authority after a successful 2025 installation.
- Special teams: Craig Aukerman replaces Marquice Williams and manages the new Nick Folk/Jake Bailey operation plus return competitions.
