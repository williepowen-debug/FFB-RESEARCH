# Jeff Ulbrich - Atlanta Falcons

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2025; retained for 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Ulbrich retains defensive coordinator authority under Stefanski. Atlanta announced his retention on 2026-01-19 after owner Arthur Blank publicly praised the defense's sack record and overall improvement. The 2026 staff then added defensive passing-game coordinator Patrick Toney, linebackers coach Barrett Ruud, outside linebackers coach John Timu, and several defensive assistants around Ulbrich.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2025 Falcons defense became an attack-style, pressure-by-committee unit. Atlanta reported that the group rose from second-to-last in sacks in 2024 to second in 2025, set a franchise record with 57 sacks, and had 14 players record at least one sack. The 2026 scheme baseline is continuity under Ulbrich, with personnel stress caused by Kaden Elliss' departure, James Pearce Jr.'s suspension, and Jalon Walker's injury.

Terrell Jr., Jessie Bates III, Xavier Watts, and nickel/DB competitors.
