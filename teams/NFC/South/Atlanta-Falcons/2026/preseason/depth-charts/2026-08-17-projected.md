This chart synthesizes the Falcons' own unofficial preseason depth chart (published for the first preseason game vs. Denver) with camp reporting and availability news through 2026-08-17. A slash indicates an unresolved competition rather than a team-declared tie. Two availability facts dominate the projection: **QB Michael Penix Jr.** is recovering from November 2025 ACL surgery and was limited to individual and 7-on-7 work (not 11-on-11) through mid-August, and **EDGE James Pearce Jr.** is suspended for the first eight games (eligible Week 9).

## Most important unresolved competitions

1. The Week 1 quarterback: Tua's reps-driven lead vs. Penix's ACL clearance for 11-on-11.
2. Right tackle stability with Jawaan Taylor just off PUP.
3. The WR2/WR3 route hierarchy (Dotson vs. Zaccheaus) and Branch's offensive snap share.
4. The edge-rush rotation for the first eight games without Pearce.
5. Nickel (Holmes vs. rookie Avieon Terrell) and the second safety spot.

## Recent changes

- Tua Tagovailoa takes the first-team 11-on-11 reps and started the preseason opener; Stefanski declined to name a Week 1 starter.
- Michael Penix Jr. remained limited to individual and 7-on-7 work (not 11-on-11) and did not play the preseason opener.
- Jawaan Taylor was activated off active/PUP (~Aug 10-11); RT remains a flagged concern with Michael Jerrell as the alternative.
- James Pearce Jr. was suspended for the first eight games, reshaping the early-season edge rotation.
- Rookies Zachariah Branch (WR/returner), Harold Perkins Jr. (LB), and Avieon Terrell (nickel) are pressing for roles.
