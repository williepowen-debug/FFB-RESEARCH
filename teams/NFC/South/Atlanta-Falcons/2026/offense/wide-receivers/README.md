# Atlanta Falcons 2026 wide receivers

This directory treats the Falcons' 2026 receiver room as a one-alpha structure: Drake London is the clear, extended WR1, with a flexible veteran WR2/WR3 competition (Jahan Dotson, Olamide Zaccheaus) and a rookie speed/return piece (Zachariah Branch) behind him. The analytical question is not who the WR1 is; it is whether a Stefanski/Rees run-and-play-action offense generates enough pass volume for any secondary receiver to matter in fantasy.

## Files

- [`personnel-and-roster-paths.md`](personnel-and-roster-paths.md): the room, roster locks vs. bubble, and projected alignment roles.
- [`scheme-and-fantasy-impact.md`](scheme-and-fantasy-impact.md): how the new offense distributes WR targets and where each receiver fits.
- [`monitoring.md`](monitoring.md): the camp and preseason signals that will confirm or break the projections.
- [`drake-london-role-audit.md`](drake-london-role-audit.md): the WR1 target-share anchor.
- [`zachariah-branch-role-audit.md`](zachariah-branch-role-audit.md): the rookie's realistic Year-1 offensive vs. return role.

## Evidence boundary

Facts: the roster, transactions, contracts, draft capital, and camp reports are sourced. Inference: exact target shares, alignment splits, and Branch's offensive role are preseason projections until first-team usage is charted.
