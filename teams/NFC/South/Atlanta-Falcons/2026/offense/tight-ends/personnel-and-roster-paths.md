# Finding: The tight-end room is a defined, complementary trio, not a Pitts-vs-Hooper target fight

## Scope

- Unit: tight ends
- Area: 2026 roster construction and roles
- Season: 2026
- Status: active preseason baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Atlanta's tight-end room has three defined jobs. Kyle Pitts Sr. is the move/flex receiver who can line up in-line, in the slot, out wide, or in the backfield; Austin Hooper is the true in-line "Y" and the 12-personnel complement; Charlie Woerner is the blocking specialist. Hooper is a complement to Pitts' pass-game role, not a competitor for his targets. The only open question is the developmental fourth spot.

## Fantasy implication

- Pitts is the only fantasy-relevant tight end; his role is secure, and Hooper does not threaten his target share.
- The room's structure supports Pitts' passing usage while offloading blocking snaps to Hooper and Woerner.
- Hooper has deep-league/streaming appeal only if Pitts misses time.

## Evidence

### Facts

- The team's camp preview lists Pitts, Hooper, Woerner (a regarded run blocker), Joshua Simon (second-year UDFA, a minicamp receiving standout), and rookie UDFA Jack Velling; departures include Feleipe Franks and Teagan Quitoriano. (Atlanta Falcons, 2026-07-08)
- Stefanski says Pitts can line up "in-line tight end, line up in the backfield, line up outside." (Atlanta Falcons, 2026-07-08)
- Hooper spent his first four NFL seasons in Atlanta and two seasons with Stefanski in Cleveland; he is entering his 11th season and could occupy the in-line role in 12- or 13-personnel alongside Pitts. (Atlanta Falcons, 2026-07-08)
- Pitts signed a three-year extension (reported at about $54M) after the franchise-tag window. (4for4, 2026-07-23)

### Inferences

- Roster locks: Pitts and Hooper; Woerner is a likely blocking/special-teams keeper; Simon vs. Velling is the developmental competition for a fourth spot.
- Role split: Pitts (move/flex receiver), Hooper (in-line Y and 12-personnel complement), Woerner (pure blocker).
- The structure is designed to feature Pitts in the passing game without asking him to carry the blocking load.

- Confidence: medium
