# Finding: The Stefanski/Rees scheme is a genuine tailwind for Pitts, and the QB winner sets his ceiling

## Scope

- Unit: tight ends, offensive scheme
- Area: how the new offense uses tight ends and the QB-dependent range for Pitts
- Season: 2026
- Status: active preseason baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

The new system's under-center, play-action, multi-tight-end profile is the exact usage that historically inflates Stefanski tight-end volume, and Pitts' alignment versatility lets him be the pass-game beneficiary while Hooper absorbs blocking snaps. The remaining swing is the quarterback: 2025 data favored Pitts with a rhythm/timing pocket passer (a Tua profile) over Penix. This finding extends the earlier play-action note in [`../tight-end-and-play-action.md`](../tight-end-and-play-action.md).

## Fantasy implication

- Pitts is a high-floor back-end TE1 with league-winning spike weeks; the 88-catch 2025 volume floor is real.
- A Tua win nudges Pitts up; a healthy Penix win is the specific bearish case for Pitts.
- Clear TE-premium and PPR value, but a run-leaning offense and target competition with London cap the weekly ceiling.

## Evidence

### Facts

- The system is described as far more balanced shotgun/under-center passing with heavy play-action and multi-tight-end sets. (Roto Street Journal, 2026-02-26)
- As Browns head coach (2020-2025), Stefanski's offenses ranked second in the NFL in tight-end targets and third in receptions over that span. (Roto Street Journal, 2026-02-26)
- 2025 QB split for Pitts: Kirk Cousins targeted him 70 times over eight starts; Michael Penix Jr. targeted him 53 times over nine starts. Cousins departed to Las Vegas. (4for4, 2026-07-23)
- London-dependency split: with London healthy, Pitts averaged 6.2 targets per game (7.4 PPR); when London missed time, 8.8 targets per game (15.5 PPR). (4for4, 2026-07-23)

### Inferences

- Play-action plus under-center plus 12-personnel is the usage profile that has historically driven Stefanski tight-end volume; Pitts' versatility lets him capture it.
- Hooper complements rather than caps Pitts by absorbing the blocking snaps in heavier personnel.
- The QB read-through: a timing pocket passer (Tua) is the better fit for Pitts' target efficiency than Penix, based on 2025 usage.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: the scheme fit is well supported, but the QB winner and 2026 route rate are not yet known.
- Fact/inference boundary: Stefanski's TE-usage history and the 2025 QB/London splits are facts; the 2026 projection is inference.
- What would invalidate this: a Penix win paired with low Pitts usage, a run-heavier lean than Stefanski's norm, or Pitts being used primarily in-line as a blocker.
- Next review: preseason route participation and the QB-to-Pitts target rate.
