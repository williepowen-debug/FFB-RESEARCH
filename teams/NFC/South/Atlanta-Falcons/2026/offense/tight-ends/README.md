# Atlanta Falcons 2026 tight ends

This directory treats the Falcons' tight-end room as a defined, complementary group rather than a target competition: Kyle Pitts Sr. is the move/flex receiving tight end, Austin Hooper is the in-line/12-personnel complement (and a Stefanski holdover from Cleveland), and Charlie Woerner is the blocking specialist. The analytical question is whether a tight-end-friendly Stefanski/Rees scheme finally converts Pitts' volume into consistent fantasy scoring.
