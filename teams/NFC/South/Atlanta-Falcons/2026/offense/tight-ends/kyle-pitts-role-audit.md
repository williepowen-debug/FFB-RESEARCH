# Finding: Pitts is a volume-floor TE1 whose bounce-back hinges on TD rate and the QB winner

## Scope

- Unit: tight ends
- Area: Kyle Pitts' 2026 role and fantasy range
- Season: 2026
- Player: Kyle Pitts Sr. (see [canonical profile](../../../../../../../players/kyle-pitts/profile.md))
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Pitts enters 2026 off a career-high 88-catch season on a TE-friendly new scheme, with a defined complementary room around him. The volume floor is real, but his recurring fantasy limiter is a low touchdown rate, and his ceiling is QB-dependent. The realistic outcome is a mid-range TE1 with league-winning spike weeks, tilting up if Tua wins and London misses time, and down toward streamer territory if a healthy Penix wins the job.

## Fantasy implication

- Draft as a back-end/mid-range TE1 in TE-premium and PPR; the 88-catch floor supports the price.
- Upside case: a Tua-led rhythm passing game plus any London absence pushes Pitts toward top-five.
- Downside case: continued low TD rate, target competition with London, and week-to-week volatility.

## Evidence

### Facts

- 2025: 88 receptions on about 114-118 targets, 928 yards, 5 TD; started all 17 games; first career All-Pro; 10.5 yards per reception; a Week 15 line of 11 receptions, 166 yards, 3 TD. (4for4, 2026-07-23; Roto Street Journal, 2026)
- Over the final six weeks of 2025 he led all NFL tight ends in receiving yards (469). (Roto Street Journal, 2026)
- Volatility: 40.1 fantasy points in Week 15, but under six points in eight other games. (4for4, 2026-07-23)
- 4for4 finished him as TE2 in Half-PPR total but TE6 per game in 2025 and projects TE8 for 2026, calling him a "risky pick." (4for4, 2026-07-23)

### Inferences

- Bounce-back case: full health, a TE-friendly scheme, a defined room, and a possible upgrade to a timing-based QB.
- Bust risk: a run-leaning offense, target competition with London, extreme volatility, and the weaker Pitts numbers seen with Penix in 2025.
- Net: a mid-range TE1; the swing factors are the QB winner and London's availability.
