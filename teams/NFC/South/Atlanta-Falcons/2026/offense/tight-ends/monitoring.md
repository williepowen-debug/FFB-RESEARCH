# Finding: Pitts' fantasy value turns on route rate, alignment, and red-zone share, not the contract

## Scope

- Unit: tight ends
- Area: the preseason evidence that resolves Pitts' 2026 usage
- Season: 2026
- Status: active monitoring baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

The Pitts questions that matter are all usage-based: how often he runs a route, whether he lines up as a receiver or in-line, how frequently Atlanta plays 12-personnel, who he catches passes from, and whether he sees the play-action "move" role that produces Stefanski tight-end targets. Contract and reputation are settled; usage is not.

## Fantasy implication

- Draft Pitts as a back-end TE1 and treat preseason usage as the tiebreaker for how aggressively to move him up.
- The single most valuable data point is the QB-to-Pitts target rate once Penix reaches 11-on-11.
- A high in-line/blocking rate would be the clearest bearish tell.

## Evidence

### Facts

- Pitts is participating fully in camp, and Stefanski publicly emphasizes his multi-alignment usage. (Atlanta Falcons, 2026-07-08)
- An August 12 end-of-game live drill with the first-team offense featured completions to Bijan Robinson and Drake London; Pitts was not specifically named on that rep. (Atlanta Falcons, 2026-08-12)
- Tua takes the first-team 11-on-11 reps while Penix is limited to 7-on-7 and individual work. (Atlanta Falcons, 2026-08-12)

### Inferences

- Track: Pitts' route participation rate; in-line vs. slot vs. wide alignment split; 12-/13-personnel frequency and red-zone target distribution; which QB throws to him more; and whether he sees the play-action bootleg/keeper "move" role.
- Because early live reps have flowed to Bijan and London, watch whether Pitts is folded into the first-team passing script as the offense installs more.
