# Atlanta Falcons 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats Atlanta's offense as a new Stefanski/Rees system built around retained premium skill players. Bijan Robinson, Drake London, Kyle Pitts Sr., and Matthew Bergeron are the core. Tua Tagovailoa, Brian Robinson Jr., Jahan Dotson, Olamide Zaccheaus, Austin Hooper, Jawaan Taylor, and rookie Zachariah Branch are the major additions.

## Files

- [`quarterback-and-skill-transition.md`](quarterback-and-skill-transition.md): the Tua/Penix competition and how the new complements affect Robinson, London, Pitts, and Branch.
- [`backfield-usage.md`](backfield-usage.md): the Bijan Robinson / Brian Robinson Jr. workload split and goal-line TD equity.
- [`tight-end-and-play-action.md`](tight-end-and-play-action.md): Pitts' contract, Stefanski/Rees tight-end signals, and under-center/play-action implications.
- [`wide-receivers/`](wide-receivers/README.md): receiver personnel, scheme/fantasy impact, monitoring, and role audits (London, Branch).
- [`tight-ends/`](tight-ends/README.md): tight-end personnel, scheme/fantasy impact, monitoring, and the Kyle Pitts role audit.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

Facts: the staff, transactions, contracts, and quarterback-health reports are sourced. Inference: exact play-caller, target shares, red-zone allocation, and pace are preseason hypotheses until first-team usage is charted.
