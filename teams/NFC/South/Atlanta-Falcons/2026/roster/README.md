# Atlanta Falcons 2026 Roster Changes

Machine-readable transaction data supporting the 2026 Core-tier module. Update status fields rather than deleting historical rows.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): exits, releases, and outgoing signings.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): free-agent signings and incoming trade/claim additions.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): re-signings, extensions, options, tenders, and restructures.
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): all six selections in the 2026 draft.
- [`undrafted-free-agents.csv`](undrafted-free-agents.csv): initial post-draft UDFA class.

## Headline changes

- Atlanta changed the football leadership structure: Kevin Stefanski became head coach, Ian Cunningham became general manager, and Matt Ryan became president of football.
- The quarterback room changed materially: Tua Tagovailoa was signed, Kirk Cousins was released, and Michael Penix Jr. is returning from a 2025 ACL injury with an open competition frame.
- The offensive core was retained around Bijan Robinson, Drake London, Kyle Pitts, and Matthew Bergeron, while Jahan Dotson, Olamide Zaccheaus, Brian Robinson Jr., Austin Hooper, and rookie Zachariah Branch were added as role-specific complements.
- The defense retained Jeff Ulbrich after a 57-sack 2025 season, but Kaden Elliss departed and the edge room must absorb James Pearce Jr.'s eight-game suspension and Jalon Walker's injury.
