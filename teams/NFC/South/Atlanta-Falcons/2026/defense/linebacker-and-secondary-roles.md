# Finding: Deablo is the defensive communication bridge while young defenders define the role volatility

## Scope

- Unit: linebackers and secondary
- Area: green-dot transition, linebacker rotation, and secondary snap competitions
- Season: 2026
- Status: active preseason baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Kaden Elliss' departure pushes Divine Deablo into a larger communication role, while Christian Harris, Kendal Daniels, Harold Perkins Jr., and Troy Andersen shape the linebacker competition around him. Atlanta also added Avieon Terrell in Round 2, giving the secondary another high-upside option alongside A.J. Terrell Jr., Jessie Bates III, Xavier Watts, Billy Bowman Jr., and nickel/depth competitors. The unit has continuity in coordinator but not in every role.

## Evidence

### Facts

- The Falcons' free-agency tracker reported Kaden Elliss' expected signing with New Orleans and later team content framed Deablo as taking the green dot in 2026.
- Atlanta signed Christian Harris and Channing Tindall, restructured Troy Andersen, and drafted Kendal Daniels plus Harold Perkins Jr.
- A 2026 camp report described Harris as a primary linebacker next to Deablo during OTAs and noted rookies Perkins and Daniels rotating into the linebacker mix.
- Atlanta selected Avieon Terrell at No. 48 overall and described him as entering a position open for competition.
- The 2025 defense produced 16 interceptions and 23 takeaways under Ulbrich, with Jessie Bates III and Xavier Watts leading safety turnover production.

### Inferences

- Deablo's green-dot role is the clearest IDP tackle-volume path, but he still needs every-down usage confirmed.
- Harris is the most likely first partner; Daniels and Perkins are upside/volatility players who may appear by package before they become full-time starters.
- Avieon Terrell can affect slot/outside matchups quickly if he wins snaps, but rookie defensive-back roles should be treated as open until charted.

## Fantasy implication

- Deablo is the first Falcons linebacker to monitor for IDP volume.
- Opponent matchup reads should track whether Atlanta's linebacker coverage is stable or exploitable early.
- Avieon Terrell's emergence would improve secondary depth and may reduce easy outside/slot targets, but rookie-role volatility keeps early passing matchups fluid.
