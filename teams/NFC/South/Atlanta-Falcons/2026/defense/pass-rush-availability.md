# Finding: Atlanta's sack-record baseline is real, but early 2026 availability is the stress test

## Scope

- Unit: defensive line and edge rushers
- Area: pressure continuity, suspension/injury risk, and matchup effect
- Season: 2026
- Status: active preseason baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Atlanta retained Jeff Ulbrich after a 2025 defense that set the franchise record with 57 sacks and produced pressure by committee. That baseline supports D/ST upside, but the 2026 opening stretch is less stable than the headline suggests: James Pearce Jr. is suspended for the first eight games, Jalon Walker has an injury context to monitor, and the Falcons added several veteran rushers to keep the rotation afloat.

## Evidence

### Facts

- Atlanta reported that the 2025 defense set a single-season franchise record with 57 sacks after finishing second-to-last in the league with 31 sacks in 2024.
- Pearce led the 2025 team with 10.5 sacks as a rookie.
- Ulbrich was retained as defensive coordinator on 2026-01-19 and the team cited the defensive improvement and sack record.
- Atlanta signed Azeez Ojulari, Samson Ebukam, Cameron Thomas, Da'Shawn Hand, Chris Williams, and re-signed LaCale London as part of the defensive-front plan.
- Official team news on 2026-08-17 listed related items on Pearce's eight-game suspension and the need for others to step up with Walker injured.

### Inferences

- The 2025 pressure ecosystem was not a one-player rush, but losing Pearce for half the fantasy regular season changes the early D/ST projection.
- Ojulari and Ebukam are the most important veteran additions for keeping pressure high without excessive blitzing.
- If Atlanta must blitz more to compensate, sack upside may remain but explosive-pass risk can rise.

## Fantasy implication

- Atlanta D/ST is more attractive as a full-season upside play than as an unqualified early-season lock.
- Opposing passing games may face volatility: pressure can create sacks and turnovers, but replacement-rusher busts can also create explosive-play windows.
- IDP managers should track edge snap shares weekly until Pearce returns.
