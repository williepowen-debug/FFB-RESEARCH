# Carolina Panthers 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the Panthers offense as a connected preseason baseline: play-caller transition, Bryce Young's next step, offensive-line health, receiver consolidation, tight-end change, and backfield allocation.

## Files

- [`idzik-play-calling-transition.md`](idzik-play-calling-transition.md): the Canales-to-Idzik play-calling handoff and fantasy evidence tests.
- [`young-supporting-cast.md`](young-supporting-cast.md): Young's 2026 support structure after line, receiver, and tight-end changes.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions to test.

## Evidence boundary

Facts: official role changes, transactions, draft picks, current roster statuses, and published preseason previews.

Inference: target hierarchy, pass rate, protection quality, and touchdown distribution. These need preseason and early regular-season charting.
