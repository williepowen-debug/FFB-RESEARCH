# Finding: Idzik controls the new offensive evidence stream

## Scope

- Team/player: Carolina Panthers offense
- Area: play-calling authority, sequencing, and fantasy translation
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

The Panthers' offensive system has continuity in staff and quarterback, but not in play-calling process. Canales called plays for his first two Carolina seasons; in 2026, Idzik is assigned to call plays so Canales can manage the broader team. This means past Canales tendencies are context, not a direct 2026 rate projection.

## Fantasy implication

Bryce Young, Chuba Hubbard, Tetairoa McMillan, Jalen Coker, Xavier Legette, Darren Waller, and the tight-end group should be projected with wider usage bands until Idzik's regular-season decisions are charted. The key fantasy tests are early-down pass rate, two-minute personnel, red-zone play selection, and whether the passing game stays aggressive behind an unsettled offensive line.

## Evidence

### Facts

- The Panthers reported on 2026-02-24 that offensive coordinator Brad Idzik would call plays in 2026.
- The same report said Canales called plays during the first two seasons of his Carolina tenure and wanted a more global head-coach focus.
- The official 2026 staff announcement keeps Idzik as offensive coordinator and adds Darrell Bevell and Carl Smith as senior offensive support.
- The offense enters camp with Young as the clear starter and under a fifth-year option through 2027.

### Inference

Idzik's prior work with Young and Canales supports terminology continuity. It does not prove same run/pass rates, target distribution, or red-zone behavior. The new play-caller could preserve the Canales structure while changing weekly aggression and sequencing.
