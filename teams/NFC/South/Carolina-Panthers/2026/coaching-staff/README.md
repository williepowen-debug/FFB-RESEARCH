## Authority model

| Area | Primary authority | Important supporting voices |
|---|---|---|
| Team | Dave Canales | Dan Morgan; Brad Idzik; Ejiro Evero; Tracy Smith |
| Offense | Brad Idzik - play-caller | Dave Canales; Harold Goodwin; Darrell Bevell; Mike Bercovici; position coaches |
| Defense | Ejiro Evero | Peter Hansen; Jonathan Cooley; AC Carter; Todd Wash; Renaldo Hill |
| Special teams | Tracy Smith | Daren Bates; Dwayne Stukes |

The major 2026 authority change is not a coordinator title change. It is Canales handing regular-season offensive play-calling to Idzik so Canales can take a broader head-coach role. That makes Idzik's sequencing and game-management interaction with Canales a live fantasy variable.
