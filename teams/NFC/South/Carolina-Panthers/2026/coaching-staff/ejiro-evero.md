# Ejiro Evero - Carolina Panthers

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2023
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Evero remains defensive coordinator. The 2026 staff adds Peter Hansen as assistant defensive coordinator/linebackers and preserves key position coaches, giving Evero continuity around a defense that added Jaelan Phillips, Devin Lloyd, Lee Hunter, and secondary depth.

## Scheme and tendencies

The available evidence points to continuity in defensive structure with better personnel. Official preseason framing emphasizes Derrick Brown as the foundation, Phillips as the pass-rush swing piece, Lloyd as the middle-of-defense organizer, and Jaycee Horn/Mike Jackson as a settled cornerback pair.

## Personnel usage

Lloyd's green-dot role and fit with Trevin Wallace are central. Phillips is the lead pass-rush addition, but Nic Scourton's injured-reserve placement changes the young edge rotation. Interior usage depends on Derrick Brown, Bobby Brown III, Lee Hunter, and Tershawn Wharton's availability after PUP placement.

## Pace and situational decisions

Track early-down front choices, third-down pressure rates, and whether Evero can create pressure without exposing the secondary. The secondary looks more stable than recent Panthers groups, but safety and rookie subpackage usage still need preseason evidence.

## Fantasy implications

Carolina D/ST has a clearer sack and run-defense path than in prior seasons, but Scourton's injury and defensive-line availability keep the floor matchup-dependent. Lloyd is the most obvious IDP tackle-volume candidate. Phillips carries sack upside if his snap share and health hold.

## Statements and reporting

The official staff directory and 2026 staff announcement keep Evero as defensive coordinator. Team preseason defense preview identifies Phillips and Lloyd as major additions and notes Brown's central role.

## Reliability cautions

Roster quality improved on paper, but defensive fantasy value should be tied to pressure rate, run fits, and explosive plays allowed rather than names alone.
