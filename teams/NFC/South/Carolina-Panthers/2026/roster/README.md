# Carolina Panthers 2026 roster-change datasets

These CSV files preserve the 2025-to-2026 transition as row-level data. They are a preseason baseline verified on 2026-08-17 against the official Panthers transaction ledger and linked source stories.

- `departures.csv`: major veterans or role players removed by trade, release, retirement, injured reserve, or contract expiration.
- `veteran-additions.csv`: non-draft additions and in-camp roster moves relevant to role competitions.
- `retentions.csv`: extensions, re-signings, tenders, and option decisions that define the retained core.
- `draft-class.csv`: all seven 2026 draft selections.
- `undrafted-free-agents.csv`: the original 10-player UDFA class and known status changes.

Status fields are time-sensitive. Update status at cutdowns and after transactions; do not delete the acquisition row.
