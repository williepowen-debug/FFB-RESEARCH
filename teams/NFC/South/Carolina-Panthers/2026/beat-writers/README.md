# Carolina Panthers source-monitoring guide

This directory tracks the official records, reporters, analysts, and data sources used to monitor the 2026 Carolina Panthers for fantasy-relevant injuries, practice participation, role changes, usage, scheme, and roster construction.

## Files

- `registry.md` is the editorial monitoring rotation.
- `sources.csv` normalizes source identity, function, priority, and status.
- `endpoints.csv` records usable monitoring locations.
- `candidates.csv` preserves the discovery and omission-pass audit.

## Workflow

Start with official transactions, injury reports, and press conferences. Use independent reporters for direct practice observations and organizational context, then use film specialists to interpret role and scheme. Confirm material conclusions with measured snaps, routes, carries, targets, and alignments once games begin.

Team-employed reporters are direct-access official voices, not independent confirmation. Attribute reports to individuals, distinguish observation from evaluation, follow aggregators to the original report, and never reproduce paywalled text. Reverify assignments before camp, after newsroom changes, and at least once during the regular season.
