# New Orleans Saints 2026 Defensive Ecosystem

This directory frames the Saints defense as Brandon Staley Year 2: a strong 2025 baseline with edge continuity, but meaningful transition at linebacker and hybrid defensive-back roles after Demario Davis and Alontae Taylor departed.

## Files

- [linebacker-and-star-transition.md](linebacker-and-star-transition.md): Elliss/Werner/Blackmon role transition and opponent matchup implications.
- [pass-rush-continuity.md](pass-rush-continuity.md): edge continuity, Jordan's final season, and D/ST streaming baseline.
- [hypotheses.csv](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable defensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records use official team transaction/staff sources and public camp analysis through 2026-08-17. They do not treat 2025 defensive rankings as a guarantee because Davis' leadership/tackle role and Taylor's coverage versatility left the roster.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. Does Kaden Elliss become an every-down replacement for Demario Davis?
2. Does Julian Blackmon hold the star role, and how does that affect slot/TE/RB matchups?
3. Does the Young/Jordan/Granderson edge group stay healthy and productive enough for D/ST streaming?
4. Does Tyree Wilson contribute immediately or remain a developmental depth player?
5. Does rookie Christen Miller earn early interior run-defense work?
