# Finding: Linebacker and Star Transition

## Scope

- Team/player: New Orleans Saints defense
- Area: linebacker and hybrid defensive-back roles
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Reported facts: Demario Davis departed for the Jets and Alontae Taylor departed for the Titans. Saints training-camp analysis identified Kaden Elliss and Pete Werner as the apparent starting linebackers and Julian Blackmon as the leading candidate for the hybrid "star" role, while noting that depth and rotation sorting remain important.

Inference: Brandon Staley's 2026 defense has coordinator continuity but meaningful role transition at two high-communication positions. The Saints can remain a strong unit, but early-season matchup analysis should test linebacker coverage, nickel/hybrid fit, and tackle redistribution.

## Fantasy implication

IDP tackle volume may shift from Davis to Elliss/Werner, with Elliss the main role monitor. Opposing tight ends, slot receivers, and receiving backs deserve extra matchup attention until the Elliss/Werner/Blackmon structure proves it can handle space assignments.

## Evidence

- NFL.com's departure tracker lists Davis and Taylor among notable Saints offseason departures.
- The Saints' training-camp preview said Elliss and Werner seemed settled as starting linebackers and described the hybrid star position as valuable in Staley's scheme.
- The same Saints preview identified Blackmon as a likely leading option while noting several players could fit the role.
