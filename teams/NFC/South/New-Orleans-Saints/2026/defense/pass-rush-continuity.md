# Finding: Pass Rush Continuity and Depth

## Scope

- Team/player: New Orleans Saints defense
- Area: edge rush and D/ST baseline
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Reported facts: Brandon Staley's official profile says Cameron Jordan and Chase Young each reached double-digit sacks in 2025. Jordan re-signed on 2026-06-16 and announced 2026 as his final season. The Saints also added Tyree Wilson by trade and signed Anfernee Jennings, while training-camp analysis listed Chase Young, Jordan, and Carl Granderson as set at the top of the edge rotation.

Inference: The Saints' D/ST floor is more stable through edge continuity than through back-seven continuity. The pass rush can support matchup weeks, but depth snaps and Jordan's age/final-season workload should be tracked before assuming every-week D/ST value.

## Fantasy implication

New Orleans should be considered in D/ST streaming when favored or facing weaker pass protection, especially if Young/Jordan/Granderson remain healthy. IDP edge value is more sack-dependent than tackle-secure; Tyree Wilson is a developmental monitor rather than a projected immediate fantasy contributor.

## Evidence

- Staley's official Saints profile cites double-digit sacks from Jordan and Young in 2025.
- The Saints announced Jordan's one-year contract on 2026-06-16 and his final-season campaign on 2026-06-17.
- Saints Day 3 draft recap states New Orleans acquired Tyree Wilson and a seventh-round pick from Las Vegas for pick No. 150.
- Saints camp preview lists Young, Jordan, and Granderson as locks atop the edge rotation and puts Rumph, Diggs, Jennings, and Wilson in the snap competition.
