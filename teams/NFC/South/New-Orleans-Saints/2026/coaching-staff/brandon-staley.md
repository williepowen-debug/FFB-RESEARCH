# Brandon Staley - New Orleans Saints

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2025
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Staley enters his second season as Saints defensive coordinator in 2026. The official profile credits his 2025 defense with finishing ninth in opponent net yards per game, fourth against the pass, third on opponent third-down conversion rate, and top-12 in EPA against both run and pass.

## Scheme and tendencies

Reported fact: New Orleans' 2025 defense was strong by yardage, pass defense, forced fumbles, yards per rush allowed, red-zone touchdown rate, and third-down defense in Staley's first season. The official bio also notes double-digit sacks from Cameron Jordan and Chase Young.

Inference: Staley has already established enough success in New Orleans to treat continuity as the defensive baseline. The personnel turnover is concentrated at leadership linebacker and defensive back roles, not coordinator identity.

## Personnel usage

The key 2026 changes are Demario Davis and Alontae Taylor out, Kaden Elliss, Tyree Wilson, Anfernee Jennings, Martin Emerson, and rookie defenders in, and Cameron Jordan returning for his announced final season. Training-camp reporting identified Kaden Elliss and Pete Werner as the apparent linebacker starters and Julian Blackmon as a leading "star" or hybrid defensive-back option.

## Pace and situational decisions

For fantasy and matchup work, chart pressure source and coverage presentation rather than only sack totals. Staley's 2025 group created strong third-down outcomes, but 2026 opponent pass-game decisions depend on whether the linebacker and nickel replacements hold up in space.

## Fantasy implications

The Saints D/ST has a plausible floor because the coordinator and pass rush remain stable, but the first-month matchup profile should include volatility against offenses that can isolate linebackers or nickel defenders. IDP value concentrates around Chase Young/Cameron Jordan sack chances, Kaden Elliss/Pete Werner tackle roles, and Julian Blackmon's hybrid deployment.

## Statements and reporting

Saints training-camp preview content framed Year 2 of Staley's defense around the edge rotation, the Elliss/Werner linebacker pairing, and the hybrid "star" role in the secondary.

## Reliability cautions

The 2025 defensive ranking is a useful baseline, not a guarantee. Davis' departure changes communication and tackle distribution, and Taylor's exit removes a versatile coverage piece.
