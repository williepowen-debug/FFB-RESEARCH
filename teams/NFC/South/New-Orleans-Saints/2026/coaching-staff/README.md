# New Orleans Saints 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. New Orleans is in Year 2 of the Kellen Moore build, with Doug Nussmeier and Brandon Staley retained as coordinators.

## Files

- [staff.csv](staff.csv): coordinators and key position coaches, with retained-from-2025 flags.
- [leadership-transition.csv](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership continuity and additions.
- [kellen-moore.md](kellen-moore.md): head-coach authority and offensive continuity.
- [doug-nussmeier.md](doug-nussmeier.md): coordinator profile focused on Shough development and offensive integration.
- [brandon-staley.md](brandon-staley.md): Year 2 defensive identity, personnel turnover, and matchup translation.
