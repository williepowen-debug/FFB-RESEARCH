# Kellen Moore - New Orleans Saints

- Role: Head coach
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: named head coach on 2025-02-11
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Moore is the final football authority on the coaching staff and the offensive identity still carries his imprint. The Saints' official profile says he was hired after coordinating Philadelphia's Super Bowl LIX offense and that his first New Orleans season ended with four wins in the final five games. The same profile credits Moore and the staff with building a scheme around rookie quarterback Tyler Shough, Kelvin Banks Jr., Taliese Fuaga, Chris Olave, and Juwan Johnson.

## Scheme and tendencies

Reported fact: New Orleans lists Doug Nussmeier as offensive coordinator, but Moore's background is offensive play design and quarterback development. The official team bio says Moore and the staff accentuated Shough's abilities and finished 5-4 with him as starter in 2025.

Inference: The 2026 offensive prior should be Moore/Nussmeier continuity rather than a new installation. The personnel additions - Travis Etienne, Jordyn Tyson, Noah Fant, and David Edwards - point toward improving explosive-play access and protection around Shough rather than changing the quarterback-centric build.

## Personnel usage

Moore's 2026 problem is allocation, not lack of options. Chris Olave is retained as the target anchor, Tyson adds first-round perimeter upside, Juwan Johnson and Noah Fant give the staff receiving tight-end flexibility, and Etienne joins Alvin Kamara in a backfield where receiving work and late-career workload management must be charted.

## Pace and situational decisions

No stable 2026 pace profile should be assigned from preseason context alone. Track first-team tempo, fourth-down aggressiveness, red-zone personnel, and whether Moore leans on Shough's mobility near the goal line after Shough led the 2025 Saints in rushing touchdowns per the official coach bio.

## Fantasy implications

The clearest fantasy implication is a better support structure for Shough and Olave. Shough becomes a superflex watch rather than an automatic fade if the offensive line and added weapons hold. Olave remains the safest passing-game projection; Tyson, Fant, Johnson, Etienne, and Kamara require route, personnel, and red-zone charting before setting firm weekly ranks.

## Statements and reporting

NFL.com's NFC South camp preview framed Shough's 2026 as a Year 2 proof point after a promising finish and noted that Moore has a quarterback he can mold. The same preview highlighted Etienne and Tyson as the key new offensive weapons.

## Reliability cautions

The public optimism around Shough is a hypothesis. Treat the late-2025 finish and offseason additions as reasons to monitor, not proof of a weekly fantasy ceiling.
