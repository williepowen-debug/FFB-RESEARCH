# Finding: Tyler Shough Year 2 Support Structure

## Scope

- Team/player: New Orleans Saints offense / Tyler Shough
- Area: quarterback ecosystem
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Reported facts: The Saints kept Kellen Moore, Doug Nussmeier, and Scott Tolzien in place around Shough. The official Moore and Nussmeier bios state that Shough went 5-4 as the 2025 starter, completed 221 of 327 passes for 2,384 yards with 10 touchdowns, and earned PFWA All-Rookie recognition. The Saints then added guard David Edwards, running back Travis Etienne, tight end Noah Fant, and first-round receiver Jordyn Tyson.

Inference: New Orleans appears to be treating Shough as the starter to develop, not as a placeholder. The 2026 offense should be evaluated as a Year 2 quarterback-support build: continuity in coaching, improved interior protection, and more explosive receiving options.

## Fantasy implication

Shough is a superflex and player-props watch rather than a locked draft target. His ceiling rises if Tyson earns immediate perimeter routes and Etienne improves checkdown/spacing value, but the baseline remains lower than established QB1/QB2 profiles until early-season efficiency and volume confirm the late-2025 finish.

## Evidence

- The official Saints coach roster says Moore and staff implemented a Shough-tailored scheme and finished 5-4 with Shough as starter in 2025.
- Nussmeier's official profile credits Moore and Nussmeier with devising the Shough scheme.
- NFL.com's NFC South camp preview described Shough's late-season rise as the central Saints storyline and framed 2026 as proof of whether the optimism is justified.
- The Saints' 2026 additions include Etienne, Tyson, Fant, and Edwards, each directly supporting a young quarterback.
