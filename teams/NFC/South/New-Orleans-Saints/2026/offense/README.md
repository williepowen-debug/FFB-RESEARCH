# New Orleans Saints 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the offense as a connected system: Year 2 of Tyler Shough under Kellen Moore and Doug Nussmeier, Chris Olave locked in, and new support through Travis Etienne, Jordyn Tyson, Noah Fant, and David Edwards.

## Files

- [shough-year-two-support.md](shough-year-two-support.md): quarterback ecosystem and support-structure finding.
- [skill-position-allocation.md](skill-position-allocation.md): receiver, tight end, and running back allocation baseline.
- [hypotheses.csv](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the preseason baseline as of 2026-08-17. They do not assume Shough's late-2025 finish automatically becomes a full-season 2026 breakout, and they do not assign final target or touch shares before preseason and Week 1 evidence.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. Does Shough's Year 2 operation confirm he is more than a matchup streamer?
2. Does Jordyn Tyson immediately become the WR2 behind Chris Olave?
3. How do Etienne and Kamara split early downs, routes, two-minute work, and goal-line touches?
4. Does the tight-end room create one fantasy-relevant route leader or a package committee?
5. Does David Edwards stabilize the interior protection enough to raise passing efficiency?
