# New Orleans Saints - 2026 Year 2 Moore Build

- Conference/division: NFC South
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Scope: changes from the 2025 team to the 2026 preseason roster and staff
- Build tier: Core

New Orleans enters 2026 as a continuity-and-support build around head coach Kellen Moore and quarterback Tyler Shough. The Saints retained Moore, offensive coordinator Doug Nussmeier, and defensive coordinator Brandon Staley, then added offensive help through Travis Etienne, David Edwards, Noah Fant, and first-round receiver Jordyn Tyson. The defense carries Staley continuity and a strong 2025 baseline, but must replace Demario Davis and Alontae Taylor while integrating Kaden Elliss, Tyree Wilson, and young defensive backs.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Kellen Moore is in his second season as head coach, with Doug Nussmeier retained as offensive coordinator and Brandon Staley retained as defensive coordinator.
- Tyler Shough finished 2025 as the starter and is treated in team/NFL reporting as the Year 2 quarterback to develop.
- Chris Olave agreed to a multi-year extension on 2026-07-30 after his All-Pro 2025 season.
- New Orleans added Travis Etienne, Noah Fant, David Edwards, Zach Wilson, Kaden Elliss, Tyree Wilson, Anfernee Jennings, Martin Emerson, and Ryan Wright.
- The 2026 draft class is led by WR Jordyn Tyson at No. 8, DT Christen Miller at No. 42, and TE Oscar Delp at No. 73.
- Notable departures include Demario Davis, Alontae Taylor, Taysom Hill, Foster Moreau, Jack Stoll, Luke Fortner, Jonathan Bullard, and Jonah Williams.
- Cameron Jordan re-signed for 2026 and announced it will be his final NFL season.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

These are projections to test, not established 2026 outcomes:

- Shough's fantasy profile improves because the coaching structure and supporting cast are stronger, but he remains a superflex watch until volume and efficiency are confirmed.
- Olave is the safest receiving projection; Tyson is the highest-upside new variable and should be charted for immediate WR2 route share.
- Etienne and Kamara can both matter, but weekly value depends on routes, two-minute work, and goal-line usage.
- Staley's defense has pass-rush continuity, but linebacker and hybrid defensive-back transition can create early matchup volatility.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

New Orleans kept the Moore/Nussmeier/Shough structure intact and upgraded around it. Etienne gives the backfield another receiving-capable lead option, Edwards supports the interior offensive line, Fant and Delp reset the tight-end room after multiple departures, and Tyson adds a first-round perimeter receiver next to Olave.

### Questions to resolve

- Whether Shough's late-2025 finish translates into stable 2026 efficiency.
- Whether Tyson immediately runs as the WR2 or rotates with Devaughn Vele and other depth receivers.
- Whether Etienne or Kamara leads passing-down and two-minute snaps.
- Whether Johnson/Fant/Delp creates one fantasy-relevant tight end or a package committee.

The durable offensive baseline is maintained in the [offensive ecosystem module](offense/README.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Staley returns after a 2025 defense that the official team profile says finished ninth in opponent net yards per game, fourth against the pass, third on third down, and strong against both run and pass by EPA. The top edge group still includes Chase Young, Cameron Jordan, and Carl Granderson, while Wilson and Jennings deepen the competition.

The main transition points are Davis leaving at linebacker and Taylor leaving the secondary. Saints camp analysis points to Elliss and Pete Werner as the likely linebacker starters and Julian Blackmon as a leading hybrid "star" candidate.

### Questions to resolve

- Whether Elliss becomes an every-down communicator and tackle-volume replacement.
- Whether Blackmon holds the hybrid star role.
- Whether Jordan's final-season workload stays high enough to preserve the edge-rush floor.
- Whether Tyree Wilson and Christen Miller provide useful defensive-line depth immediately.

## Coaching and scheme

New Orleans is not installing a new staff. The relevant leadership table is continuity:

| Unit | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Head coach | Kellen Moore | Kellen Moore |
| Offense | Doug Nussmeier | Doug Nussmeier |
| Defense | Brandon Staley | Brandon Staley |
| Special teams | Phil Galiano | Phil Galiano |

The fantasy consequence is that personnel changes matter more than schematic reset language. Moore and Nussmeier must turn the new skill talent into a cleaner Shough ecosystem, while Staley must replace Davis' communication/tackle role and Taylor's versatility without losing the defense's 2025 efficiency.

## Special teams

Ryan Wright was added as the punter and Barion Brown enters with return-specialist credentials. Track kicker competition, return roles, and whether Brown's return value helps him claim a game-day offensive package.
