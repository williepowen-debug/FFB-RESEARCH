# Tampa Bay Buccaneers source-monitoring guide

This directory tracks official records, reporters, analysts, and data sources used to monitor the 2026 Tampa Bay Buccaneers for fantasy-relevant availability, practice roles, usage, scheme, and roster construction.

## Files

- `registry.md` is the editorial monitoring rotation.
- `sources.csv` normalizes identities, roles, and handling.
- `endpoints.csv` contains usable monitoring locations.
- `candidates.csv` preserves discovery and omission-pass decisions.

Begin with official records and direct observations. Use independent reporting for changes in practice roles and organizational context, specialist work for film and camp interpretation, and measured regular-season usage to test conclusions. Team-employed reporters are official voices, not independent corroboration. Follow aggregation to its original source and do not reproduce subscription text.
