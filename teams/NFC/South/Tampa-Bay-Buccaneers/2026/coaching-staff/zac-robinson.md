# Zac Robinson - Tampa Bay Buccaneers

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Robinson replaces Josh Grizzard as offensive coordinator and inherits Baker Mayfield, Bucky Irving, Chris Godwin Jr., Emeka Egbuka, Jalen McMillan, Cade Otton, and a returning offensive line core. The team also added Kenneth Gainwell, Ted Hurst, Billy Schrauth, and Bauer Sharp while losing Mike Evans and Rachaad White.

## Scheme and tendencies

The official hiring story ties Robinson to the Sean McVay/Rams tree and notes his two seasons as Atlanta's offensive coordinator. Tampa's training-camp preview says his Falcons tendencies are the best public starting point, but he is expected to adapt to the Buccaneers' personnel. Mayfield has prior Rams overlap with Robinson, creating a cleaner teaching bridge than a brand-new QB/play-caller pairing.

## Personnel usage

The post-Evans receiver room is deeper than it is settled. Godwin is expected to remain central inside, Egbuka can settle into a Z role after a productive rookie season, McMillan offers alignment flexibility, Tez Johnson adds slot/speed depth, and Hurst was drafted as a classic X/deep-threat profile. Otton's extension signals weekly tight-end involvement, and Gainwell is the likely receiving complement to Irving.

## Pace and situational decisions

Fantasy impact will come from distribution more than raw offensive quality. The decisive early evidence is first-team formation usage, boot/keeper frequency for Mayfield, red-zone target replacement for Evans, and whether Irving or Gainwell controls high-value passing and goal-line snaps.

## Fantasy implications

Facts: Tampa hired Robinson, lost Evans and White, retained Otton, and added Gainwell and Hurst. Inference: the offense can remain efficient but with lower certainty on perimeter target concentration and backfield receiving distribution. Fantasy implication: Mayfield remains viable in superflex and matchup starts; Godwin, Egbuka, McMillan, Irving, Gainwell, Otton, and Hurst need route and touch charting before aggressive redraft assumptions.

## Statements and reporting

Bowles said Robinson had a plan to get the most out of Tampa's offensive talent. Buccaneers.com later framed the offense as having the pieces to return to top-five form on paper while learning another new system.

## Reliability cautions

This is Mayfield's fourth Tampa Bay offensive coordinator. Public offseason reporting can describe intent, but fantasy ranks should wait for preseason first-team usage and early regular-season pass-rate/target data.
