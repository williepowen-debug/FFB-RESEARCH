# Todd Bowles - Tampa Bay Buccaneers

- Role: Head coach; defensive play-calling authority
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2019 as defensive coordinator; head coach since 2022
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Bowles remains the head coach for his fifth season and continues to define Tampa Bay's defensive identity. The official 2026 staff page does not list a separate defensive coordinator, so defensive authority should be attributed to Bowles unless the team later announces a titled DC.

## Scheme and tendencies

The durable Bowles profile is run defense first, pressure second, and coverage flexibility behind it. Buccaneers.com described Bowles' seven-year Tampa defensive span as allowing the fewest rushing yards, generating the third-most sacks, and producing the fifth-most turnovers. The 2026 defensive preview also noted Tampa Bay was third in NFL blitz rate in 2025 at 35.0% and wants more four-man pressure in 2026.

## Personnel usage

The 2026 roster build matches that stated direction. Tampa Bay added Alex Anzalone, Al-Quadin Muhammad, A'Shawn Robinson, Rakeem Nunez-Roches, Rueben Bain Jr., Josiah Trotter, Keionte Scott, and DeMonte Capehart while replacing Jamel Dean, Logan Hall, Christian Izien, and potentially Lavonte David. Bowles has more front-seven size and edge bodies, but the secondary roles remain fluid.

## Pace and situational decisions

The key tactical question is whether improved rush depth lets Bowles reduce pressure dependency. If Bain, Muhammad, Yaya Diaby, David Walker, Vita Vea, Calijah Kancey, and A'Shawn Robinson create pressure without extra rushers, Tampa can leave more defenders in coverage and become less vulnerable to explosive pass plays.

## Fantasy implications

Facts: Tampa invested in edge rush, interior size, and linebacker coverage. Inference: the DST ceiling improves if those additions lower the need for blitz-heavy pressure. Fantasy implication: Tampa Bay can be treated as a matchup-sensitive DST with sack upside, but opponent passing-game quality and corner health should decide weekly starts.

## Statements and reporting

Bowles said after the 2025 season that Tampa needed changes on defense, either schematically or physically. The team's March and April moves followed that stated front-seven and defensive-edge priority.

## Reliability cautions

The defense has a clear coaching identity but several open personnel questions: Trotter's coverage workload, the Morrison/Parrish/Scott secondary alignment, Kancey's durability, and whether the edge additions convert pressures into sacks.
