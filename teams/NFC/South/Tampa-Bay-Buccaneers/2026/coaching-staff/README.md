# Tampa Bay Buccaneers 2026 Coaching Staff

Tampa Bay's 2026 authority model is a head-coach continuity build with major coordinator turnover on offense and special teams. Todd Bowles remains head coach and defensive architect. Zac Robinson replaces Josh Grizzard as offensive coordinator and arrives with T.J. Yates, Ken Zampese, and Chandler Whitmer as major passing-game support. Danny Smith replaces Thomas McGaughey on special teams.

The official staff page does not list a separate defensive coordinator. Treat Bowles as the defensive play-calling authority and Larry Foote as the defensive leadership profile for front/run-game and outside-linebacker influence.
