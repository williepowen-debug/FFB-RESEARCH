# Finding: Irving, Gainwell, and Otton define Tampa Bay's high-percentage touches

## Scope

- Team/player: Buccaneers running backs and tight ends
- Area: high-value touches and personnel usage
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Fact: Rachaad White left for Washington, and Tampa signed Kenneth Gainwell to a two-year contract. Fact: Buccaneers.com described Gainwell as a natural pass-catcher who had 73 catches and 1,023 yards from scrimmage for Pittsburgh in 2025. Fact: Cade Otton signed a three-year extension, and the free-agency tracker notes he played more offensive snaps than any NFL tight end over the prior three seasons.

Inference: Bucky Irving remains the lead-back bet, but Gainwell is a direct threat to passing-down and two-minute work. Otton's extension and snap history make him the stabilizing underneath option during the Robinson transition.

## Fantasy implication

Irving's redraft value depends on whether he keeps goal-line and early-down volume while Gainwell handles only complementary receiving work. Gainwell is more valuable in PPR, best ball, and contingency builds than standard scoring unless he earns goal-line usage. Otton is a volume-floor tight end whose ceiling depends on Robinson's red-zone design after Evans' departure.

## Evidence

- White is listed among players signed by other teams, and Gainwell is listed as a March 12 new arrival.
- The training-camp offensive preview states Gainwell complements Irving with a similar size and running-style profile.
- Otton's extension came on March 12 and was framed by the team as valuing his all-around role.
- The draft added Bauer Sharp, but Otton remains the only tight end with clear every-down evidence.
