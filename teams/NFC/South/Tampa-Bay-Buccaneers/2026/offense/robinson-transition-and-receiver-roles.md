# Finding: Zac Robinson transition makes receiver roles the core offensive variable

## Scope

- Team/player: Tampa Bay Buccaneers offense
- Area: coordinator transition and target distribution
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Fact: Tampa Bay hired Zac Robinson as offensive coordinator on January 24, 2026 after parting with Josh Grizzard. Fact: Mike Evans signed with San Francisco, while Tampa retained Chris Godwin Jr. and drafted Georgia State receiver Ted Hurst in Round 3. Fact: Buccaneers.com framed Hurst as a deep-threat/X candidate and described a room with Godwin, Emeka Egbuka, Jalen McMillan, Tez Johnson, and Hurst.

Inference: the offense can remain efficient with Baker Mayfield, but the 2026 fantasy edge is identifying which receivers absorb Evans' perimeter and red-zone work, not simply assuming the prior target tree repeats.

## Fantasy implication

Godwin has the cleanest established role if he is back in his primary slot/YAC function. Egbuka and McMillan carry route-volume paths, while Hurst is the clearest rookie archetype for Evans' old X/deep role. Mayfield's ceiling remains tied to whether Robinson creates enough explosive and red-zone answers without Evans.

## Evidence

- Robinson was hired as offensive coordinator and replaces Grizzard.
- Tampa's free-agency tracker states Evans signed a three-year deal with San Francisco.
- The post-draft offensive review says Hurst lined up at X in college and led the FBS in 20-plus-yard catches over the prior two seasons.
- Offseason reporting says Robinson wanted Egbuka to settle into the Z role, Godwin historically works best from the slot, and McMillan can play multiple spots.
