# Finding: Front-seven rebuild is designed to reduce blitz dependency

## Scope

- Team/player: Buccaneers front seven
- Area: pass rush, run defense, and DST profile
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Fact: Todd Bowles said after 2025 that Tampa needed changes on defense. Fact: Tampa added Al-Quadin Muhammad and A'Shawn Robinson in free agency, re-added Rakeem Nunez-Roches, and used first- and second-round picks on Rueben Bain Jr. and Josiah Trotter. Fact: Buccaneers.com reported Tampa was third in blitz percentage in 2025 and wants more consistent pressure with four rushers.

Inference: the offseason was built to raise the DST ceiling while making the defense less dependent on pressure calls. The pass-rush question is not whether Tampa has more bodies; it is whether Bain, Muhammad, Diaby, Walker, Kancey, Vea, and Robinson convert pressure without exposing the secondary.

## Fantasy implication

Tampa Bay DST should move up in matchups against shaky offensive lines or immobile quarterbacks. Bain is the rookie IDP watch, Diaby and Muhammad are sack-variance options in deeper formats, and Anzalone/Trotter affect tackle floors behind a bigger front.

## Evidence

- Muhammad had an 11-sack 2025 season in Detroit according to the Buccaneers' free-agency tracker.
- Bain was selected 15th overall after an 83-pressure 2025 college season, per the team's draft recap.
- The defensive preview says Tampa hopes added front talent lets it rush four and put more players in coverage.
- Bowles' 2026 defensive review came after a 2025 season with low sack conversion and pass-defense problems.
