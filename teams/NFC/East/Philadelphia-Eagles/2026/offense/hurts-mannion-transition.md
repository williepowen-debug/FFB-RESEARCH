# Finding: Hurts remains the anchor, but Mannion creates a scheme-transition checkpoint

## Scope

- Team/player: Philadelphia Eagles offense and Jalen Hurts
- Area: offensive coordinator transition, quarterback usage, and scheme installation
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Jalen Hurts remains the offense's central fantasy projection because his rushing profile, scoring environment, and supporting infrastructure are still strong. The change is that 2026 adds another offensive coordinator transition. Sean Mannion is in his first season as Eagles offensive coordinator after two seasons with Green Bay, and team reporting has framed the offense as a blend of Mannion's background and successful Eagles concepts.

The practical implication is not to downgrade Hurts heavily before seeing the offense. It is to chart how Mannion changes shotgun/under-center balance, designed quarterback runs, play-action, pocket movement, and target distribution. Hurts has already worked through repeated play-caller changes, but fantasy projections should treat the exact 2026 shape as unsettled until preseason usage or Week 1 evidence confirms it.

## Fantasy implication

- Hurts remains a high-end quarterback and superflex anchor because the offense still runs through him.
- A pass-efficiency bump is plausible if Mannion imports Green Bay-style spacing and protection answers.
- A designed-run or goal-line reduction would matter more than a small pass-rate change because Hurts' rushing touchdowns drive elite fantasy separation.
- New terminology and a rebuilt receiver group can increase early-season volatility for weekly props and DFS stacking.

## Evidence

- The Eagles hired Mannion as offensive coordinator on January 29, 2026.
- Mannion's official Eagles profile says he worked with Green Bay in 2024-25 and supported efficient passing and drive metrics during that period.
- Team reporting on February 24 described Mannion as a first-time play-caller and said Philadelphia would combine his background with concepts that already fit Hurts.
- Team reporting on May 21 said Mannion was building a blend and working to marry the run game and pass game.
- A July 27 team story noted Hurts entering his seventh season with his sixth offensive coordinator and focusing on building with Mannion.
