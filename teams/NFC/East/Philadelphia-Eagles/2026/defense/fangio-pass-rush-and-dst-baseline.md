# Finding: Fangio continuity keeps D/ST ceiling, but edge rotation needs health confirmation

## Scope

- Team/player: Philadelphia Eagles defense
- Area: defensive coordinator continuity, pass rush, and D/ST projection
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Vic Fangio's return keeps Philadelphia's defensive structure highly credible for fantasy matchup grading and D/ST projection. His official Eagles profile cites elite 2024-25 defensive output, and the 2026 roster still has a strong interior foundation with Jalen Carter and Jordan Davis extended or retained, plus Moro Ojomo and Ty Robinson in the rotation.

The caution is edge health and rotation clarity. The Eagles acquired Jonathan Greenard from Minnesota, added Arnold Ebiketie and A.J. Epenesa, and retained Nolan Smith Jr. and Jalyx Hunt through the first August depth chart. But Greenard was placed on Active/PUP on July 29, and official position-preview reporting described him as working back from injury after a December surgery. That makes the D/ST ceiling attractive but dependent on pressure depth beyond the interior line.

## Fantasy implication

- Eagles D/ST should remain in the draftable and streamable tier if Greenard is active and the edge rotation stabilizes.
- Interior disruption from Carter and Davis supports weekly sack/turnover projection even if one edge role is unsettled.
- If Greenard misses time or remains limited, matchup grading should downgrade pressure ceiling while preserving coverage respect.
- IDP managers should prioritize full-time roles over rotation names until Smith/Hunt/Greenard/Ebiketie/Epenesa usage is visible.

## Evidence

- The Eagles list Fangio as defensive coordinator in his third season with the team.
- Fangio's official profile credits the Eagles with first-ranked defensive points allowed, passing defense, and opponent passer rating across 2024-25.
- The transactions page records Jordan Davis' extension through 2029, Jalen Carter's extension through 2031, and the fifth-year options for Carter and Nolan Smith Jr.
- The Eagles acquired Greenard on April 24, added Arnold Ebiketie and A.J. Epenesa, and placed Greenard on Active/PUP on July 29.
- The July 16 edge preview reported Greenard was expected to participate in team drills at training camp after working back from surgery, while Smith and Hunt were the returning edge starters.
- The August official depth chart listed Nolan Smith Jr. and Jalyx Hunt first at edge.
