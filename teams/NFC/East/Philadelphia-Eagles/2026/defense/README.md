# Philadelphia Eagles 2026 Defense

Philadelphia's defensive baseline remains Fangio-led and talent-rich, but the 2026 version has moving pieces. Jalen Carter and Jordan Davis are retained long term, Nolan Smith Jr. and Jalyx Hunt are continuity edge pieces, Jonathan Greenard was acquired but opened camp on PUP, and the secondary has a new safety/corner structure after Reed Blankenship left and Sydney Brown was traded.
