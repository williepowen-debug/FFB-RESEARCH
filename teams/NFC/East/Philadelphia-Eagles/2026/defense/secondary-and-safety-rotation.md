# Finding: Cornerback ceiling is high, but safety rotation is the defensive volatility point

## Scope

- Team/player: Philadelphia Eagles secondary
- Area: cornerback additions, safety turnover, and Fangio coverage roles
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Philadelphia has a high-end cornerback ceiling with Quinyon Mitchell, Cooper DeJean, and Riq Woolen, but the safety structure should be treated as the main defensive volatility point. Reed Blankenship left in free agency and Sydney Brown was traded. The team retained Marcus Epps, added J.T. Gray and Jonathan Jones, and is evaluating Andrew Mukuba, Michael Carter II, Andre Sam, Kapena Gushiken, Cole Wisniewski, and Cooper DeJean's hybrid usage.

The official depth chart lists Woolen and Mitchell at outside corner, DeJean at nickel, and DeJean/Michael Carter/Marcus Epps plus Andrew Mukuba/Andre Sam/Cole Wisniewski at safety. The safety preview directly described the competition as open. For fantasy matchup purposes, that means the Eagles can still be a difficult passing matchup, but weekly grading should account for communication and role clarity at safety.

## Fantasy implication

- Wide receivers facing Philadelphia should be graded against a potentially elite corner group if Woolen locks down the outside job.
- Tight ends and slot receivers require more specific charting because DeJean's nickel/safety split changes who handles interior coverage.
- Eagles D/ST projection is strong when secondary assignments are stable; it becomes more volatile if safety communication breaks down.
- IDP safety value should be held until a full-time role emerges.

## Evidence

- The Eagles' free-agency tracker lists Reed Blankenship leaving for Houston, Sydney Brown being traded to Atlanta, and J.T. Gray/Jonathan Jones/Riq Woolen arriving.
- The transactions page records the Sydney Brown trade and Riq Woolen/J.T. Gray/Jonathan Jones acquisitions.
- The July 13 cornerback preview said Mitchell and DeJean were 2025 first-team All-Pros and described Woolen as competing for the outside corner spot across from Mitchell and DeJean.
- The July 14 safety preview identified Mukuba as a favorite for one safety spot and said Blankenship and Brown would likely be replaced in house.
- The same safety preview quoted Fangio describing the safety competition as open and naming Epps, DeJean, Michael Carter II, Andre Sam, Kapena Gushiken, and J.T. Gray as evaluation pieces.
- The August depth chart lists DeJean at nickel and in the safety structure, with Mukuba and Epps also in the safety picture.
