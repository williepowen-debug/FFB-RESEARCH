# Vic Fangio - Philadelphia Eagles Defensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Fangio remains Philadelphia's defensive coordinator for 2026. The Eagles' official profile lists him in his third year as defensive coordinator and credits the Eagles with elite defensive results across 2024-25, including strong points allowed, pass defense, opponent passer rating, completion-rate prevention, and forced-fumble production.

## Scheme and tendencies

Fangio's public profile is built around disguise, coverage structure, and limiting explosive passing. The current roster fits that identity around Jalen Carter and Jordan Davis inside, Nolan Smith Jr. and Jalyx Hunt on the edge, Zack Baun and Jihaad Campbell at linebacker, and Quinyon Mitchell/Cooper DeJean/Riq Woolen in the secondary.

The 2026 defense is not pure continuity. Reed Blankenship left in free agency, Sydney Brown was traded, Riq Woolen and Jonathan Jones were added, Jonathan Greenard was acquired, and the edge rotation added Arnold Ebiketie and A.J. Epenesa. Fangio's task is integrating those additions while keeping coverage stable.

## Personnel usage

The official depth chart lists Cooper DeJean at nickel corner and also in the safety structure, with Andrew Mukuba, Marcus Epps, Michael Carter II, and J.T. Gray in the safety mix. The team's safety preview quoted Fangio describing the competition as open and indicated base packages could use DeJean differently than nickel packages.

At edge, the first August depth chart lists Nolan Smith Jr. and Jalyx Hunt first, with Greenard on PUP and veteran additions competing behind them. This keeps early D/ST projections tied to health and rotation clarity.

## Pace and situational decisions

For fantasy, Fangio's defense should be evaluated by pressure without blitzing, third-down personnel, red-zone coverage, and whether the safety rotation creates communication errors. The coordinator baseline is strong, but early matchups should not ignore new-player volatility.

## Fantasy implications

- Philadelphia D/ST remains a viable fantasy target if the front creates pressure and the safety rotation avoids explosive breakdowns.
- Jalen Carter and Jordan Davis are the defensive-floor anchors; Greenard's health can raise the pass-rush ceiling.
- Mitchell, DeJean, and Woolen give the Eagles a high-end corner ceiling, but outside-corner and safety roles should be checked before overrating matchup difficulty.
- IDP value is more concentrated in Carter/Baun/Campbell/edge rotation than in the safeties until full-time roles settle.

## Statements and reporting

The Eagles' official Fangio profile cites two seasons of top-tier defensive performance. The safety preview included Fangio's public acknowledgement that the safety competition is open, naming Epps, DeJean, Michael Carter II, Andre Sam, Kapena Gushiken, and J.T. Gray as parts of the evaluation.

## Reliability cautions

Official depth charts are compiled by the communications department, not necessarily the coaching staff. Use them as a baseline, then update with snap counts and preseason first-team usage.
