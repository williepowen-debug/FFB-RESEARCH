# Sean Mannion - Philadelphia Eagles Offensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Mannion is Philadelphia's new offensive coordinator for 2026 after two seasons on Green Bay's staff. The Eagles list him as offensive coordinator, and the hiring article says the team announced him for the role on January 29, 2026.

This is his first NFL offensive coordinator job and first identified full-offense play-calling role. His work must be evaluated through early-season play selection rather than assumed from Green Bay output alone.

## Scheme and tendencies

Official team reporting ties Mannion to Green Bay and the Shanahan/McVay coaching tree. His Eagles profile credits the 2024-25 Packers offense with strong yards-per-attempt, points-per-drive, sack-rate, passer-rating, and third-down outcomes while he worked as offensive assistant and quarterbacks coach.

The Eagles have described the 2026 offense as a blend of Mannion's experience and concepts already proven with Hurts. Mannion also said in May that the offense was still being built and that he wanted to marry the run game and pass game.

## Personnel usage

The offense has more new variables than the usual Eagles baseline. A.J. Brown was traded to New England, Makai Lemon was drafted in Round 1, Eli Stowers was drafted in Round 2, Dontayvion Wicks was acquired from Green Bay, Hollywood Brown and Elijah Moore arrived in free agency, and Dallas Goedert returned on a one-year deal.

Mannion also works with a new offensive line coach after Jeff Stoutland stepped away and Chris Kuper took over the unit. That makes run-game structure and protection calls especially important for Hurts and Barkley projections.

## Pace and situational decisions

No reliable 2026 regular-season pace sample exists yet. The most actionable preseason charting items are first-team tempo, under-center usage, play-action depth, screen/RPO frequency, red-zone quarterback run rate, and whether Barkley is used more as a pass-game connector.

## Fantasy implications

- Hurts' floor remains elite because of rushing and supporting talent, but a sixth coordinator in seven seasons adds early-season scheme uncertainty.
- Barkley should remain a high-volume starter if the offense stays efficient; fullback or multi-TE packages could change his touchdown and receiving mix.
- Smith has a path to target leadership, but Lemon, Wicks, Brown, Moore, Goedert, and Stowers create more spread-out outcomes than a simple A.J. Brown vacancy replacement.
- Goedert retains immediate TE relevance, but Stowers is a dynasty and tight end premium monitor.

## Statements and reporting

The Eagles' May 21 recap said Mannion was building a blend of his past work and recent Eagles concepts. The July 27 Hurts story said Hurts was focused on building with Mannion and learning one language within the offense.

## Reliability cautions

Most public evidence is team-site reporting from installation periods. It is strong for facts about the hire and stated intent, but low-to-medium for projecting exact play-call rates.
