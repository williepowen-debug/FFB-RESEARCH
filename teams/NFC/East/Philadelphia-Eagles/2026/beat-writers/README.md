# Philadelphia Eagles source-monitoring guide

This directory tracks official records and independent reporting used to monitor the 2026 Eagles.

## Files

- [`registry.md`](registry.md): schema-validated editorial registry.
- [`sources.csv`](sources.csv): normalized source identities and roles.
- [`endpoints.csv`](endpoints.csv): monitoring pages and access notes.

## Signal hierarchy

1. Official transactions, injury reports, gamebooks, participation, and roster status.
2. Measured snaps, routes, touches, targets, alignments, and special-teams usage.
3. Direct practice observations from credentialed reporters.
4. Corroborated independent reporting and on-record comments.
5. Film or scheme interpretation tied to observable evidence.
