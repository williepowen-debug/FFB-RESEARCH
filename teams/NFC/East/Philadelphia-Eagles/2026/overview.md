# Philadelphia Eagles - 2026 Mannion Transition and Fangio Continuity

- Conference/division: NFC East
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Scope: changes from the 2025 team to the 2026 preseason roster and staff
- Build tier: Core

Philadelphia remains a premium fantasy ecosystem because Jalen Hurts, Saquon Barkley, DeVonta Smith, Dallas Goedert, a strong offensive line, Vic Fangio, Jalen Carter, Jordan Davis, Quinyon Mitchell, and Cooper DeJean all remain central to the team. The 2026 module is still a transition file: Sean Mannion replaces Kevin Patullo as offensive coordinator, Jeff Stoutland has stepped away, A.J. Brown was traded to New England, Makai Lemon and Eli Stowers arrive through the draft, and the defense has new edge and safety/corner variables.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Sirianni remains head coach, Mannion is the new offensive coordinator, Fangio remains defensive coordinator, and Michael Clay remains special teams coordinator.
- Hurts remains QB1 on the official depth chart with Andy Dalton, Tanner McKee, and rookie Cole Payton behind him.
- Barkley remains the first running back, with Tank Bigsby, Will Shipley, Dameon Pierce, Carson Steele, and Ja'Quinden Jackson forming the depth and package competition.
- A.J. Brown was traded to the Patriots, leaving Smith as the clearest returning receiver anchor.
- The Eagles added Lemon, Hollywood Brown, Elijah Moore, and Wicks to a receiver room that also includes Darius Cooper and depth/special-teams candidates.
- Goedert and Calcaterra returned, while Stowers was selected in Round 2.
- Fangio remains over a defense built around Carter, Davis, Smith, Hunt, Baun, Campbell, Mitchell, DeJean, Woolen, and Mukuba/Epps safety competition.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

- Hurts remains a top quarterback projection, but Mannion's first season requires charting of designed runs, goal-line usage, and play-action/pocket movement.
- Smith has the cleanest route to lead targets, while Lemon and Wicks are the highest-leverage role monitors.
- Barkley remains the lead back; the uncertainty is whether the new blocking structure or depth packages change his target/goal-line mix.
- Goedert is still the immediate tight end baseline, with Stowers more of a dynasty and tight end premium watch unless early routes climb.
- Eagles D/ST remains viable if Greenard's ramp and the safety rotation stabilize.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

Mannion replaces Patullo after two years on Green Bay's staff, and Kuper replaces Stoutland as offensive line coach. The pass-catching room has been rebuilt around Smith rather than Brown, with Lemon, Wicks, Hollywood Brown, Elijah Moore, and Stowers all added or elevated into major monitoring roles.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Mannion keep Hurts' designed rushing and goal-line involvement intact?
- Which of Lemon, Wicks, Hollywood Brown, and Elijah Moore earns full-time routes with Smith?
- Does Goedert remain a clear full-time tight end, or does Stowers immediately force two-TE/package usage?
- How does Kuper's offensive line room handle the first year after Stoutland?
- Does Barkley's receiving and goal-line role stay elite?

The evidence and invalidation tests are maintained in the [offensive research module](offense/README.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Fangio remains the defensive coordinator, and Carter/Davis remain the interior anchors. The edge room has both continuity and volatility: Smith and Hunt are first-depth-chart edge names, while Greenard was acquired but placed on Active/PUP, and Ebiketie/Epenesa add veteran competition. In the secondary, Woolen and Jonathan Jones arrive after Blankenship left and Sydney Brown was traded.

### Questions to resolve

- Is Greenard healthy enough to raise the third-down pass-rush ceiling by Week 1?
- Does Woolen secure the outside-corner role opposite Mitchell?
- How often does DeJean play nickel versus safety?
- Do Mukuba, Epps, Michael Carter II, J.T. Gray, and the rookies create a stable safety rotation?

Philadelphia should still be graded as a difficult passing matchup when its front and coverage structure are intact, but early-season grades should track safety communication and edge availability. See the [defensive research module](defense/README.md).

### Analytical projection

The Eagles should be treated as a strong but volatile fantasy offense until target and run-game usage stabilize. Hurts and Barkley remain elite baseline assets, Smith has an expanded target path, and Lemon/Wicks/Brown/Moore/Goedert/Stowers require route evidence. Defensively, Fangio's unit remains a serious D/ST and matchup factor, with Greenard health and safety rotation the main checks.

## Special teams

Michael Clay remains special teams coordinator. Braden Mann re-signed and is listed as punter/holder, Jake Elliott is listed as kicker, and Rocco Underwood is listed at long snapper. Britain Covey, Lemon, Elijah Moore, Shipley, Pierce, and Bigsby appear in the return structure on the unofficial depth chart. This remains Core-tier background unless return roles become fantasy or roster-relevant.
