# Klayton Adams - Dallas Cowboys Offensive Coordinator

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: retained from 2025
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Adams remains Dallas' offensive coordinator under Schottenheimer. The official staff page lists him in that role, and team reporting described him as a first-time NFL coordinator in 2025 who helped design concepts while Schottenheimer called plays.

## Scheme and tendencies

Adams' public offseason emphasis was that Dallas cannot simply recycle the 2025 offense because opponents now have a full year of film. The offense retained its main fantasy pieces: Prescott, Lamb, Pickens, Ferguson, Williams, and a line anchored inside by Tyler Smith, Cooper Beebe, Tyler Booker, and Terrence Steele, with left tackle still unresolved.

## Personnel usage

Adams is especially relevant to offensive-line development and run/pass design. He praised Tyler Guyton's offseason work while Schottenheimer framed left tackle as a competition between Guyton and Nate Thomas. The offense also added Drew Shelton in the fourth round as a tackle with possible inside cross-training.

## Pace and situational decisions

Adams is not currently identified as the play-caller, so pace and fourth-down tendencies should be attributed to Schottenheimer unless official reporting changes. Adams' fantasy relevance is more about weekly plan evolution and line/run-game problem solving.

## Fantasy implications

- Adams' Year 2 adjustments matter for whether Lamb and Pickens can both sustain premium target and touchdown value.
- The left tackle outcome is a material Prescott and Pickens/Lamb efficiency variable.
- Williams' three-year extension gives the run game continuity, but preseason usage must confirm whether Dallas keeps him in a workhorse profile.

## Statements and reporting

Team reporting from February said Adams wanted the offense to "seek the edge" in Year 2 rather than repeat 2025 unchanged. June updates credited Adams with positive comments on Guyton's offseason while noting the left tackle battle had not been settled.

## Reliability cautions

The exact Adams-Schottenheimer division of design authority is not fully public. Treat Adams as a major offensive architect but not the game-day play-caller unless later reporting says otherwise.
