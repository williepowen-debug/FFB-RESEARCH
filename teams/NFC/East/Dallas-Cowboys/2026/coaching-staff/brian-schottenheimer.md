# Brian Schottenheimer - Dallas Cowboys Head Coach

- Role: Head coach and offensive play-caller
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: retained as head coach from 2025
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Schottenheimer remains Dallas' head coach and offensive play-caller. Official team reporting after the 2025 season described the 2025 offense as one of the league's most prolific under Schottenheimer, while the defensive failure drove the coordinator change from Matt Eberflus to Christian Parker.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2026 offensive baseline is continuity rather than redesign. Dak Prescott returns with CeeDee Lamb and franchise-tagged George Pickens, while Javonte Williams was retained after a 1,201-yard rushing season. Schottenheimer and Klayton Adams enter Year 2 with opponents now having a full year of film on the offense, making self-scout and concept evolution a key preseason test.

## Personnel usage

The fantasy-relevant usage questions sit around three groups: Lamb/Pickens/secondary receivers, Williams' high-value backfield workload, and the offensive line's left tackle competition. Schottenheimer publicly opened the left tackle job between Tyler Guyton and Nate Thomas, with Tyler Smith's guard/tackle flexibility functioning as the emergency best-five lever.

## Pace and situational decisions

No stable 2026 pace sample exists yet. Treat 2025 success as a starting point, then verify whether Year 2 adjustments lean more pass-heavy around Prescott's receivers or more balanced after Williams' extension.

## Fantasy implications

- Prescott, Lamb, Pickens, and Williams begin as the bankable fantasy core because coaching and top-end personnel mostly carried over.
- Pickens' signed tag reduces holdout risk for 2026 but does not remove long-term dynasty contract risk.
- Guyton/Thomas left tackle uncertainty is the main offensive floor risk for Prescott efficiency and downfield passing.
- Schottenheimer's ability to counter defensive adjustments will decide whether Dallas repeats a high-end offensive environment.

## Statements and reporting

Official Cowboys reporting said Schottenheimer led the defensive coordinator search after Dallas moved on from Eberflus, and later reported that Schottenheimer remained pleased with offseason additions while acknowledging inside linebacker and defensive rebuilding needs. The current official staff page lists him as head coach.

## Reliability cautions

The 2026 offensive projection is an inference from 2025 production, roster continuity, and offseason reporting. It should be updated after preseason first-team snaps and early regular-season neutral-script pass rate.
