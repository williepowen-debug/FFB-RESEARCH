# Dallas Cowboys 2026 Coaching Staff

Dallas keeps Brian Schottenheimer and Klayton Adams on offense after a productive 2025 passing/rushing base, but the defensive authority model changed sharply. Matt Eberflus is out, Christian Parker is in as defensive coordinator, and Parker's staff was rebuilt with Ryan Smith, Derrick Ansley, Marcus Dixon, Chidera Uzo-Diribe, Scott Symons, Robert Muschamp, Demeitre Brim, and J.J. Clark.

## Authority model

Schottenheimer remains the top offensive authority and play-caller. Adams is the offensive coordinator shaping the Year 2 evolution, with Ken Dorsey, Junior Adams, Derrick Foster, Conor Riley, Steve Shimko, and Lunda Wells supporting position and passing-game detail. Parker is the new defensive play-calling authority and is installing a multiple structure around Quinnen Williams, Kenny Clark, Rashan Gary, Caleb Downs, Malachi Lawrence, and a rebuilt secondary/linebacker group.
