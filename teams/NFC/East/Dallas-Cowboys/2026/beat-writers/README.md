# Dallas Cowboys source-monitoring guide

Candidate discovery and omission-pass decisions are recorded in [candidates.csv](candidates.csv).

This directory tracks official records and differentiated reporting used to monitor the 2026 Cowboys.

## Files

- [`registry.md`](registry.md): schema-validated source rotation and coverage audit.
- [`sources.csv`](sources.csv): normalized source identities, roles, and handling rules.
- [`endpoints.csv`](endpoints.csv): usable monitoring and assignment-verification pages.

## Signal hierarchy

1. Official transactions, injury reports, roster status, gamebooks, and participation.
2. Measured snaps, routes, touches, targets, alignments, and special-teams usage.
3. Direct observations from credentialed practice reporters.
4. Corroborated independent reporting and on-record comments.
5. Film and scheme interpretation tied to observable evidence.

## Monitoring focus

Track the backfield and receiver rotations, offensive-line combinations, Christian Parker's defensive
packages, and first-unit special-teams roles. Team-produced podcasts supply access and observation but
do not independently confirm organizational claims.
