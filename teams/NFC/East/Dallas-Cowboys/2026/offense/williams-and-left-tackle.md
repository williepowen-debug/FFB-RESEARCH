# Finding: Javonte Williams is secured, but left tackle is the offensive floor variable

## Scope

- Team/player: Dallas Cowboys run game and offensive line
- Area: running back retention, left tackle competition, offensive floor
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Dallas made Williams one of the clearest offensive priorities, signing him to a three-year, $24 million contract after he produced a career-best 1,201 rushing yards and 11 rushing touchdowns in 2025. Team reporting credited the Cowboys with the ninth-best rushing offense in 2025 by yards per game and a 4.6 yards-per-carry team rate.

The run-game floor still depends heavily on the line. Schottenheimer identified 2026 as a major year for Tyler Guyton, discussed Guyton's injuries and technical development, and later opened left tackle competition between Guyton and Nate Thomas. Fourth-round rookie Drew Shelton adds tackle/swing depth and was also tried at guard during rookie work.

## Fantasy implication

- Williams should open as Dallas' projected lead back and a strong touchdown-environment bet.
- His ceiling depends on whether Dallas preserves high-value touches rather than simply paying him as a stabilizer.
- Left tackle uncertainty is a material efficiency risk for Williams, Prescott, Lamb, and Pickens.
- Shelton is more of a dynasty/offensive-line stability note than a 2026 fantasy player.

## Evidence

- Williams agreed to a three-year, $24 million contract on February 21.
- Cowboys reporting cited his 1,201 rushing yards and 11 rushing touchdowns in 2025.
- The same report said Dallas averaged 125.6 rushing yards per game and 4.6 yards per carry in 2025.
- Schottenheimer said Guyton has a chance to be elite but needs a healthy offseason and technical growth.
- In June, Schottenheimer announced a left tackle competition between Guyton and Nate Thomas.
- Draft reporting described Shelton as able to play both tackle spots, with early guard cross-training.
