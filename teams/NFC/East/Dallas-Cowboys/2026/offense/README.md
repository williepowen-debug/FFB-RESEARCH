# Dallas Cowboys 2026 Offense

The Cowboys offense is a continuity build with a few high-leverage questions. Brian Schottenheimer remains head coach and play-caller, Klayton Adams remains offensive coordinator, George Pickens signed the franchise tag, Javonte Williams signed a three-year extension, and Dak Prescott still has CeeDee Lamb at the center of the passing game. Left tackle is the most important unresolved variable.
