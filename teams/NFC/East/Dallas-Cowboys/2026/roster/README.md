# Dallas Cowboys 2026 Roster Changes

These files preserve the personnel transition supporting the Cowboys Core-tier module. Dallas is an offense-continuity and defense-rebuild team: Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb, George Pickens, and Javonte Williams remain the fantasy core, while the defense was remade through Christian Parker, Rashan Gary, Caleb Downs, Malachi Lawrence, Jaishawn Barham, Dee Winters, Cobie Durant, P.J. Locke, and Jalen Thompson.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): material player exits and retirements.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): external veteran additions and trades.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): re-signings, tags, and tenders.
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): seven-player 2026 draft class.
- [`undrafted-free-agents.csv`](undrafted-free-agents.csv): official post-draft UDFA class.

## Headline changes

- Kept Javonte Williams on a three-year deal after his 2025 rushing breakout.
- Kept George Pickens on the franchise tag for 2026, preserving the Lamb/Pickens receiving ceiling.
- Added Rashan Gary, Dee Winters, Cobie Durant, Jalen Thompson, P.J. Locke, and defensive-line depth.
- Drafted five defensive players with seven picks, led by Caleb Downs and Malachi Lawrence.
- Traded Osa Odighizuwa and Solomon Thomas after investing heavily in Quinnen Williams and Kenny Clark.
