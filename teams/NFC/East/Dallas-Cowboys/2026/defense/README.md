# Dallas Cowboys 2026 Defense

The Cowboys defense is a high-change rebuild. Christian Parker replaces Matt Eberflus, most of the defensive staff changed, and Dallas added Rashan Gary, Dee Winters, Cobie Durant, P.J. Locke, Jalen Thompson, Caleb Downs, Malachi Lawrence, Jaishawn Barham, Devin Moore, and LT Overton around Quinnen Williams and Kenny Clark. The fantasy question is how quickly the talent converts to pressure, turnovers, and fewer explosive plays.
