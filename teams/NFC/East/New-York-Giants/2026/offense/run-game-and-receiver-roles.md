# Finding: Run-game investment and receiver crowding define the Giants offense

## Scope

- Team/player: Giants skill players and offensive line
- Area: backfield roles, Nabers health, receiver hierarchy, and run-game structure
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

New York's offseason points toward a more physical offensive identity, but the fantasy shape is unsettled. The Giants drafted Mauigoa No. 10 overall, signed Ricard, retained Eluemunor, and have Skattebo back in team work after his 2025 injury. Giants.com reported Skattebo was good to go for camp and took part in team drills on July 29.

The receiver room has quantity but not clarity behind Nabers. Nabers is working back from a Week 4 knee injury, and Schoen said the team had a plan for him while stopping short of guaranteeing Week 1 readiness. The Giants added Mooney, Austin, Beckham, Berrios, Smith-Schuster, and rookie Malachi Fields, making preseason route order more important than name recognition.

## Fantasy implication

- Skattebo has the best backfield upside if he owns early-down and goal-line work in a heavier structure.
- Tracy remains relevant if he keeps passing-down or explosive-changeup work.
- Nabers should be treated as the target-share bet when healthy, but his ramp is a real draft-risk input.
- Mooney and Likely have the cleanest archetype paths to usable roles; Beckham, Smith-Schuster, Austin, Berrios, Slayton, and Fields need route evidence.
- Mauigoa starting would improve the run-game floor, but it could also reduce passing volume if the staff plays through defense and field position.

## Evidence

- The Giants traded Dexter Lawrence for the No. 10 pick and used that pick on Mauigoa.
- The Giants signed Ricard and retained Eluemunor during the March free-agency wave.
- Giants.com projected a run-game lean in the new offense because of Harbaugh, Roman, Mauigoa, Ricard, and Skattebo.
- On July 29, Schoen said Skattebo was ready to do everything at the start of camp.
- On July 29, Schoen described Nabers' Week 1 return as a wait-and-see situation with a plan in place.
- The Giants added Mooney, Austin, Beckham, Berrios, Smith-Schuster, and Fields to a room that still lists Nabers and Slayton on the current roster.
