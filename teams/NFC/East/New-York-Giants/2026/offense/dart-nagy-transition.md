# Finding: Jaxson Dart enters Year 2 in a blended Nagy offense

## Scope

- Team/player: New York Giants passing offense
- Area: quarterback development and first-year coordinator transition
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Dart is the offensive hinge. The Giants moved from the Daboll/Kafka structure to Harbaugh with Nagy as offensive coordinator, Callahan as passing game coordinator/quarterbacks coach, and Roman as senior offensive assistant. Giants.com described the 2026 offense as having Chiefs-system shades from Nagy plus influence from Callahan and Roman.

The staff has publicly emphasized Dart's traits, but the structure is unlikely to be pure pass volume. The same official preview projected a run-game lean because of Harbaugh, Roman, first-round tackle Francis Mauigoa, and fullback Patrick Ricard. That makes Dart's fantasy profile a balance between improved support and possible volume suppression.

## Fantasy implication

- Dart is a superflex and two-QB growth target if his rushing and off-platform traits remain featured.
- The passing game can support Nabers and one secondary option, but it needs efficiency if run rate rises.
- Tight end Isaiah Likely is a watchlist player if the Chiefs/Callahan influence creates movable receiving work.
- A conservative run-first build would lower Dart's weekly ceiling unless red-zone rushing and explosive play rate compensate.

## Evidence

- Harbaugh announced Nagy as offensive coordinator and Callahan as passing game coordinator/quarterbacks coach on February 18, 2026.
- Giants.com reported Nagy spent nine of the previous 13 years working with Andy Reid and the Chiefs.
- Nagy said he watched Dart from Kansas City and came away impressed by Dart's accuracy, toughness, running ability, and competitiveness.
- Giants.com's July offense preview said the offense should include Nagy, Callahan, and Roman influences.
- The same preview projected a run-game lean based on Harbaugh, Roman, Mauigoa, and Ricard.
