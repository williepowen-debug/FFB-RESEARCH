# New York Giants source-monitoring guide

Candidate discovery and omission-pass decisions are recorded in [candidates.csv](candidates.csv).

This directory tracks official records and differentiated reporting used to monitor the 2026 Giants.

## Files

- [`registry.md`](registry.md): schema-validated source rotation and coverage audit.
- [`sources.csv`](sources.csv): normalized identities and handling rules.
- [`endpoints.csv`](endpoints.csv): monitoring and assignment-verification pages.

## Signal hierarchy

1. Official transactions, injuries, roster status, gamebooks, and participation.
2. Measured snaps, routes, touches, targets, alignments, and special-teams usage.
3. Direct credentialed practice observations.
4. Corroborated independent reporting.
5. Film analysis tied to observable evidence.

## Monitoring focus

Track John Harbaugh's new program, offensive installation, the quarterback and backfield workload,
receiver availability, and first-unit line combinations. Separate team access from independent confirmation.
