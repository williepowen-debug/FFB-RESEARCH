# Finding: Run-defense repair is the Giants defense's proof point

## Scope

- Team/player: Giants run defense and secondary
- Area: matchup profile, DST floor, and opposing fantasy decisions
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Wilson's defense cannot become a fantasy downgrade until the run defense improves. Giants.com reported the 2025 defense finished 28th in yards, 26th in scoring, and allowed a league-high 5.31 yards per carry. That was followed by the Lawrence trade, making the interior defensive-line replacement plan a central 2026 question.

The Giants responded with Edmunds, Reese, DJ Reader, Shelby Harris, Bobby Jamison-Travis, and Jack Kelly while adding Greg Newsome II, Ar'Darius Washington, Jason Pinnock, and Colton Hood to the coverage mix. The coverage talent can help the pressure plan, but the DST floor depends on whether early-down runs stop creating efficient opponent scripts.

## Fantasy implication

- Do not automatically downgrade opposing running backs until early-season run fits improve.
- Giants DST should be viewed as matchup-dependent until the run-defense floor is established.
- Pressure and coverage upgrades can create spike weeks against pass-heavy or trailing opponents.
- IDP tackle volume could favor Edmunds if opponents test the run early.

## Evidence

- Giants.com identified run defense as a major Wilson and Harbaugh priority after the 2025 defense allowed 5.31 yards per carry.
- The Giants traded Lawrence to Cincinnati for the No. 10 pick on April 19.
- New York signed Edmunds, Reader, Harris, Newsome, Washington, and Pinnock and drafted Reese, Hood, Jamison-Travis, and Kelly.
- Giants.com's current roster verifies Burns, Thibodeaux, Carter, Edmunds, Reese, Reader, Newsome, Nubin, Washington, Pinnock, and Hood are active as of verification.
