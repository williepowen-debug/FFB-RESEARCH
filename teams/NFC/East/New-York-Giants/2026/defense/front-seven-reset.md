# Finding: The Giants rebuilt the front seven around multiplicity

## Scope

- Team/player: Giants front seven
- Area: pressure packages, linebacker roles, and IDP/DST relevance
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

New York's defensive bet is multiplicity. Wilson takes over with Burns, Thibodeaux, Carter, Edmunds, and Reese all capable of creating pressure or changing the pre-snap picture. Giants.com described the plan as "organized chaos" because those linebackers and edge players have similar size profiles and can rush or drop.

Reese is the key rookie variable. The Giants drafted him fifth overall, and Harbaugh said he can line up at inside linebacker, in multiple gaps, off the edge, and in passing-situation games with the veteran rushers. That creates IDP upside if he earns full-time work, but it also creates usage risk if the staff treats him as a package weapon early.

## Fantasy implication

- The Giants DST has sack-path upside if Wilson's pressure packages translate quickly.
- Edmunds should be the safest IDP bet because Wilson discussed his middle-of-defense role and range.
- Reese has high dynasty and IDP upside but needs snap stability before redraft trust.
- Burns, Thibodeaux, and Carter can cannibalize splash opportunities while also lifting team pressure rate.

## Evidence

- Wilson was announced as defensive coordinator on February 18, 2026.
- The Giants signed Edmunds on March 13 and drafted Reese fifth overall on April 23.
- Giants.com reported Wilson's defense will prioritize physicality and run defense.
- Harbaugh said Reese can align inside, in several gaps, and off the edge.
- Giants.com's July linebacker feature emphasized that Burns, Edmunds, Reese, Thibodeaux, and Carter can rush or drop.
