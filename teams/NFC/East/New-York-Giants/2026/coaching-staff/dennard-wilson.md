# Dennard Wilson - New York Giants Defensive Coordinator

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026-02-18 staff announcement
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Wilson takes over a defense that changed leadership late in 2025 and was rebuilt around second-level size and multiplicity. He coordinates the defense with Charlie Bullen as run game coordinator/outside linebackers coach and Donald D'Alesio as defensive pass coordinator/secondary coach.

## Scheme and tendencies

Wilson publicly framed the defense around unpredictability, decisiveness, and physicality. The stated priority is correcting a 2025 run defense that Giants.com reported finished 28th in yards, 26th in scoring, and allowed a league-high 5.31 yards per carry.

## Personnel usage

Tremaine Edmunds is the imported middle linebacker and defensive communicator. Arvell Reese was drafted fifth overall and Harbaugh described him as a flexible player who can line up off ball, in multiple gaps, and in pass-rush packages with Burns, Thibodeaux, Carter, and Edmunds.

## Pace and situational decisions

The key defensive tendency to chart is simulated pressure and linebacker rotation. The Giants have five front-seven bodies with similar size and drop/rush flexibility, but the defense cannot become fantasy friendly through missed fits or explosive run leaks.

## Fantasy implications

- Giants DST becomes a high-variance watchlist option if pressure packages produce sacks and takeaways.
- IDP relevance centers on Edmunds as the full-time tackler and Reese if he earns every-down work.
- Opposing running backs should not be downgraded until the 2025 run-defense weakness is visibly corrected.
- The pass rush can become a matchup problem for low-mobility quarterbacks if Wilson's pressure structure stabilizes.

## Statements and reporting

Giants.com reported Wilson's four defensive pillars in April and later described the Burns, Edmunds, Reese, Thibodeaux, and Carter group as an "organized chaos" plan because all five can rush or drop.

## Reliability cautions

The talent profile is intriguing but the Lawrence trade removed a proven interior anchor. Run-defense improvement depends on Edmunds, Reader, Reese, and the new staff fitting together quickly.
