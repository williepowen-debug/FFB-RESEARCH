# Matt Nagy - New York Giants Offensive Coordinator

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026-02-18 staff announcement
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Nagy leads the Giants offense under Harbaugh. The staff also includes Brian Callahan as passing game coordinator/quarterbacks coach and Greg Roman as senior offensive assistant, so Nagy's 2026 profile should be read as coordinator authority within a blended staff.

## Scheme and tendencies

Giants.com described the 2026 offense as carrying shades of the Chiefs system from Nagy's recent Kansas City work, with influence from Callahan and Roman. The same preview projected a run-game lean based on Harbaugh, Roman, the drafting of Francis Mauigoa, and the signing of Patrick Ricard.

## Personnel usage

Dart is the developmental centerpiece after a record-setting rookie season. Nagy publicly said Dart impressed him while Nagy was still in Kansas City, citing accuracy, toughness, running ability, and competitiveness. The receiver room is unsettled behind Nabers because the Giants added Mooney, Austin, Beckham, Berrios, Smith-Schuster, and rookie Malachi Fields.

## Pace and situational decisions

The offense should be charted for first-down run rate, Ricard usage, 12/21 personnel, and designed movement for Dart. The staff's Roman influence and Mauigoa/Ricard investments make run volume more likely than a pure Reid-style spread pass build.

## Fantasy implications

- Dart has superflex growth appeal if the offense keeps designed movement and explosives while improving protection.
- Nabers remains the priority receiver when healthy, but his Week 1 ramp matters.
- Skattebo and Tracy benefit from the run-game investment, while Singletary and Ricard can create role friction.
- Likely can become fantasy relevant if Nagy uses him as a movable receiving tight end rather than a rotational complement.

## Statements and reporting

Nagy said he watched Dart from Kansas City and came away impressed before becoming his coordinator. Giants.com's July offense preview explicitly framed Dart's second-year jump and Nabers' health as the two major passing-game questions.

## Reliability cautions

Nagy's Bears head-coaching sample, Chiefs coordinator context, Roman's run-game history, and Callahan's quarterback background do not guarantee a single clear offensive identity. Treat target share and personnel rate as preseason hypotheses.
