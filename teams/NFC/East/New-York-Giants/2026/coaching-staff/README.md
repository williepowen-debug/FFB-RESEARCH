# New York Giants 2026 Coaching Staff

New York made a full leadership reset after Brian Daboll was relieved of duties in November 2025 and Mike Kafka finished as interim head coach. John Harbaugh is the 2026 head coach, Matt Nagy coordinates the offense, Dennard Wilson coordinates the defense, and Chris Horton is assistant head coach/special teams coordinator.
