# Finding: Washington rebuilt the front for Jones' pressure plan

## Scope

- Team/player: Washington defensive front
- Area: pass rush and defensive identity
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Washington's clearest defensive investment was up front. The free-agency tracker lists Odafe Oweh, K'Lavon Chaisson, Charles Omenihu, and Tim Settle among additions, while the draft tracker adds Joshua Josephs. Official team analysis said the defensive front was overhauled after the 2025 defense struggled to rush quarterbacks.

Jones' reported approach is aggressive. Oweh called the defense an attack style, and team reporting said he was viewed as a fit for the new system. If Oweh and Chaisson command starter-level edge snaps with Payne, Omenihu, Wise, Newton, and Josephs rotating, Washington can move from a targetable defense to a volatile but fantasy-relevant pressure unit.

## Fantasy implication

- Washington D/ST is not an automatic draft priority, but it belongs on matchup-streaming lists if pressure is real in September.
- Opposing quarterbacks and offensive lines should be downgraded if Washington shows high pressure without coverage busts.
- Oweh and Chaisson can matter in sack props; Josephs is a deeper dynasty/IDP watch if he earns early rotational rush work.

## Evidence

- Washington added Oweh, Chaisson, Omenihu, and Settle through free agency.
- Washington drafted Josephs in Round 5 at No. 147.
- Team camp-storyline reporting said the Commanders needed to improve from 127 pressures in 2025 and expected Oweh and Chaisson to function as primary pass rushers.
- Oweh publicly described Jones' defense as an attack mentality and a style he likes.
