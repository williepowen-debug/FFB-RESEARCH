# Daronte Jones - Washington Commanders

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Jones is Washington's 2026 defensive coordinator. The official 2026 staff announcement lists him in that role, replacing the prior defensive leadership structure.

## Scheme and tendencies

Team reporting connects Jones' system to pressure, multiplicity, and Brian Flores influence from Minnesota. His introductory coverage noted that he would pull from multiple defensive philosophies. Later reporting described a system that asks players to learn multiple positions, creates pressure and confusion, and is friendly to linebackers.

## Personnel usage

Washington rebuilt the front and second level for Jones. Odafe Oweh, K'Lavon Chaisson, Charles Omenihu, Leo Chenal, Nick Cross, Amik Robertson, and Sonny Styles are the central additions to monitor. Oweh described the defense as an attack style, and Chenal specifically cited linebacker usage as a fit.

## Pace and situational decisions

The key defensive tendency variables are blitz rate, simulated pressure frequency, off-ball linebacker rush usage, five-man fronts, and whether Washington can maintain coverage discipline behind pressure.

## Fantasy implications

The Commanders D/ST has rebound upside if pressure volume translates into sacks and turnovers. Offensive opponents could face more negative plays than in 2025, but aggressive looks also create explosive-play risk until coverage roles stabilize.

## Statements and reporting

Official team analysis said Washington invested heavily to fix a defense that ranked near the bottom of major categories in 2025 and specifically highlighted Oweh as a fit for Jones' approach. Chenal described the system as linebacker-friendly and personalized to the roster.

## Reliability cautions

The coordinator change and personnel additions are high-confidence facts. The DST ceiling is an inference because Jones' Washington defense has not yet produced regular-season evidence.
