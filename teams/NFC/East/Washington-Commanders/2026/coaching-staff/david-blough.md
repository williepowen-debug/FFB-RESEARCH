# David Blough - Washington Commanders

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2024
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Washington announced on 2026-01-10 that Blough was named offensive coordinator. The team described him as entering his third season with the organization after working as assistant quarterbacks coach in 2024-25 and taking on increased quarterback-room responsibilities in 2025.

## Scheme and tendencies

The public offensive thesis is a new vision without fully dismantling Daniels' support system. Team reporting said Washington parted ways with Kliff Kingsbury, elevated Blough, wanted to put Daniels under center more often, and wanted to lean more on a running game that had been productive over the prior two seasons.

## Personnel usage

Blough inherits Daniels, McLaurin, Okonkwo, a crowded backfield, and an unresolved WR2 group. Reporting around McLaurin said Blough wanted the offense built around creating high target volume and explosive chances for McLaurin. The WR2 role remains open among Treylon Burks, Antonio Williams, Dyami Brown, Van Jefferson, Luke McCaffrey, Jaylin Lane, and possible tight-end answers.

## Pace and situational decisions

Blough's NFL play-calling profile is not established. Preseason and Weeks 1-3 should be charted for under-center rate, play-action usage, early-down pass rate, quarterback designed runs, red-zone personnel, and two-minute target distribution.

## Fantasy implications

Daniels keeps high fantasy upside if Blough preserves quarterback mobility and improves structure. McLaurin's target floor is the clearest positive signal. Okonkwo can matter in tight-end premium if Washington uses him as the nontraditional No. 2 receiving answer. The running backs are harder to price until high-value touches separate.

## Statements and reporting

Daniels publicly praised working with Blough in July. Team reporting also described Blough as a coach who helped prepare multiple quarterbacks to start in 2025 and contributed to Daniels' rookie-year development environment in 2024.

## Reliability cautions

The promotion and staff role are facts. Target concentration, under-center rate, and backfield allocation are projections until first-team snaps are charted.
