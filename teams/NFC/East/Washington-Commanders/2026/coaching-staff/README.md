# Washington Commanders 2026 Coaching Staff

Washington kept Dan Quinn as the program lead but changed both coordinator identities. David Blough was elevated from assistant quarterbacks coach to offensive coordinator, while Daronte Jones became defensive coordinator and brings pressure-system influence from Minnesota.

## Authority Model

- Dan Quinn remains the head coach and culture/game-management lead.
- David Blough is the offensive coordinator and first-time NFL play-caller to monitor.
- Daronte Jones is the defensive coordinator and primary defensive tendency change.
- Larry Izzo remains special teams coordinator.
