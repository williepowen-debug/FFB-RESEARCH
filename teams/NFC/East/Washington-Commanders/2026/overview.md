# Washington Commanders - 2026 Coordinator Reset

- Conference/division: NFC East
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Scope: changes from the 2025 team to the 2026 preseason roster and staff
- Build tier: Core

Washington is a coordinator-reset module around a franchise quarterback. Dan Quinn remains head coach, but David Blough takes over the offense and Daronte Jones takes over the defense. The offense must translate Jayden Daniels' relationship with Blough into a more balanced structure while solving the target tree behind Terry McLaurin. The defense was rebuilt with pressure and speed additions, including Odafe Oweh, K'Lavon Chaisson, Leo Chenal, Nick Cross, Amik Robertson, Rasul Douglas, and first-round linebacker Sonny Styles.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Quinn returns as head coach for Year 3.
- Blough was promoted from assistant quarterbacks coach to offensive coordinator.
- Jones is the new defensive coordinator.
- Daniels, McLaurin, Marcus Mariota, Laremy Tunsil, Chris Paul, Andrew Wylie, Treylon Burks, Tress Way, Deatrich Wise Jr., and Drake Jackson were retained or extended.
- Washington added White, Ford, Okonkwo, Brown, Jefferson, Oweh, Chaisson, Omenihu, Chenal, Cross, Robertson, and Douglas.
- Washington drafted Styles, Antonio Williams, Joshua Josephs, Kaytron Allen, Matt Gulbin, and Athan Kaliakmanis.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

- Daniels stays a QB1 candidate if Blough preserves rushing involvement and raises offensive efficiency.
- McLaurin remains the clearest Washington target bet.
- Okonkwo, Williams, Burks, Brown, and Jefferson are competing to define the non-McLaurin receiving volume.
- The backfield is a committee until red-zone and passing-down roles separate.
- Washington D/ST can become streamable if Jones' pressure plan turns the defensive overhaul into sacks and takeaways.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

The offensive leadership changed from Kliff Kingsbury to Blough. Team reporting framed Blough's promotion as a middle path: retain a Daniels development relationship while installing a new vision with more under-center and run-game elements. The roster gives him a clear alpha in McLaurin but an unresolved WR2/tight-end/backfield allocation problem.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Blough preserve Daniels' designed-run and scramble value?
- Does McLaurin command alpha target volume in live work?
- Is the WR2 answer Williams, Burks, Brown, Jefferson, or a committee?
- Does Okonkwo become the second receiving option, especially in tight-end premium?
- Which running back earns two-minute, goal-line, and early-down trust?

The evidence and invalidation tests are maintained in the [offensive research module](offense/README.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Jones replaces the 2025 defensive leadership, and Washington materially changed the front seven and secondary. Official team analysis tied the reset to an aggressive, pressure-oriented approach influenced by Jones' Minnesota experience. Oweh and Chaisson project as central edge pieces, while Styles and Chenal can change second-level athleticism.

### Questions to resolve

- Does Jones produce a materially higher pressure rate?
- Are Oweh and Chaisson full-time edge players or rotation pieces?
- Does Styles earn an every-down linebacker role immediately?
- Can the rebuilt corner/safety group hold up behind aggression?
- Does Washington convert pressure into turnovers, or simply create high-variance shootouts?

Washington begins as a defense with upside but not a locked matchup downgrade. See the [defensive research module](defense/README.md).
