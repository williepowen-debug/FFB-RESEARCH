# Finding: Daniels' 2026 value depends on Blough's transition

## Scope

- Team/player: Washington Commanders offense and Jayden Daniels
- Area: play-calling transition
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Washington changed offensive leadership by promoting David Blough from assistant quarterbacks coach to offensive coordinator. The team-framed rationale was a middle path: keep a Daniels support relationship in the building while moving away from Kliff Kingsbury's 2025 offense.

The reported direction includes more under-center work and more run-game emphasis. That matters because Daniels' fantasy ceiling comes from the combination of passing efficiency, designed/secondary rushing value, and touchdown environment. If Blough improves structure without suppressing Daniels' legs, Daniels remains a high-upside fantasy quarterback.

## Fantasy implication

- Daniels should stay in the QB1/superflex priority pool, but his final ranking needs preseason evidence on designed runs and play-action.
- A better run-game structure can improve red-zone efficiency without necessarily hurting Daniels if option and boot concepts stay active.
- The main downside is a more conservative offense that lowers dropback volume while giving goal-line touches to backs.

## Evidence

- Washington announced Blough as offensive coordinator on 2026-01-10 after he spent 2024-25 as assistant quarterbacks coach.
- Team reporting credited Blough with increased quarterback-room responsibility in 2025 and involvement in Daniels' 2024 rookie-year development environment.
- A July team article said Washington chose Blough after the offense underperformed in 2025 and wanted a new vision without removing Daniels' support system.
- The same July framing said Washington wanted more under-center work and more reliance on the running game.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: The staff change and public direction are well sourced, but Blough has no Washington regular-season play-calling sample yet.
- Fact/inference boundary: Blough's promotion and roster context are facts. Daniels' fantasy ceiling under Blough is an inference.
- What would invalidate this: Blough removes designed quarterback runs, Washington plays very slowly, or Daniels shows reduced rushing willingness.
- Next review: Preseason first-team play-calling and Week 1-3 designed-run charting.
