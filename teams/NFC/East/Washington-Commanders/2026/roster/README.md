# Washington Commanders 2026 Roster

Core-tier roster tracking focuses on transactions that materially affect fantasy roles, trench quality, or defensive matchup evaluation.

## Files

- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): major veteran acquisitions and late-camp additions.
- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): notable outgoing or removed players with role implications.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): extensions and re-signings that stabilize the 2026 roster.
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): 2026 draft picks and immediate monitoring questions.
- [`undrafted-free-agents.csv`](undrafted-free-agents.csv): college free agents relevant to camp depth.

## Roster Baseline

Washington's 2026 roster was rebuilt around Jayden Daniels' return, a new offensive coordinator, and a defensive overhaul under Daronte Jones. The most fantasy-relevant additions are Rachaad White, Jerome Ford, Chig Okonkwo, Dyami Brown, Van Jefferson, Odafe Oweh, K'Lavon Chaisson, Leo Chenal, Nick Cross, Amik Robertson, and the rookie class led by Sonny Styles and Antonio Williams.
