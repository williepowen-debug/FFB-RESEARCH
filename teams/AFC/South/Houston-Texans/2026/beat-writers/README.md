# Houston Texans Source-Monitoring Guide

This directory tracks the people, outlets, official records, analysis, and data sources used to monitor the 2026 Houston Texans. It is designed for fantasy-football research: injuries, availability, practice participation, depth-chart movement, player usage, coaching and scheme, transactions, and contract context.

## Files

- [registry.md](registry.md) is the human-readable, schema-validated seasonal record.
- [sources.csv](sources.csv) contains one normalized row per person, collective, outlet, or official source.
- [endpoints.csv](endpoints.csv) contains websites, social accounts, feeds, and other monitoring endpoints.

## Signal Hierarchy

1. Official injury reports, transactions, gamebooks, and participation records.
2. Direct observations of attendance, participation level, first-team repetitions, position, and drill work.
3. Measured regular-season usage: snaps, routes, carries, targets, alignments, and special-teams work.
4. Consistent observations from multiple independent credentialed reporters.
5. On-record player and coach comments, interpreted with incentives and context in mind.
6. Film or scheme interpretation tied to observable evidence.
7. Uncorroborated camp praise, anonymous aggregation, and opinion without supporting evidence.

## Monitoring Workflow

During camp and preseason, start with official transactions and injury information, then record absences, participation, first-team repetitions, position experiments, and return-to-play progression from credentialed observations. Use team-produced analysis for access and context, but do not treat it as independent confirmation.

During the regular season, start with the official injury report and final game designation. After games, use NFL gamebooks, participation records, snap counts, routes, targets, carries, goal-line work, two-minute work, pass protection, and special-teams usage before moving conclusions into weekly records.

## Source-Handling Rules

- Separate reported facts, interpretation, and fantasy implications in every downstream record.
- Attribute a claim to the individual reporter or organization, not merely the outlet.
- Label team-employed analysis from Houston Texans staff.
- Follow aggregations to the original report before citing consequential claims.
- Do not reproduce paywalled text, paid data exports, podcast transcripts, or substantial copyrighted material.
- Reverify active roles and endpoints before camp, after major newsroom changes, and at least once during the regular season.
