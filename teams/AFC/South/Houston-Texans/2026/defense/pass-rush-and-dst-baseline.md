# Finding: Continuity and edge depth keep Houston D/ST in the draftable tier

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans defense; pass rush; D/ST
- Area: pressure floor, defensive continuity, and fantasy matchup value
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Houston enters 2026 with defensive continuity and premium pressure talent. Matt Burke remains defensive coordinator, DeMeco Ryans remains head coach, Will Anderson Jr. and Danielle Hunter were extended, and Jadeveon Clowney was added to a front that also drafted Kayden McDonald in Round 2.

That combination supports a high baseline for Texans D/ST and for matchup downgrades against opposing pass games. The open question is not whether the defense has talent; it is how Burke distributes edge/interior snaps and whether the 2025 takeaway and efficiency levels can repeat.

## Fantasy implication

- Houston D/ST is draftable because it combines pass rush, corner talent, continuity, and proven takeaways.
- Anderson is the cleanest IDP pressure profile, with Hunter still viable in sack-heavy formats.
- Opposing quarterbacks and outside receivers should be treated cautiously until injuries or usage show this defense has slipped.
- Positive defensive scripts can help David Montgomery while lowering Houston passing volume.

## Evidence

- The official 2026 staff retained Burke as defensive coordinator.
- Burke's official profile credits the 2025 defense with franchise records in total yards and points allowed per game, top-10 ranks in sacks and takeaways, and multiple Pro Bowl/All-Pro defenders.
- Houston announced Anderson, Stingley, and Hunter as 2025 AP All-Pro honorees; Anderson and Hunter were extended in 2026.
- The 2026 roster adds Clowney and second-round DT Kayden McDonald to a line already including Anderson, Hunter, Rankins, Jones, Horton, Hall, and others.
- Current camp reporting described Houston's pass rush and rookie class as rising through the first two weeks of camp.
