# Finding: Betting market prices Montgomery ahead of Marks

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans running backs; David Montgomery; Woody Marks
- Area: betting-market and player-prop signal
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Public betting-market snippets price David Montgomery as Houston's more bankable rushing-volume back, but the market does not support a clean bell-cow conclusion. The available odds and reporting fit a lead early-down/scoring role for Montgomery with committee risk from Woody Marks, especially on passing downs or if camp split-rep reports continue.

The clearest market signal is Montgomery's available season-long rushing yardage line. Comparable current season-long Woody Marks rushing-yard odds were not found during this 2026-08-17 search pass.

## Fantasy implication

- Montgomery remains the Texans RB to attach to Houston's favorable early RB schedule.
- Treat the target label as "lead early-down and scoring-area back with committee risk," not "clear three-down feature back."
- Marks remains a bench/PPR-stash or contingent-upside watch unless preseason usage shows he is splitting early downs, two-minute work, or goal-line carries.
- The most useful next evidence is not another projection blurb; it is first-team snap distribution, third-down routes, inside-10 carries, and updated player-prop markets after preseason.

## Evidence

- RotoWire's 2026 odds page for Montgomery showed a DraftKings season rushing yards line of 824.5, with the over listed at -120 and under at +100 before vig removal.
- BettingPros' Week 1 Montgomery props page showed rushing-yard lines around 52.5 to 53.5 yards for Houston's Week 1 game against Buffalo.
- CBS Sports' Woody Marks outlook framed his best case as a committee with Montgomery and his worst case as a Montgomery handcuff, while expecting Montgomery to handle most early downs and short-yardage work and Marks to work more on passing downs.
- KPRC reporting quoted Texans running backs coach Danny Barrett describing Montgomery as a "three-down back" and said Montgomery was slated for a heavy workload with talented backup Woody Marks.
- A Sports Illustrated fantasy note cited a report that Montgomery and Marks were expected to split lead-back reps in training camp, which is the main ceiling-risk counterpoint for Montgomery.
- Search pass did not surface a current 2026 season-long rushing-yard market for Marks comparable to Montgomery's 824.5 line.

## Assessment

- Confidence: low
- Reason: Betting markets are useful role signals, but the accessible evidence is incomplete and includes both strong Montgomery volume pricing and a camp-report committee warning.
- Fact/inference boundary: The Montgomery rushing-yard line, Week 1 prop range, and cited reporting are source-attributed facts. The fantasy conclusion that Montgomery is the preferred SOS target is an inference.
- What would invalidate this: Marks receives first-team early-down or goal-line work, Marks gets a comparable season-long rushing line near Montgomery's, Montgomery's line falls materially, or preseason usage shows a true 50/50 split.
- Next review: After Houston's next preseason game and again when major books post fuller Week 1 and season-long RB markets.
