# Finding: Houston intentionally rebuilt around a more credible run game

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans offense; David Montgomery; offensive line; Danny Barrett; Cole Popovich
- Area: roster construction, coaching continuity, and fantasy backfield projection
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-18

## Finding

Houston's 2026 offensive changes point to a deliberate run-game and protection reset rather than a simple depth patch. The team kept its main offensive structure in place with Nick Caley as offensive coordinator, Danny Barrett as Assistant Head Coach / Running Backs, and Cole Popovich as Offensive Line / Offensive Run Game Coordinator. It then moved off Joe Mixon, acquired David Montgomery by trade, traded out Juice Scruggs and Tytus Howard, retained or added several veteran offensive linemen, and drafted Keylan Rutledge in Round 1 plus Febechi Nwaiwu in Round 4.

The clearest fantasy takeaway is not that Montgomery is guaranteed every passing-down snap. It is that Houston paid real trade cost for a veteran back while pairing him with a senior RB coach, a dedicated offensive run game coordinator, and multiple offensive-line investments. That combination supports treating Montgomery as the preferred early-down and scoring-area back until preseason usage says otherwise.

## Fantasy implication

Montgomery's touchdown and volume thesis is stronger than a generic veteran-committee projection because the roster and staff structure both point toward a more physical offensive identity. In standard and half-PPR formats, he should remain attached to Houston's early schedule and inside-the-10 work unless Woody Marks, Jawhar Jordan, or another back clearly takes goal-line or two-minute work.

C.J. Stroud, Nico Collins, Dalton Schultz, and Marlin Klein remain tied to the pass-protection side of the same bet. If the new line improves protection without forcing backs and tight ends to stay in protection, the full offense can rebound. If the line only improves by using more chips and max protection, Montgomery can still benefit while secondary route volume becomes more fragile.

## Evidence

- The Texans' official 2026 staff lists Danny Barrett as Assistant Head Coach / Running Backs and Cole Popovich as Offensive Line / Offensive Run Game Coordinator.
- Barrett's official Texans profile describes him as entering his ninth season coaching Houston running backs and his fourth season as Assistant Head Coach / Running Backs.
- Popovich's official Texans profile describes 2026 as his second season as Offensive Line / Offensive Run Game Coordinator after serving as assistant offensive line coach in 2023-24.
- Barrett has publicly framed Montgomery as a three-down back with finishing value as a runner and blocker, while framing Marks' 2026 role around more third-down, special-teams, and passing-game work.
- DeMeco Ryans' camp comments after Montgomery's 80-yard wheel-route touchdown framed Montgomery as a player Houston added to spark and lift the offense.
- Nick Caley publicly praised Montgomery's toughness, pro approach, contact balance, block setup ability, and fit with Houston's offensive identity.
- Cole Popovich has not surfaced in public reporting as giving a detailed Marks-specific role quote, but his line/run-game comments emphasize tough, smart, physical play and opening holes for Montgomery.
- Houston released Joe Mixon on 2026-03-06 and officially acquired Montgomery by trade on 2026-03-11.
- NFL.com reported the Montgomery trade price as Juice Scruggs, a 2026 fourth-round pick, and a 2027 seventh-round pick.
- Houston traded Tytus Howard to Cleveland, extended Trent Brown, re-signed Ed Ingram, signed Braden Smith, Evan Brown, and Wyatt Teller, then drafted Keylan Rutledge and Febechi Nwaiwu.
- PFF's 2026 Texans draft recap framed the offensive-line picks as pass-protection investments and noted Nwaiwu's strong college pass-protection profile.
- KPRC reporting on Montgomery quoted Barrett praising Montgomery's mentality and three-down value, while also identifying Woody Marks as a talented backup.
- NFL.com reporting via Aaron Wilson quotes Barrett saying Marks' role should grow through third downs, kickoff returns, and especially the passing game, while Marks said Caley has him learning more of the route tree.
