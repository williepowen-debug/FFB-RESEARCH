# Finding: Montgomery and deeper tight ends reshape touch distribution

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans offense; running backs; tight ends
- Area: backfield allocation, 12 personnel, and high-value touches
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Houston released Joe Mixon, traded for David Montgomery, retained Dalton Schultz, signed Foster Moreau, and drafted Marlin Klein. That creates a clearer early-down backfield favorite and a deeper tight-end room for Caley, whose background and 2025 Houston profile already point toward meaningful tight-end usage.

The likely direction is Montgomery leading carries and goal-line chances while Schultz remains the primary receiving tight end. The uncertain parts are passing-down back work, whether Moreau/Klein take enough routes to cap Schultz, and whether heavier personnel improves efficiency or merely reduces receiver routes.

## Fantasy implication

- Montgomery has immediate touchdown and standard/half-PPR relevance if he owns early downs and inside-the-10 work.
- Woody Marks and Jawhar Jordan need route and two-minute evidence before they are more than contingent PPR stashes.
- A dated betting-market snapshot as of 2026-08-17 prices Montgomery ahead of Marks, but with enough committee risk to avoid treating him as a locked three-down back.
- Schultz remains draftable in PPR and tight-end-premium formats, but a larger tight-end room can lower route share even while helping the real offense.
- Moreau and Klein are better treated as usage-watch players than draft targets unless preseason routes show a concentrated role.

## Evidence

- The Texans' official transaction ledger shows Mixon's release on 2026-03-06 and Montgomery's acquisition via trade on 2026-03-11.
- Schultz signed a contract extension on 2026-03-10 after the official Caley profile credited him with a Texans tight-end single-season receptions record in 2025.
- Houston signed Moreau on 2026-03-16 and selected Klein in Round 2 at pick 59.
- The official roster lists Montgomery, Woody Marks, Jawhar Jordan, British Brooks, and multiple rookie or depth backs on the active roster as of 2026-08-17.
- Houston Chronicle roster projection reporting placed Montgomery ahead of a competition involving Marks, Jordan, Brooks, and Noah Whittington.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: The personnel movement strongly implies the role changes, but pass-game and route distribution are still preseason questions.
- Fact/inference boundary: Mixon's release, Montgomery's trade, Schultz's extension, Moreau's signing, and Klein's draft slot are facts. Montgomery's touchdown share and Schultz's route share are projections.
- What would invalidate this: Marks or Jordan taking first-team passing downs, Montgomery leaving the field near the goal line, or Moreau/Klein significantly out-routing Schultz in first-team packages.
