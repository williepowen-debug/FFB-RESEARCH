# Finding: Stroud's Year 2 Caley ceiling depends on protection becoming real

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans offense; C.J. Stroud; offensive line
- Area: protection, play-calling continuity, and fantasy efficiency
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Houston did not change offensive coordinators after 2025; it changed the offensive support structure around Nick Caley and C.J. Stroud. The Texans retained Caley, moved Jerry Schuplinski into the quarterbacks role, traded Juice Scruggs and Tytus Howard, signed or retained multiple veteran linemen, and drafted Keylan Rutledge in Round 1 plus Febechi Nwaiwu in Round 4.

The direction is coherent: more Stroud ownership, more line competition, and a more physical interior. It is still a hypothesis rather than a solved problem. Current camp reporting noted signs of red-zone improvement but continued offensive-line concerns, so the fantasy projection should stay conditional until the starting five handles pressure in preseason and early regular-season games.

## Fantasy implication

- Stroud's QB1 path requires the line improvements to reduce negative plays without forcing Caley into a low-volume protection shell.
- Nico Collins is the clearest receiving projection because his role and production survived Caley's first season.
- Tank Dell, Jayden Higgins, Jaylin Noel, and Lewis Bond need route and first-read confirmation behind Collins.
- If the line remains volatile, Schultz and the running backs could collect shorter-area targets while downfield volume becomes less stable.

## Evidence

- The official staff retained Caley as offensive coordinator and listed Schuplinski as quarterbacks coach.
- The official Caley profile credits Houston's 2025 offense with low turnovers, improved sack totals, a career-high Stroud completion percentage, a third straight 3,000-yard Stroud season, a third straight 1,000-yard Nico Collins season, and a Texans tight-end receptions record for Dalton Schultz.
- Houston traded Scruggs and Howard, signed or retained Trent Brown, Ed Ingram, Braden Smith, Evan Brown, and Wyatt Teller, then drafted Rutledge and Nwaiwu.
- Team media quoted Davis Mills describing Year 2 in Caley's offense as much further ahead than Year 1 because the team was no longer installing everything from scratch.
- Houston Chronicle reporting described Caley's self-scout, the offense being built more around Stroud and the personnel, and lingering camp concerns on the offensive line.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: Staff continuity and personnel investments are confirmed, but the actual protection result is not yet proven.
- Fact/inference boundary: Transactions, staff roles, and official 2025 production notes are facts. Improved Stroud fantasy efficiency is an inference.
- What would invalidate this: Continued pressure problems, heavy max protection, stalled early downs, or a starting line that keeps rotating because of performance.
- Next review: Texans-Raiders joint practice, remaining preseason games, and Weeks 1-2 pressure/sack/play-action charting.
