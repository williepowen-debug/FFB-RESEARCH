# Houston Texans 2026 Offensive Line

## Finding

Houston has materially raised the offensive-line ceiling, but the unit is still a projection rather than a settled fantasy upgrade. The current working five is Aireontae Ersery, Wyatt Teller, Keylan Rutledge, Ed Ingram, and Trent Brown, with Braden Smith still live at right tackle and Evan Brown/Jake Andrews behind the center competition.

The central change is not one addition; it is the combination of veteran mass, rookie investment, and a possible Rutledge move to center. That can help C.J. Stroud only if the group communicates cleanly enough to reduce pressure without forcing backs and tight ends into routine protection.

**Preseason grade: high-variance with run-game optimism ahead of pass-protection proof.** The opener and camp reports create useful direction, but the decisive test is first-unit protection against opponent starters.

## Fantasy implication

- **C.J. Stroud:** should not receive a full efficiency upgrade until pressure quality drops while target depth and five-eligible releases remain intact.
- **Nico Collins and secondary receivers:** benefit most if the tackles hold without constant chips and the offense preserves intermediate/deep route access.
- **Dalton Schultz, Marlin Klein, and backs:** snaps must be separated from routes because protection help can erase fantasy opportunity.
- **David Montgomery and Woody Marks:** Montgomery gains rushing and touchdown efficiency if the line improves first contact; Marks gains PPR relevance only if his checkdown usage comes after route releases, not protection holds.

Camp reporting has logged Rutledge run-game flashes, pressure-pickup positives, and a later Chronicle projection that places Rutledge at center and Trent Brown at right tackle. Those are facts about current reporting. Unit quality, sack prevention, and fantasy effects remain hypotheses.
