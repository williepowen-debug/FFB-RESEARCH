# Finding: Run blocking has the cleaner early optimism signal

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans offensive line, David Montgomery, Woody Marks
- Area: run blocking and early-down efficiency
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-18

## Finding

Houston's run-blocking outlook is more straightforward than its pass-protection outlook. Teller, Ingram, Rutledge, and Montgomery all fit a more physical run-game premise, and early camp reporting has attached specific positive run plays to Rutledge and the interior.

The question is whether this creates efficient first contact for Montgomery without making the offense too condensed. Short-yardage, inside-zone displacement, puller success, and second-level contact should be tracked separately from total rushing yards.

## Fantasy implication

Montgomery gains touchdown and volume stability if Houston can win early downs and inside the 10. Marks and the depth backs gain contingent value only if they show route and protection competence in addition to rushing efficiency. Stroud benefits if the run game creates credible play-action and fewer obvious third-and-long snaps.

## Evidence

- Harris logged Rutledge's early camp run-game impact and later trench work.
- Houston's roster build added Montgomery and multiple offensive linemen who point toward a more physical run-game plan.
- The preseason monitoring file now tracks first-unit carries, short-yardage work, and yards-before-contact evidence.
