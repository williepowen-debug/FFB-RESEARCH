# Finding: Houston has more line options, but center and tackle contingencies are expensive

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans offensive line depth
- Area: injury response, reserve roles, and lineup-change cost
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-18

## Finding

Houston has more credible offensive-line bodies than the prior protection problem implied, but not all contingencies cost the same. Evan Brown is the cleanest interior fallback because of center/guard flexibility. Braden Smith and Trent Brown create a real right-tackle competition. Blake Fisher is the main tackle-depth watch, while Febechi Nwaiwu is more developmental but roster-relevant after a fourth-round investment.

The most expensive failure modes are Rutledge not being ready at center and the right tackle loser not stabilizing as swing depth.

## Fantasy implication

If Houston loses a guard, the offense can probably preserve its structure with Brown or Nwaiwu. If it loses center communication or tackle stability, Stroud's pressure exposure, eligible-release count, and drive quality become immediate downgrade signals.

## Evidence

- Official roster and draft records confirm the current candidate pool.
- Chronicle's projected 53 placed Jake Andrews outside the roster, which would increase the importance of Evan Brown as the center contingency.
- Brown/Smith/Fisher/Teller/Ingram/Rutledge/Nwaiwu give the room multiple paths, but preseason evidence is still needed to rank them by functional role.
