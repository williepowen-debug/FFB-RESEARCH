# Finding: Houston offensive-line monitoring framework

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans offensive line
- Area: preseason and weekly charting framework
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-18

## Finding

Houston's line should be monitored through discrete tests rather than broad camp sentiment. The primary questions are the opening five, Rutledge's center role, right-tackle winner, pressure source, protection help, first-contact quality, and whether improved blocking preserves route participation.

## Fantasy implication

This framework prevents overreacting to sacks or rushing yards alone. It turns line evidence into start/sit and projection rules for Stroud, Collins, Schultz/Klein, Montgomery, Marks, and secondary receivers.

## Evidence

| Priority | Question | Review trigger |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Is Rutledge the Week 1 center? | every first-unit rep and depth-chart update |
| 1 | Does Trent Brown or Braden Smith win right tackle? | every preseason first-unit series |
| 1 | Can the line pass off stunts and blitzes? | every opponent pressure presentation |
| 1 | How often do backs and tight ends stay in? | every Stroud dropback |
| 2 | Does Montgomery get cleaner first contact? | every first-unit run |
| 2 | Are Marks checkdowns actual route releases? | every third-down and two-minute snap |
| 2 | Does Klein's role include routes or mostly protection? | every 12 personnel snap |
| 3 | Which reserve is the first center, guard, and tackle contingency? | every substitution and final 53 signal |

Minimum chart fields: lineup, down/distance, personnel, defensive front, rush count, first pressure source, help assignment, throw depth, route releases by TE/RB, run concept, first contact, and result.
