# Finding: Houston offensive-line personnel map

## Scope

- Team/player: Houston Texans offensive line
- Area: personnel, position battles, and depth
- Season/week: 2026 preseason
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-18

## Finding

Houston's personnel map is built around a new left side, a possible rookie center, and an unresolved right tackle. The current working order is Ersery at left tackle, Teller at left guard, Rutledge at center, Ingram at right guard, and Trent Brown narrowly ahead of Braden Smith at right tackle.

Rutledge is the key swing player. He was drafted as a guard/center and current camp reporting has him trending toward center, which would push Jake Andrews and Evan Brown into reserve or contingency roles. Brown remains valuable because he can cover center and guard if Rutledge is not ready.

## Fantasy implication

The personnel group gives Stroud a plausible improvement path, but fantasy projections should not treat the line as solved. Center and right tackle are the transmission points for pressure rate, timing, and whether tight ends or backs can release into routes.

## Evidence

- Official roster and transaction files confirm the presence of Ersery, Teller, Ingram, Rutledge, Evan Brown, Trent Brown, Braden Smith, Blake Fisher, Febechi Nwaiwu, and Jake Andrews.
- Harris logged early camp evidence of Rutledge making run-game impact and holding up in competitive trench work.
- Chronicle's August 17 roster projection placed Rutledge at center, Trent Brown at right tackle, Braden Smith behind Brown, and Andrews outside the projected 53.
