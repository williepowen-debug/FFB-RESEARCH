# Matt Burke - Houston Texans

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Joined team: 2023
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Burke remains defensive coordinator for a fourth season. His staff includes Bill Davis at linebackers, Cory Undlin as defensive passing game coordinator, Dino Vasso at defensive backs, Stephen Adegoke at safeties, and Rod Wright as defensive line/defensive run game coordinator.

## Scheme and tendencies

Houston's official profile credits Burke's 2025 defense with franchise records in total yards allowed per game and points allowed per game, plus top-10 league ranks in yards, rushing yards, passing yards, sacks, and takeaways. The structure is built around edge pressure from Will Anderson Jr. and Danielle Hunter, a strong outside corner in Derek Stingley Jr., and multiple safeties who can rotate or fit the run.

The 2026 personnel layer adds Kayden McDonald inside, Reed Blankenship and Kamari Ramsey at safety, and Jadeveon Clowney at edge without requiring a system reset.

## Personnel usage

Monitor whether McDonald becomes a first-unit run defender or a rotational tackle, whether Clowney is a true third edge or matchup rusher, and how Burke allocates snaps among Pitre, Bullock, Blankenship, Stewart, Reed, and Ramsey. The linebacker room also needs confirmation because E.J. Speed opened camp on PUP while Azeez Al-Shaair remains the central communicator.

## Pace and situational decisions

Burke's defense can create short fields and protect leads, so its fantasy effect is two-sided: it raises Texans D/ST floor and can create positive scripts for Montgomery, but may reduce fourth-quarter pass attempts when Houston leads. Track pressure rate without blitzing and red-zone touchdown rate allowed.

## Fantasy implications

- Houston D/ST starts the preseason as a viable weekly option because the defense combines continuity, premium edge talent, and takeaway history.
- Opposing quarterbacks and perimeter receivers draw a harder matchup when Stingley, Lassiter, and the safety group are healthy.
- IDP relevance starts with Anderson and Al-Shaair, with Hunter and Stingley as format-dependent options; rookie McDonald needs snap confirmation.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed facts: Burke is retained; the team profile documents 2025 defensive production; Anderson, Hunter, and Stingley earned AP All-Pro recognition after the 2025 season; Anderson and Hunter were retained through extensions. Analytical inference: the Texans are more likely refining defensive depth and pressure packages than changing identity.

## Reliability cautions

Elite prior performance can regress through injury, turnover luck, or schedule. Treat the defense as a strong baseline, but do not overreact to camp pass-rush wins unless they translate into preseason and early regular-season pressure without exposing coverage.
