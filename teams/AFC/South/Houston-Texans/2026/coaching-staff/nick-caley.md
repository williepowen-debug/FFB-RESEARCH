# Nick Caley - Houston Texans

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Joined team: 2025
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Caley enters his second season as Houston's offensive coordinator. The official staff keeps him as the top offensive authority, with Ben McDaniels as wide receivers/offensive passing game coordinator, Cole Popovich as offensive line/offensive run game coordinator, Jerry Schuplinski as quarterbacks coach, Danny Barrett over running backs, and James Ferentz over tight ends.

## Scheme and tendencies

Caley's background is tight ends, fullbacks, and passing-game work from New England and the Rams. Houston's own 2026 profile credits his 2025 offense with franchise-level points, low turnovers, improved sack totals, a second straight high-volume Nico Collins season, and a Texans tight-end receptions record for Dalton Schultz.

The 2026 personnel plan gives him more ways to protect Stroud and diversify personnel: David Montgomery, Foster Moreau, Marlin Klein, Wyatt Teller, Braden Smith, Evan Brown, Keylan Rutledge, and Febechi Nwaiwu all support heavier or more flexible offensive structures.

## Personnel usage

Primary preseason roles to verify are the starting offensive-line five, Montgomery's route and goal-line share, Schultz's target durability with Moreau and Klein in the room, and whether Jayden Higgins/Jaylin Noel/Lewis Bond materially change the receiver rotation behind Nico Collins and Tank Dell.

## Pace and situational decisions

Caley's first year reportedly included early overreach, midseason simplification, and improved late-season output. Track whether Year 2 produces cleaner protection checks, more Stroud ownership before the snap, and less dependency on long-yardage dropbacks. Neutral pace is less important than early-down pass rate, play-action usage, and how often Houston releases five eligible receivers behind the rebuilt line.

## Fantasy implications

- Stroud has continuity and a more tailored supporting cast, but his ceiling depends on the line moving from investment to actual pass-protection competence.
- Collins remains the cleanest fantasy projection because his role survived the 2025 transition and the team profile notes another 1,000-yard season.
- Schultz remains relevant in PPR and tight-end-premium formats, but added tight ends can redistribute snaps even if the offense uses more 12 personnel.
- Montgomery projects for lead rushing and scoring-area work, while passing-down allocation remains an open preseason question.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed facts: Caley is retained as offensive coordinator; Schuplinski is listed as quarterbacks coach; Houston added multiple offensive linemen, a veteran running back, and two tight ends. Reported analysis from the Houston Chronicle described Caley as expecting improvement in Year 2 after reassessing his first season and shaping the offense more around Stroud and the personnel.

## Reliability cautions

Coordinator continuity is not proof of offensive stability. The offensive line has many new pieces, camp reporting still flagged line concerns, and preseason usage is required before projecting Stroud, Montgomery, Schultz, and the WR2/WR3 roles with high confidence.
