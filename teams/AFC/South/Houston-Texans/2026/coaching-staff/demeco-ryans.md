# DeMeco Ryans - Houston Texans

- Role: Head coach
- Joined team: 2023
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Ryans retains full team authority in his fourth season as Houston's head coach. The Texans' official 2026 staff lists Ryans over a continuity coordinator structure with Nick Caley on offense, Matt Burke on defense, and Frank Ross on special teams.

The roster work matches Ryans' stated identity: Houston retained core defensive stars, extended Danielle Hunter and Will Anderson Jr., added front-seven depth, and invested heavily in offensive-line physicality with veteran signings and first-round guard Keylan Rutledge.

## Scheme and tendencies

Ryans' strongest fingerprints remain on the defense: a fast, attacking 4-3 structure built around edge pressure, rally tackling, and disruptive coverage players. The team's official bio credits Houston with top-tier defensive performance across Ryans' first three seasons, including 2025 franchise marks in yards and points allowed.

Offensively, Ryans is not the primary designer, but the personnel profile indicates a preference for better line-of-scrimmage balance after the offense needed a late-season correction in Caley's first year.

## Personnel usage

Ryans' 2026 roster should be monitored through trench deployment. The offense added David Montgomery, Wyatt Teller, Braden Smith, Evan Brown, Foster Moreau, and two drafted guards, which points to more run-game and protection flexibility. The defense retained Anderson, Hunter, Azeez Al-Shaair, Derek Stingley Jr., Jalen Pitre, Calen Bullock, and Kamari Lassiter while adding Kayden McDonald, Reed Blankenship, Kamari Ramsey, and Jadeveon Clowney.

## Pace and situational decisions

Ryans owns game management, fourth-down posture, and risk tolerance. Because the defense is strong enough to win lower-possession games, track whether Houston leans conservative when ahead or lets Caley continue to build Stroud's pass-game rhythm on early downs.
