# Grant Udinski - Jacksonville Jaguars Offensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Udinski returns as offensive coordinator after being hired on February 5, 2025. Jaguars.com credits him with helping the 2025 offense set franchise records in points, touchdowns, and first downs, and the team announced on January 27, 2026 that he would remain for the 2026 season.

## Scheme and tendencies

The offense remains Coen-led but Udinski is a core planning and quarterback-development voice. Public team reporting framed retaining Udinski as critical for Lawrence and rare staff continuity. The 2026 additions around Udinski point toward more run efficiency and personnel flexibility: Picucci for the run game, Rodriguez in the backfield, Strange extended, and two rookie tight ends drafted.

## Personnel usage

The preseason watch is distribution rather than basic competency. Lawrence, Brian Thomas Jr., Jakobi Meyers, Parker Washington, Strange, and Hunter give Udinski enough passing options to win through 11 personnel, while Boerkircher and Koziol create heavier answers. Tuten/Rodriguez/Allen usage will determine whether the offense has one fantasy-stable back or a matchup rotation.

## Pace and situational decisions

Jacksonville's 2025 offense ranked sixth in points per game and fifth in touchdowns according to the official coaches roster. The 2026 question is whether more efficient early-down rushing adds plays and red-zone trips or compresses pass volume.

## Fantasy implications

- Lawrence has a higher-confidence environment than most coordinator-change quarterbacks.
- Brian Thomas Jr. and Meyers remain primary passing-game monitors, while Hunter's offensive package can create spike weeks without guaranteed full routes.
- Strange benefits if heavier personnel becomes a weekly base rather than a tendency breaker.
- Tuten and Rodriguez need first-team carry, target, and goal-line charting before assigning a stable draft tier.

## Statements and reporting

Jaguars.com reported that Lawrence called Udinski a major piece of the offense, while Coen emphasized continuity with all three coordinators returning.

## Reliability cautions

The coordinator retention and 2025 production are official facts. Udinski's exact play-calling authority and target preferences should not be overstated from public comments alone.
