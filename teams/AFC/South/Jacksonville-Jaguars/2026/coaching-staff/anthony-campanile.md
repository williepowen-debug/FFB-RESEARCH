# Anthony Campanile - Jacksonville Jaguars Defensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Campanile returns as defensive coordinator after being hired on January 30, 2025. The Jaguars announced on January 27, 2026 that he would remain with the team, preserving the main defensive voice after a major 2025 turnaround.

## Scheme and tendencies

Jaguars.com credits Campanile's 2025 defense with leading the NFL in run defense and setting franchise records in takeaways, interceptions, passes defensed, and turnover differential. The 2026 staff added Mathieu Araujo as defensive pass game coordinator, which makes coverage-pass-rush integration a specific monitoring point.

## Personnel usage

The defense is strong at the top with Josh Hines-Allen, Travon Walker, DaVon Hamilton, Arik Armstead, Foye Oluokun, Travis Hunter, Montaric Brown, Jourdan Lewis, Eric Murray, and Antonio Johnson. The unsettled spots are interior pass-rush depth, the linebacker role vacated by Devin Lloyd, and how often Walker moves inside in pressure packages.

## Pace and situational decisions

Campanile's 2025 foundation was stopping the run and creating long-yardage/turnover opportunities. Jaguars Media analysts repeatedly identified interior pass rush and linebacker replacement as the path from good defense to elite defense entering 2026.

## Fantasy implications

- Jacksonville DST has a strong floor if the run defense and turnover profile repeat.
- The weekly ceiling depends on sack growth after a 2025 defense that was better at run defense and takeaways than raw sack volume.
- Hunter's primary defensive role can raise secondary quality while limiting his offensive-route certainty.
- IDP watch points are Oluokun stability, the Miller/Combs role, and Walker's pass-rush deployment.

## Statements and reporting

The official coaches roster describes 2025 franchise defensive records under Campanile. Jaguars Media's July defensive analysis focused on interior disruption, Walker/Hines-Allen pressure, secondary coverage, and the Lloyd replacement.

## Reliability cautions

The 2025 defensive production and staff retention are factual. Projecting repeated takeaway rate is lower confidence because turnovers are less stable than role and usage.
