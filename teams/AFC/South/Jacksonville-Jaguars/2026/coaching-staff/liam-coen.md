# Liam Coen - Jacksonville Jaguars Head Coach

## Responsibilities

Coen remains Jacksonville's head coach and offensive architecture authority in his second season. The Jaguars' official coaching roster says he was named head coach on January 24, 2025 and enters 2026 after a 13-4 first season, an AFC South title, and top-10 team rankings in both points scored and points allowed.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2026 structure is built around continuity with Trevor Lawrence, Grant Udinski, and a deeper set of personnel packages. Jacksonville retained Udinski, added Brian Picucci as run game coordinator, extended Brenton Strange, drafted Nate Boerkircher and Tanner Koziol, and moved into camp with a Tuten/Rodriguez backfield competition after Travis Etienne Jr. left.

## Personnel usage

Coen's immediate 2026 offensive levers are the running-back redistribution, multi-tight-end packages, and Travis Hunter's two-way workload. The first unofficial depth chart listed Hunter as a starting cornerback and second-team wide receiver, with Tuten or Rodriguez co-listed at running back.

## Pace and situational decisions

The public record supports an efficient offense rather than a pure volume bet: the 2025 Jaguars set franchise records in points, touchdowns, and first downs. The preseason review point is whether Coen and Udinski use heavier personnel to improve early-down rushing efficiency without lowering Trevor Lawrence's weekly passing ceiling.

## Fantasy implications

- Lawrence remains tied to a stable offensive staff and deeper formation menu.
- Brian Thomas Jr., Jakobi Meyers, Parker Washington, Brenton Strange, and Travis Hunter need route and personnel tracking because heavier groupings can change target distribution.
- Tuten and Rodriguez have immediate redraft relevance, but the lead-back thesis is unsettled.
- Coen's continuity raises the offense's floor; the fantasy edge is identifying how the new tight-end and backfield pieces alter high-value touches.

## Statements and reporting

Jaguars.com reported on February 26, 2026 that Coen called the coordinators' return important, and that Udinski, Campanile, and Heath Farwell were returning from the 2025 AFC South Championship staff.

## Reliability cautions

Staff continuity and roster transactions are facts. Any target-share, carry-share, or Hunter two-way snap expectation is an inference until preseason and Week 1 usage confirm it.
