# Finding: Hunter and the Lloyd replacement define Jacksonville's coverage questions

## Scope

- Team/player: Jaguars secondary and linebackers
- Area: coverage quality, linebacker replacement, and IDP/DST effects
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Jacksonville's back seven has high-end upside and real transition risk. Travis Hunter returns from a knee injury and is listed as a starting cornerback, while Jourdan Lewis, Montaric Brown, Jarrian Jones, Eric Murray, Antonio Johnson, and rookie Jalen Huskey give Campanile and Araujo a deep secondary. The bigger unresolved defensive role is Devin Lloyd's replacement next to Foye Oluokun.

The first unofficial depth chart listed Ventrell Miller or Branson Combs as co-starters at one linebacker spot, with Parker Hughes behind them. That validates Jaguars Media's July concern that the defense must replace Lloyd's production after he left for Carolina.

## Fantasy implication

- Hunter's defensive role can improve Jacksonville DST and suppress opposing wide receivers, but it also limits confidence in his offensive fantasy volume.
- Oluokun remains the stable IDP linebacker anchor.
- Miller and Combs are preseason IDP watch names only until one wins full-time snaps.
- Better secondary coverage could indirectly raise sacks by giving Hines-Allen and Walker more time.

## Evidence

- Lloyd left for Carolina after a 2025 season in which Jaguars.com credited him with five interceptions and second-team Associated Press All-Pro recognition.
- The first unofficial depth chart listed Hunter as a starting cornerback and Miller or Combs as co-starters at linebacker.
- Jaguars Media's July defensive analysis identified Lloyd replacement and secondary coverage as key defensive storylines.
- The official coaches roster credits the 2025 defense with 22 interceptions, 107 passes defensed, and a 78.5 opponent passer rating.
- Araujo was hired as defensive pass game coordinator after working with Miami defensive backs.
