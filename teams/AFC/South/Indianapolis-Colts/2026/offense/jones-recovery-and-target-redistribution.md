# Finding: Daniel Jones' recovery and post-Pittman target redistribution define the Colts passing game

## Scope

- Team/player: Indianapolis Colts passing game
- Area: quarterback health and receiver target hierarchy
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

The Colts have a confirmed QB1 in Daniel Jones and a confirmed target-share opening after trading Michael Pittman Jr. to Pittsburgh. Jones re-signed after a 2025 season in which Colts.com credited him with 3,101 passing yards, 68.0 percent completions, 8.1 yards per attempt, 19 touchdowns, eight interceptions, and a 100.2 passer rating across 13 starts. His Achilles recovery remains the health variable, but Colts camp reporting from July 29 said he participated in full-team work and moved well in the opening practice.

Pittman's departure removes an 80-catch, 784-yard, seven-touchdown 2025 line. The strongest internal candidates to absorb value are Alec Pierce, Josh Downs, and Tyler Warren. Pierce was re-signed after a 1,003-yard season and a second straight yards-per-catch title. Downs is being publicly framed for more opportunities after lower 2025 output in a heavier personnel structure.

## Fantasy implication

- Jones has superflex and best-ball appeal if his health and the 2025 efficiency profile hold.
- Pierce has weekly ceiling but needs proof of expanded first-read and intermediate work.
- Downs is the cleanest PPR volume bet if the staff follows through on offseason role-growth messaging.
- Warren can siphon enough middle-field and red-zone targets to prevent a simple two-receiver consolidation.

## Evidence

- Jones re-signed with Indianapolis on March 12 after ranking top six in several passing-efficiency measures cited by the team.
- Jones returned to full-team practice on July 29 after the December 2025 Achilles injury.
- Pittman was traded to Pittsburgh on March 13 after catching 80 passes for 784 yards and seven touchdowns in 2025.
- Pierce re-signed on March 9 after a 47-catch, 1,003-yard season.
- Colts reporting said Downs is expected to have a more significant 2026 role after the 2025 heavy-personnel shift reduced his opportunities.
