# Finding: Jonathan Taylor and Tyler Warren shape the Colts' personnel floor

## Scope

- Team/player: Indianapolis Colts run game and tight-end personnel
- Area: rushing volume, heavy personnel, and red-zone structure
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Taylor remains the offense's stable volume anchor while Warren remains the player most responsible for the Colts' heavier personnel identity. Colts reporting said Indianapolis played 383 snaps with two or more tight ends in 2025, a significant jump from its 2023-24 average, and tied that shift to Warren's impact. That matters for fantasy because heavy personnel can support Taylor's rushing efficiency and touchdown access while reducing pure three-receiver volume.

The open question is whether Pittman's trade changes the personnel mix. If the Colts solve receiver depth internally, Warren can remain a central tight-end premium option and Taylor can benefit from condensed formations. If the staff leans more on 11 personnel to replace receiver routes, Warren's route share and target certainty need to be rechecked.

## Fantasy implication

- Taylor remains the highest-confidence Colts skill player because role and team commitment are stable.
- Warren has tight-end premium appeal if he retains starter routes in heavy personnel.
- Increased heavy personnel can help Jones and Taylor efficiency but may cap WR3 fantasy relevance.
- Seth McGowan and undrafted backs are depth watches rather than immediate threats unless high-value touches leak in preseason.

## Evidence

- Colts reporting framed Taylor as pursuing the franchise rushing record and continuing as a long-term offensive centerpiece.
- Colts reporting said two-plus-tight-end usage rose to 383 snaps in 2025.
- Downs' 2025 opportunity dip was attributed partly to the heavier-personnel shift.
- Pittman's trade creates a reason to test whether the offense stays heavy or needs more three-wide volume.
- McGowan was drafted in the seventh round and multiple rookie backs were signed as depth competition.
