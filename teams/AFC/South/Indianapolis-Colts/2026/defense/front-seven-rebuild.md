# Finding: The Colts front seven is rebuilt around youth and rotational depth

## Scope

- Team/player: Indianapolis Colts defensive line and linebackers
- Area: snap replacement, pressure, and IDP role changes
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Indianapolis enters 2026 with a front-seven rebuild rather than simple continuity. Colts.com reported the defense had to replace more than 5,000 defensive snaps, including Zaire Franklin's 1,112 snaps and Kwity Paye's 721 snaps. The team traded Franklin for Colby Wooden, lost Paye and Samson Ebukam, added veteran linemen Arden Key, Micheal Clemons, Derrick Nnadi, Jerry Tillery, and Wooden, and drafted linebackers CJ Allen/Bryce Boettcher plus edges George Gumbs Jr./Caden Curry.

The defensive tackle core still has DeForest Buckner and Grover Stewart, while Laiatu Latu is the pass-rush centerpiece. The linebacker room is the highest-leverage fantasy and real-football unknown because Franklin's communication and tackle volume are gone.

## Fantasy implication

- Colts DST has upside if rotational depth supports Latu and the corners force longer quarterback holds.
- Opposing rushing attacks should not be downgraded until linebacker and safety fits prove stable.
- CJ Allen is the primary IDP watch if he wins green-dot or every-down work.
- Gumbs and Curry are deeper dynasty/DST pressure watches rather than immediate fantasy starters.

## Evidence

- Franklin was traded to Green Bay for Wooden on March 11.
- Colts reporting identified Franklin, Cross, and Paye as three of the largest 2025 snap losses.
- Key, Clemons, Nnadi, Tillery, Wooden, and Davis-Gaither joined during the offseason.
- Allen, Boettcher, Gumbs, and Curry were selected in the 2026 draft.
- Anarumo publicly described Allen as a high-priority rookie to develop after the draft.
