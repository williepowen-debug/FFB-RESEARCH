# Indianapolis Colts 2026 Coaching Staff

The Colts keep the Shane Steichen/Jim Bob Cooter offensive structure and Lou Anarumo defense for 2026. The notable staff change is on the defensive line, where Marion Hobby joins after previously working with Anarumo in Cincinnati. Brian Mason remains special teams coordinator.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): 2026 coach directory.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025 to 2026 leadership changes.
- [`shane-steichen.md`](shane-steichen.md): head coach and offensive authority.
- [`jim-bob-cooter.md`](jim-bob-cooter.md): offensive coordinator profile.
- [`lou-anarumo.md`](lou-anarumo.md): defensive coordinator profile.

## Authority model

Steichen remains the program lead and offensive architect. Cooter remains offensive coordinator inside a Steichen-led system that must preserve the 2025 Daniel Jones efficiency while reallocating Michael Pittman Jr.'s vacated volume. Anarumo remains the defensive play-caller with more man-coverage flexibility around Sauce Gardner and a heavily rebuilt front-seven depth chart.
