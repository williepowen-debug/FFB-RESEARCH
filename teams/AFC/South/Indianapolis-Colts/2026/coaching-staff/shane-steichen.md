# Shane Steichen - Indianapolis Colts Head Coach

## Responsibilities

Steichen returns for his fourth season as Colts head coach after owner and CEO Carlie Irsay-Gordon kept both Steichen and general manager Chris Ballard for 2026. Steichen remains the offensive architect and top program authority, with Jim Bob Cooter as offensive coordinator and Brian Mason/Lou Anarumo leading the other phases.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2026 offense is built around Daniel Jones' return from an Achilles injury, Jonathan Taylor's established rushing role, Tyler Warren's heavy-personnel flexibility, Alec Pierce's vertical profile, and Josh Downs' expected role growth after Michael Pittman Jr. was traded. Indianapolis used substantially more multi-tight-end personnel in 2025 after adding Warren, and the public offseason messaging points to preserving that efficiency while redistributing vacated receiver targets.

## Personnel usage

Jones is the unquestioned QB1 entering camp, with Anthony Richardson Sr. and Riley Leonard competing for QB2. Taylor remains the lead back. Pierce is the retained outside vertical receiver, Downs is the separator expected to get more opportunities, and the WR3/outside role is open among Ashton Dulin, Nick Westbrook-Ikhine, Laquon Treadwell, Deion Burks, and other camp competitors.

## Pace and situational decisions

Public evidence supports a continuity assumption rather than a new play-caller assumption. The main tracking points are Jones' post-Achilles movement, Steichen's use of Warren and multiple tight ends, and whether Pittman's departure shifts the offense toward more Downs slot volume, Pierce all-level work, or heavier personnel.

## Fantasy implications

- Jones' fantasy floor depends on health and whether the 2025 efficiency survives without Pittman.
- Taylor remains the clearest volume bet, with offense quality determining touchdown ceiling.
- Pierce has spike-week and all-level growth upside, but his target profile must be verified.
- Downs is the best candidate for target growth in PPR formats.
- Warren's route and red-zone usage remain central because heavier personnel shaped the 2025 offense.

## Statements and reporting

Colts.com reported the 2025 Colts were the NFL's top scoring offense during the 8-2 start before injuries, and that Jones' first-half efficiency was a major driver. Camp reporting on July 29 said Jones fully participated in the opening practice and moved well.

## Reliability cautions

The fact base is strong on staff continuity and roster changes. The uncertain part is role distribution after Pittman's trade and Jones' Achilles recovery, so preseason route participation and Week 1 usage should override offseason intent.
