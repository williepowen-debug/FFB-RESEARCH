# Lou Anarumo - Indianapolis Colts Defensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Anarumo returns as defensive coordinator and defensive play-caller. The 2026 staff also adds Marion Hobby as defensive line coach, reconnecting Anarumo with a position coach who worked under him in Cincinnati.

## Scheme and tendencies

Anarumo's defense is built for matchup flexibility. Colts reporting around the Sauce Gardner acquisition emphasized press-man capability, third-down man coverage, and the pass-rush benefit of tighter coverage. The 2026 roster adds more defensive-line depth and gives Anarumo a longer runway with Gardner after Gardner played only 196 Colts snaps in 2025.

## Personnel usage

The defense has major front-seven and safety turnover. Franklin, Cross, Paye, Ebukam, Gallimore, and Thomas departed. Wooden, Key, Clemons, Nnadi, Tillery, Davis-Gaither, Taylor-Britt, Owens, Thomas, CJ Allen, A.J. Haulcy, Bryce Boettcher, George Gumbs Jr., and Caden Curry enter the role mix. Buckner, Grover Stewart, Laiatu Latu, Sauce Gardner, Kenny Moore II, Cam Bynum, and Charvarius Ward Sr. are the veteran anchors to track.

## Pace and situational decisions

The fantasy-relevant question is whether Anarumo can use Gardner and the cornerback room to force longer quarterback holds without exposing the rebuilt linebacker/safety middle. A stronger man-coverage profile should raise DST pressure and turnover upside if the front four creates enough rush.

## Fantasy implications

- Colts DST ceiling rises if Gardner, Ward, Moore, Buckner, Stewart, and Latu are healthy together.
- Opposing outside receivers may face more matchup-specific man coverage.
- The linebacker room is an IDP watch because Franklin's 2025 snap volume is gone.
- Rookie CJ Allen can become relevant quickly if he wins communication and nickel work.

## Statements and reporting

Colts.com reported the defense needed to replace more than 5,000 defensive snaps from 2025, including Franklin and Cross each topping 1,000 snaps. The team also framed Gardner as a full-season impact piece after an injury-interrupted 2025 sample.

## Reliability cautions

Personnel facts and coordinator continuity are strong. Projection confidence is lower for linebacker and safety usage until preseason and Week 1 snap counts establish the new hierarchy.
