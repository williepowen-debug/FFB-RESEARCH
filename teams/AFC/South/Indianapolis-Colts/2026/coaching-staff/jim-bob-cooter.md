# Jim Bob Cooter - Indianapolis Colts Offensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Cooter is listed as the Colts' 2026 offensive coordinator. The public staff announcement does not describe a play-calling change, so the working model is Cooter coordinating inside Steichen's offensive structure rather than independently redefining the offense.

## Scheme and tendencies

The offense enters 2026 with continuity at quarterback and coordinator but a material receiving change after Pittman was traded. Colts reporting said 2025 multiple-tight-end usage rose sharply with Tyler Warren, creating a heavier-personnel path that helped the passing efficiency and run game before Jones was injured.

## Personnel usage

Cooter's immediate fantasy-relevant problem is distributing Pittman's vacated volume. Pierce was retained after a 1,003-yard season and is being discussed as more than a pure deep threat. Downs is expected to receive more opportunities. Warren and the tight-end group remain usage drivers, and Taylor anchors the backfield.

## Pace and situational decisions

No 2026 public evidence confirms a pace or play-calling shift. The monitoring priority is whether Indianapolis stays with the heavy-personnel efficiency profile or increases three-wide snaps to solve the WR3 competition.

## Fantasy implications

- Jones is viable if timing and mobility are intact after the Achilles injury.
- Downs gains PPR relevance if the offense manufactures more slot and motion touches.
- Pierce's ceiling rises if the staff expands his route tree and first-read volume.
- Warren can remain a weekly fantasy factor if multiple-tight-end packages keep him central rather than replacing receiver routes.

## Statements and reporting

Colts.com reported Ballard expects Downs to have a larger role and described Downs as the best separator among the pass catchers. Colts camp preview reporting framed wide receiver and Jones' mobility as major August questions.

## Reliability cautions

This profile has medium confidence because the coordinator title is confirmed but public play-calling authority remains less explicit than the head-coach and defensive-coordinator authority.
