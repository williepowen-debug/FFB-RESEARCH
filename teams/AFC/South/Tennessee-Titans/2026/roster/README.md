# Tennessee Titans 2026 Roster Movement

This Core-tier roster folder captures the 2025-to-2026 personnel spine for fantasy and matchup research. It is not a live 91-man roster; use the official team roster and transaction log for daily camp changes.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): major 2025 contributors or meaningful depth pieces no longer in the 2026 plan.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): veteran signings, trades, claims, and waiver additions with role notes.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): extensions, re-signings, and tenders.
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): eight-player 2026 draft class and immediate monitoring questions.
- [`undrafted-free-agents.csv`](undrafted-free-agents.csv): selected UDFA/camp names relevant to Core-tier role monitoring.
