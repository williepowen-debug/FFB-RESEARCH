# Tennessee Titans - 2026 Saleh-Daboll Reset

- Conference/division: AFC South
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Scope: Core-tier 2026 preseason team module
- Build tier: Core

Tennessee is one of the AFC's cleanest staff-reset teams for 2026. Robert Saleh replaced Brian Callahan as head coach, Brian Daboll replaced Nick Holz as offensive coordinator, and Gus Bradley replaced Dennard Wilson as defensive coordinator. The roster was also reshaped: the Titans entered camp with a 91-man roster, eight 2026 draftees, more than 30 new veterans, and the full 2025 draft class returning. The fantasy frame is a second-year Cam Ward offense with a new quarterback developer, paired with a Saleh/Bradley defensive front rebuild.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Saleh is the new head coach, Daboll is the new offensive coordinator, Bradley is the new defensive coordinator, and John Fassel returns as assistant head coach/special teams coordinator.
- Ward is the quarterback-development priority entering Year 2; Mitchell Trubisky was added as veteran backup competition, with Will Levis and Hendon Hooker also in camp.
- The receiver room added Wan'Dale Robinson and fourth-overall pick Carnell Tate to Calvin Ridley, Chimere Dike, Elic Ayomanor, Xavier Restrepo, K.J. Osborn, and others.
- Tony Pollard and Tyjae Spears remain the backfield baseline, while fifth-round rookie Nick Singleton is the rookie pressure point.
- The defense shifted to Saleh/Bradley leadership, added Jermaine Johnson II, John Franklin-Myers, Jordan Elliott, Alontae Taylor, Cor'Dale Flott, Joshua Williams, Tony Adams, and others, and drafted Keldric Faulk and Anthony Hill Jr.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

These are preseason projections, not established 2026 outcomes:

- Ward's superflex value rises if Daboll improves efficiency and consolidates the target tree.
- Robinson is the early PPR chemistry watch; Tate is the highest-ceiling rookie; Ridley needs route/health confirmation.
- Pollard and Spears should be modeled as a committee until preseason and Week 1 usage define touch roles.
- The Titans DST has matchup-play potential only if the rebuilt front creates pressure and the new cornerback group stabilizes.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

The offense has a new coordinator and position-coach structure around Ward. Daboll brings a long coordinator/head-coach resume, and Tennessee added Robinson, Tate, Trubisky, Singleton, Daniel Bellinger, Kylen Granson, Cordell Volson, Austin Schlottmann, and Andre James. Official camp reports describe Ward's offseason as uneven but improving late, with Robinson chemistry a specific note.

### Questions to resolve

- Ward's consistency and whether Daboll raises his efficiency quickly.
- Whether Robinson, Tate, Ridley, Dike, Ayomanor, or Restrepo becomes the target leader.
- Pollard vs. Spears phase split, especially routes and goal-line work.
- Whether Singleton creates a three-back problem for fantasy projections.
- Whether any tight end separates from the Bellinger/Granson/Helm rotation.

The durable offensive baseline and fantasy translation are maintained in the [offensive ecosystem module](offense/README.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Bradley is the new defensive coordinator under Saleh, and the official linebacker preview reports a move from a 3-4 to a 4-3 scheme. Tennessee kept Jeffery Simmons as its foundational interior player, swapped T'Vondre Sweat for Jermaine Johnson II, added John Franklin-Myers and other front pieces, drafted Faulk and Hill, and rebuilt the cornerback room after moving on from Sneed, McCreary, and Brownlee.

### Questions to resolve

- Whether Simmons, Johnson, Franklin-Myers, Faulk, and the rotation generate enough pressure without blitz help.
- Cedric Gray and Anthony Hill Jr. roles in the 4-3 linebacker structure.
- The outside cornerback pairing and nickel role, especially Marcus Harris' competition.
- Hooker/Winston safety health and explosive-pass prevention.

The durable defensive baseline is maintained in the [defensive ecosystem module](defense/README.md).

## Coaching and scheme

Tennessee's coordinator transition is:

| Unit | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Head coach | Brian Callahan | Robert Saleh |
| Offense | Nick Holz | Brian Daboll |
| Defense | Dennard Wilson | Gus Bradley |
| Special teams | John Fassel | John Fassel |

Saleh controls the whole-team identity. Daboll is the offensive authority for Ward's Year 2 development. Bradley, with Saleh's defensive background behind him, owns the defensive install. Fassel is the continuity piece and adds assistant head coach title.

### Analytical projection

The reasonable preseason prior is higher variance than a continuity team: better coaching pedigree and roster depth, but many new roles. The fantasy-sensitive checks are Ward efficiency, receiver route concentration, backfield route/goal-line distribution, four-man pressure, and nickel/secondary stability.

## Special teams

Fassel returns after a strong 2025 special-teams season and adds assistant head coach title. Tennessee retained long snapper Morgan Cox and kicker Joey Slye and signed punter Tommy Townsend. Special teams should be treated as a relative continuity area inside an otherwise major staff reset.
