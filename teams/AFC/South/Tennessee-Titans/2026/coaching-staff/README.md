# Tennessee Titans 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from authority and fantasy-relevant scheme analysis. Tennessee made a full leadership reset for 2026: Robert Saleh replaced Brian Callahan as head coach, Brian Daboll replaced Nick Holz as offensive coordinator, and Gus Bradley replaced Dennard Wilson as defensive coordinator. John Fassel was retained and given assistant head coach/special teams coordinator title.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): coordinators, position coaches, assistants, and retained-from-2025 flags.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership changes.
- [`robert-saleh.md`](robert-saleh.md): head coach profile and whole-team identity frame.
- [`brian-daboll.md`](brian-daboll.md): offensive coordinator profile focused on Cam Ward's Year 2 reset.
- [`gus-bradley.md`](gus-bradley.md): defensive coordinator profile and 4-3 transition notes.
