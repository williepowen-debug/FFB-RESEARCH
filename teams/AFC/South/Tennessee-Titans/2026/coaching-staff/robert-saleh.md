# Robert Saleh - Tennessee Titans

- Role: Head coach
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Saleh is the whole-team authority for a new Titans staff. The team announced him as the 22nd head coach in franchise history on January 22, 2026, replacing Brian Callahan. Tennessee's official camp preview says Saleh is running his first Titans training camp after arriving with 21 NFL seasons of coaching experience, most recently as San Francisco's defensive coordinator in 2025 and before that as Jets head coach from 2021 through 2024.

## Scheme and tendencies

Saleh's background points to a defensive identity built around front speed, four-man pressure, rotation, and coverage discipline rather than an offense-first head coach model. The 2026 staff pairs that defensive head coach with veteran coordinators Brian Daboll and Gus Bradley. That matters for fantasy because the offense is a coordinator-led install around second-year quarterback Cam Ward, while the defense is a head-coach-plus-DC rebuild with many new veterans.

## Personnel usage

The personnel signal is consistent with Saleh's defensive background: Tennessee added Jermaine Johnson II, John Franklin-Myers, Jordan Elliott, Solomon Thomas, Keldric Faulk, Anthony Hill Jr., Alontae Taylor, Cor'Dale Flott, Joshua Williams, and Tony Adams while retaining Jeffery Simmons. On offense, the team surrounded Ward with Daboll, quarterbacks coach Shea Tierney, first-round wideout Carnell Tate, slot receiver Wan'Dale Robinson, and several tight ends and interior linemen.

## Pace and situational decisions

Do not treat Saleh's Jets game-management sample as a clean Tennessee offensive tendency. The better preseason prior is: defense and field position should become more central to team identity, while Daboll controls offensive structure. Verify fourth-down aggression, neutral pass rate, and game-script behavior after the first regular-season month.

## Fantasy implications

- Ward's development is the offensive priority, but his fantasy value depends on Daboll's usage and whether the rebuilt supporting cast stabilizes quickly.
- The receiving group has upside but uncertain distribution: Calvin Ridley, Wan'Dale Robinson, Carnell Tate, Chimere Dike, Elic Ayomanor, and Xavier Restrepo all have camp relevance.
- A Saleh/Bradley defensive improvement could reduce extreme negative scripts, helping Tony Pollard and Tyjae Spears carry volume, but that is still an inference.
