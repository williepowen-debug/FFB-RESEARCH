## Responsibilities

Bradley is Tennessee's defensive coordinator under Robert Saleh. The Titans announced the hire on February 4, 2026. The official release notes Bradley's extensive NFL coordinator history with Seattle, the Chargers, Raiders, and Colts, plus a prior head-coaching run in Jacksonville and overlap with Saleh in Seattle, Jacksonville, and San Francisco.

## Scheme and tendencies

Bradley is a veteran four-down-front defensive coach whose NFL history is tied to zone coverage, front discipline, and limiting explosives. Tennessee's camp previews explicitly describe a change from a 3-4 to a 4-3 structure for linebackers. That makes the first-year installation important: new roles for front-seven holdovers matter as much as the veteran additions.
