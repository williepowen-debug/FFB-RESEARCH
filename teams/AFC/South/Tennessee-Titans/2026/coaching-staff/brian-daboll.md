# Brian Daboll - Tennessee Titans

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: 2026
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Responsibilities

Daboll is the offensive coordinator under Robert Saleh. Tennessee announced the hire on January 27, 2026. The official release lists prior NFL coordinator stops with Cleveland, Miami, Kansas City, and Buffalo, a Giants head-coaching run from 2022 through 2025, and New England/Alabama championship experience. He is the central offensive authority for Cam Ward's Year 2 reset.

## Scheme and tendencies

Daboll's best fantasy-relevant prior is quarterback adaptation rather than one static formation family. In Buffalo, his public reputation was tied to developing Josh Allen and building a quarterback-friendly, aggressive passing structure. In Tennessee, the official reporting repeatedly connects Daboll to Ward, and the camp quarterback preview frames Ward's growth under a new head coach, new OC, new QB coach, and new skill-player group as the central task.

## Personnel usage

The offensive personnel is deeper but unsettled. Ward is the starter-level development focus; Mitchell Trubisky was added as a veteran backup with Daboll familiarity from Buffalo. The receiver room adds Wan'Dale Robinson and first-round pick Carnell Tate to Calvin Ridley, Chimere Dike, Elic Ayomanor, Xavier Restrepo, K.J. Osborn, and others. The backfield returns Tony Pollard and Tyjae Spears and adds Nick Singleton and Michael Carter. Tight end is a rotation after adding Daniel Bellinger and Kylen Granson with Gunnar Helm returning and Jaren Kanak now on injured reserve.

## Pace and situational decisions

The preseason evidence points to an offense still syncing timing and chemistry. Ward had uneven open OTA results but finished the offseason better, while Wyatt reported stronger chemistry developing with Wan'Dale Robinson. Until Tennessee plays regular-season games, pace and pass rate should be treated as low-confidence assumptions.

## Fantasy implications

- Ward has a better coaching environment than in 2025 but also a new install and target hierarchy; he is a superflex watch, not a locked-in weekly starter.
- Robinson has early chemistry signals with Ward and should be monitored for PPR volume.
- Tate has draft-capital upside, but official camp preview language warns that rookie receivers can have growing pains.
- Pollard and Spears remain the immediate backfield baseline, with Singleton a pressure point if preseason usage rises.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: Daboll was hired January 27, 2026; Saleh publicly connected Ward's development to improved structure and Daboll's role; the camp previews identify Ward consistency, receiver chemistry, and Pollard/Spears/Singleton backfield allocation as open questions. Inference: Daboll's first Tennessee offense should be evaluated through quarterback efficiency and target consolidation before assigning aggressive fantasy ceilings.

## Reliability cautions

Open OTA completion counts are useful context but not predictive enough by themselves. Do not overfit camp catch totals or individual practice reports. The decisive checks are first-team routes, preseason scripted drives, regular-season neutral pass rate, and red-zone roles.
