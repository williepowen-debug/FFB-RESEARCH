# Finding: Saleh and Bradley are rebuilding the defense around a deeper 4-3 front

## Scope

- Unit: defensive line and linebackers
- Area: scheme transition, pass rush, rotation, front-seven personnel
- Season: 2026
- Status: active preseason baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Tennessee's defensive reset is front-led. Robert Saleh replaced Brian Callahan as head coach, Gus Bradley replaced Dennard Wilson as defensive coordinator, and the linebacker preview reports a switch from a 3-4 to a 4-3 scheme. The personnel supports that: the Titans retained and extended Jeffery Simmons, traded T'Vondre Sweat for Jermaine Johnson II, signed John Franklin-Myers, Jordan Elliott, Solomon Thomas, Jacob Martin, Malik Herring, and Khalen Saunders, and drafted Keldric Faulk, Anthony Hill Jr., and Jackie Marshall.

## Fantasy implication

The Titans DST has a plausible path to usefulness if the new front wins with four rushers, but it should not be upgraded on names alone. For matchup research, treat opposing quarterbacks and tackles as pressure-sensitive checks until Tennessee proves rush finish and coverage timing. IDP relevance is strongest for Simmons, Cedric Gray, Johnson, Faulk, and Hill, but roles need preseason confirmation.

## Evidence

### Facts

- Bradley was hired as defensive coordinator on February 4, 2026, after spending 2025 with Saleh in San Francisco.
- The linebacker camp preview says the Titans changed from a 3-4 to a 4-3 defense.
- The defensive end preview lists Jermaine Johnson II, Keldric Faulk, Femi Oladejo, Jaylen Harrell, Malik Herring, Jacob Martin, Truman Jones, and David Ebuka Agoha in camp.
- The defensive tackle preview lists Jeffery Simmons, John Franklin-Myers, Solomon Thomas, Jordan Elliott, Jackie Marshall, and other interior defenders, and says Tennessee plans to use a rotation inside and outside.
- Simmons received a multi-year extension and entered camp after an offseason elbow cleanup, according to the official defensive tackle preview.

### Inferences

- The front is deeper and more scheme-coherent than the 2025 version, but new assignments create early-season variance.
- Simmons remains the foundational pressure player; Johnson and Faulk determine how threatening the edge group becomes.
- Rotation language reduces confidence in projecting every lineman's snap share.
