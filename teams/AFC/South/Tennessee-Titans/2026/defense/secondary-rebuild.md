# Finding: Cornerback and safety roles are materially rebuilt and still need live confirmation

## Scope

- Unit: cornerbacks and safeties
- Area: coverage personnel, nickel role, opponent passing-game matchups
- Season: 2026
- Status: active preseason baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Tennessee's secondary changed heavily from the 2025 season. The Titans released L'Jarius Sneed and Xavier Woods, had previously traded Roger McCreary and Jarvis Brownlee Jr., then signed Alontae Taylor, Cor'Dale Flott, Joshua Williams, Tony Adams, Nazeeh Johnson, and other defensive backs while retaining Amani Hooker and Jerrick Reed II. The official cornerback preview identifies Marcus Harris as a player to watch and a nickel contender; the safety preview centers Kevin Winston Jr.'s Year 2 health rebound behind Hooker.

## Fantasy implication

Do not assign Tennessee a shutdown-coverage label before roles settle. The pass rush can help the secondary, but opponent WR/TE matchups should keep a role-specific lens: slot receivers matter if nickel remains unsettled, while boundary receivers remain viable until Taylor and Flott prove down-to-down consistency. Titans DST upside depends on coverage and pressure improving together.

## Evidence

### Facts

- Tennessee released Sneed on March 13, 2026, after an injury-limited Titans tenure.
- The team released Woods on February 25, 2026, while Hooker had signed a multi-year extension in September 2025.
- Tennessee signed Alontae Taylor, Cor'Dale Flott, Joshua Williams, and Tony Adams in March, then added Nazeeh Johnson in July.
- The official cornerback preview lists Taylor, Flott, Marcus Harris, Joshua Williams, Micah Robinson, and others in camp.
- The same preview says Harris is a contender for the nickel spot.
- The safety preview lists Hooker, Kevin Winston Jr., Tony Adams, Kendell Brooks, Erick Hallett II, Sanoussi Kane, Jerrick Reed II, and Bishop Fitzgerald.
- Hooker and JC Latham started camp on PUP, and Hooker was activated from PUP on July 28.

### Inferences

- The nickel role is one of the highest-leverage matchup variables for PPR slot receivers.
- Winston's health and Hooker's availability are important for explosive-pass prevention.
- New cornerback signings give Tennessee more options, but the group still needs regular-season cohesion.
