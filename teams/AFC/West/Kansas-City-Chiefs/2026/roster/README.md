# Kansas City Chiefs 2026 Roster Changes

These files preserve the personnel transition supporting the Chiefs Core-tier module. Kansas City made a rare high-leverage reset: Eric Bieniemy returned to the offense, Kenneth Walker joined the backfield, Justin Fields entered as the backup quarterback, Travis Kelce returned, and Trent McDuffie was traded for draft capital that drove a defensive reload.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): material releases and trades.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): external veteran additions relevant to the 2026 structure.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): re-signings and returns that preserve continuity.
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): all seven 2026 selections and their immediate monitoring questions.

## Headline changes

- Traded Trent McDuffie to the Rams for four draft picks, then used two first-rounders and a second-rounder on defensive reinforcements.
- Added Kenneth Walker to change the backfield's explosive run profile.
- Acquired Justin Fields as a higher-upside backup and possible package quarterback behind Patrick Mahomes.
- Re-signed Travis Kelce for a 14th season and Tyquan Thornton after his 2025 breakout.
- Released Jawaan Taylor and Mike Danna, and later traded Wanya Morris, making offensive tackle and edge depth key camp checks.
