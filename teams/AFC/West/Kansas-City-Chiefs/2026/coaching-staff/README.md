# Kansas City Chiefs 2026 Coaching Staff

Kansas City retains the Reid-Spagnuolo-Toub leadership base but changes the offensive coordinator chair by bringing Eric Bieniemy back. The staff additions around Bieniemy matter for fantasy because DeMarco Murray takes over running backs after Kenneth Walker arrives, and Chad O'Shea takes over a receiver room that needs clearer hierarchy.

## Authority model

Reid remains the offensive identity setter and final authority. Bieniemy's return is still meaningful because he brings prior Chiefs coordinator history and recent run-game success from Chicago. Spagnuolo remains the defensive architect, and his 2026 challenge is reloading the secondary and pass rush after trading McDuffie while adding Delane, Woods, Thomas, Canady, and several veterans.
