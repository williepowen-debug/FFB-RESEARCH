# Eric Bieniemy — Kansas City Chiefs Offensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Bieniemy returns as Kansas City's offensive coordinator for 2026. His responsibilities include coordinating Reid's offense, integrating Walker, managing Fields' backup/package value, and sharpening a receiver room that was inconsistent in 2025.

## Scheme and tendencies

Chiefs reporting cited Bieniemy's prior 2018-22 coordinator run, when Kansas City led the league in points per game, touchdowns, third-down rate, and yards per game. It also cited his 2025 work with Chicago running backs, including D'Andre Swift's career-high rushing output and strong rookie production from Kyle Monangai.

## Fantasy implications

- Walker should be evaluated as a scheme-feature candidate, not just a generic early-down addition.
- Mahomes' fantasy ceiling depends on whether Bieniemy helps restore efficiency while the receiver room sorts out.
- Fields could have emergency QB1 value if Mahomes misses time and may carry low-volume package risk for touchdown distribution.
- Kelce and the receivers require route and red-zone monitoring because the offense may lean more on the run than older Chiefs baselines.
