# Andy Reid — Kansas City Chiefs Head Coach

## Responsibilities

Reid remains Kansas City's head coach and offensive identity setter. Eric Bieniemy returns as offensive coordinator, but Chiefs fantasy projections still start with Reid, Patrick Mahomes, and the weekly passing-game design.

## Scheme and tendencies

Kansas City is still a Mahomes-led offense, but 2026 has more run-game emphasis than prior versions after signing Kenneth Walker and hiring DeMarco Murray as running backs coach. Reid also gets Travis Kelce back for a 14th season and Justin Fields as a backup quarterback with designed-run potential.

## Fantasy implications

- Mahomes remains the central offensive projection, but his knee-injury ramp and offensive-tackle changes require preseason protection checks.
- Walker's arrival raises the offense's explosive rushing ceiling and may reduce fragile pass-volume assumptions.
- Kelce's return keeps a familiar middle-field target alive, but age and route rate require monitoring.
- Receiver roles remain open behind Xavier Worthy, Tyquan Thornton, Jalen Royals, and rookie Cyrus Allen.
