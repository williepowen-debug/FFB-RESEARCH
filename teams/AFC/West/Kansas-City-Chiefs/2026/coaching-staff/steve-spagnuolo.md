# Steve Spagnuolo — Kansas City Chiefs Defensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Spagnuolo remains defensive coordinator and must integrate a defensive-heavy draft class after the McDuffie trade. His unit still has Chris Jones, Nick Bolton, Chamarri Conner, and Trent McDuffie's replacement candidates, but the 2026 structure depends on rookies earning real roles quickly.

## Scheme and tendencies

Spagnuolo's defense traditionally uses pressure, disguise, and defensive-back flexibility. The 2026 personnel plan supports that: Mansoor Delane and Jadon Canady add coverage options, Peter Woods joins the interior front, R Mason Thomas adds edge depth, and veterans such as Kader Kohou, Alohi Gilman, Kaiir Elam, and L'Jarius Sneed give the secondary more paths.

## Fantasy implications

- The Chiefs defense should not be downgraded solely because McDuffie left, but corner role clarity is a first-order matchup variable.
- Rookie defensive back performance will affect whether perimeter receivers get a matchup bump against Kansas City early.
- Jones plus Woods can create interior disruption that matters for opposing quarterbacks and run games.
- Spagnuolo's pressure usage can create turnover upside but also explosive-play exposure if young corners are not ready.
