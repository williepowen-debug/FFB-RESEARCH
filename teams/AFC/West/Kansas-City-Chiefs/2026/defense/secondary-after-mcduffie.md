# Finding: The Chiefs rebuilt the secondary after trading Trent McDuffie

## Scope

- Team/player: Kansas City Chiefs defensive backs
- Area: coverage roles, matchup quality, and post-McDuffie volatility
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Kansas City made a deliberate secondary reset by trading McDuffie to the Rams for four picks and then adding Delane, Canady, Kohou, Gilman, Elam, and Sneed. Delane is the centerpiece because the team used the No. 6 pick on him, and Chiefs pre-camp coverage stated he would have every opportunity to earn a starting role.

The slot and safety picture is still complex. Chamarri Conner led the team in defensive snaps in 2025 while functioning heavily as a slot/box defender, and Gilman adds another safety option. Spagnuolo has enough pieces to stay aggressive, but the early-season matchup profile depends on how fast Delane and the rebuilt corner group settle.

## Fantasy implication

- The Chiefs should remain a difficult schematic matchup if Delane is immediately viable outside.
- Perimeter receivers gain early-season upside if the young corners struggle or rotate heavily.
- Slot receiver matchups require specific charting because Conner, Kohou, and Canady could change alignment patterns.
- Defensive back IDP relevance depends on which rookies earn full-time roles.

## Evidence

- Kansas City traded McDuffie to the Rams for four draft selections, including the No. 29 pick in 2026 and a 2027 third-rounder.
- The Chiefs drafted Delane at No. 6 and Canady at No. 109.
- The pre-camp defensive backs breakdown described Conner as the 2025 defensive snap leader and primary slot corner.
- The same breakdown highlighted Delane's 2025 LSU coverage production and stated he would have every opportunity to earn a starting role.
- The 2026 transaction log lists Kohou, Gilman, Elam, and Sneed as additions.
