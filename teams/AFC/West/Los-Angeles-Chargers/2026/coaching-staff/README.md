The central fantasy frame is **stable head coach, new play-callers**. McDaniel represents a genuine offensive reset. O'Leary represents more philosophical continuity, but his first NFL play-calling season still requires early evidence.

Historical results and public statements establish hypotheses, not final 2026 behavior. Verify motion, protection, personnel grouping, pressure, and coverage rates from preseason and early regular-season charting.
