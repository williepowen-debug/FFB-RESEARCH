# Jim Harbaugh — Los Angeles Chargers

- Role: Head coach
- Joined team: 2024
- Last verified: 2026-08-16

## Responsibilities

Harbaugh retains final authority over the full team, staff, game management, and the physical identity of the roster. The 2026 season is his third with the Chargers. After consecutive 11-win playoff seasons ended without a postseason victory, he initiated an offensive reset by parting with coordinator Greg Roman and offensive-line coach Mike Devlin, then hired Mike McDaniel as the offense's primary architect and play-caller.

Harbaugh's coordinator choices define the practical authority model: McDaniel has broad control of the offense, while Chris O'Leary takes over the defense after Jesse Minter became Baltimore's head coach. General manager Joe Hortiz continues to control personnel in partnership with Harbaugh.

## Scheme and tendencies

Harbaugh's durable preference is a physical team built through both lines, a committed run game, and complementary defense. The 2026 changes do not abandon that preference; they change how it is expressed. McDaniel brings motion, outside-zone relationships, play-action, and quick-game answers, while the roster added lighter-moving interior linemen and four drafted blockers. Harbaugh publicly described McDaniel's run/pass marriage as a way to reduce pure dropback exposure for Justin Herbert.

Defensively, Harbaugh selected O'Leary from the same broad coaching tree as Minter and Mike Macdonald. Continuity in core principles is more likely than a full system teardown, but O'Leary is expected to add his own pressure and coverage answers.

## Personnel usage

Harbaugh's influence is clearest in the investment pattern. The Chargers rebuilt the offensive-line interior, added fullback Alec Ingold and blocking tight end Charlie Kolar, then used four of eight draft choices on offensive linemen. Those moves support a multiple run game rather than an offense that asks Herbert to solve every down from empty or straight dropback structure.

The head coach has not declared camp winners prematurely. Roles at guard, in the backfield, and across defensive subpackages must be established by preseason and regular-season usage.

## Pace and situational decisions

McDaniel's offense may play with more pre-snap motion and urgency, but Harbaugh still owns fourth-down, timeout, challenge, and end-of-half decisions. Track whether the new offensive structure raises early-down pass efficiency without creating a major jump in neutral pace. Also track whether playoff failures make Harbaugh more aggressive on fourth down or more conservative around protection risk.

## Fantasy implications

- Herbert gains an experienced play-caller and a structure designed to reduce pure dropback stress, but the offense still needs its rebuilt line to function together.
- The continued investment in blocking and the run game protects Omarion Hampton's volume outlook while leaving receiving and change-of-pace work open to competition.
- A younger receiver room may be distributed through motion and formation rather than produce a single Keenan Allen-style underneath target replacement.
- Harbaugh's defense-first and line-of-scrimmage priorities can support weekly game control, but fantasy scoring depends on McDaniel turning that control into touchdowns rather than merely lower-variance drives.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: Harbaugh retained the head-coaching role, dismissed Roman and Devlin, and hired McDaniel and O'Leary. Reported by the team: Harbaugh wanted a "fresh start" and a "head coach of the offense," and he has repeatedly described the McDaniel-Herbert pairing as the central offensive opportunity. Analytical: the 2026 model is Harbaugh's physical roster philosophy executed through McDaniel's more conflict-oriented offense.

## Reliability cautions

Public enthusiasm for a coordinator hire is not evidence of successful implementation. Harbaugh's historical preferences can constrain or complement McDaniel, and only game usage will show which influence dominates. Do not project Miami's peak production directly onto Los Angeles without adjusting for personnel, protection, and pace.
