# Finding: A deep, movable front gives O'Leary multiple pressure paths

## Scope

- Team/player: Los Angeles Chargers defensive front and linebackers
- Area: pass rush, run defense, rotation, and IDP opportunity
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-16

## Finding

The Chargers can preserve a three-edge pressure structure despite Odafe Oweh's departure. Tuli Tuipulotu returns after a 13-sack season, Khalil Mack was extended, and first-round pick Akheem Mesidor was selected to play immediately as a third edge who can reduce inside on passing downs. Behind them, the fourth edge role remains contested.

The interior was built for flexibility rather than a fixed nose/end depth chart. Teair Tart and developing linemen Jamaree Caldwell and Justin Eboigbe return, Dalvin Tomlinson adds veteran durability, and rookie Nick Barrett adds early-down depth. Defensive run-game coordinator Mike Elston has publicly discussed cross-training the group at multiple alignments. That gives Chris O'Leary personnel to create pressure without revealing the rush before the snap, but the exact rotation remains unsettled.

## Fantasy implication

- Tuipulotu has the strongest established sack and snap profile and is the safest Chargers edge for IDP.
- Mesidor's value depends on whether the third-edge role produces starter-level passing-down snaps and whether the fantasy platform preserves edge eligibility when he reduces inside.
- Mack can remain efficient while ceding volume at age 35; name value should not be treated as evidence of a full-time snap share.
- A deep interior can improve team run defense and create favorable long-yardage rush situations without producing a reliable individual defensive tackle.
- Daiyan Henley's green-dot and every-down role gives him the clearest linebacker tackle floor.

## Evidence

- Tuipulotu recorded 13 sacks and 20 tackles for loss in 2025.
- Mesidor produced 12.5 sacks, 17.5 tackles for loss, and four forced fumbles at Miami in 2025; O'Leary highlighted his ability to rush from the edge or interior.
- The team identified Mack, Tuipulotu, and Mesidor as the top three edge options entering camp, with Bud Dupree, Kyle Kennard, and Nadame Tucker competing behind them.
- Tart started every Chargers game in 2025 and received a multi-year extension after the season.
- Eboigbe recorded six sacks in 2025; Caldwell played all 17 games; Tomlinson has started all 142 regular-season games of his NFL career through 2025.
- Henley led the 2025 Chargers with 103 tackles, added 3.5 sacks and two interceptions, and retained the defensive communication helmet for 2026.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: The top personnel and intended flexibility are documented, but O'Leary has no NFL play-calling sample and the rotation is not established.
- Fact/inference boundary: Prior production, roster moves, and stated alignment flexibility are facts. Pressure distribution and individual fantasy volume are projections.
- What would invalidate this: Mesidor functioning only as a low-snap backup, Mack or Tuipulotu losing substantial healthy snaps, or the interior requiring fixed roles that limit disguise.
- Next review: Each preseason game, then the first four regular-season games with snap, alignment, rush-opportunity, pressure, and linebacker-usage charting.
