# Offensive-line monitoring framework

## Finding

The Chargers line should be updated through repeatable deployment and play-level evidence, not isolated camp wins, depth-chart labels or headline sack totals. Monitoring must distinguish starting-unit proof from reserve evidence and separate design, blocking, quarterback behavior and play result.

## Evidence

The current lineup projection, availability risks, open left-guard competition and Houston reserve deployment identify the monitoring questions below. They do not resolve the competitive outcome.

## Purpose

Dated observations belong in [`../../preseason/evidence-log.csv`](../../preseason/evidence-log.csv), and current answers belong in [`../../preseason/priority-status.csv`](../../preseason/priority-status.csv). This file defines the evidence required to change the durable assessment.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Regular-season all-22 or clear broadcast reps against starting defenders.
2. Preseason first-unit reps with identifiable assignments.
3. Joint-practice team periods corroborated by multiple reporters.
4. Repeated standard practice observations.
5. Coach/player statements about role, technique or installation.
6. Team-authored highlights and isolated one-on-one results.

Lower-tier evidence can open or narrow a question; it should not close one by itself. Houston reserve success ranks below the coming first-unit San Francisco work for the starting-five assessment.

## Minimum chart fields

### Pass plays

- date/game, quarter, down, distance and score state;
- five-man lineup and eligible protectors;
- defensive front and pressure presentation;
- protection type when identifiable;
- chip/stay-in help;
- time to pressure;
- pressure and primary responsibility category;
- Herbert response and play result;
- target depth and route-release effect.

### Run plays

- five-man lineup, personnel, formation and motion;
- concept family, direction and intended gap;
- box count and front;
- first contact and yards before contact;
- primary and combination-block result;
- defender held by boot/read/action;
- play result and situational value.

## Update states

- **Open:** insufficient competitive evidence.
- **Leaning confirmed:** repeated evidence in one competitive setting.
- **Confirmed:** repeated evidence across at least two meaningful settings or a settled deployment backed by direct usage.
- **Leaning rejected:** repeated contrary evidence in one competitive setting.
- **Rejected:** contrary evidence persists across settings or the personnel premise changes.

Do not declare the problem solved because a quick-game plan prevents sacks. Do not declare the line poor because Herbert holds one clean pocket too long. Always state lineup, opponent quality, sample size and help usage.

## Escalation triggers

Immediately update the module when:

- Slater or Alt misses practice, has a status change or receives a snap limit;
- Los Angeles changes the first-unit left guard;
- the same stunt or identification error repeats;
- backs or tight ends are routinely retained;
- Slaughter moves into the starting lineup at guard or center;
- a reserve tackle takes first-unit work;
- a lineman is signed, waived or placed on reserve;
- run concepts or direction narrow materially;
- route releases or target depth change with a lineup move.

## Fantasy implication

This framework connects line evidence to player decisions: Herbert through clean-pocket volume and target depth, Hampton through first contact and protection duty, and pass catchers through route releases and help assignments.
