# Vance Joseph — Denver Broncos Defensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Joseph remains Denver's defensive coordinator after the unit led the league in sacks in back-to-back seasons and helped the team reach the AFC Championship Game. His 2026 responsibilities are maintaining pass-rush pressure, increasing takeaways, and integrating a changed linebacker/safety depth chart.

## Scheme and tendencies

Denver's defense is built around pressure and coverage synergy. Bonitto leads a deep pass-rush group, Surtain anchors the secondary, and Hufanga/Jones/McMillian/Moss/Barron provide coverage flexibility. Joseph and Payton both identified turnover creation as an improvement area after the 2025 defense produced only 14 regular-season takeaways.

## Fantasy implications

- Denver remains one of the league's most difficult fantasy matchups if the sack pressure repeats.
- The secondary can suppress perimeter receivers, especially when Surtain is used in high-leverage assignments.
- Running-back receiving and middle-field production should be evaluated against the Singleton/Strnad linebacker pairing.
- DST value depends on converting pressure into more takeaways.
