# Denver Broncos 2026 Coaching Staff

Denver retains its main leadership spine but changes the offensive play-caller. Sean Payton remains head coach and signed a new contract through 2030. Davis Webb is promoted to offensive coordinator and will call plays, while Vance Joseph continues to coordinate a defense that led the NFL in sacks in consecutive seasons.

- [`vance-joseph.md`](vance-joseph.md): defensive coordinator profile.

## Authority model

Payton remains the program and offensive-architecture authority even after handing game-day play-calling to Webb. Webb's promotion matters because it changes in-game sequencing for Bo Nix, Jaylen Waddle, Courtland Sutton, Evan Engram, and the running backs. Joseph remains a high-confidence defensive authority with continuity across pass rush and secondary.
