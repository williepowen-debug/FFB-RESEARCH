# Sean Payton — Denver Broncos Head Coach

## Responsibilities

Payton remains Denver's head coach and offensive architect. He signed a new five-year contract through the 2030 season and continues to shape the roster around Bo Nix after Denver reached the AFC Championship Game.

## Scheme and tendencies

The 2026 offense is still Payton's structure, but Davis Webb now calls plays. Denver invested in speed and yards-after-catch by trading for Waddle, retained Dobbins, and added Coleman to deepen a run game Payton has emphasized as a complementary driver for the defense.

## Fantasy implications

- Nix's fantasy value remains tied to Payton's structure, but Webb's sequencing must be charted.
- Waddle adds explosive target potential and should raise the efficiency ceiling for Nix, Sutton, Mims, and Engram.
- Dobbins, Harvey, and Coleman can improve offensive balance while creating touch-share ambiguity.
- Payton's long-term extension supports continuity for dynasty evaluation of Nix and Denver skill players.
