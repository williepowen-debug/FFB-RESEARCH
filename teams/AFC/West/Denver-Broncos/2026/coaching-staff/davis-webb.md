# Davis Webb — Denver Broncos Offensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Webb is Denver's 2026 offensive coordinator and game-day play-caller. He was promoted after serving as pass game coordinator/quarterbacks coach, and he remains closely tied to Nix's development.

## Scheme and tendencies

Webb worked with Nix through the quarterback's first two seasons, a span in which the Broncos credited Nix with 7,500 passing yards, 50 touchdowns, and at least 20 wins. Payton said Webb will call plays while Payton remains involved in offensive design. Webb inherits more speed and depth with Waddle, Dobbins, Harvey, Coleman, Sutton, Mims, Engram, and the tight-end room.

## Fantasy implications

- Webb's first play-calling sample is the main unknown for pace, pass rate, and red-zone distribution.
- Nix's mobility and timing with Waddle should be checked early after his ankle recovery.
- Waddle can increase explosive-play rate, but target concentration with Sutton, Mims, and Engram is unresolved.
- The backfield may be deeper than fantasy managers prefer even if the real offense improves.
