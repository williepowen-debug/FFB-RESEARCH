# Denver Broncos 2026 Roster Changes

These files preserve the personnel transition supporting the Broncos Core-tier module. Denver is closer to a contender-continuity build than a reset: Sean Payton and Vance Joseph remain in place, Bo Nix returns from an ankle injury, Jaylen Waddle joins the receiver room, J.K. Dobbins returns, and the defense keeps its established secondary and pass-rush foundation.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): material player exits and releases.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): external veteran additions and trade additions with structural relevance.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): re-signings and tenders that preserve the 2025 core.
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): all seven 2026 selections and their immediate monitoring questions.

## Headline changes

- Acquired Jaylen Waddle from Miami to raise the passing game's speed and target ceiling.
- Re-signed J.K. Dobbins and drafted Jonah Coleman, deepening a backfield that already included RJ Harvey and Jaleel McLaughlin.
- Promoted Davis Webb to offensive coordinator and play-caller while retaining Payton's offensive oversight.
- Retained Ja'Quan McMillian, Alex Singleton, Justin Strnad, Adam Trautman, Lucas Krull, and other role players.
- Released Dre Greenlaw and lost P.J. Locke, making linebacker rotation and safety depth the main defensive role checks.
