# Finding: Denver's pass rush remains the defense's fantasy engine

## Scope

- Team/player: Denver Broncos pass rush and linebackers
- Area: pressure, sacks, and second-level role changes
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Denver enters 2026 with one of the clearest defensive strengths in the league: repeated pass-rush production. The Broncos led the NFL in sacks in 2024 and 2025, setting a franchise record with 68 sacks in 2025 after 63 the prior year. Bonitto has led the team in sacks in both seasons and has 27.5 sacks since 2024, second in the league over that span.

The main role change is at inside linebacker. Greenlaw was released, and Strnad is expected to start alongside Singleton after re-signing. That creates a specific coverage and tackle-volume monitoring point, while rookie Red Murdock adds special-teams and turnover depth.

## Fantasy implication

- Denver remains a difficult matchup for quarterbacks and offensive lines because pressure is the defensive identity.
- Opposing passing games carry elevated sack and turnover risk, but explosive plays can still occur if blitzes leave coverage stressed.
- Strnad has IDP watch value if his nickel role holds.
- Murdock is a deep special-teams/turnover watch rather than a seasonal projection.

## Evidence

- Denver posted 68 sacks in 2025 and 63 sacks in 2024, leading the league in both seasons.
- Bonitto led the team in each of the last two seasons and recorded 27.5 sacks from 2024-25.
- Seventeen Broncos recorded at least half a sack in 2025, and linebackers accounted for more than half the team total.
- Denver released Greenlaw with a post-June 1 designation after he appeared in eight regular-season games.
- Strnad re-signed and was described by Joseph as ready for his chance to start.

## Assessment

- Confidence: high
- Reason: Multi-year pass-rush production and coordinator continuity are strong; linebacker coverage is the main unknown.
- Fact/inference boundary: Sack totals, re-signings, release, and stated role expectations are facts. Repeated elite pressure is a projection.
- What would invalidate this: Bonitto or multiple rushers being limited, Strnad losing nickel snaps, or pressure falling sharply without blitz help.
