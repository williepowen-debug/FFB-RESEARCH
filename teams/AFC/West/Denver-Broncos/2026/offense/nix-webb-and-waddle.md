# Finding: The Broncos paired Bo Nix's Year 3 leap with Webb and Waddle

## Scope

- Team/player: Denver Broncos passing offense
- Area: quarterback health, play-calling transition, and receiver hierarchy
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Denver's passing-game thesis is that Nix can keep ascending with more speed and a familiar teacher calling plays. Nix returned to practice after the ankle injury that ended his 2025 postseason, and both he and Payton said his mobility should not be affected. Webb, who worked closely with Nix for two seasons, is now offensive coordinator and play-caller.

The Waddle trade is the major personnel accelerant. Denver paid premium draft capital to acquire a receiver with three 1,000-yard seasons in his first five years. Waddle joins a group that already includes Sutton, Mims, Engram, and depth options, so the important fantasy question is whether Waddle becomes a concentrated first read or simply makes the ecosystem more efficient.

## Fantasy implication

- Nix remains a priority quarterback projection if his mobility is intact and Webb maintains Payton's efficiency structure.
- Waddle has immediate WR2/ceiling appeal, but target share must be charted against Sutton, Mims, and Engram.
- Sutton and Mims can benefit from spacing while losing some target share to Waddle.
- Engram's seam role can remain valuable if Webb uses Waddle to widen coverage.

## Evidence

- Nix returned to the practice field during mandatory minicamp after an ankle injury and said doctors deemed the ankle ready for 2026.
- Payton named Webb offensive coordinator and later announced Webb would call plays.
- The Broncos credited Webb with helping Nix produce 7,500 passing yards and 50 touchdowns across his first two seasons.
- Denver acquired Waddle and a fourth-round pick from Miami for first-, third-, and fourth-round picks in the 2026 draft.
- Waddle has three 1,000-yard seasons in his first five NFL seasons.
