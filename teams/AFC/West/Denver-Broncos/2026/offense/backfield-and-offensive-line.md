# Finding: Denver's run game has better depth but less touch clarity

## Scope

- Team/player: Denver Broncos running backs and offensive line
- Area: rushing efficiency, backfield roles, and protection baseline
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Denver's backfield is deeper than it was entering 2025. Dobbins re-signed after rushing for 772 yards at 5.0 yards per carry in 10 games, Harvey returns after nearly 900 scrimmage yards and 12 touchdowns as a rookie, and Coleman joins as a physical fourth-round back with receiving and protection experience. That gives Payton/Webb real options but creates fantasy role risk.

The offensive line is the stabilizer. Denver's camp preview identified the line as a team strength and highlighted Garett Bolles and Quinn Meinerz as first-team All-Pros and Protector of the Year finalists. If that line remains intact, the offense can support useful rushing volume even if individual touches rotate.

## Fantasy implication

- Dobbins is the first back to project, but health and workload need preseason confirmation.
- Harvey's passing-game and touchdown profile keeps him relevant even if Dobbins starts.
- Coleman can cap both backs if he earns short-yardage or pass-protection trust quickly.
- Strong line play raises the floor for the backfield and Nix efficiency, but it may also enable a committee.

## Evidence

- Dobbins re-signed on a two-year contract after a 772-yard 2025 season in 10 games.
- Denver's running-back camp preview described Dobbins, Harvey, and Coleman as the core competition and noted Harvey's 12 rookie touchdowns and 896 scrimmage yards.
- Coleman rushed for 758 yards and 15 touchdowns with 31 catches in 2025 at Washington and was selected 108th overall.
- Payton emphasized run-game consistency and building around the strength of the unit.
- Bolles and Meinerz were first-team All-Pros and finalists for the inaugural Protector of the Year Award.

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: The line and top backs are established, but touch shares are unresolved.
