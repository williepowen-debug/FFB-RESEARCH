# Las Vegas Raiders 2026 Roster Changes

These files preserve the personnel transition supporting the Raiders Core-tier module. The 2026 roster is built around a quarterback bridge plan, a reworked offensive line, and heavy defensive investment.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): material trades and free-agent exits.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): external veteran additions with plausible structural or fantasy impact.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): re-signings and tenders that preserve the roster core.
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): all 10 2026 selections and their immediate monitoring questions.

## Headline changes

- Added Kirk Cousins while drafting Fernando Mendoza No. 1 overall, creating a veteran bridge and rookie-development plan.
- Signed Tyler Linderbaum to stabilize center and help the Kubiak/Dennison run-game installation.
- Added Jalen Nailor and Malik Benson to a receiver room led by Tre Tucker, Jack Bech, and Dont'e Thornton Jr.
- Added Mike Washington Jr. behind Ashton Jeanty while retaining Brock Bowers and Michael Mayer as a high-leverage tight-end pairing.
- Rebuilt the defensive front seven with Kwity Paye, Quay Walker, Nakobe Dean, Keyron Crawford, and Treydan Stukes while retaining Malcolm Koonce, Eric Stokes, Thomas Booker IV, and the Crosby-centered defensive core.
