# Las Vegas Raiders 2026 Coaching Staff

The 2026 Raiders are a full staff reset under first-time Head Coach Klint Kubiak. Andrew Janocko coordinates the offense, with Rick Dennison and Mario Jeberaeel central to the run-game installation. Rob Leonard coordinates the defense, supported by Joe Woods in the secondary and Al Holcomb as a senior defensive assistant.

## Authority model

Kubiak is the identity setter and likely offensive architect. Janocko carries coordinator title and quarterback-development relevance, while Dennison's offensive-line role matters for the outside-zone/run-action fit. Leonard owns the defensive call sheet, with Woods' coverage background important because the roster added several defensive backs and linebackers at once.
