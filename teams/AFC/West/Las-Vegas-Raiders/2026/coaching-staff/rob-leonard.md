# Rob Leonard — Raiders Defensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Leonard is the Raiders' 2026 defensive coordinator. He inherits Maxx Crosby and a retained interior rotation, but the front seven and secondary were reshaped with Kwity Paye, Quay Walker, Nakobe Dean, Taron Johnson, Treydan Stukes, Jermod McCoy, Dalton Johnson, and Hezekiah Masses.

## Scheme and tendencies

The personnel points toward a defense that can pressure with a deeper edge group and disguise behind better linebacker and nickel-corner talent. Raiders coverage of camp described the defense as still forming its identity, with Crosby returning from knee rehab and the new additions increasing unpredictability.

## Fantasy implications

- Las Vegas can become a tougher matchup if Crosby is healthy and Paye/Koonce/Crawford create enough pressure without blitz dependency.
- Dean and Walker are important for limiting running-back receiving and quarterback scramble efficiency.
- The rookie-heavy secondary creates upside and volatility; matchup confidence should remain conditional until roles settle.
- Early IDP value concentrates around Crosby, Dean/Walker tackle volume, and any rookie defensive back who wins full-time snaps.
