# Klint Kubiak — Raiders Head Coach

## Responsibilities

Kubiak is Las Vegas' 2026 head coach and the central figure in the offensive transition. The official staff announcement places Andrew Janocko at offensive coordinator, Rick Dennison on the offensive line, and Mario Jeberaeel as offensive run-game coordinator, but the team context points to Kubiak as the scheme setter.

## Scheme and tendencies

The roster build supports a Kubiak-family offense: veteran quarterback Kirk Cousins, No. 1 pick Fernando Mendoza, center Tyler Linderbaum, a reworked line, Ashton Jeanty as the backfield anchor, and heavy tight-end usage built around Brock Bowers and Michael Mayer. Kubiak has publicly framed Cousins as the starting quarterback entering camp while wanting Mendoza and Aidan O'Connell to push him.

## Fantasy implications

- Cousins is the short-term stabilizer, but Mendoza's development is a season-long contingency that can change receiver and tight-end projection.
- Jeanty's value depends on whether Kubiak keeps him as a three-down anchor or uses Mike Washington Jr. as a meaningful complement.
- Bowers remains the most stable target projection because the scheme and camp reports both support heavy tight-end involvement.
- The offense should be treated as more stable than a pure rookie-QB build, but still a new system with uncertain pass volume.
