# Andrew Janocko — Raiders Offensive Coordinator

## Responsibilities

Janocko is the 2026 offensive coordinator under Kubiak. His most fantasy-relevant responsibilities are quarterback sequencing, pass-game installation, and translating the new system through Cousins, Mendoza, Bowers, Jeanty, and a rebuilt offensive line.

## Scheme and tendencies

The offense is expected to be run-game connected rather than isolated dropback volume. Tyler Linderbaum was added at center, Rick Dennison coaches the line, and camp notes have emphasized the offense learning Kubiak's system while the Cousins-Bowers connection develops. Janocko's early comments on the line have focused on competition and daily improvement.

## Fantasy implications

- The quarterback room should be charted by first-team reps and preseason sequencing rather than draft capital alone.
- Bowers can remain a first-read and red-zone engine if Janocko/Kubiak use him as the offensive problem-solver.
- Jeanty's rushing efficiency should be helped if Linderbaum and the line stabilize the zone game.
- Receiver roles remain less settled than tight end and running back, making Tre Tucker, Jalen Nailor, Jack Bech, Dont'e Thornton Jr., and Malik Benson monitoring priorities.
