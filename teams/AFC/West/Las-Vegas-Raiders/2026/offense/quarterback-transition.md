# Finding: The Raiders are using Kirk Cousins as a bridge for Fernando Mendoza

## Scope

- Team/player: Las Vegas Raiders quarterbacks
- Area: starter sequencing, pass-game stability, and rookie development
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Las Vegas turned over its quarterback room by trading Geno Smith, signing Kirk Cousins, and drafting Fernando Mendoza first overall. Kubiak named Cousins the starting quarterback entering camp, while Mendoza moved into meaningful second-team work after being selected No. 1. This creates a bridge structure rather than a pure rookie-start plan.

The fantasy-relevant issue is not just who starts Week 1. It is whether Cousins can keep the offense on schedule long enough for Bowers, Jeanty, and the receivers to avoid the volatility that often comes with early rookie starts. Mendoza's draft capital keeps a midseason change live if the offense stalls or if Cousins' play declines.

## Fantasy implication

- Cousins raises the early-season floor for Bowers and Jeanty because the offense should operate with veteran timing.
- Mendoza is a dynasty hold and redraft watchlist quarterback rather than an immediate seasonal projection.
- A midseason transition would likely increase volatility for receivers first, while Bowers' target-earning profile gives him the best chance to survive the change.
- Offensive-line cohesion is part of the quarterback projection because Kubiak's plan depends on run-action credibility and reduced negative plays.

## Evidence

- The Raiders signed Cousins on 2026-04-06 after he spent 2024-25 with Atlanta and brought extensive starting experience.
- The Raiders drafted Mendoza No. 1 overall in the 2026 NFL Draft.
- Raiders coverage reported Kubiak named Cousins the starter entering camp and wanted Mendoza and Aidan O'Connell to push him.
- Training-camp reports described Mendoza working with the second unit and showing improved command after offseason installation work.
- The team added Tyler Linderbaum at center and built a coaching staff with Dennison and Jeberaeel supporting the run-game structure.
