# Finding: Bowers and Jeanty are the Raiders' clearest fantasy anchors

## Scope

- Team/player: Las Vegas Raiders running backs, tight ends, and receivers
- Area: touch and target hierarchy
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-25

## Finding

Brock Bowers and Ashton Jeanty enter the Raiders' 2026 offense with cleaner roles than the wide receivers. Bowers led the team in receptions, receiving yards per game, and receiving touchdowns in an injury-shortened 2025 and has been featured in camp reports with Cousins. Jeanty is described by the team as the steady backfield anchor entering Year 2, with rookie Mike Washington Jr. added as a complementary piece.

Jeanty left the August 23 practice, and Kubiak described him as “on the mend” on August 25 without
giving a diagnosis or return timetable. That preserves the lead-back baseline but leaves his next
practice participation as the immediate availability checkpoint.

The receiver room is more fluid. Tre Tucker led the team in 2025 receiving yards, Jalen Nailor was added in free agency, Jack Bech and Dont'e Thornton Jr. are second-year options, and Malik Benson's speed has shown up with Mendoza in camp notes. That creates best-ball appeal, but the route hierarchy needs proof before seasonal confidence.

## Fantasy implication

- Bowers is the highest-confidence Raiders pass catcher across redraft, best ball, and tight-end-premium formats.
- Jeanty should be valued as the primary back, but Washington's role must be checked for goal-line or passing-down leakage.
- Tucker and Nailor are the first receiver names to monitor, while Bech, Thornton, and Benson require route evidence.
- Cousins stabilizes the early target environment; a Mendoza transition would likely favor Bowers and designed throws before perimeter receivers.

## Evidence

- Bowers was No. 60 on the NFL Top 100 list and led the Raiders in receptions, receiving yards per game, and receiving touchdowns in 2025 despite missing five games.
- Raiders camp updates reported Cousins and Bowers strengthening their connection with red-zone touchdowns.
- Raiders coverage framed Jeanty as the backfield's steady anchor entering Year 2, with Washington joining as a new complement.
- Kubiak said Jeanty was “on the mend” on August 25 but supplied no diagnosis or timetable.
- The pre-draft roster review identified Tucker as the 2025 receiving-yardage leader and Nailor as the biggest receiver addition.
- Camp updates noted the Mendoza-to-Malik Benson connection was growing while Jalen Nailor and Tucker produced red-zone moments.
