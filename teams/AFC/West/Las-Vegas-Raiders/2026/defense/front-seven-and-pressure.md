# Finding: The Raiders rebuilt the front seven around Crosby's return

## Scope

- Team/player: Las Vegas Raiders defensive front and linebackers
- Area: pass rush, run defense, and matchup pressure
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

Las Vegas' defensive front-seven plan starts with Maxx Crosby returning from a knee injury and adds more proven support than the 2025 unit had. The Raiders signed Kwity Paye, retained Malcolm Koonce and Thomas Booker IV, drafted Keyron Crawford, and rebuilt linebacker with Quay Walker and Nakobe Dean. That is enough talent to move the defense from a matchup target toward a matchup-dependent unit if health and role clarity hold.

The key risk is synchronization. Crosby is ramping back after rehab, Paye is new to the system, and Dean/Walker must define coverage and run-fit responsibilities. Leonard can be more multiple, but early-season fantasy defense evaluation should wait for snap counts and pressure data.

## Fantasy implication

- Offensive players against Las Vegas should not receive an automatic matchup bump if Crosby looks healthy and Paye plays starter snaps.
- Quarterbacks and perimeter receivers still benefit if Leonard needs blitz volume before the coverage structure is settled.
- Running backs face more second-level speed if Dean and Walker stay on the field together.
- Crosby remains the most stable IDP piece; Dean/Walker tackle volume and Paye pressure share need preseason confirmation.

## Evidence

- Crosby returned to the practice field at the start of camp after knee rehab and said he felt physically ready.
- Paye signed as an unrestricted free agent after starting 74 of 75 games with Indianapolis and recording 30.5 career sacks.
- Dean signed after four seasons with Philadelphia and a 2025 season with 55 tackles, seven tackles for loss, four sacks, and two forced fumbles.
- Raiders coverage described the defense as adding Paye, Crawford, Stukes, Dean, and Walker around Crosby.
- The official defensive-line position breakdown highlighted a reinforced line and a retained rotation including Koonce, Adam Butler, Jonah Laulu, Thomas Booker IV, Tonka Hemingway, and JJ Pegues.
