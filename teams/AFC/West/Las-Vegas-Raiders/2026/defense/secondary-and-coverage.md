# Finding: The Raiders added coverage talent but still need role clarity

## Scope

- Team/player: Las Vegas Raiders secondary
- Area: cornerback/safety roles and fantasy matchup quality
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-17

## Finding

The Raiders attacked the secondary with both veteran and rookie additions. Taron Johnson arrived by trade to stabilize nickel coverage, while Treydan Stukes, Jermod McCoy, Dalton Johnson, and Hezekiah Masses were drafted across safety and corner. Eric Stokes was retained, but camp reporting still describes an open cornerback competition.

That combination creates more upside than certainty. If Johnson controls the slot and the young defensive backs settle into defined roles, Crosby and Paye can rush with better coverage help. If the competition remains unresolved, the Raiders can still be a target for efficient passing attacks early in the season.

## Fantasy implication

- Avoid treating Las Vegas as a clear pass-funnel or shutdown matchup until preseason roles settle.
- Slot receivers and tight ends should be evaluated against Johnson/Bowers-style nickel usage and linebacker coverage rather than last year's baseline.
- Rookie defensive backs are IDP watchlist names only until snaps are earned.
- Pressure can protect the secondary, but coverage instability can force Leonard into higher-risk calls.

## Evidence

- The Raiders acquired Taron Johnson and a 2026 seventh-round pick from Buffalo for a sixth-round pick.
- Las Vegas drafted safety Treydan Stukes at No. 38, corner Jermod McCoy at No. 101, safety Dalton Johnson at No. 150, and corner Hezekiah Masses at No. 175.
- The free-agency tracker shows Eric Stokes re-signed and Darnay Holmes/Lonnie Johnson Jr. signed elsewhere.
- Camp updates highlighted a wide-open corner competition with plays from Jermod McCoy and Greedy Vance.
- The staff includes Joe Woods as pass-game coordinator/defensive backs coach under Leonard.
