## Headline changes

- Mike Tomlin stepped down and Mike McCarthy was hired as head coach, reuniting with quarterback Aaron Rodgers, who returned on a one-year deal for what he has declared his final NFL season.
- The offense was upgraded around Rodgers: traded for wide receiver Michael Pittman Jr. to pair with DK Metcalf, and signed running back Rico Dowdle.
- The secondary was reworked with cornerback Jamel Dean and safeties Jaquan Brisker and Darnell Savage; the defensive line added Sebastian Joseph-Day.
- The interior offensive line lost veteran guard Isaac Seumalo; the Steelers drafted a developmental quarterback (Drew Allar) behind Rodgers.

Colleges and terms are populated where confirmed by reputable trackers; blank or approximate fields mark values not yet verified to source standard. Partial dates (`YYYY-MM`) mark moves whose exact day was not confirmed.
