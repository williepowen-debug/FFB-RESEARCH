# Pittsburgh Steelers 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the offense as a connected system. Pittsburgh's 2026 story is a veteran reunion and win-now reset: Mike McCarthy hired as head coach, Aaron Rodgers returning for a farewell season, and a skill group upgraded by the Michael Pittman Jr. trade and the Rico Dowdle signing. The central variables are who authors the passing game and how the receiver and backfield touches divide.

## Files

- [`passing-game-and-rodgers.md`](passing-game-and-rodgers.md): the Metcalf–Pittman target split, Rodgers's volume and durability, Freiermuth's role, and the play-calling question.
- [`backfield-committee.md`](backfield-committee.md): the three-way Warren/Dowdle/Johnson committee and the reset interior line.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not convert the McCarthy hire, the Rodgers reunion, or the skill additions into settled 2026 usage.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. Does Angelichio call the plays, or does McCarthy author the offense?
2. Does Rodgers play a full pass-forward workload, and how does age/durability affect it?
3. How do DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. split the target volume?
4. Does a clear lead back emerge from the Warren/Dowdle/Johnson committee?
5. Does the interior line stabilize after Isaac Seumalo's departure?
