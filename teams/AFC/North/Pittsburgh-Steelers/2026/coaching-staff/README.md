# Pittsburgh Steelers 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. Pittsburgh's 2026 change is a coaching regime change: Mike Tomlin stepped down after a long tenure, and Mike McCarthy was hired to lead an almost entirely new staff. General manager Omar Khan remains, so the front office is continuous while the coaching staff was rebuilt.
