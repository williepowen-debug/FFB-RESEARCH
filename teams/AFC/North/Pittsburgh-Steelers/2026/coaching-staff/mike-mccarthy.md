# Mike McCarthy — Pittsburgh Steelers

- Role: Head coach; veteran offensive-minded coach
- Joined team: 2026 (previously head coach of Green Bay 2006–2018 and Dallas 2020–2024)
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

McCarthy controls whole-team standards, staff structure, and game management, and brings a long offensive-play-calling background to a team that just hired a first-time coordinator. He was hired on 2026-01-24 after Mike Tomlin stepped down following a wild-card playoff loss, ending one of the longest head-coaching tenures in the league. The hire reunites McCarthy with quarterback Aaron Rodgers, whom he coached for 13 seasons in Green Bay.

This is a veteran-coach-plus-veteran-quarterback reunion built to win now. General manager Omar Khan remains, so the front office is continuous while the coaching staff was almost entirely rebuilt.

## Scheme and tendencies

McCarthy's history is a West Coast passing structure with a strong play-action and boot game. The 2026 Steelers offense should be treated as a new install: new head coach, new coordinator (Brian Angelichio, the reported play-caller), and new offensive position coaches. Whether McCarthy takes practical control of the passing-game design or genuinely delegates to Angelichio is a specific early-season watchpoint, because it determines whose tendencies the offense actually reflects.

## Personnel usage

McCarthy inherits an upgraded skill group: Rodgers at quarterback, a receiver room now led by DK Metcalf and the newly acquired Michael Pittman Jr., a backfield of Jaylen Warren, Kaleb Johnson, and the added Rico Dowdle, and tight end Pat Freiermuth. His usage of Rodgers — how much no-huddle, motion, and quick game — is the central variable. On defense he defers to coordinator Patrick Graham.

## Pace and situational decisions

McCarthy has a long head-coaching sample elsewhere, but none in Pittsburgh with this staff and roster. Track pass rate over expectation, no-huddle usage, red-zone design, and late-game management. A reunion offense with a 40-something quarterback on a farewell tour is a win-now profile with real health and continuity risk.

## Fantasy implications

- Rodgers's fantasy value depends on the pass volume and structure McCarthy and Angelichio build; the reunion suggests a pass-forward lean, but that must be charted.
- DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. give Rodgers two established target earners; the target split between them is the key receiver question.
- A veteran quarterback on a final-year deal raises both the ceiling (timing passing game) and the downside risk (age, durability) relative to a stable young starter.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: Tomlin stepped down; McCarthy was hired as head coach on 2026-01-24; Rodgers returned on a one-year deal and declared 2026 his final season. Reported: Angelichio is expected to call the offensive plays. Analytical: "veteran reunion, win-now offense" is the working frame until game evidence shows who designs and calls the passing game.

## Reliability cautions

A long track record elsewhere does not guarantee the same output with a new roster, a first-time coordinator, and an aging quarterback. Separate McCarthy's career reputation from what Pittsburgh actually runs in 2026, and watch whether the play-calling truly sits with Angelichio.
