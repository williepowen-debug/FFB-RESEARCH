# Pittsburgh Steelers 2026 Defensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the defense as a connected system under new coordinator Patrick Graham. Pittsburgh retained its elite edge and interior anchors (T.J. Watt, Alex Highsmith, Cameron Heyward) and reworked the secondary through free agency. The pass-rush talent is a known strength; the coverage identity and secondary roles are the unestablished variables.

## Files

- [`edge-and-front.md`](edge-and-front.md): the returning edge and interior talent, the Joseph-Day addition, and pressure projection under Graham.
- [`secondary-rework.md`](secondary-rework.md): the reworked back end (Dean, Brisker, Savage), open coverage roles, and early exploitability.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable defensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not convert the coordinator change or the secondary additions into a settled scheme, role chart, or pressure projection.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. What coverage and pressure identity does Graham install?
2. Does T.J. Watt sustain premier edge production in the new scheme?
3. How does the interior hold up with Joseph-Day added and Heyward aging?
4. Do the reworked secondary's coverage roles settle, or is Pittsburgh exploitable early?
5. Does the front generate enough pressure to drive takeaways?
