# Pittsburgh Steelers — 2026 McCarthy-Rodgers Reunion and a Win-Now Reset

- Conference/division: AFC North
- Last verified: 2026-08-13
- Scope: changes from the 2025 team to the 2026 preseason roster and staff
- Build tier: Core

Pittsburgh changed its coaching leadership for the first time in a generation and doubled down on winning immediately. Mike Tomlin stepped down after a wild-card loss, and Mike McCarthy was hired as head coach, reuniting with quarterback Aaron Rodgers, who returned on a one-year deal for what he has declared his final NFL season. General manager Omar Khan stayed and upgraded the skill group around Rodgers. This is a veteran reunion and win-now reset — a distinct shape from the coaching-reset-over-continuous-core in Baltimore or the continuity-with-upgrade story in Buffalo elsewhere in the conference.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

### Confirmed baseline

- Mike Tomlin stepped down; Mike McCarthy was hired as head coach in January 2026. GM Omar Khan remains.
- New coordinators: Brian Angelichio (offense, reported play-caller) and Patrick Graham (defense); special-teams coordinator Danny Crossman. The prior OC (Arthur Smith) and DC (Teryl Austin) left.
- Aaron Rodgers returned on a one-year deal and declared 2026 his final season; the QB room adds rookie Drew Allar (Round 3) behind him with Mason Rudolph and Will Howard.
- The offense was upgraded: traded for wide receiver Michael Pittman Jr. to pair with DK Metcalf, and signed running back Rico Dowdle into a room with Jaylen Warren and Kaleb Johnson. Pat Freiermuth returns at tight end.
- The secondary was reworked (cornerback Jamel Dean; safeties Jaquan Brisker and Darnell Savage) and the defensive line added Sebastian Joseph-Day, around the T.J. Watt, Cameron Heyward, and Alex Highsmith core.
- The interior offensive line lost veteran guard Isaac Seumalo. Pittsburgh made 10 draft selections.

### Working fantasy hypotheses

These are projections to test, not established 2026 outcomes:

- Rodgers's fantasy value depends on the pass volume and structure McCarthy and Angelichio build; the reunion points to a pass-forward lean, but that must be charted, and age/durability is a live risk.
- DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. give Rodgers two established target earners; the split between them is the key receiver question.
- The backfield (Warren, Johnson, Dowdle) is a committee to chart, not a settled lead-back situation.
- The defense should remain strong off the edge with Watt and Highsmith, but the reworked secondary's coverage identity under Graham is unestablished.

## Offensive identity

### Confirmed changes

The offense turns over at the top — new head coach, new coordinator, new offensive position coaches — while adding proven skill talent. Rodgers returns at quarterback under his former Green Bay head coach. Michael Pittman Jr. was acquired by trade to pair with DK Metcalf, Rico Dowdle was added to the backfield, and Pat Freiermuth returns at tight end. The interior line lost Isaac Seumalo and drafted guard Gennings Dunker plus tackle Max Iheanachor.

### Questions to resolve

- Whether McCarthy or Angelichio actually authors and calls the passing game.
- The target split between DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr., and Freiermuth's route and red-zone role.
- The backfield division of early-down and passing-down work among Warren, Johnson, and Dowdle.
- Rodgers's volume, mobility, and durability in a farewell season.
- The interior offensive line after Seumalo's departure.

## Defensive matchup profile

### Confirmed changes

Pittsburgh installed a new defensive coordinator (Patrick Graham for Teryl Austin), retained its elite edge and interior anchors (T.J. Watt, Alex Highsmith, Cameron Heyward), added interior lineman Sebastian Joseph-Day, and reworked the secondary with cornerback Jamel Dean and safeties Jaquan Brisker and Darnell Savage.

### Questions to resolve

- Graham's coverage identity and blitz rate over elite returning edge talent.
- The coverage roles in a reworked secondary and whether it is exploitable early.
- The interior rotation with Joseph-Day added.
- Whether the pass rush plus new secondary translates into more takeaways.

## Coaching and scheme

Mike Tomlin stepped down and Mike McCarthy was hired as head coach in January 2026. The coordinator changes were:

| Unit | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Offense | Arthur Smith | Brian Angelichio |
| Defense | Teryl Austin | Patrick Graham |
| Special teams | Danny Smith | Danny Crossman |

McCarthy is a veteran offensive head coach with his own long play-calling history, so the reported arrangement — Angelichio as coordinator and play-caller — is a specific thing to verify rather than assume. Quarterbacks coach Tom Arth and inside linebackers coach Scott McCurley are points of staff continuity. The complete role-level staff and retention flags are in [staff.csv](coaching-staff/staff.csv); authority and coach profiles are in the [coaching intelligence module](coaching-staff/README.md).
