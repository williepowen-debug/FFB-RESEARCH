# Cleveland Browns 2026 Roster Changes

Cleveland begins a new coaching era with a younger roster, a rebuilt offensive line and receiving corps, and a defense transitioning from Myles Garrett to Jared Verse.

## Headline changes

- The Browns traded Garrett to the Rams for Verse and three future premium picks.
- Joel Bitonio retired; Wyatt Teller and Ethan Pocic departed; Cleveland added Tytus Howard, Elgton Jenkins, Zion Johnson, and first-round tackle Spencer Fano.
- David Njoku and Jerome Ford left, making Harold Fannin Jr. and Quinshon Judkins central fantasy pieces.
- Two of the first three draft choices were receivers KC Concepcion and Denzel Boston.
