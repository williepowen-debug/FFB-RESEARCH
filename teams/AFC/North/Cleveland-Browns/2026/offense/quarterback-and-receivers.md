# Finding: Cleveland's receiving upside is gated by an unresolved quarterback competition

## Scope

- Team/player: Browns quarterbacks and pass catchers
- Area: Week 1 starter, routes, and target hierarchy
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Finding

Deshaun Watson and Shedeur Sanders split the majority of first-team offseason reps and remained the leading Week 1 candidates entering camp. Jerry Jeudy and Cedric Tillman return, but Cleveland invested picks 24 and 39 in KC Concepcion and Denzel Boston after Jeudy fell to 602 yards in 2025. Harold Fannin Jr. also returns from a 72-catch, 731-yard rookie season and no longer shares the top tight-end role with David Njoku.

## Fantasy implication

Fannin and Concepcion offer the most role-created upside, but weekly passing reliability cannot be assumed before the starter and protection quality are known. Jeudy is a rebound candidate rather than an established alpha, while Boston and Tillman compete for boundary work.

## Evidence

- Watson and Sanders split first-team minicamp work; Monken said neither had separated.
- Sanders started seven games as a rookie and threw for 1,400 yards and seven touchdowns; Watson missed all of 2025 after two Achilles procedures.
- Jeudy caught 50 of 106 targets for 602 yards and two touchdowns in 2025.
- Concepcion produced 919 yards and nine touchdowns at Texas A&M; Boston brings boundary size and contested-catch ability.
- Fannin set the Browns rookie reception record and scored six touchdowns in 2025.
