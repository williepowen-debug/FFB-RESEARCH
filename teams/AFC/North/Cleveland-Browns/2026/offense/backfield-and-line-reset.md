# Finding: Judkins leads a young backfield behind a completely rebuilt line

## Scope

- Team/player: Quinshon Judkins, Dylan Sampson, offensive line
- Area: workload, health, and blocking environment
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Finding

Judkins returns from a dislocated ankle and fractured fibula after leading Cleveland with 827 rushing yards and seven scores as a rookie. Sampson established a passing-game role with 271 receiving yards, while Jerome Ford departed. Their line replaces multiple long-term starters with Howard, Elgton Jenkins, Zion Johnson, and first-rounder Spencer Fano.

## Fantasy implication

Judkins has the clearest touchdown and early-down path but carries health and line-cohesion risk. Sampson has PPR utility if his passing-down role survives. The Monken/Switzer run-game background is favorable context, not proof that a new five-man unit will execute immediately.

## Evidence

- Judkins participated fully in OTAs and minicamp after his Week 16 injury.
- Sampson totaled 175 rushing and 271 receiving yards as a rookie.
- Ford signed with Washington.
- Bitonio retired and Teller and Pocic departed; Cleveland added three veteran linemen and drafted Fano No. 9.
