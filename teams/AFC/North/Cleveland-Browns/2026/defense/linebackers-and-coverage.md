# Finding: Schwesinger and Williams form a high-speed linebacker core behind an established secondary

## Scope

- Team/player: Browns linebackers and secondary
- Area: tackle volume, coverage continuity, and new coordinator transition
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Finding

Defensive Rookie of the Year Carson Schwesinger returns after 146 tackles and retains the green-dot profile. Quincy Williams joins him after four straight 100-tackle seasons and reunites with Rutenberg. Behind them, Denzel Ward and Tyson Campbell anchor the corners while Grant Delpit and 100-tackle safety Ronnie Hickman return. That core gives the new coordinator a strong coverage spine despite Martin Emerson's departure.

## Fantasy implication

Schwesinger is the safest Cleveland IDP volume bet. Williams has every-down upside, but two fast, productive linebackers can redistribute tackles. Ward and Campbell plus the club's 2025 pass-defense results keep Cleveland a difficult passing matchup until the new scheme shows otherwise.

## Evidence

- Schwesinger led all rookies with 146 tackles and 11 tackles for loss in 2025.
- Williams recorded 554 tackles over five Jets seasons and earned first-team All-Pro honors in 2023.
- Cleveland finished third in pass defense at 167.2 yards allowed per game and fourth in total defense in 2025.
- Ward has 104 passes defensed since 2018; Campbell recorded 18 in 2025 after arriving in-season.
