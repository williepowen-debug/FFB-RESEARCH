# Finding: Verse preserves a premium edge presence but cannot replicate Garrett's structural effect by assumption

## Scope

- Team/player: Jared Verse and Cleveland defensive front
- Area: pressure, run defense, and protection response
- Season/week: 2026 preseason baseline
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Finding

Cleveland traded reigning Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett after his NFL-record 23-sack season, receiving 25-year-old Jared Verse and first-, second-, and third-round future picks. Verse enters with two Pro Bowls, a Defensive Rookie of the Year award, and 150 pressures through 33 games. He joins Mason Graham, Maliek Collins, Alex Wright, and Isaiah McGuire in an attack-style front.

## Fantasy implication

Verse remains a premium IDP edge and Cleveland does not become an automatic weak front. Yet Garrett's extreme sack production and double-team gravity are not transferable. Opposing offenses may have more flexibility until Verse and Graham prove the new pressure distribution.

## Evidence

- Garrett recorded 23 sacks and 33 tackles for loss in 2025.
- Verse produced 12 sacks, five forced fumbles, and 22 tackles for loss across his first two seasons.
- Graham started all 17 games as a rookie; Collins returns from a season-ending 2025 injury.
- The coaching staff says it will retain a penetrating, disruptive front under Rutenberg.
