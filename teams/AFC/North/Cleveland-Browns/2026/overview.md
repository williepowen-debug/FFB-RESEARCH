# Cleveland Browns — 2026 New-Era Overview

- Conference/division: AFC North
- Last verified: 2026-08-13
- Build tier: Core

Cleveland is a genuine reset module: new head coach and coordinators, an unresolved quarterback contest, a five-man offensive-line reconstruction, two premium rookie receivers, and a landmark Garrett-for-Verse trade. The roster is younger and more flexible, but fantasy certainty is scarce outside a few returning role anchors.

## Fantasy-relevant snapshot

- Watson and Sanders lead an unresolved Week 1 quarterback competition.
- Judkins projects as the lead back after returning to full offseason work from a dislocated ankle and fractured fibula.
- Fannin has a strong route-growth path after 72 rookie receptions and Njoku's departure.
- Concepcion and Boston can reshape the receiver hierarchy immediately.
- Verse, Graham, Schwesinger, Williams, Ward, and Delpit preserve defensive talent despite the loss of Garrett and Schwartz.

## Offensive identity

Monken and Switzer bring a run-game foundation and adaptable passing concepts, but the quarterback outcome will define execution. The line has new starters at nearly every position. Judkins and Sampson form a complementary young backfield; Jeudy, Tillman, Concepcion, Boston, and Fannin create more pass-catching options than the 2025 production suggests.

See [`offense/README.md`](offense/README.md), [`quarterback-and-receivers.md`](offense/quarterback-and-receivers.md), and [`backfield-and-line-reset.md`](offense/backfield-and-line-reset.md).

## Defensive matchup profile

Verse is an unusually strong replacement for a traded star, but Garrett's record production and protection gravity leave a real gap. Graham and Collins provide interior strength; Schwesinger and Williams form a fast linebacker pair; the veteran secondary retains a strong recent pass-defense baseline.

See [`defense/README.md`](defense/README.md), [`verse-and-front-transition.md`](defense/verse-and-front-transition.md), and [`linebackers-and-coverage.md`](defense/linebackers-and-coverage.md).

## Coaching and scheme

Monken replaces Stefanski, Switzer replaces Tommy Rees, and Rutenberg replaces Schwartz. Position-coach retention on defense offers some continuity, but both systems must be treated as new until regular-season rates emerge.

See [`coaching-staff/README.md`](coaching-staff/README.md).

## Special teams

Byron Storer replaces Bubba Ventrone as coordinator. Corey Bojorquez and the core specialists return; the unit remains scaffolded at Core tier.
