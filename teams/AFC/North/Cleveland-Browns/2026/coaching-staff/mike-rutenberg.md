# Mike Rutenberg — Cleveland Browns

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Season: first as Browns defensive coordinator
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Rutenberg replaces Jim Schwartz and inherits a defense that finished fourth in total defense in 2025 but traded record-setting pass rusher Myles Garrett. He coordinates an attack-style front led by Jared Verse and an experienced back end.

## Scheme and tendencies

The staff says the front will remain penetrating and disruptive. Rutenberg's recent Atlanta work emphasized pass-game coordination and safety production. Cleveland's retained position coaches should preserve some technique continuity even as calls and coverage structure change.

## Personnel usage

Verse leads an edge group with Alex Wright and Isaiah McGuire. Mason Graham and Maliek Collins anchor the interior. Carson Schwesinger and Quincy Williams project as a fast every-down linebacker pair; Denzel Ward, Tyson Campbell, Grant Delpit, and Ronnie Hickman provide continuity behind them.

## Pace and situational decisions

Track four-man rush rate, safety rotations, slot personnel, and whether Verse receives Garrett-like snap volume. The defense's ability to create short fields is material for a young offense.

## Fantasy implications

- Schwesinger and Williams both have strong tackle-volume paths, though two every-down linebackers can cap each other's ceiling.
- Verse is a high-value IDP edge but should not be projected for Garrett's 23 sacks.
- Cleveland remains a potentially strong matchup defense, with coordinator transition as the risk.

## Statements and reporting

The club describes the intended front as attacking and highlights Rutenberg's previous relationship with Williams. Maintaining 2025 defensive efficiency without Garrett is a hypothesis.

## Reliability cautions

Garrett drove protection plans and pressure production. Even an excellent young replacement changes how offenses block Cleveland, and Rutenberg has no Browns play-calling sample.
