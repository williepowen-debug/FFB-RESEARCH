# Travis Switzer — Cleveland Browns

- Role: Offensive coordinator
- Season: first as NFL offensive coordinator
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Switzer coordinates Monken's offense after serving as Baltimore's run-game coordinator from 2023–2025. He organizes the installation and connects the run game to a new pass-game and line staff; Monken retains the larger offensive authority.

## Scheme and tendencies

Switzer's Baltimore run games led the league across his three-year run in rushing yards per game, yards per carry, and explosive runs. Cleveland's use of Judkins, Sampson, a fullback, and mobile-capable quarterbacks makes run-game structure central, but the rebuilt line and different quarterback profile prevent a clean projection.

## Personnel usage

Judkins is the probable early-down lead; Sampson's 271 receiving yards as a rookie make him the best passing-down complement. The rookie receiver pair gives the staff movable and boundary options, while Fannin can align as a high-volume tight end.

## Pace and situational decisions

Track personnel grouping, play action, quarterback movement, two-minute back usage, and red-zone distribution. Switzer has no independent NFL play-calling sample.

## Fantasy implications

- A functional run game can support Judkins even if quarterback play is uneven.
- Sampson's receiving role may be more stable than his carry share.
- Designed touches for Concepcion could create value independent of conventional target volume.

## Statements and reporting

Monken identified the ability to run the football as the starting point for Switzer's promotion. The staff has installed broad at-the-line adjustment responsibilities for the quarterbacks.

## Reliability cautions

Switzer's résumé is closely tied to Monken and Baltimore. Treat all Cleveland usage expectations as low-confidence until preseason personnel and regular-season rates are observed.
