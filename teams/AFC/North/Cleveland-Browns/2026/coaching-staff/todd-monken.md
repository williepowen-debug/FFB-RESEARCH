# Todd Monken — Cleveland Browns

- Role: Head coach and offensive identity owner
- Season: first as Browns head coach
- Joined team: 2026 (previously Browns OC in 2019)
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Monken replaces Kevin Stefanski and owns the club's offensive installation, quarterback decision, and game-management structure. His first major choice is a Week 1 starter from Deshaun Watson and Shedeur Sanders, with Dillon Gabriel and rookie Taylen Green also in the room.

## Scheme and tendencies

Monken's 2023–2025 Baltimore offenses ranked first in rushing yards and yards per play, second in total yards, and third in points across that span. Cleveland is importing that adaptable run-and-pass framework, but its quarterbacks do not reproduce Lamar Jackson's skill set. The offense should be evaluated as a fresh installation, not a direct Baltimore copy.

## Personnel usage

Quinshon Judkins projects as the lead back, with Dylan Sampson offering receiving and change-of-pace work. Jerry Jeudy and Cedric Tillman return, while first-rounder KC Concepcion and second-rounder Denzel Boston demand early roles. Harold Fannin Jr. is the lead tight end after David Njoku's exit.

## Pace and situational decisions

Track neutral pass rate by starting quarterback, pre-snap adjustment authority, use of fullback Michael Burton, designed quarterback movement, and fourth-down decisions. Monken has coordinator history but no prior NFL head-coaching sample.

## Fantasy implications

- The quarterback competition lowers early confidence in all pass catchers.
- Judkins has a lead-back path but enters a new system after a serious late-2025 leg injury.
- Fannin's route share can grow after Njoku's departure, especially given Monken's tight-end history.

## Statements and reporting

Monken has publicly said Watson and Sanders both earned the chance to continue the competition. He emphasized player development and scheme fit when assembling the staff. A productive Browns offense remains a projection, not an extension of Baltimore's results.

## Reliability cautions

Monken's Baltimore rates were shaped by Jackson and Derrick Henry. Cleveland's quarterback uncertainty, young receivers, and five-man line rebuild make direct historical translation especially fragile.
