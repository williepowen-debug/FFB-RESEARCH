# Cleveland Browns 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. Cleveland's 2026 change is a full coaching regime change: head coach Kevin Stefanski was fired after six seasons (45-56), and offensive play-caller Todd Monken was hired as head coach and installed an all-new coordinator group. A handful of position coaches were retained.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): coordinators and position coaches, with retained-from-2025 flags.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership changes.
- [`todd-monken.md`](todd-monken.md): first-time head coach with a play-caller reputation; the offensive identity.
- [`travis-switzer.md`](travis-switzer.md): new offensive coordinator; run-game influence under a play-calling head coach.
- [`mike-rutenberg.md`](mike-rutenberg.md): new defensive coordinator; a coverage-oriented new install after the Myles Garrett trade.
