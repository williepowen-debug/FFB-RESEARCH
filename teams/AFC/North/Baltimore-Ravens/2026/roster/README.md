- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): free-agent signings and incoming veterans.

## Headline changes

- Head coach John Harbaugh was dismissed after 18 seasons; Jesse Minter was hired as head coach and installed an all-new, unusually young coordinator group.
- The offensive skill core is continuous — Lamar Jackson, Derrick Henry, Zay Flowers (extended through 2030), Rashod Bateman, and Mark Andrews all return — but under a new play-caller.
- The pass rush was rebuilt through free agency: Baltimore agreed to trade two first-round picks for Maxx Crosby, voided the deal after his failed physical, then signed Trey Hendrickson away from division-rival Cincinnati.
- The interior offensive line was reset by the loss of center Tyler Linderbaum; the tight-end room lost Isaiah Likely and Charlie Kolar.

Colleges and terms are populated where confirmed by the official draft page or reputable trackers; blank or approximate fields mark values not yet verified to source standard. Partial dates (`YYYY-MM`) mark moves whose exact day was not confirmed.
