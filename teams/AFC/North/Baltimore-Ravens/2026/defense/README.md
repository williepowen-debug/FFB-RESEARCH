# Baltimore Ravens 2026 Defensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the defense as a connected system under new coordinator Anthony Weaver, who arrives from Miami with a new position-coach group. Baltimore returns coverage anchors (Kyle Hamilton, Marlon Humphrey, Nate Wiggins) and interior/linebacker cores (Nnamdi Madubuike, Roquan Smith), rebuilt the edge rush through the Trey Hendrickson signing, and reworked the safety group. The unit's coverage and pressure identity is unestablished and must be charted.

## Files

- [`pass-rush-rebuild.md`](pass-rush-rebuild.md): the voided Maxx Crosby trade, the Hendrickson pivot, and edge-rotation and pressure projection under Weaver.
- [`secondary-and-coverage.md`](secondary-and-coverage.md): returning coverage stars, the open safety spot, Kyle Hamilton's alignment, and early exploitability.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable defensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not convert the coordinator change or the Hendrickson signing into a settled scheme, role chart, or pressure projection.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. What coverage and pressure identity does Weaver install?
2. Does Trey Hendrickson deliver premier edge production, and who is the second edge?
3. How is Kyle Hamilton aligned, and what does that do to his IDP profile and the coverage structure?
4. Who wins the starting safety spot next to Hamilton after Alohi Gilman's departure?
5. Is the back end exploitable early before the new coverage identity settles?
