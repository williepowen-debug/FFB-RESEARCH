# Baltimore Ravens 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the offense as a connected system. Baltimore's 2026 story is continuity of personnel under a new play-caller: Lamar Jackson, Derrick Henry, Zay Flowers, Rashod Bateman, and Mark Andrews all return, but the offense changes hands from Todd Monken to Declan Doyle, one of the NFL's youngest coordinators. The tight-end room and interior line also turned over, so the system — not the skill talent — is the central variable.

## Files

- [`receiving-hierarchy.md`](receiving-hierarchy.md): target-hierarchy hypotheses around the extended Zay Flowers, Bateman, and a thinned tight-end room.
- [`backfield-and-new-system.md`](backfield-and-new-system.md): Derrick Henry's lead role, the new-play-caller variable, Jackson's goal-line cap, and the reset interior line.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not convert the coaching change, the Flowers extension, or Doyle's coaching lineage into settled 2026 usage.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. Does Doyle preserve the efficient 2024-2025 offense, or reshape it around his own concepts?
2. Does Lamar Jackson's rushing floor hold while his passing volume tracks the new system?
3. Is Zay Flowers a clear high-volume WR1, or does a distributed offense cap his share?
4. Does Derrick Henry keep lead-back volume and goal-line access, and how much does Jackson's rushing cap the backfield?
5. Does the interior line stabilize at center and guard after Tyler Linderbaum's departure?
