# Finding: Derrick Henry anchors the backfield, but the new play-caller and Jackson's goal-line rushing frame his ceiling

## Scope

- Unit: running backs and the run-game system
- Area: workload distribution, passing-down usage, and touchdown scripting
- Season: 2026
- Status: active preseason baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Finding

Derrick Henry returns as Baltimore's lead back with Justice Hill in a change-of-pace and passing-down role and rookie Adam Randall added as depth. The backfield's talent and roles are largely continuous, so the fantasy variable is the offensive system: new coordinator Declan Doyle replaces Todd Monken and is the reported play-caller. Henry's high-volume, touchdown-dependent profile is exposed to two things Doyle controls — early-down carry volume and the goal-line split with Lamar Jackson — plus a reset interior offensive line after center Tyler Linderbaum's departure.

## Evidence

### Facts

- Derrick Henry and Justice Hill both return; rookie Adam Randall (Round 5, Clemson) adds depth.
- Declan Doyle, hired 2026-02-02, designs and is reported to call the offense in place of Monken (who left for Cleveland).
- Center Tyler Linderbaum departed for Las Vegas (three years, $81M); Baltimore signed guard John Simpson and drafted first-round guard Vega Ioane, resetting the interior line in front of the run game.
- Lamar Jackson remains the quarterback; his designed and scramble rushing has historically competed with the running backs for short-yardage and goal-line work.

### Inferences

- Henry's touchdown ceiling is the most script-dependent part of his value; a new play-caller could raise or trim his early-down volume and goal-line carries.
- Jackson's goal-line rushing is a persistent cap on backfield touchdown upside and should be treated as a structural factor, not a week-to-week accident.
- The interior-line reset introduces run-blocking uncertainty until the new center and guards settle.
- Justice Hill's passing-down role is the clearest secondary source of back value, and Doyle's usage is the variable.

## Fantasy implication

- Henry remains a priority back on volume and goal-line access, but his touchdown-heavy profile carries more scripting risk under a new play-caller than his talent alone implies.
- Justice Hill holds standalone and contingency value tied to passing-down snaps; monitor whether Doyle expands or trims that role.
- The interior-line reset is worth watching for early-season run efficiency; a shaky front would compress Henry's yardage floor.

## Assessment

- Confidence: low
- Reason: the backfield roles are known, but 2026 usage under a new play-caller and behind a reset interior line has not been charted.
- Fact/inference boundary: the personnel, the coordinator change, and the line turnover are facts; the workload and touchdown projections are inference.
- What would invalidate this: preseason and early-season usage showing a clearly defined early-down and goal-line split, or an interior line that clearly stabilizes or struggles.
- Next review: first two preseason games and the first goal-line and short-yardage sequences.
