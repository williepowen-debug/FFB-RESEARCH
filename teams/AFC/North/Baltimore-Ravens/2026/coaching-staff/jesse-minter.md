# Jesse Minter — Baltimore Ravens

- Role: Head coach (first-time NFL head coach); defensive background
- Joined team: 2026 (previously a Ravens defensive assistant, 2017–2020)
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Minter controls whole-team standards, staff structure, game management, and the defensive identity he was hired to install. He was hired as the fourth head coach in franchise history on 2026-01-22, replacing John Harbaugh, who was dismissed on 2026-01-06 after 18 seasons and an 8-9 finish. General manager Eric DeCosta remains, so the football-operations spine is continuous even though the coaching staff was rebuilt.

This is a first-time head coach hire from the defensive side. Minter delegates offensive play-calling to new coordinator Declan Doyle and is expected to be closely involved with the defense alongside coordinator Anthony Weaver.

## Scheme and tendencies

Minter's background is defensive: a Ravens defensive assistant under the previous regime (2017–2020), then a college defensive coordinator, then an NFL defensive coordinator. The reasonable prior is a multiple, disguise-oriented defense that varies coverage and pressure by situation, but his exact structural preferences as a play-caller-turned-head-coach in Baltimore must be charted from preseason and early-season evidence rather than assumed.

On offense, Minter is not the designer; Doyle is. Any 2026 offensive identity should be attributed to Doyle's system, not to the head coach.

## Personnel usage

Minter inherits an established offensive skill core (Lamar Jackson, Derrick Henry, Zay Flowers, Rashod Bateman, Mark Andrews) and a defense he helped reshape through free agency, headlined by edge rusher Trey Hendrickson. His personnel fingerprints will show first on defense — snap distribution in the front seven and secondary — while the offensive personnel plan runs through Doyle.
