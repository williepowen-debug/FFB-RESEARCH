# Anthony Weaver — Baltimore Ravens

- Role: Defensive coordinator
- Joined team: 2026 (defensive coordinator, Miami Dolphins, 2024–2025); previously a Ravens defensive assistant
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Weaver runs the 2026 Ravens defense, replacing Zach Orr, who was not retained in the coaching change. Hired on 2026-02-02, Weaver returns to Baltimore, where he previously coached on defense, after coordinating the Miami Dolphins' defense in 2024–2025. He works closely with defensive-minded head coach Jesse Minter, so defensive authority is effectively shared between a first-time head coach and an experienced coordinator.

## Scheme and tendencies

Weaver's history is with multiple fronts and a physical, pressure-capable defense. The 2026 Ravens defense should be treated as a new install rather than a continuation of the 2025 unit: new coordinator, new position coaches, a reshaped pass rush, and a partly rebuilt back end. The specific coverage shell mix (single-high versus split-safety), pressure rate, and front structure are hypotheses until preseason and early-season charting confirm them.

## Personnel usage

Weaver inherits a front anchored by the newly signed Trey Hendrickson at edge and returning core players including Nnamdi Madubuike inside, Roquan Smith at linebacker, and a secondary led by Kyle Hamilton, Marlon Humphrey, and Nate Wiggins. The open questions are the second edge and interior rotation, the starting safety spot next to Hamilton after Alohi Gilman's departure, and how aggressively Weaver deploys Hamilton in the box versus deep. Snap distribution in the front seven is the most fantasy-relevant IDP variable.

## Pace and situational decisions

No Baltimore sample under Weaver exists yet. Track blitz rate, sub-package usage, and how the defense holds up on third down and in the red zone. Pairing a proven pass rusher with a disguise-oriented staff is the bet; production must still be observed.
