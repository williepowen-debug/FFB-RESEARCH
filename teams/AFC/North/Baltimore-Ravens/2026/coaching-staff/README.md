# Baltimore Ravens 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. Baltimore's 2026 change is a full coaching regime change: head coach John Harbaugh was dismissed after 18 seasons, and Jesse Minter was hired to lead an all-new, unusually young coordinator group. General manager Eric DeCosta remains, so the front office is continuous while the coaching staff was rebuilt.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): coordinators and position coaches, with retained-from-2025 flags.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership changes.
- [`jesse-minter.md`](jesse-minter.md): first-time head coach, defensive background, and the offense-by-delegation structure.
- [`declan-doyle.md`](declan-doyle.md): new offensive coordinator and expected play-caller; the biggest offensive variable of the season.
- [`anthony-weaver.md`](anthony-weaver.md): new defensive coordinator arriving from Miami; the rebuilt front and secondary.
