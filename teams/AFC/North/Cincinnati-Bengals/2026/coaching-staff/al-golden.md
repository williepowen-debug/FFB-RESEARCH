# Al Golden — Cincinnati Bengals

- Role: Defensive coordinator (retained for a second season)
- Joined team: 2025
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Golden runs the Cincinnati defense and was retained despite a 2025 unit that finished near the bottom of the league in yards and points allowed. His retention was the central, and reportedly debated, decision of the Bengals' continuity offseason. He now leads a heavily reworked front seven.

## Scheme and tendencies

Golden's defense should be read as scheme continuity with a major personnel upgrade up front. The 2026 questions are whether new interior anchor Dexter Lawrence and edge additions Boye Mafe and Jonathan Allen let Golden generate pressure without over-blitzing, and whether the coverage and back-end problems from 2025 improve. The specific coverage-shell mix and pressure rate are hypotheses to chart, but the coordinator and system are unchanged.

## Personnel usage

Golden inherits a front rebuilt around Lawrence inside and Mafe off the edge after losing franchise sack leader Trey Hendrickson, plus a secondary that lost safety Geno Stone and added Bryan Cook alongside Jordan Battle and Daxton Hill. How he deploys Lawrence and distributes the pass rush is the most fantasy-relevant defensive variable.

## Pace and situational decisions

Track third-down and red-zone defense, blitz rate, and explosive-play prevention — the areas where the 2025 unit struggled. A better front should help, but the coordinator's situational tendencies are the same ones that produced a poor 2025 result.

## Fantasy implications

- The rebuilt front (Lawrence, Mafe, Allen) raises the pass-rush and IDP ceiling if the pieces cohere.
- Cincinnati's defense/special-teams outlook depends on whether better personnel offsets the coverage issues that sank the 2025 unit.
- Opposing offenses may still find early-season value against the back end until it proves improved.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: Golden was retained as defensive coordinator for 2026. Analytical: "same coordinator, upgraded front, unproven back end" is the working frame; the personnel improved more than the coaching did.

## Reliability cautions

A retained coordinator coming off a bottom-tier defensive season is a bet that better players fix the unit. Do not assume improvement from the personnel alone; verify pressure, coverage, and explosive-play rates from live evidence.
