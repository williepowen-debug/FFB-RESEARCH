# Dan Pitcher — Cincinnati Bengals

- Role: Offensive coordinator (retained)
- Joined team: 2016 (offensive coordinator since 2024)
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Pitcher returns as offensive coordinator in a fully retained staff. He works within Zac Taylor's offense, which Taylor play-calls, contributing to game-planning, quarterback detail, and passing-game structure. His retention preserves continuity for Joe Burrow and the passing game.

## Scheme and tendencies

The Bengals' offense is a timing-based spread passing attack that concentrates volume on its top receivers and leans on Burrow's accuracy and quick decision-making. With the staff and scheme intact, 2026 should look schematically like 2025: pass-first, 11-personnel-heavy, with the run game in a complementary role behind a lead back. There is no coordinator-driven identity change to project.

## Personnel usage

The system funnels targets to Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins, uses the running back (Chase Brown) in both phases, and now must resolve a tight-end role after Noah Fant's departure. Pitcher's continuity means the target hierarchy is among the most predictable in the division at the top.

## Pace and situational decisions

Track early-down pass rate, no-huddle usage, and red-zone personnel — all expected to resemble 2025. The main variable is whether an improved defense yields more neutral or ahead game scripts, which could modestly rebalance run and pass.

## Fantasy implications

- Continuity supports confident volume projections for Chase and Higgins when Burrow is healthy.
- The tight-end role is an open, lower-priority target-share question after Fant left.
- Chase Brown's dual-phase usage is the clearest running-back value, tied to game script.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: Pitcher was retained as offensive coordinator for 2026. Analytical: "scheme continuity" is well supported; the offense's shape is known and stable.

## Reliability cautions

A retained system is predictable, but personnel changes (tight end, offensive-line health) and Burrow's availability can still move outcomes. Separate the stable design from week-to-week health and script.
