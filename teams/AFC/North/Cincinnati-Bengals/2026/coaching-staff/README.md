# Cincinnati Bengals 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. Cincinnati's 2026 decision was continuity: after a 6-11 season, head coach Zac Taylor and personnel lead Duke Tobin were retained, and Taylor kept his entire coordinator group — a choice reportedly questioned by franchise players given a defense that finished near the bottom of the league.
