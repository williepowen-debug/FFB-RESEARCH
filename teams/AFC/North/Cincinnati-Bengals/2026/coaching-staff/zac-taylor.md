# Zac Taylor — Cincinnati Bengals

- Role: Head coach (eighth season); offensive background and offensive play-caller
- Joined team: 2019
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Taylor controls whole-team standards, staff management, game management, and the offensive system he play-calls. After a 6-11 finish in 2025, Cincinnati retained Taylor along with personnel lead Duke Tobin, and Taylor publicly stated he would not make coaching-staff changes for 2026 — keeping offensive coordinator Dan Pitcher, defensive coordinator Al Golden, and special-teams coordinator Darrin Simmons. This is a continuity decision under scrutiny, reportedly questioned by franchise players after a season in which the defense finished near the bottom of the league.

## Scheme and tendencies

Taylor's offense is a modern spread passing system built around Joe Burrow's timing and accuracy and the Ja'Marr Chase–Tee Higgins receiver duo. Because the staff and system return intact, the reasonable prior is offensive continuity: heavy 11-personnel, quick game and play-action off Burrow's rhythm, and target concentration on the top two receivers. The variable is not scheme change but execution, health, and whether the defense's rebuild lets the offense play with more favorable game scripts.

## Personnel usage

Taylor concentrates targets on Chase and Higgins and has built the run game around a lead back (Chase Brown). The 2026 personnel are largely continuous on offense, so his usage patterns are the most stable in the division. The open questions are the tight-end role after Noah Fant's departure and the backup-quarterback situation behind Burrow.

## Pace and situational decisions

Taylor has a long head-coaching sample. Track red-zone efficiency, fourth-down decisions, and whether an improved defensive front changes his in-game aggressiveness. His teams have historically leaned on the passing game and can play from behind, which supports pass-catcher volume but can compress the run game.

## Fantasy implications

- The offense is one of the most continuous and pass-heavy in the league, supporting stable projections for Chase and Higgins and for Burrow when healthy.
- Burrow's health is the single biggest swing factor for the entire Cincinnati offense; his availability drives the receivers' ceilings.
- Chase Brown's workload and the tight-end vacancy are the secondary usage questions.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: Cincinnati retained Taylor and Tobin for 2026, and Taylor said he would keep his coordinators and staff. Analytical: "offensive continuity, defense-dependent ceiling" is the working frame — the offense's identity is known; the season hinges on the rebuilt defense and Burrow's health.

## Reliability cautions

Retaining a staff after a losing season is a bet on continuity that in-season results will test, particularly on defense. Public offseason confidence is not on-field production. Watch whether the retained defensive staff improves with new personnel.
