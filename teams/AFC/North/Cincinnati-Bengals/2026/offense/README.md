# Cincinnati Bengals 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the offense as a connected system. Cincinnati's 2026 story on offense is continuity: the same head coach and play-caller (Zac Taylor), the same coordinator (Dan Pitcher), and the same core of Joe Burrow, Ja'Marr Chase, Tee Higgins, and Chase Brown. The only notable subtraction is tight end Noah Fant. The variables are health and script, not scheme.

## Files

- [`receiving-hierarchy.md`](receiving-hierarchy.md): the Chase–Higgins concentration, the tight-end vacancy, and the offense's dependency on Burrow's health.
- [`backfield-and-chase-brown.md`](backfield-and-chase-brown.md): Chase Brown's lead role, passing-down usage, and game-script dependency.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They treat the offense's stability as a strength but do not assume Burrow's availability or a specific week-to-week target split.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. Does Burrow stay healthy — the single biggest swing factor for the whole offense?
2. How do Chase and Higgins split targets, and does either separate as the clear alpha?
3. Does Chase Brown hold a clear dual-phase lead role, and does game script lift his workload?
4. Who fills the pass-catching tight-end role after Noah Fant?
