# Cincinnati Bengals 2026 Defensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the defense as a connected system under retained coordinator Al Golden, whose 2025 unit finished near the bottom of the league. Cincinnati's response was a personnel overhaul rather than a coaching change: it lost franchise sack leader Trey Hendrickson, traded a first-round pick for defensive tackle Dexter Lawrence, and signed edge Boye Mafe and interior lineman Jonathan Allen, while reworking the safety group. Whether upgraded talent lifts the unit under the same coordinator is the season's central defensive question.

## Files

- [`defensive-line-overhaul.md`](defensive-line-overhaul.md): the Hendrickson departure, the Dexter Lawrence trade, the Mafe/Allen signings, and pressure projection.
- [`secondary-and-coverage.md`](secondary-and-coverage.md): the reworked safety group, the coverage questions, and early exploitability.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable defensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not assume the upgraded personnel fixes a unit that struggled in 2025 under the same coordinator.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. Does the rebuilt front generate pressure without heavy blitzing?
2. Is Dexter Lawrence the disruptive interior anchor the trade cost implies?
3. Does Boye Mafe adequately replace Hendrickson's edge production?
4. Can retained coordinator Al Golden convert upgraded talent into a better defense?
5. Is the reworked secondary exploitable early after a poor 2025?
