# Cincinnati Bengals 2026 Roster Changes

Machine-readable transaction data supporting the 2026 module. Update status fields rather than deleting historical rows.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): exits and outgoing free agents.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): free-agent signings and the incoming Dexter Lawrence trade.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): re-signings and extensions (intentionally short — Cincinnati's 2026 story is additions and departures, not re-signings).
- [`draft-class.csv`](draft-class.csv): all seven selections in the 2026 draft.

## Headline changes

- Zac Taylor retained his entire coaching staff after a 6-11 season, including defensive coordinator Al Golden.
- The offensive core is fully continuous: Joe Burrow, Ja'Marr Chase, Tee Higgins, and Chase Brown all return.
- The defense was aggressively rebuilt in the trenches: Cincinnati lost franchise sack leader Trey Hendrickson to division-rival Baltimore, then traded its No. 10 overall pick to the New York Giants for star defensive tackle Dexter Lawrence and signed edge Boye Mafe and interior lineman Jonathan Allen.
- The Bengals entered the draft without a first-round pick (spent on Lawrence) and used all seven selections outside Round 1, weighted toward defense.

Colleges and terms are populated where confirmed by the official draft page, the ESPN tracker, or reputable recaps; approximate or reported fields are marked. Partial dates (`YYYY-MM`) mark moves whose exact day was not confirmed.
