# Mike Vrabel — New England Patriots

- Role: Head coach
- Joined team: 2025 (16th head coach in franchise history)
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Vrabel enters his second season having led New England to a 2025 Super Bowl appearance (a loss to Seattle) in his first year, with Drake Maye finishing as an MVP runner-up. He controls whole-team standards, staff structure, and game management, and holds strong influence over roster construction. Offensive coordinator Josh McDaniels and special-teams coordinator Jeremy Springer return; the notable staff change is on defense, where Zac Kuhr becomes defensive coordinator and 2025 coordinator Terrell Williams moves to assistant head coach.

This is a continuity-and-ascension situation, not a rebuild: the head coach, quarterback, and offensive play-caller all return, and the front office spent aggressively to build around Maye.

## Scheme and tendencies

Vrabel's teams are associated with physical, situationally disciplined football and an aggressive, multiple defense. With McDaniels running the offense, the offensive scheme is a veteran McDaniels system tailored to Maye's mobility and arm. The 2026 change to watch is Kuhr's defensive installation relative to Williams's 2025 unit; treat any specific front or pressure label as a hypothesis until charted.

## Personnel usage

The offseason was built around Maye: the Patriots signed receiver Romeo Doubs, traded for alpha receiver A.J. Brown, added guard Alijah Vera-Tucker, and drafted tackle Caleb Lomu in the first round to upgrade protection and weapons. The backfield remains a Rhamondre Stevenson–TreVeyon Henderson tandem. On defense, edge Dre'Mont Jones and safety Kevin Byard headline the additions.

## Pace and situational decisions

Vrabel has an established reputation for fourth-down aggression and situational sharpness. With a Super Bowl run already logged, the 2026 questions are whether the offense takes another step with upgraded weapons and whether the new defensive coordinator maintains the unit's level.
