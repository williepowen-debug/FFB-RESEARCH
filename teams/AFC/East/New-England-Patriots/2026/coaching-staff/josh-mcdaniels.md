# Josh McDaniels — New England Patriots

- Role: Offensive coordinator and play-caller
- Joined team: 2025 (returned to New England under Mike Vrabel)
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

McDaniels returns for a second season as offensive coordinator and play-caller, having overseen the 2025 offense that reached the Super Bowl with Drake Maye. The system carries forward with a materially upgraded personnel group: A.J. Brown and Romeo Doubs at receiver, Alijah Vera-Tucker and first-round tackle Caleb Lomu on the line, and a returning Stevenson/Henderson backfield.

## Scheme and tendencies

McDaniels runs a veteran, multiple, personnel-driven pro system with heavy situational packaging and play-action. With a proven alpha receiver now in the building, the specific question is whether the offense concentrates volume through A.J. Brown or continues distributing across Doubs, the slot, and the backfield. His history includes both high-concentration and distributed passing offenses depending on personnel; charting is required.

## Personnel usage

Watch McDaniels's target distribution (Brown vs. a spread approach), the Stevenson/Henderson early-down and passing-down split, and how quickly rookie protection (Lomu) and the new interior line settle after the Garrett Bradbury trade. Maye's mobility should continue to support movement and play-action concepts.

## Pace and situational decisions

McDaniels's red-zone and third-down menus are the areas where an alpha receiver most changes outcomes. Track whether Brown draws defined red-zone and third-down targets, which would raise the offense's scoring efficiency and Maye's touchdown projection.

## Fantasy implications

- A.J. Brown's arrival gives the offense a defined No. 1 target for the first time in this era, which supports both his own projection and Maye's ceiling.
- The backfield timeshare is the main drag on running-back projections; McDaniels's usage split is the deciding variable.
- Tight end Hunter Henry's role and rookie Eli Raridon's development are secondary passing-game questions.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: McDaniels returns as offensive coordinator and play-caller; the Patriots added A.J. Brown, Doubs, Vera-Tucker, and Lomu. Analytical: whether the offense concentrates targets on Brown is the key structural question, pending charting.

## Reliability cautions

A continuous scheme with new personnel can still change its distribution and tempo. Do not assume 2025 usage patterns carry over now that the weapon hierarchy has changed. Establish target and backfield splits from preseason and early-season charting.
