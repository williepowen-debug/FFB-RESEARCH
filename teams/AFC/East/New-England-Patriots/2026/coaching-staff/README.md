# New England Patriots 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. New England's 2026 situation is continuity and ascension: head coach Mike Vrabel returns for year two after a 2025 Super Bowl appearance, offensive coordinator and play-caller Josh McDaniels returns, and the only coordinator change is on defense, where Zac Kuhr is promoted and Terrell Williams moves to assistant head coach.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): coordinators and key position coaches, with retained-from-2025 flags.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership changes.
- [`mike-vrabel.md`](mike-vrabel.md): head-coach authority, situational identity, and the ascension frame.
- [`josh-mcdaniels.md`](josh-mcdaniels.md): returning offensive coordinator/play-caller and the A.J. Brown target-concentration question.
- [`zac-kuhr.md`](zac-kuhr.md): promoted defensive coordinator inheriting a Super Bowl defense.
