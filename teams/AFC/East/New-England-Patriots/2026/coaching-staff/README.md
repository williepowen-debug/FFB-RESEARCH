# New England Patriots 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. New England's 2026 situation is continuity and ascension: head coach Mike Vrabel returns for year two after a 2025 Super Bowl appearance, offensive coordinator and play-caller Josh McDaniels returns, and the only coordinator change is on defense, where Zak Kuhr was promoted on 2026-03-16 and Terrell Williams moved to assistant head coach. The official promotion analysis states that Kuhr had already been the de facto defensive coordinator and play-caller for the final 20 games of 2025 during Williams's medical leave, so the "new play-caller" label applies to the title, not to the game sample.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): coordinators and key position coaches, with retained-from-2025 flags. Names follow the official coaches roster (Zak Kuhr, Clinton McMillan); `coach_id` values keep their original slugs.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership changes.
- [`mike-vrabel.md`](mike-vrabel.md): head-coach authority, situational identity, and the ascension frame.
- [`josh-mcdaniels.md`](josh-mcdaniels.md): returning offensive coordinator/play-caller and the A.J. Brown target-concentration question.
- [`zac-kuhr.md`](zac-kuhr.md): promoted defensive coordinator (file name keeps the earlier spelling; the record title uses the official "Zak").
