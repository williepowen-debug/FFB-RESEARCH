# Zak Kuhr — New England Patriots

- Role: Defensive coordinator (official spelling "Zak"; the record ID and file name keep the earlier "zac" slug)
- Joined team: 2025 (inside linebackers); promoted to defensive coordinator 2026-03-16
- Last verified: 2026-09-09

## Responsibilities

Kuhr is New England's 2026 defensive coordinator. The official 2026-03-16 announcement states that he "assumed the responsibilities of defensive coordinator when Williams took a medical leave of absence following the Patriots regular-season opener" and "became the team's de facto defensive coordinator and defensive play-caller for the final 20 games of the season," including the postseason run. Terrell Williams remains as assistant head coach, a role Vrabel described as "a strong role on the defense" with emphasis on the front. Vinny DePalma was promoted to inside linebackers coach behind Kuhr, and B.J. Edmonds was added as a defensive assistant. The 2026 change is therefore a title formalizing an in-season succession, not a new voice installing a system.

## Scheme and tendencies

Facts (official promotion analysis, 2026-03-16): in the 2025 playoffs Kuhr's defense blitzed on 44.3% of opponent drop-backs (37.4% pressure rate) and played man coverage 36.8% of the time, versus a 27.6% blitz rate and 29.8% man rate in the regular season, leaning on the Gonzalez–Carlton Davis–Marcus Jones cornerback trio. Inference: the reasonable prior is continuity with the aggressive, multiple 2025 defense, with the open question being whether the playoff blitz-and-man formula becomes the 2026 baseline or a situational package. Assign no fixed 2026 front or pressure rate before charting.

## Personnel usage

Returning cornerstones include cornerback Christian Gonzalez (reported four-year extension, 2026-09-07) and the interior of Christian Barmore and Milton Williams; the additions are Dre'Mont Jones on the edge, Kevin Byard and traded-for Jaylen Reed at safety, drafted edge Gabe Jacas and corner Karon Prunty, and waiver claim Darius Muasau at linebacker. Week 1 status (verified 2026-09-09): Harold Landry III opened on reserve/PUP (at least four games; no practices this season), so the edge rotation is Jones, Elijah Ponder, and Jacas with rookie Quintayvious Hutchins fourth; Barmore carried no Week 1 designation after full practices Monday and Tuesday.
