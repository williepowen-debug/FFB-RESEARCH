# Finding: Stevenson is the projected lead back, but Henderson's dual-threat upside makes it a live timeshare

## Scope

- Unit: running backs
- Area: early-down, passing-down, and goal-line usage split
- Season: 2026
- Status: active preseason baseline
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Finding

New England returns a two-back tandem of Rhamondre Stevenson and TreVeyon Henderson. Stevenson is projected to open as the starter after finishing 2025 strongly, but Henderson led the team in touches, scrimmage yards, and touchdowns as a 2025 rookie and carries clear RB1 upside. Neither back has a locked workhorse role, so this is a genuine timeshare whose resolution — especially passing-down and goal-line work — determines each player's fantasy ceiling.

## Evidence

### Facts

- Stevenson is projected to remain the starting back in 2026; he averaged 5.7 yards per carry over the final eight games of 2025 (including playoffs), second among running backs in that span.
- Henderson led the Patriots in touches (215), scrimmage yards (1,132), and total touchdowns (10) as a rookie in 2025.
- The 2025 tandem combined for roughly 2,080 scrimmage yards, among the top NFL running-back duos.
- Reporting frames Henderson's 2026 breakout path as earning more passing-game and goal-line touches; the "full takeover never came" while Stevenson was active in 2025.
- The offense returns offensive coordinator Josh McDaniels and added interior and tackle help (Vera-Tucker, first-round tackle Caleb Lomu) after trading center Garrett Bradbury.

### Inferences

- Stevenson's late-2025 efficiency and early-down role give him the current lead, but Henderson's receiving and explosive-play profile make him the more valuable back if the passing-down and goal-line share shifts.
- The most fantasy-relevant battleground is third-down and red-zone usage; whoever wins those has the higher ceiling regardless of raw carry counts.
- A first-round tackle and new interior line could raise overall backfield efficiency, benefiting whichever back leads.

## Fantasy implication

- Both backs are startable in the right weeks, but the timeshare caps each one's ceiling until roles clarify.
- Henderson is the higher-upside dynasty and best-ball target given his dual-threat profile; Stevenson is the steadier early-down and short-yardage bet.
- Track preseason and Week 1 passing-down and goal-line snaps as the deciding signal.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: both roles are established as real, but the 2026 split is unresolved.
- Fact/inference boundary: 2025 production and the projected starter designation are facts; the 2026 split projection is inference.
- What would invalidate this: one back seizing a clear three-down role, or a decisive passing-down/goal-line assignment in the preseason.
- Next review: first two preseason games and Week 1 usage charting.
