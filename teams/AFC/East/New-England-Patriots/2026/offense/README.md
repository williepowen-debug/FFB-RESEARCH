# New England Patriots 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the offense as a connected system. New England's 2026 story is ascension: a rising franchise quarterback (Drake Maye, 2025 MVP runner-up) returns with a returning play-caller (Josh McDaniels) and a materially upgraded supporting cast — headlined by the June trade for alpha receiver A.J. Brown and the signing of Romeo Doubs, plus offensive-line investment (Alijah Vera-Tucker, first-round tackle Caleb Lomu).

## Files

- [`receiving-hierarchy.md`](receiving-hierarchy.md): the A.J. Brown-led target hierarchy after the Kayshon Boutte trade.
- [`backfield-timeshare.md`](backfield-timeshare.md): the Rhamondre Stevenson / TreVeyon Henderson split and its passing-down/goal-line battleground; Week 1 is a Henderson-absent sample.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the baseline as of 2026-09-09, the eve of Week 1. They absorb the official cutdown (2026-08-30), the Boutte trade (2026-08-25), the Corey Kiner acquisition (2026-08-28), the Greg Van Roten signing (2026-08-18), and the Week 1 injury designations (Henderson OUT, Ben Brown OUT). They do not convert the A.J. Brown trade or the 2025 Super Bowl run into settled 2026 usage, and no regular-season snap, route, or target data exists yet.
