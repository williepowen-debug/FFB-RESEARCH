# Chris Tabor — Miami Dolphins

- Role: Special-teams coordinator
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-05

## Responsibilities

Tabor controls the kicking operation, punt and kickoff units, returns, coverage, and special-teams player evaluation. His access to the full roster makes him an important voice in bottom-of-roster and game-day-active decisions. Hafley has given the phase substantial dedicated practice time, often at the beginning of sessions.

## Scheme and tendencies

Tabor views returns as chances to score or create the next score and prioritizes tackle-breaking on kick returns and open-field creation on punt returns. Under the dynamic-kickoff rules, he expects more return and coverage opportunities and treats them as meaningful field-position plays.

His recent units have produced strong return and coverage results: Buffalo ranked fourth in kick-return average and eighth in field-goal percentage in 2025 despite using three kickers. His longer record includes productive returners, blocked kicks, and strong specialist seasons, though team context and personnel changed across stops.

## Personnel usage

The immediate decisions are the kicker competition, returner assignments, coverage roles, and the game-day value of bubble players. Tabor has evaluated Jalen Reagor and other receivers as return candidates while emphasizing secure catches and execution over reputation. Bradley Pinion is central to punt placement and field-position work.

Dedicated early-practice periods should create cleaner evaluation tape than inserting special teams between offensive and defensive periods. That makes participation and unit placement meaningful roster evidence.

## Pace and situational decisions

Track kickoff return rate, starting field position, touchback avoidance, punt placement, net punting, operation time, field-goal attempts by distance and hash, and coverage penalties. Also track Hafley's fourth-down decisions because they determine Tabor's opportunity volume.
