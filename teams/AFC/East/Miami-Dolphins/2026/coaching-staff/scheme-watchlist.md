# Finding: Miami Dolphins 2026 Coaching and Scheme Watchlist

## Scope

- Team: Miami Dolphins
- Area: coaching authority, offensive design, defensive structure, and special teams
- Season: 2026 preseason and early regular season
- Status: active measurement plan
- Last verified: 2026-08-05

## Finding

Miami has documented authority lines and stated design principles, but not enough game evidence to assign stable 2026 scheme rates. The highest-value next step is to measure whether Slowik's offense, the Hafley–Duggan defense, and Tabor's special teams match those intentions.

## Fantasy implication

The module should change projections only after the measurements change role, volume, or efficiency expectations. The most sensitive variables are Achane's route/target share, Willis's designed rushing, receiver route concentration, pressure protection, defensive package concentration, and return/kicker winners.

## Evidence

- Slowik has confirmed a shared Shanahan-family foundation, strong run-game belief, precision passing, and a commitment to extensive motion.
- Hafley has confirmed that he will call the defense while Duggan builds and coordinates the unit.
- Hafley and Duggan have described multiple fronts, mixed coverages, situational pressure, and player-driven adaptation.
- Miami mandates takeaway work at the beginning of defensive individual periods and defensive meetings.
- Tabor has substantial dedicated practice time and is evaluating returns, coverage, kicking, and field position.
