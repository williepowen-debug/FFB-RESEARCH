# Sean Duggan — Miami Dolphins

- Role: Defensive coordinator; Hafley remains the defensive play-caller
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-05

## Responsibilities

Duggan translates Hafley's defensive vision into construction, installation, meetings, preparation, and in-game collaboration. Hafley said Duggan's command of the unit would let him function as the overall head coach before taking over the defensive calls. Duggan has prior defensive-coordinator experience at Boston College and a long working relationship with Hafley.

The public evidence supports substantial operational authority but not final call-sheet authority. That distinction should be preserved in matchup analysis.

## Scheme and tendencies

Duggan describes the defense as multiple, adaptable, fast, and physical. The core teaching principle is to simplify complex calls enough for players to execute at full speed. The front must stop the run, remain square, control gaps, and coordinate pressure with coverage.

The menu includes even and odd spacing, situational Wide-9 alignments, press and off coverage, man and zone, four-man rushes, simulated pressure, and heavier pressure. Duggan said blitz decisions should respond to opponent, situation, player strengths, and explosive-play risk rather than a fixed rate.

## Personnel usage

Duggan's linebacker background and 2025 Green Bay work make the Brooks–Dodson–Rodriguez rotation important to watch. Ryan Downard carries Green Bay pass-game continuity, Joe Barry supplies run-game and coordinator experience, and retained line/secondary coaches provide Miami personnel context.

Chart front structure, rush lanes, edge width, linebacker depth, nickel personnel, safety responsibilities, and whether Chris Johnson remains outside or moves inside by package.

## Pace and situational decisions

The unresolved operational question is how Hafley and Duggan communicate between calls and how quickly the unit adjusts within a drive. Track sideline/booth location, headset process, substitution speed, third-down packages, red-zone calls, and post-series adjustments.

## Fantasy implications

- Package concentration will determine whether complementary edge and linebacker players earn usable IDP volume.
- Multiple fronts can change which defender receives one-on-one rush opportunities.
- Man/zone and pressure choice will shape opponent passing efficiency and explosive-play risk.
- Takeaway emphasis may help fantasy defense upside, but role stability and pressure production are more durable inputs.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: Duggan is the defensive coordinator and installation leader; Hafley calls the defense. Confirmed philosophy includes multiple fronts and coverages, situational pressure, run-first front integrity, and player-specific adaptation.

Inference: “Hafley–Duggan defense” accurately describes shared construction but must not imply equal play-calling authority.

## Reliability cautions

Duggan's independent NFL coordinator sample begins in 2026. Green Bay outcomes belong primarily to Hafley's unit and cannot be attributed to Duggan alone. Staff titles do not reveal the complete game-day communication chain.
