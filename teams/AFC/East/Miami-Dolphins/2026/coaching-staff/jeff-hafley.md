# Jeff Hafley — Miami Dolphins

- Role: Head coach; confirmed defensive play-caller
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-05

## Responsibilities

Hafley controls whole-team standards, game management, staff coordination, and the defensive call sheet. He described Sean Duggan as the coordinator who allows him to operate across all three phases until it is time to call the defense. That makes Duggan operationally important without transferring final defensive play-calling authority.

Hafley also has experienced secondary voices: Chris Tabor works across the entire roster through special teams, Bobby Slowik independently controls the offense, and Joe Barry supplies former-coordinator experience on defense.

## Scheme and tendencies

The defense should be described as multiple and personnel-responsive, not assigned one fixed front or coverage label. The stated menu includes four- and three-down fronts, situational Wide-9 alignments, press and off coverage, man and zone, and opponent-specific pressure. Hafley and Duggan repeatedly tied structure to player strengths and situation.

Hafley has made takeaways a process priority. Defensive meetings begin with takeaways, the first two minutes of every defensive individual period must attack the football, and the staff links zone eyes, pass-rush disruption, and pressure to turnover opportunities. Turnover totals remain volatile; the measurable commitment is the practice and coverage process.

His 2024 Green Bay defense improved to fifth in total defense, sixth in scoring defense, fourth in takeaways, and tied eighth in sacks. Green Bay remained top-half in several efficiency categories despite injuries in 2025. Those results are relevant history, not a guarantee for a rebuilt Miami defense.

## Personnel usage

The structure is intended to fit the roster. Front choice, Wide-9 deployment, press usage, and blitz frequency should therefore be charted by opponent, down, distance, personnel, and game state. Chris Johnson's outside/nickel split, the second safety, the complementary edge role, and linebacker packages are the most important early personnel indicators.

No reliable Miami game-management sample exists yet. Track fourth-down choices, timeout use, end-of-half aggression, challenge decisions, and how Hafley balances head-coach duties with defensive sequencing. His decision to retain the defensive call sheet makes communication speed with Duggan a specific early-season watchpoint.
