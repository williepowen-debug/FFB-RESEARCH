# Miami Dolphins 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): current 26-coach directory, including head coach Jeff Hafley and late addition CJ Conrad.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership changes.
- [`staff-influence-map.csv`](staff-influence-map.csv): all coaches, documented connections, likely responsibilities, control level, and fantasy relevance.
- [`scheme-watchlist.md`](scheme-watchlist.md): measurable hypotheses for preseason and early-season verification.
- [`jeff-hafley.md`](jeff-hafley.md): head-coach authority, defensive play-calling, and game-management profile.
- [`bobby-slowik.md`](bobby-slowik.md): offensive design, play-calling history, failure modes, and fantasy watchpoints.
- [`sean-duggan.md`](sean-duggan.md): defensive installation and coordination beneath Hafley.
- [`chris-tabor.md`](chris-tabor.md): kicking, return, coverage, and field-position profile.

## Authority model

| Area | Primary authority | Important supporting voices |
|---|---|---|
| Team | Jeff Hafley | Chris Tabor; Bobby Slowik; Sean Duggan; senior assistants |
| Offense | Bobby Slowik — designer and play-caller | Kevin Patullo; position coaches |
| Defense | Jeff Hafley — play-caller | Sean Duggan — installation and coordination; Joe Barry; position coaches |
| Special teams | Chris Tabor | Brock Olivo; Darius Eubanks |

“Hafley–Duggan defense” is an analytical shorthand, not an official scheme name. It captures the documented split between Hafley's call sheet and Duggan's responsibility for translating the head coach's vision into an installed, prepared unit.

Historical results and public statements establish hypotheses, not Miami's final 2026 identity. Dated observations belong in [`../preseason/evidence-log.csv`](../preseason/evidence-log.csv); current answers belong in [`../preseason/priority-status.csv`](../preseason/priority-status.csv).
