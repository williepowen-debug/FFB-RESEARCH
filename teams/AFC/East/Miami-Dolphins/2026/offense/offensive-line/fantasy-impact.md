# Fantasy impact

## Finding

The line should not be converted into one generic upgrade or downgrade. Run blocking, conventional pass protection, scheme mitigation, quarterback behavior, and replacement quality affect fantasy roles differently.

## Evidence

The unit's projected five, run-concept range, availability risks, and Willis-centered scheme provide the factual inputs. The player effects below are conditional inferences that require charting.

## Fantasy implication

### De'Von Achane

**Upside path:** the line executes multiple concepts, the Paul–Proctor side creates movement, and Willis holds backside pursuit. Achane gains cleaner entries and can sustain volume without needing every run to reach the perimeter.

**Risk path:** interior leakage produces early contact, or Miami uses a larger back for the most condensed short-yardage work. Willis can also capture some designed and improvised rushing touchdowns.

**Track:** yards before contact by concept and direction, inside-10 carries, short-yardage personnel, route share after protection changes, and whether backs release or stay in.

### Malik Willis

**Upside path:** stable protection adds passing volume and explosive attempts to his designed-rushing floor.

**Misleading path:** poor protection produces scrambles that preserve Willis's fantasy score while receivers lose routes and target quality. Good quarterback fantasy output is not sufficient evidence that the offense or line is healthy.

**Track:** designed runs versus scrambles, time to pressure, avoidable sacks, throws from clean pockets, and protection responsibility.

### Wide receivers

**Upside path:** five-man protection allows Miami to release all eligible receivers and sustain intermediate/deep concepts.

**Risk path:** quick interior pressure shortens route depth; tackle problems create chip assignments and condensed formations; max protection removes receivers from the pattern.

**Track:** routes per dropback, average route depth, first-read targets, pressure splits, and target quality with/without extra protection.

### Tight ends

**Upside path:** tackles hold without help and receiving tight ends release freely into the route.

**Risk path:** nominal starters spend high-value passing snaps chipping or staying in. Multiple-tight-end usage can be good for disguise but does not automatically create receiving opportunity.

**Track:** route rate, chip rate, pass-block snaps, alignment, and third-down/two-minute work.

### Ollie Gordon II, Jaylen Wright, and reserve backs

A diverse, physical run menu can create real volume behind Achane. Gordon is especially relevant if duo, power, and short-yardage work becomes a distinct role. Wright benefits more if he is used as a direct Achane replacement in the same concepts.

## Weekly matchup translation

| Opponent trait | Miami vulnerability or answer | Fantasy action |
|---|---|---|
| dominant interior rusher | tests both young guards and compresses Willis's launch point | downgrade deeper passing; monitor Achane outlet/design targets |
| coordinated stunt/twist front | tests five-man communication | raise sack/scramble volatility until exchanges prove reliable |
| elite edge opposite backup tackle | forces chips or faster concepts | downgrade affected perimeter route volume and target depth |
| light box/two-high structure | invites varied run menu | upgrade rushing efficiency if Miami stays patient |
| aggressive man pressure | Willis can escape or punish with designed answers | raise QB rushing ceiling but separate it from pass-catcher outlook |
| disciplined edges against zone/read | reduces cutback and keeper conflict | require interior displacement; lower explosive-run expectation |

## Projection adjustment rules

- Do not move a player materially on one camp rep or one long preseason run.
- Upgrade Achane's rushing efficiency only when success appears across concepts or repeats against starting-caliber fronts.
- Downgrade pass catchers when protection problems change routes and assignments, not merely when the team allows one sack.
- Treat a Brewer absence as an offense-wide event.
- Treat a single guard replacement as more containable if Salyer enters and the other four positions stay stable.
- Separate Willis's personal fantasy floor from the fantasy health of the offense around him.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium on the direction of the causal relationships; low-to-medium on 2026 magnitude.
- What would invalidate this: stable production that does not respond to the predicted lineup or protection changes.
- Next review: first preseason route/protection chart and every material line injury.
