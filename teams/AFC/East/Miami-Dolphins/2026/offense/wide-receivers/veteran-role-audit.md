# Veteran wide-receiver role audit

## Finding

Jalen Tolbert and Tutu Atwell are credible opening starters because their functions fit Miami's current needs, not because either has established a stable NFL lead-receiver floor. Tolbert offers the best X/red-zone case; Atwell offers the best vertical/motion case. Terrace Marshall Jr. and Jalen Reagor need special teams or a clear specialty role to overcome limited recent receiving production.

## Evidence

### Jalen Tolbert

| Season | Games | Receptions | Yards | TD |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2022 | 8 | 2 | 12 | 0 |
| 2023 | 17 | 22 | 268 | 2 |
| 2024 | 17 | 49 | 610 | 7 |
| 2025 | 13 | 18 | 203 | 1 |

- His 2024 season is the room's best recent combination of receptions, yards, and touchdowns.
- He played behind CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens in 2025 and lost standing within Dallas's depth chart; reduced opportunity and performance both require consideration.
- Miami's own analysis emphasizes size, quarterback trust, red-zone utility, and a pre-existing relationship with Willis.
- PFF's July late-round analysis cited spring reporting that Tolbert had separated from the group at X.
- On August 6, he caught a 30-yard back-shoulder pass from Quinn Ewers for the official notebook's biggest passing play.

**Projection:** opening X and high-value boundary candidate.
**Failure mode:** Douglas or Bell proves more dynamic, or Tolbert earns routes without first-read/target value.

### Tutu Atwell

| Season | Games | Receptions | Yards | TD | Yards/catch |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2022 | 13 | 18 | 298 | 1 | 16.6 |
| 2023 | 16 | 39 | 483 | 3 | 12.4 |
| 2024 | 17 | 42 | 562 | 0 | 13.4 |
| 2025 | 10 | 6 | 192 | 1 | 32.0 |

- Career 14.6 yards per reception, including catches of 88, 62, 54, and 50 yards over 2024-25.
- Miami highlighted his motion usage: 31 of his 2024 targets came on plays with pre-snap motion.
- Minicamp produced repeated intermediate receptions, showing a possible route menu beyond clear-outs and deep shots.
- His five-year career has never produced a stable high-volume fantasy season.

**Projection:** starting Z/motion/vertical player whose route value can exceed target volume.
**Failure mode:** package-only use, blocking limitations, or Douglas taking the outside speed role.

### Terrace Marshall Jr.

- Career: 67 catches, 808 yards, and one touchdown in 43 games.
- Best season: 28 catches for 490 yards in 2022.
- He did not record a regular-season catch in 2025 and joined Miami on a reserve/future contract.
- Size and catch radius are useful, but Tolbert, Douglas, and Bell create direct boundary overlap.

**Projection:** outside depth and contested-catch roster competition.
**Roster gate:** special teams or clear separation from the other big receivers.

### Jalen Reagor

- Career: 86 catches, 1,037 yards, and four receiving touchdowns.
- Return history includes 498 kick-return yards with a touchdown and 488 punt-return yards with a touchdown.
- He has not recorded an NFL regular-season reception since 2024.
- Tabor said Miami immediately wanted to evaluate the return skill set that made Reagor attractive as a prospect.

**Projection:** speed and return specialist competition.
**Roster gate:** secure returns, coverage-unit usefulness, and enough offensive versatility to justify overlap with Atwell.

## Comparative role test

| Question | Tolbert | Atwell | Marshall | Reagor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| best evidence for full outside routes | strongest | plausible | weak | weak |
| vertical stress | plausible | strongest | historical | plausible |
| red-zone/catch-point role | strongest | weak | plausible | weak |
| motion/manufactured use | limited evidence | strongest | limited | plausible |
| return value | limited | possible | little evidence | strongest challenger |
| recent stable production | 2024 only | 2023-24 moderate | no | no |

## Fantasy implication

- Tolbert is the veteran to draft first because a full X route share can create targets at every level and in the red zone.
- Atwell is better suited to best ball and opponent-specific props than weekly PPR lineups.
- Do not draft Marshall or Reagor in standard leagues; their first win is making the active roster.
- A Tolbert/Atwell opening start is not proof of target concentration. Washington, Achane, tight ends, and rookies can still fragment the passing tree.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium on functional fit; low on route and target order.
- Fact/inference boundary: career production and attributed usage are facts. Starting jobs, route shares, and roster outcomes are projections.
- What would invalidate this: rookies take first-unit outside work, another veteran wins a core special-teams role, or Miami adds a receiver.
