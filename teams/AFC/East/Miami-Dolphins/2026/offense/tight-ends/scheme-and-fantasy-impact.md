# Tight-end scheme and fantasy impact

## Finding

Miami's personnel supports a flexible 11/12/13-personnel offense in which tight ends can change strength, block from multiple locations, release into play action, and create middle-field answers for Malik Willis. That structure is plausible, not yet measured. Slowik's prior offenses support the possibility of a useful lead receiving tight end but do not prescribe Dulcich's target share or Miami's personnel rates.

The core fantasy tension is straightforward: **multiple tight ends can improve the offense while fragmenting individual routes and targets.**

## Evidence

### Bobby Slowik

Slowik has publicly emphasized:

- a run-game foundation;
- keeping the defense off balance;
- extensive motion;
- precision and quarterback discipline;
- adapting the common Shanahan-tree structure to available personnel.

As Houston's offensive coordinator in 2023 and 2024, lead tight end Dalton Schultz produced:

| Season | Games | Receptions | Yards | Touchdowns |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 15 | 59 | 635 | 5 |
| 2024 | 17 | 53 | 532 | 2 |

That history establishes room for functional tight-end volume. Schultz was an established NFL starter with a different quarterback and receiver group. His totals are a prior, not a projection for Dulcich.

## Causal relationships

### Kacmarek to Dulcich

If Kacmarek handles the hardest attached blocks, Dulcich can align in the slot, motion, or release from a reduced blocking burden. That is the positive-complement path.

If Miami alternates them by play type, defenses can diagnose personnel tendencies and Dulcich loses snaps. That is the fragmented/tipping path.

### Protection to routes

Miami's projected line has tackle-availability and young-guard questions. Tight ends may:

- chip before releasing;
- delay release;
- remain in protection;
- align to help an edge;
- reduce the total number of eligible routes in max protection.

Raw tight-end snap share can therefore rise as receiving opportunity falls.

### Willis to target distribution

Willis's mobility can extend plays and create scramble-drill targets, but it can also replace designed outlets with runs. His limited Green Bay passing sample does not establish how he will distribute full-season volume. Track designed first reads, progression targets, outlets, and scramble-drill targets separately.

### Run identity to target quality

Successful multiple-TE rushing can create:

- linebacker and safety conflict;
- play-action seams and crossers;
- leak routes after blocking action;
- condensed red-zone coverage mismatches.

The passing benefit may appear as target efficiency rather than target volume.

## Fantasy implication

### A. Dulcich route consolidation

- Dulcich leads tight-end routes by a clear margin.
- Kacmarek handles attached work without taking third-down/two-minute routes.
- Miami maintains enough pass volume for six or more targets in favorable games.

**Effect:** Dulcich becomes a volatile but usable TE1/TE2; Kacmarek remains a real-football contributor.

### B. Efficient committee

- Dulcich and Kacmarek both play.
- Sims or Traore enters specialty packages.
- Targets rotate by concept and situation.

**Effect:** offense can function, but no tight end earns reliable weekly volume. Dulcich remains best-ball and matchup oriented.

### C. Protection drain

- tackle or interior problems require tight-end help.
- route release falls despite high snaps.
- Willis scrambles before outlets develop.

**Effect:** downgrade all Miami receiving tight ends; Willis's personal rushing score can conceal the problem.

### D. Kacmarek receiving surprise

- Kacmarek stays in for core pass concepts.
- defenses overplay run and he earns seams, leaks, and zone targets.
- his third-down or red-zone route share grows.

**Effect:** dynasty value rises first. Dulcich can retain a receiving role, but his ceiling falls if Kacmarek takes high-value targets.

### E. Traore development

- Traore earns active status through special teams.
- his movement and vertical skill create a distinct receiving package.
- route detail and assignments improve.

**Effect:** long-term upside emerges, but 2026 targets become more fragmented.

## Market boundary

Fantasy analysts disagree on Dulcich's probability, which is useful. PFF identified him as a sleeper and possible team target leader. Fantasy Life ranked him as a late TE2 and leaned toward the risk that Kacmarek plus a weak passing environment make him complementary. CBS described a top-two team target outcome as possible but still preferred more stable veteran floors.

The repo should preserve that disagreement rather than average it into false certainty. The decisive evidence is Miami deployment.
