## Most important unresolved competitions

1. The three-receiver route hierarchy and alignments.
2. RB2, short-yardage, and goal-line work behind or alongside Achane.
3. Right tackle while Jackson is day-to-day.
4. Uche versus the situational edge group by down and package.
5. Opposite outside corner, nickel, and the second safety spot.
6. Patterson versus Gonzalez.

## Recent changes

- Willis is clearly ahead of the other quarterbacks; his camp has shown both designed-rushing value and passing volatility under pressure.
- Proctor has worked at left guard and produced positive padded reps.
- Malik Washington has been the most consistent early receiver, while Douglas has produced notable red-zone work.
- Robinson is the lead edge; Uche has repeatedly reached the backfield and won one-on-ones.
- Anderson's two reported interceptions have strengthened his safety case.
- Jackson, Salyer, Dulcich, and Lonnie Johnson were described as day-to-day; Konga is out for the season.
