Machine-readable transaction data supporting the 2026 module. Update status fields rather than deleting historical rows.

## Files

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): exits, releases, and outgoing trades.
- [`veteran-additions.csv`](veteran-additions.csv): free-agent signings and incoming trades.
- [`retentions.csv`](retentions.csv): re-signings, extensions, and the Breece Hall franchise tag.

## Headline changes

- Mid-tenure retool under head coach Aaron Glenn (year two) with an overhauled staff and new offensive coordinator Frank Reich.
- Traded All-Pro cornerback Sauce Gardner to Indianapolis (November 2025) for two first-round picks and WR Adonai Mitchell.
- Acquired quarterback Geno Smith (trade) as the 2026 starter and safety Minkah Fitzpatrick (trade, three years, $40M).
- Retained the two offensive anchors: extended Garrett Wilson and franchise-tagged Breece Hall.
- Lost guards Alijah Vera-Tucker (to New England) and John Simpson, downgrading the interior line; drafted edge David Bailey second overall.
