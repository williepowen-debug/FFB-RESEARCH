# New York Jets 2026 Defensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the defense as a connected system under head coach Aaron Glenn (reported play-caller) and new coordinator Brian Duker. The unit was reshaped through subtraction and addition: All-Pro cornerback Sauce Gardner and edge Jermaine Johnson were traded out, while safety Minkah Fitzpatrick and lineman T'Vondre Sweat were traded in and edge David Bailey was drafted second overall. The coverage and pressure identity for this new group is unestablished.

## Files

- [`secondary-and-cornerback.md`](secondary-and-cornerback.md): the Fitzpatrick-anchored back end and the unproven cornerback group after the Gardner trade.
- [`front-seven-and-pass-rush.md`](front-seven-and-pass-rush.md): the rookie franchise edge, veteran interior additions, and pressure projection.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable defensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not convert the personnel turnover into a settled scheme or role chart.
