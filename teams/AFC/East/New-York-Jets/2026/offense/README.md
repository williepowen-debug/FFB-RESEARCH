# New York Jets 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the offense as a connected system. New York's 2026 story is transition: a new offensive coordinator and play-caller (Frank Reich) and a stabilizing veteran quarterback (Geno Smith, acquired by trade) built around the two returning anchors — receiver Garrett Wilson and franchise-tagged running back Breece Hall — with a downgraded interior line and young pass-game rookies.

## Files

- [`receiving-hierarchy.md`](receiving-hierarchy.md): Garrett Wilson as the alpha, the quarterback upgrade, and the unsettled complementary roles.
- [`backfield-breece-hall.md`](backfield-breece-hall.md): the franchise tag, Hall's anchor role, and interior-line risk.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not convert the coordinator change, the Geno Smith trade, or the rookie additions into settled 2026 usage.

1. Does Geno Smith stabilize the passing game relative to the failed 2025 plan?
2. Does a clear No. 2 receiver emerge behind Garrett Wilson?
3. Does the franchise-tagged Breece Hall retain a heavy three-down role, and how does Reich use him in the passing game?
4. Does the rebuilt interior line hold up after the Vera-Tucker and Simpson departures?
5. Does Reich's system improve scoring efficiency?
