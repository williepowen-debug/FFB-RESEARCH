# Frank Reich — New York Jets

- Role: Offensive coordinator and play-caller
- Joined team: 2026
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Reich is the Jets' new offensive coordinator, a veteran hire with prior NFL head-coaching experience brought in to run and call the 2026 offense. He inherits a transition-year unit built around receiver Garrett Wilson and franchise-tagged running back Breece Hall, now led by newly acquired quarterback Geno Smith. Reich has publicly praised Smith's focus and the offense's talent, and early camp reporting notes a growing Smith–Wilson connection.

## Scheme and tendencies

Reich's history is a timing-based, quarterback-friendly pro passing system with structured progressions and play-action. With a competent veteran quarterback in Geno Smith rather than the 2025 experiment, the reasonable prior is a more stable, on-schedule offense. Specific tendencies for this personnel group should be charted rather than imported from Reich's prior stops.

## Personnel usage

Watch how Reich distributes targets around Garrett Wilson, whether rookies Kenyon Sadiq (tight end) and Omar Cooper Jr. (receiver) earn early roles, and how he uses Breece Hall in both the run and pass games. The interior offensive line was downgraded (Vera-Tucker and Simpson departed), so protection and run-blocking are variables that could shape the scheme.

## Pace and situational decisions

Reich calls the plays, so red-zone and third-down design are his fingerprint. Track whether the offense improves its scoring efficiency with a stable quarterback and whether Hall retains passing-down and goal-line work.

## Fantasy implications

- Geno Smith is a stabilizing upgrade for the passing game relative to 2025, which supports Garrett Wilson's target quality.
- Breece Hall is the offense's clearest fantasy anchor; Reich's usage determines his passing-down and goal-line ceiling.
- Rookie Sadiq and Cooper are developmental bets whose roles must be observed before projection.
