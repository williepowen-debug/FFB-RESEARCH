# New York Jets 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. New York's 2026 situation is a mid-tenure retool: head coach Aaron Glenn returns for year two but overhauled his staff (roughly 12 of 22 coaches gone), hired veteran offensive coordinator Frank Reich, and named Brian Duker defensive coordinator while continuing to call the defense himself. General manager Darren Mougey remains.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): coordinators and key position coaches, with retained-from-2025 flags.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership changes.
- [`aaron-glenn.md`](aaron-glenn.md): head-coach authority, defensive play-calling, and the retool frame.
- [`frank-reich.md`](frank-reich.md): new offensive coordinator/play-caller and the Geno Smith-led offense.
- [`brian-duker.md`](brian-duker.md): defensive coordinator installing Glenn's vision over a reworked secondary.
