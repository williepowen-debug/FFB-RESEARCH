Machine-readable transaction data supporting the 2026 module. Update status fields rather than deleting historical rows.

- [`departures.csv`](departures.csv): exits, releases, and outgoing trades.

## Headline changes

- Head coach Sean McDermott was dismissed; offensive coordinator Joe Brady was promoted to head coach.
- Traded for wide receiver DJ Moore; Josh Allen returns at quarterback.
- Added edge Bradley Chubb (from Miami) and reworked the secondary (Gardner-Johnson, Stone, Alford in; Taron Johnson traded, Rapp released).
- Retained interior offensive-line continuity (McGovern re-signed, Torrence extended) while replacing left guard David Edwards through competition.

Colleges are populated where confirmed by the official draft page or reputable recaps; a blank college field marks a value not yet verified to source standard.
