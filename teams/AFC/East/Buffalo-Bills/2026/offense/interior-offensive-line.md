# Finding: Buffalo's interior line enters the season with a rehabbing center and an unresolved left guard

## Scope

- Unit: offensive line, interior (LG-C-RG)
- Area: personnel, availability, and run-blocking continuity
- Season: 2026
- Status: active
- Last verified: 2026-08-20

## Finding

Buffalo intended to open 2026 with interior continuity: Connor McGovern re-signed
through 2029 and O'Cyrus Torrence extended through 2030, with only left guard open
after David Edwards left in free agency. Two things have complicated that.

McGovern injured his lower body on 2026-08-08 during the Return of the Blue & Red
practice — reported as a suspected hamstring strain with a two-to-four-week timeline —
and has not practiced since. He missed the preseason opener, with Lloyd Cushenberry
starting at center. McGovern has said he is targeting the Week 1 opener against
Houston.

The left-guard competition is between incumbent Alec Anderson and free-agent addition
Austin Corbett. Anderson holds first-team status on the first preseason depth chart,
with Corbett second and undrafted rookie Bruno Fina third. This corrects the
2026-08-13 baseline, which framed the competition as Corbett versus Cushenberry;
Cushenberry is a center and was signed as center depth, a role that became load-bearing
once McGovern went down.

Left tackle Dion Dawkins, right guard Torrence, and right tackle Spencer Brown are
settled.

## Evidence

### Facts

- McGovern re-signed 2026-03-10 on a four-year deal through 2029; Torrence signed a
  multi-year extension through 2030.
- McGovern pulled up during the 2026-08-08 Return of the Blue & Red practice and left
  favoring his right leg. Reported timeline: two to four weeks; McGovern's stated goal
  is the Week 1 game against Houston.
- McGovern was listed among players with lower-body injuries in the Bills' 2026-08-13
  training camp report, alongside RB Ty Johnson, CB Maxwell Hairston, and S Cole Bishop.
- Cushenberry signed a one-year deal on 2026-03-26 and has 80 career regular-season
  starts since 2020. He started at center in the 2026-08-15 preseason opener.
- Sedrick Van Pran-Granger is the additional center option and took 48 snaps at center
  in the opener behind the starters.
- Alec Anderson holds first-team left guard on the first preseason depth chart; Corbett
  is second. Corbett signed a one-year deal on 2026-03-26 and started at right guard for
  the Rams' Super Bowl LVI team.
- Rookies took the bulk of non-starter interior snaps in the opener: fourth-rounder
  Jude Bowry at left guard (48 reps) and seventh-rounder Ar'maj Reed-Adams at right
  guard (52 snaps).

### Inferences

- If McGovern makes Week 1, the practical cost is lost preseason continuity with a
  possibly new left guard rather than a lost regular-season starter.
- If McGovern slips past Week 1, Buffalo opens with two new interior starters against
  the league's hardest early running-back schedule, which raises variance for James
  Cook's Weeks 1-5 efficiency without necessarily reducing his workload.
- Anderson leading Corbett suggests the coaching staff values continuity in the scheme
  over the veteran's résumé; a job that is still rotating in late preseason would be the
  bearish signal.

## Fantasy implication

- This is the main run-game variable behind James Cook, and it is a *blocking* risk, not
  a *usage* risk. Cook's touches are not in question; the yards-per-carry environment for
  the first few weeks is.
- Practical rule for the opening month: judge Cook on snap share, touch count, and
  goal-line involvement rather than efficiency. Discount early yards-per-carry.
- Josh Allen's protection is the secondary concern; an unsettled interior against a hard
  early slate is a mild negative for the whole Buffalo passing game in the same window.

## Assessment

- Confidence: medium
- Reason: the injury, timeline, and depth-chart ordering are documented, but neither the
  center's return date nor the left-guard winner is settled.
- Fact/inference boundary: the contracts, the injury and its reported timeline, the
  preseason-opener line configuration, and the depth-chart ordering are facts. The
  run-game impact is inference.
- What would invalidate this: McGovern practicing in full before Week 1 and starting the
  opener alongside a named left guard, which would collapse this to a non-issue; or
  conversely a McGovern setback or an in-season guard rotation.
- Next review: preseason game two (2026-08-22 at Cleveland) and the Week 1 injury report.
