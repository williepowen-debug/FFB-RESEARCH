# Buffalo Bills 2026 Offensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the offense as a connected system. Buffalo's 2026 story is continuity with an upgrade: the offensive designer (Joe Brady) became head coach and reportedly keeps play-calling, Josh Allen returns at quarterback, and the Bills traded for a proven perimeter receiver (DJ Moore). The new variable is offensive coordinator Pete Carmichael's influence on a system that otherwise carries forward.

## Files

- [`receiving-hierarchy.md`](receiving-hierarchy.md): target-hierarchy hypotheses after the DJ Moore trade, in a distributed Brady offense.
- [`backfield-and-cook-role.md`](backfield-and-cook-role.md): James Cook's lead role, the Carmichael usage variable, and the Allen goal-line cap.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable offensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not convert the Brady promotion, the Moore reunion narrative, or Carmichael's reputation into settled 2026 usage.

## Highest-leverage questions

1. Does Brady's retained play-calling preserve the 2025 offense, or do his head-coaching duties and Carmichael's input change it?
2. Does Buffalo concentrate targets on DJ Moore or keep distributing across Moore, Shakir, Coleman, and Kincaid?
3. Does Carmichael expand or trim James Cook's passing-down usage?
4. Does Allen's short-yardage and goal-line rushing continue to cap backfield touchdown upside?
5. Does the interior line stabilize at left guard after David Edwards's departure?
