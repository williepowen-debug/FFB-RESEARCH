## Defense

Defensive order is **not charted at this verification pass** and no ordering should be
inferred from this record. What is established from the roster and coaching records is
the personnel, not the depth order:

- Front seven anchors retained: Ed Oliver, Greg Rousseau, Terrel Bernard, Matt Milano.
- Bradley Chubb added as a projected starting edge opposite Rousseau.
- Secondary substantially reworked: C.J. Gardner-Johnson and Geno Stone signed, Dee
  Alford signed, Taron Johnson traded, Taylor Rapp released, three defensive backs
  drafted. The nickel job vacated by Taron Johnson is open.
- Jim Leonhard replaced Bobby Babich as coordinator; the coverage identity is
  unestablished.

Add an ordered defensive chart only when a published two-deep is verified against
snap-count evidence.
