# Buffalo Bills 2026 Defensive Ecosystem

This directory treats the defense as a connected system under new coordinator Jim Leonhard. Buffalo kept its front-seven anchors (Ed Oliver, Greg Rousseau, Terrel Bernard, Matt Milano) and added edge Bradley Chubb, while substantially reworking the secondary and its position-coach staff. The unit's coverage and pressure identity is unestablished and must be charted.

## Files

- [`secondary-rebuild.md`](secondary-rebuild.md): the reworked back end, the open nickel and second-safety competitions, and early exploitability.
- [`pass-rush.md`](pass-rush.md): Chubb opposite Rousseau, front-seven depth, and pressure projection under Leonhard.
- [`hypotheses.csv`](hypotheses.csv): machine-readable defensive questions with confirming and disconfirming evidence.

## Evidence boundary

These records establish the pre-preseason baseline as of 2026-08-13. They do not convert the coordinator change or the personnel turnover into a settled scheme or role chart.
