# Buffalo Bills 2026 Coaching Intelligence

This directory separates the official staff roster from analysis of authority, scheme, and fantasy relevance. Buffalo's 2026 change is a coaching succession — offensive coordinator Joe Brady was promoted to head coach after Sean McDermott was dismissed — not a front-office reset; general manager Brandon Beane remains.

## Files

- [`staff.csv`](staff.csv): coordinators and key position coaches, with retained-from-2025 flags.
- [`leadership-transition.csv`](leadership-transition.csv): 2025-to-2026 leadership changes.
- [`joe-brady.md`](joe-brady.md): head-coach authority, retained offensive play-calling, and game-management watchpoints.
- [`pete-carmichael.md`](pete-carmichael.md): new offensive coordinator; passing-game and situational influence under a Brady-called offense.
- [`jim-leonhard.md`](jim-leonhard.md): new defensive coordinator; disguise/coverage prior and the rebuilt secondary.

The central fantasy frame is **continuity, not reset**: the offensive designer became the head coach and kept play-calling, and Josh Allen remains the quarterback. The variables to chart are Carmichael's practical influence, whether Brady's expanded duties dilute the offense, and how quickly Leonhard's rebuilt secondary settles.
