# Pete Carmichael — Buffalo Bills

- Role: Offensive coordinator (Brady retains play-calling)
- Joined team: 2026 (from Denver)
- Last verified: 2026-08-13

## Responsibilities

Carmichael is the new offensive coordinator, but head coach Joe Brady is reported to keep offensive play-calling. Carmichael's role is therefore closer to co-architect and in-game process partner than to primary play-caller. He carries a long New Orleans coordinator history — including calling the Saints' offense in 2022–2023 after Sean Payton's departure — and joined Buffalo from the Denver Broncos.

Buffalo is publicly testing whether Carmichael works from the booth or the sideline, planning to try each during the preseason. That logistical question signals his intended influence on in-game adjustments and situational sequencing.

## Scheme and tendencies

Carmichael's background is West Coast dropback passing with detailed progression reads and situational passing concepts. In an offense that remains Brady-led, his likely footprint is on pass-game detail, third-down and red-zone menus, and protection/answer structure rather than a new base identity. The correct frame is "veteran passing-game influence layered onto Brady's system," not a scheme takeover.

## Personnel usage

Watch whether Carmichael's influence shifts James Cook's passing-down usage or the receiver route distribution. His Saints offenses historically fed a clear alpha receiver and used the back in the passing game; whether that translates to a defined Buffalo target hierarchy around DJ Moore is a hypothesis to test, not an established outcome.

## Pace and situational decisions

With Brady calling plays, Carmichael's situational fingerprint will show up indirectly — in the menu of red-zone and third-down concepts and in how quickly Buffalo answers pressure and coverage changes. Chart whether these situational areas change relative to the 2025 Brady offense.

## Fantasy implications

- Carmichael is the single biggest reason not to assume the 2026 offense is a carbon copy of 2025, even with the same play-caller and quarterback.
- A defined-alpha passing structure would concentrate target share (helping Moore); a distributed approach would cap any one receiver.
- Any expansion of back-in-the-passing-game concepts would support James Cook's receiving floor.

## Statements and reporting

Confirmed: Carmichael was hired as offensive coordinator; Brady calls plays; Buffalo is trialing booth vs. sideline placement in the preseason. Analytical: his practical influence is on passing detail and situational menus, pending game evidence.

## Reliability cautions

A coordinator who does not call plays leaves a subtle, hard-to-isolate fingerprint. Do not overstate his influence before charting shows concrete changes, and do not assume his New Orleans tendencies transfer directly into a Brady-controlled system.
