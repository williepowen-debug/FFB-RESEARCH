## Discovery and Admission Boundary

League transaction pages and fantasy-news roundups were used only to identify candidate events.
No candidate enters the evidence batch unless the exact claim is verified against a registered
team source or registered reporter. Repeated versions of one originating report count as one
evidence cluster.

## Materiality Gate

Admit official transactions, starter decisions, injuries with a plausible Week 1 or depth-chart
effect, and measured or directly observed role changes. Exclude generic praise, unchanged
availability, isolated highlights, and speculative fantasy interpretation without new evidence.

records every review/escalate disposition and promotes only conclusions that cross the repository's
