## Closeout Rules

1. After each team's final preseason exposure, resolve a priority only when the named test was
   actually observed; otherwise update `next_test` to roster cuts or Week 1.
2. After final cuts, resolve roster-construction questions or rewrite them as specific active
   regular-season role questions. Do not leave “first cutdown” or “preseason opener” as a live
   trigger.
3. Weekly availability, weather, matchup, and start/sit implications belong in
   `weekly/2026/week-01/`.
4. Stable role or scheme conclusions return to team findings and `hypotheses.csv`; enduring player
   evidence belongs in `players/`.
5. Re-run this audit after final cuts, targeted for 2026-08-31, before declaring preseason closed.

## Unresolved Gate

and injury reports. Prematurely setting rows to `resolved` would erase the exact uncertainty these
