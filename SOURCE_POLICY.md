# Source Policy

This is a public repository.

- Link to original reporting and attribute the reporter or outlet.
- Record publication and verification dates.
- Summarize facts in original language; do not reproduce substantial paywalled or copyrighted text.
- Separate reported facts from interpretation and fantasy implications.
- Note uncertainty, conflicts between sources, and later corrections.
- Do not commit private league information, credentials, personal data, or proprietary paid-source exports.
