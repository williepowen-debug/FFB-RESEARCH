# Research Methodology

## Evidence record

Important findings should include:

- Finding
- Fantasy implication
- Evidence
- Source and link
- Published date
- Verified date
- Applicable season and week
- Confidence
- What would invalidate the conclusion
- Next review date, when time-sensitive

## Fact and inference

Distinguish direct reporting or measured data from interpretation. A source can support a fact without supporting the fantasy conclusion drawn from it.

## Time sensitivity

Mark material as durable, seasonal, or weekly. Reverify injuries, depth charts, roles, transactions, and coach statements before using them in a current decision.

## Confidence

Use `low`, `medium`, or `high`, with a short explanation. Confidence should reflect source quality, corroboration, recency, and the distance between evidence and conclusion.
