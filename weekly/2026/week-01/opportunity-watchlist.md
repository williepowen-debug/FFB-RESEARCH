# Week 1 conditional opportunity watchlist

## Scope

Player claims are frozen to the **September 6, 2026 catch-up**. These are candidates to inspect,
not confirmed workload upgrades, rankings, waiver bids or personalized lineup advice. Each path
requires eligibility first and measured deployment second. Later transactions and game-week injury
reports can change the premise before kickoff.

Use the [matchup drafts](README.md), [September 6 priority board](../../../intelligence/2026/priority/2026-09-06/priority-board.csv)
and [usage worksheet](usage-tracking.md) together. Candidate order below follows the affected
teams, not a ranking. A roster opening does not assign its vacated carries, routes or targets.

## Finding

The strongest new opportunity paths arise from Green Bay's unavailable lead back, Pittsburgh's
backfield trade, Houston's receiver exclusions, New Orleans' reserve-list changes and Arizona's
missing backs. New England and Seattle provide the first scheduled game in which to test a
conditional backfield allocation. Confidence is higher in the documented roster changes than in
any beneficiary's resulting share.

## Fantasy implication

Use this list to prioritize checks before assigning a player a larger role. Passing-down access
matters most to PPR candidates; inside-5 work can matter disproportionately in standard scoring.
Eligibility, incomplete charting and the availability of competing players can defeat either path.
