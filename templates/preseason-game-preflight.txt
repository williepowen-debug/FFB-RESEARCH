## Timing Rules

- Immediate pass: run only for availability, held-out starters, injuries, official usage shocks,
  and clear time-sensitive changes.
- Completion pass: run after official gamebook, participation data, snap or route data, and
  independent postgame reporting have had time to settle.
- If role evidence is unavailable, record the source-access gap and do not assign a full role
  synthesis unless the gap itself is material.
