# Bijan Robinson

- Position: RB
- Current team: Atlanta Falcons
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-17)
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Bijan Robinson is an elite three-down running back: vision, contact balance, and receiving ability that make him a fit as both a wide-zone lead back and a pass-game weapon. His profile matches a Stefanski/Rees wide-zone plus play-action scheme (a Nick Chubb-style workload) but with far more receiving upside than a typical power back.

### Historical usage and production

- 2023 (rookie): 214 carries, 976 rushing yards, 4.6 YPC, 4 rushing TD; 58 receptions on 86 targets, 487 receiving yards, 4 receiving TD (17 games).
- 2024: 304 carries, 1,456 rushing yards, 4.8 YPC, 14 rushing TD; 61 receptions, 431 receiving yards, 1 receiving TD (17 games).
- 2025: 287 carries, 1,478 rushing yards, 5.2 YPC, 7 rushing TD; 79 receptions on 103 targets, 820 receiving yards, 4 receiving TD (17 games). Led the NFL with 2,298 scrimmage yards; 366 touches.
- Career: 51 games, 805 carries, 3,910 rushing yards, 25 rushing TD; 198 receptions, 1,738 receiving yards, 9 receiving TD. Has played all 17 games in each of his three seasons.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Strengths: elite efficiency (career-best 5.2 YPC in 2025), a genuine receiving role, and perfect durability to date. Limitation: volatile rushing-touchdown output (14 in 2024, 7 in 2025), now compounded by sharing goal-line work with Brian Robinson Jr. The main non-teammate risk is the offensive environment (quarterback play and scoring rate).

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Bell-cow and primary passing-down back for the 2026 Falcons, with Brian Robinson Jr. as the short-yardage/goal-line complement. See the team finding: [backfield usage](../../teams/NFC/South/Atlanta-Falcons/2026/offense/backfield-usage.md).

### Coaching/scheme fit

Strong fit in the Kevin Stefanski / Tommy Rees wide-zone, play-action scheme; the staff has framed the run game around a lead back plus a physical closer.

### Health and availability

No durability concerns; has not missed a game in three seasons. He "held in" during 2026 camp awaiting a new contract before signing his extension.

## Fantasy assessment

- Applicable format: all (premium in PPR).
- Range of outcomes: top-2 overall pick and a co-favorite for RB1 overall; his receiving floor makes him one of the safest RB1s in PPR.
- Key upside condition: retaining goal-line work and a functional offense pushes him to a clear RB1 overall.
- Key downside condition: losing passing-down/two-minute usage, a low-scoring offense capping TDs, or Brian Robinson monopolizing the goal line.
- Confidence: high.

## Team history

- Atlanta Falcons (2023-present): first-round pick, No. 8 overall in 2023 out of Texas. In August 2026 he signed a three-year extension worth up to about $75M (roughly $22.25M APY), reported as the highest-paid running back in NFL history, superseding the previously exercised fifth-year option that had covered 2027.

## What would change this assessment

- A change in his passing-down or two-minute role (would erode the PPR floor).
- A run-heavy but low-scoring offense that caps touchdown supply.
- Brian Robinson monopolizing goal-line carries.
- Any change to his durability track record.
