# Kyle Pitts Sr.

- Position: TE
- Current team: Atlanta Falcons
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-17)
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Kyle Pitts Sr. is a rare movement tight end with wide-receiver-caliber athleticism who can line up in-line, in the slot, out wide, or in the backfield. His fit is as a flex/move receiving tight end rather than an in-line blocker; his value is scheme- and quarterback-dependent.

### Historical usage and production

- 2021 (rookie): 68 receptions on 110 targets, 1,026 yards, 1 TD (17 games; Pro Bowl; second rookie tight end ever to reach 1,000 yards).
- 2022: 28 receptions on 59 targets, 356 yards, 2 TD (10 games; injury-shortened).
- 2023: 53 receptions on 90 targets, 667 yards, 3 TD (17 games).
- 2024: 47 receptions on 74 targets, 602 yards, 4 TD (17 games).
- 2025: 88 receptions on 118 targets, 928 yards, 5 TD (17 games; career highs in receptions and targets; second among tight ends in receptions and yards; 10.5 yards per reception).
- Career: 78 games, 284 receptions on 451 targets, 3,579 yards, 15 TD.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Strengths: elite athleticism and a rebuilt volume floor (88 catches in 2025). Limitations: a persistently low touchdown rate (15 in five seasons) and heavy scheme/quarterback dependence; his production dipped from 2022-2024 amid injuries and near-annual quarterback turnover before the 2025 volume rebound (used more on short/intermediate routes, hence the 10.5 YPC).

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

The move/flex receiving tight end for the 2026 Falcons, with Austin Hooper as the in-line/12-personnel complement (not a target competitor). His within-offense target competition is Drake London, not the departed Darnell Mooney. See the team finding: [Kyle Pitts role audit](../../teams/NFC/South/Atlanta-Falcons/2026/offense/tight-ends/kyle-pitts-role-audit.md).

### Coaching/scheme fit

A Stefanski/Rees play-action, multi-tight-end scheme is a genuine tailwind; Stefanski's Cleveland offenses ranked among the league leaders in tight-end targets. The swing factor is the quarterback: 2025 data favored a rhythm pocket passer (a Tua profile) over Penix for Pitts specifically.

### Health and availability

Participating fully in 2026 camp after starting all 17 games in 2025.

## Fantasy assessment

- Applicable format: tight_end_premium and PPR (mid-range/back-end TE1).
- Range of outcomes: a high-floor TE1 with league-winning spike weeks; tilts up if Tua wins and London misses time, down toward streamer if a healthy Penix wins.
- Key upside condition: a spike in red-zone/touchdown usage — the missing element in his profile.
- Key downside condition: continued low TD rate, primarily in-line usage, or a shotgun-heavy offense.
- Confidence: medium.

## Team history

- Atlanta Falcons (2021-present): first-round pick, No. 4 overall in 2021 out of Florida — the highest-drafted tight end in NFL history. Signed a three-year extension (reported at about $54M, roughly $36M guaranteed) in 2026, removing the franchise tag that had been applied for the season.

## What would change this assessment

- A spike in red-zone and touchdown usage.
- A scheme that restores downfield (higher-YPC) usage.
- Quarterback stability (a timing pocket passer helps his efficiency).
- Continued low touchdown rate and target competition keeping him a mid-range TE1.
