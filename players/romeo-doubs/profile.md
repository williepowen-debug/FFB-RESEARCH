# Romeo Doubs

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Doubs is a 6-2, 201-pound outside receiver (about 82 percent of his 2025 snaps out wide,
per team-site analysis) who wins with route detail and separation at the top of routes
rather than speed or size. His production profile is that of a possession and red-zone
receiver: 21 touchdowns in his first four seasons on 320 targets, a career 12.0 yards per
reception, and a 2025 contested-catch rate of 43.3 percent (13 of 30). He converted 41
first downs on 55 catches in 2025, with nearly 80 percent of targets at the first two
levels of the defense.

He was a fourth-round pick (132nd) out of Nevada in 2022 and was never Green Bay's
target leader in a deliberately distributed passing game.

Three patterns matter.

**Volume has been capped at a WR2/WR3 level every season.** 5.2 to 5.6 targets per game
in each of his four years; his career high is 96 targets. He has never had a 60-catch or
800-yard season.

**Yards per reception has risen three straight years** (10.1, 11.4, 13.1, 13.2) — a
slow move from a short-area role toward intermediate work.

**Touchdown rate is his best statistical feature.** 21 touchdowns on 202 receptions
(10.4 percent) and a three-touchdown game in the 2025 40-40 tie with Dallas. That rate
is a function of red-zone usage and is the part of his profile most likely to translate.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Strengths: refined route running, a strong contested-catch and red-zone record, ascending
per-catch production, and a playoff record of producing when targeted heavily.

Limitations: no season of true volume, two concussions in a six-week span in 2024 (Week
12 and the Wild Card game, the second while wearing a Guardian Cap), a 2022 ankle injury
(four games), and a one-game team suspension in October 2024 after he missed two days of
practice over frustration with his target share.

Context: he agreed to terms with New England on 2026-03-10 and signed on 2026-03-12
when the league year opened. Reported terms conflict: ESPN reported a four-year deal
worth up to $80 million; Wikipedia records $68 million; other outlets cite $70 million.
The base and maximum are evidently different figures; this profile records the deal as
four years at roughly $68-70 million base with up to $80 million in maximum value. He
turned 26 on 2026-04-15.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Projected starting outside receiver opposite A.J. Brown. The 53-man receiver room
(2026-08-30) is Brown, Doubs, DeMario Douglas (slot), Mack Hollins, Kyle Williams, and
Efton Chism III. Kayshon Boutte was traded to Houston on 2026-08-25, which removed the
one competitor for outside snaps who had a defined 2025 role; second-year receiver Kyle
Williams had a strong preseason (touchdowns of 69 and 45 yards) and is the vertical
threat the team site names as the reason Boutte was expendable. Doubs carries no
designation on the Week 1 injury report (2026-09-08).

Reiss's March framing is notable: ESPN wrote that the Doubs signing "would presumably
rule out a trade for Eagles WR A.J. Brown at this time." Brown arrived on 2026-06-01
anyway, so Doubs was signed as the presumptive No. 1 and now plays as the No. 2.

The governing hypothesis is `ne-2026-off-q04` (Doubs opens as a stable complementary
starter opposite Brown) in the
[offense hypotheses file](../../teams/AFC/East/New-England-Patriots/2026/offense/hypotheses.csv);
see the [receiving hierarchy finding](../../teams/AFC/East/New-England-Patriots/2026/offense/receiving-hierarchy.md),
which reads the Boutte trade as a cleaner opportunity path for him.

### Coaching/scheme fit

Team-site analysis of the signing described his route menu (go, hitch, out, in-breaking)
and his ability to find soft spots in zone on crossing routes, and framed him as a fit for
McDaniels's intermediate passing game. With Brown drawing coverage attention and Douglas
in the slot, Doubs's likely role is the second outside read with red-zone involvement —
close to his Green Bay usage, in a better offense.

### Health and availability

Available with no designation. Relevant history: two concussions in 2024, a 2022 ankle
injury, and 59 of 68 possible regular-season games played (87 percent).

## Fantasy assessment

- Applicable format: all; his touchdown rate helps in standard, and his reception floor
  is modest in PPR.
- Range of outcomes: a WR3/flex in redraft with a WR2 ceiling. Central expectation is
  roughly 80-100 targets, 55-65 receptions, 700-850 yards, and 5-8 touchdowns if he
  plays a full season — his Green Bay volume in a higher-scoring offense.
- Key upside condition: an injury to Brown, which would make Doubs the primary outside
  receiver in a top offense; or McDaniels distributing targets more evenly than the
  alpha hypothesis expects (which would hurt Brown and help Doubs).
- Key downside condition: Kyle Williams earning vertical-route snaps that cut Doubs
  toward a rotational role, or a concentrated Brown-Henry-Douglas hierarchy leaving him
  fourth in targets.
- Confidence: medium — the starting role is well supported, the target share is not yet
  charted, and his ceiling is structurally capped by Brown.

## What would change this assessment

- Target share over the first four 2026 games: 18 percent or more means the offense is
  spreading beyond Brown and Doubs is a weekly WR2; below 12 percent means he is the
  fourth option.
- Kyle Williams's snap and route share on the outside — the named disconfirming evidence
  for `q04`.
- Red-zone targets relative to Henry: Doubs's touchdown rate only translates if he keeps
  the red-zone role he had in Green Bay.
- Any Brown absence, which would immediately reprice Doubs upward.
- A concussion, given two in 2024; a third documented concussion would raise a
  longer-horizon availability question.
