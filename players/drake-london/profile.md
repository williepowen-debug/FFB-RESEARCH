# Drake London

- Position: WR
- Current team: Atlanta Falcons
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-17)
- Last verified: 2026-08-17
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Drake London is a big-bodied X receiver (6-4, 215) with a contested-catch, target-hog profile. He can align outside or flex inside, and he has functioned as a volume alpha even through unstable quarterback play. He fits as the concentration target in a lower-volume, play-action passing game.

### Historical usage and production

- 2022 (rookie): 72 receptions on 117 targets, 866 yards, 4 TD (17 games).
- 2023: 69 receptions on 110 targets, 905 yards, 2 TD (16 games).
- 2024 (breakout): 100 receptions on 158 targets, 1,271 yards, 9 TD (17 games).
- 2025: 68 receptions on 112 targets, 919 yards, 7 TD (12 games; missed about five with a left-knee PCL sprain).
- Career: 62 games, 309 receptions on 497 targets, 3,961 yards, 22 TD.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Strengths: elite target volume (158 in 2024), contested-catch ability, and a rising touchdown rate. Limitation: he has faced quarterback instability every season and carries a multi-year injury history (the 2025 PCL sprain being the most recent). His ceiling has historically been tethered to quarterback quality more than to his own role.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Unambiguous WR1 and target-share anchor for the 2026 Falcons, with little target competition at the position. See the team finding: [Drake London role audit](../../teams/NFC/South/Atlanta-Falcons/2026/offense/wide-receivers/drake-london-role-audit.md).

### Coaching/scheme fit

A Stefanski/Rees play-action offense with a strong run game should generate clean, high-value looks (including deep play-action shots), though the scheme's lower volume is a mild cap.

### Health and availability

Returned to full participation at the start of 2026 camp after the late-2025 knee injury and an undisclosed spring issue; reported at full strength.

## Fantasy assessment

- Applicable format: all (PPR and standard WR1).
- Range of outcomes: back-end WR1 with league-winner upside; consensus ranking (around WR7) sits ahead of ADP (around WR17 in PPR), making him a value.
- Key upside condition: a healthy, accurate Week 1 starter after years of poor quarterback play.
- Key downside condition: a run-heavier scheme trimming his target volume, or a recurrence of the injury issues.
- Confidence: high.

## Team history

- Atlanta Falcons (2022-present): first-round pick, No. 8 overall in 2022 out of USC. Signed a four-year extension worth about $141M (up to $150M, $100M guaranteed) through 2030, among the top-paid WRs by APY at signing.

## What would change this assessment

- The quarterback resolution (a healthy, accurate starter raises his ceiling toward WR1 overall).
- Confirmation that he is fully past the knee injury.
- A more run-heavy Stefanski scheme that caps volume relative to his 2024 peak.
- Sustained touchdown rate versus the lower marks earlier in his career.
