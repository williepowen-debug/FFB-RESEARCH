# Ashton Jeanty

- Position: RB
- Current team: Las Vegas Raiders
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-26)
- Last verified: 2026-08-26
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Jeanty is a compact, contact-balanced runner with the burst and receiving skill to stay on the
field across situations. His Boise State production and first-round capital support a featured
role rather than a narrow early-down projection. Las Vegas has described him as the backfield's
steady anchor entering his second season.

### Historical usage and production

The Raiders selected Jeanty sixth overall in the 2025 NFL Draft after he finished his Boise State
career with 4,769 rushing yards and 50 rushing touchdowns. His 2024 season produced 2,601 rushing
yards and 29 rushing touchdowns and made him the Heisman Trophy runner-up.

As a rookie, Jeanty started all 17 games and recorded 266 carries for 975 yards and five rushing
touchdowns. He added 55 receptions for 346 yards and five receiving touchdowns, reaching 1,321
scrimmage yards and 10 total touchdowns.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Jeanty's strongest fantasy traits are three-down receiving utility, tackle-breaking ability, and
the draft investment that gives the team reason to build around him. His rookie rushing
efficiency was less dominant than his college profile, and a changed coaching staff and offensive
line create a new 2026 context rather than a simple Year 2 extrapolation.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Jeanty is the projected lead back. Fourth-round rookie Mike Washington Jr. is the main new
complement, with his short-yardage and passing-down use the clearest possible source of role
leakage.

### Coaching/scheme fit

Klint Kubiak's offense gives Jeanty a plausible path to zone-run volume and designed receiving
work. The veteran-rookie quarterback bridge between Kirk Cousins and Fernando Mendoza makes a
stable run game and backfield outlet especially valuable.

### Health and availability

Jeanty left the August 23 practice. On August 25, Kubiak said he was "on the mend" but provided no
diagnosis or timetable. The comment is directionally positive; it does not clear the next-practice
or Week 1 availability checkpoint.
