# Josh Sweat

- Position: Edge defender
- Current team: Arizona Cardinals

- Last verified: 2026-08-26

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Sweat is a long, explosive edge defender who can win with speed and power and has enough run-game
utility for more than a designated-pass-rush role. His fantasy relevance is strongest in
big-play IDP formats, where pressure conversion and snap share matter more than steady tackle
volume.

### Historical usage and production

Philadelphia selected Sweat in the fourth round of the 2018 NFL Draft out of Florida State. He
developed from a rotational rusher into a starter and Pro Bowl edge before joining Arizona in
2025. His first Cardinals season established him as a central pass-rush piece in Nick Rallis'
defense.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Sweat's strengths are first-step burst, length, and the ability to create pressure without being
hidden in a sub-package. His production remains more sack- and pressure-dependent than that of
high-tackle IDP positions, and his 2026 knee rehabilitation makes early snap volume a separate
question from roster activation.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Sweat projects as a leading edge in a front that also includes Zaven Collins, Baron Browning,
Darius Robinson, Walter Nolen III, and other rotational pieces. Arizona needs his pressure to keep
the defense viable without excessive blitz dependence.

### Coaching/scheme fit

Sweat returns to Rallis' defense after producing within it in 2025. That continuity supports role
confidence, while the changed interior rotation makes the unit's overall pressure quality an
early-season test.

### Health and availability

Sweat opened camp on PUP while rehabilitating a knee issue. Arizona activated him on August 24,
clearing the roster checkpoint. Activation alone does not establish full practice participation,
opening-week snap share, or pass-rush effectiveness.
