# Makai Lemon

- Position: WR
- Current team: Philadelphia Eagles
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-26)
- Last verified: 2026-08-26
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Lemon is a 5-foot-11, 192-pound receiver with inside/outside flexibility, separation ability
against man coverage, physical play, and after-catch value. Philadelphia's draft profile and Howie
Roseman's post-draft assessment emphasize versatility rather than a boundary-only archetype. His
college usage also included rushing, return, and occasional passing work, supporting motion and
designed-touch packages.

### Historical usage and production

Lemon won the 2025 Biletnikoff Award and earned first-team Associated Press All-America and
first-team All-Big Ten honors. In his final USC season he started 12 games and recorded 79
receptions for 1,156 yards and 11 touchdowns, plus two rushing touchdowns. His three-year college
receiving line was 137 catches for 2,008 yards and 14 touchdowns.

Philadelphia traded up from pick 23 to pick 20 in the 2026 first round to select him, sending picks
23, 114, and 137 to Dallas and receiving pick 20 plus a 2027 seventh-round pick. He signed his
four-year rookie contract on May 1.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Strengths include alignment flexibility, route separation, competitiveness through contact, and
production after the catch. His special-teams and backfield history gives the offense more ways to
manufacture touches than a conventional outside-only receiver would offer.

The immediate limitation is NFL sample size. Lemon missed roughly two camp weeks with a hamstring
injury, so depth-chart placement and isolated designed plays currently provide more information
than a stable route-rate sample.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Philadelphia's August depth chart lists Lemon with the first receiver line alongside DeVonta Smith
and Dontayvion Wicks. The broader room includes Hollywood Brown, Elijah Moore, Darius Cooper, and
other veteran depth, so the listing does not guarantee an every-down role.

Lemon returned to full participation on August 24 and took part in all team drills. At the August
25 public practice, he aligned out of the backfield during a red-zone team period, caught a Jalen
Hurts pass in the left flat, and scored. That is direct evidence of first-team package and
high-value-touch potential, not yet evidence of full route participation.

### Coaching/scheme fit

Sean Mannion's offense has already tested Lemon in a nontraditional alignment. His college
versatility and the current backfield/red-zone rep fit a motion and manufactured-touch role, while
his first-round capital keeps a conventional starting-receiver ceiling open.

### Health and availability

Lemon injured his hamstring four days into training camp. He was limited in the August 19-20 joint
practices at New England, then returned to full participation on August 24 and remained involved in
competitive team work on August 25. No renewed limitation was reported through August 26.
