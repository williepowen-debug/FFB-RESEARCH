# Mike Evans

- Position: WR
- Current team: San Francisco 49ers
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-26)
- Last verified: 2026-08-26
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Evans is a large boundary receiver whose release skill, catch radius, vertical tracking, and
red-zone leverage have sustained elite touchdown production. His profile supplies San Francisco
with an established outside and contested-catch role rather than another motion-first receiver.

### Historical usage and production

Tampa Bay selected Evans seventh overall in the 2014 NFL Draft. Through 2025 he had played 176
regular-season games and caught 866 passes for 13,052 yards and 108 touchdowns. He opened his
career with 11 consecutive 1,000-yard receiving seasons from 2014 through 2024. An
injury-shortened 2025 produced 30 receptions for 368 yards and three touchdowns in eight games.

Evans earned six Pro Bowl selections and two second-team All-Pro selections before joining San
Francisco.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Evans' durable strengths are boundary isolation, deep-ball tracking, and end-zone winning. Age and
the interrupted 2025 season raise availability and volume questions, while a crowded 49ers room
can make target totals less stable than his red-zone role.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Evans signed a three-year contract with San Francisco on March 12, 2026 and projects as a leading
perimeter receiver. Christian Kirk, Deebo Samuel Sr., Demarcus Robinson, and rookie De'Zhaun
Stribling make the broader route distribution a live question.

### Coaching/scheme fit

Kyle Shanahan gains a true size and boundary specialist for play-action shot plays and red-zone
isolations. Evans' skill set complements the offense's existing middle-of-field and after-catch
options, though scheme diversity can spread weekly targets.

### Health and availability

Evans missed the August 25 practice with groin tightness. The team expected him to return the
following week, but that expectation is not an official Week 1 status or proof of unrestricted
practice participation.
