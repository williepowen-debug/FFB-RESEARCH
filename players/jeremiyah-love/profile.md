# Jeremiyah Love

- Position: RB
- Current team: Arizona Cardinals
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-26)
- Last verified: 2026-08-26
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Love is an explosive runner with receiving utility and the draft capital to project beyond a
specialized rookie role. His acceleration and open-field ability create lead-back upside, while
his first NFL season still requires evidence on pass protection, short yardage, and weekly
workload durability.

### Historical usage and production

Arizona selected Love third overall in the 2026 NFL Draft out of Notre Dame and signed him to a
four-year rookie contract with a fifth-year option. In his first preseason game, he carried 11
times for 58 yards and caught three passes for 14 yards, giving the team an early sample across
rushing and receiving situations.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Love's strongest indicators are top-three draft investment, big-play ability, and three-down
athletic traits. His limitations are an unproven NFL protection/short-yardage sample and an ankle
issue that interrupted the preseason before the depth-chart competition was settled.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

The August 17 depth chart listed Tyler Allgeier ahead of Love, James Conner, and the other backs.
Trey Benson's August 24 waived/injured transaction narrowed the immediate competition. Love's
draft slot and debut usage preserve a lead-back ceiling, but no current evidence makes him a
locked Week 1 starter.

### Coaching/scheme fit

Mike LaFleur and Nathaniel Hackett bring an offense with Shanahan/McVay-family run-game and
play-action roots. Love's burst and receiving flexibility fit that structure, assuming he earns
the protection trust needed for passing-down work.

### Health and availability

Arizona held Love out after he suffered a high ankle sprain following his preseason debut. On
August 24, Mike LaFleur said Love was progressing but would not practice that week, including the
Green Bay joint session, or play in the preseason finale. His return-to-practice date, final
depth-chart placement, and Week 1 status remain open checkpoints.
