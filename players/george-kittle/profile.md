# George Kittle

- Position: TE
- Current team: San Francisco 49ers
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-26)
- Last verified: 2026-08-26
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Kittle is a complete tight end whose blocking keeps him attached to the formation while his speed,
contact balance, and route skill create elite receiving efficiency. His value is not limited to
detached or obvious passing situations, which supports strong route participation when healthy.

### Historical usage and production

San Francisco selected Kittle in the fifth round of the 2017 NFL Draft. Through 2025 he had played
124 regular-season games with 116 starts, catching 595 passes for 8,008 yards and 52 touchdowns.
He started 11 games in 2025 and produced 57 receptions for 628 yards and seven touchdowns.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Kittle's strengths are yards after contact, explosive-play creation, route versatility, and
every-down blocking value. His aggressive style and injury history create recurring availability
risk, and his weekly target volume can fluctuate in a run-oriented offense with multiple weapons.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Kittle remains San Francisco's primary tight end when available. His role security is much clearer
than his opening-week workload following the 2026 Achilles injury.

### Coaching/scheme fit

Kyle Shanahan can use Kittle as both a blocking fulcrum and an explosive middle-of-field target.
That dual value supports a full role after medical clearance, but it also makes blocking workload
part of the return-to-play test rather than routes alone.

### Health and availability

Kittle tore his Achilles on January 11, 2026. San Francisco activated him from PUP on August 23.
He completed a second consecutive practice on August 25 but remained in noncompetitive route work
with quarterbacks and offensive linemen. Competitive team-period progression and Week 1 practice
reports remain unresolved.
