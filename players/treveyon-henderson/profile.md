## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Henderson is a 5-10, 202-pound explosive-play back whose value is breakaway speed and
receiving-down versatility rather than volume. As a 2025 rookie he tied the NFL rookie
record with four rushing touchdowns of 50-plus yards and set the career record with two
games containing two such runs; his 5.1 yards per carry came with a 69-yard long. He
also returned kickoffs (9 returns, 23.2 average). He is a Christian who has spoken
publicly about his faith; that context is biographical only.

He led the 2025 Patriots in touches (215), scrimmage yards (1,132), and total touchdowns
(10) despite starting only four games, and was a finalist for Offensive Rookie of the
Year and AFC Offensive Player of the Month for November.

Three patterns matter.

**Production was front-loaded and explosive-play dependent.** His three signature games
(Week 10 at Tampa Bay, 147 yards and two 50-plus touchdowns; Week 11 vs. the Jets, three
touchdowns; Week 15 vs. Buffalo, two more 50-plus touchdowns) account for most of his
touchdown total. Team-site reporting (2026-09-08) states his snaps decreased as the
season wore on, with Stevenson assuming the lead role down the stretch and in the
playoffs; the playoff line (30 carries, 2.5 per carry) reflects that.

**Receiving usage was real but modest.** 42 targets and 35 receptions in 17 games — 2.5
targets per game — is a change-of-pace receiving role, not a hub. The passing-down role
that would raise his ceiling was projected, not yet earned.

**Durability in college was mixed.** He missed significant time in 2022 (8 games) and
2023 (10 games) at Ohio State, then played all 17 in 2025 before the current ankle
injury.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Strengths: elite long speed, a rookie-record explosive-touchdown rate, receiving and
return versatility, and a fully guaranteed rookie deal that signals organizational
investment.

Limitations: a shrinking late-2025 role behind Stevenson, low per-game target volume, a
poor postseason efficiency sample, college injury history, and a current ankle injury
that has kept him off the field since 2026-08-24.

Context: he was the 38th overall pick in 2025, two picks after his Ohio State running
mate Quinshon Judkins, and signed a four-year, $11.143 million fully guaranteed rookie
contract on 2025-07-18.

### Coaching/scheme fit

McDaniels's offense used Henderson as the explosive complement in 2025 and the reporting
consensus entering 2026 framed his breakout path as earning passing-game and goal-line
touches. Nothing in the 2026 preseason or Week 1 window has tested that; he has been
unavailable since late August.
