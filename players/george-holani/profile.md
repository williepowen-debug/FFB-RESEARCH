# George Holani

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Holani is a former Boise State lead back whose NFL work has primarily been depth and
special teams. Seattle's official biography lists 3,596 college rushing yards and
1,000-yard seasons in 2019 and 2022. That corrects the earlier 3,569-yard figure and
claim of two 1,100-yard seasons. His NFL sample is 25 carries for 83 yards through 2025;
college production alone does not prove an NFL three-down role.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Strengths: a receiving and pass-protection reputation that gives him a defined
passing-down role behind a rookie; experience in Seattle’s offense; exclusive-rights tender keeps him cheap and retained.

Limitations: 3.3 yards per carry on 25 NFL attempts; zero NFL starts; a hamstring
injury that ended his 2025 regular season on 2025-11-29 (injured after the 2025-11-23
Tennessee game); a first-round rookie ahead of him and a veteran (Emanuel Wilson)
beside him.

Context: retained on an exclusive-rights tender (tendered 2026-03-02, signed
2026-03-16) for 2026 only.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Third on the official depth chart as of 2026-09-09 (Price, Wilson, Holani), down from
first on the 2026-08-17 chart — an ordering change, not a usage measurement. The
official 53-man analysis (2026-08-30) said Price and Holani expect to split the
workload early; the Week 1 preview (2026-09-07) named him among the backs behind Price
so that Price does not carry the load alone; ESPN's Brady Henderson (2026-08-31)
described his role as heavy involvement on passing downs and third down. He carries no
Week 1 designation. Zach Charbonnet is on reserve/PUP for at least four games. See
[post-Walker backfield](../../teams/NFC/West/Seattle-Seahawks/2026/offense/post-walker-backfield.md)
and the [SEA synthesis 2026-09-09](../../intelligence/2026/syntheses/2026-09-09/SEA.md).

### Coaching/scheme fit

Fleury's Shanahan-tree structure uses the back heavily in the screen and check-down
game, which is the role Holani is described as filling. The trade-off is that the same
structure concentrates carries in the lead back once one is established. See
[Fleury continuity finding](../../teams/NFC/West/Seattle-Seahawks/2026/offense/fleury-continuity-offense.md).

### Health and availability

Available, no designation. 2025: hamstring injury, injured reserve 2025-11-29,
activated for the NFC Championship Game. His official transaction history also records a 2024 ankle absence on practice-squad injured reserve.

## Fantasy assessment

- Applicable format: full PPR (passing-down role) and deep or dynasty leagues as a
  handcuff; not a standard-scoring starter.
- Range of outcomes: from a pure passing-down back at 3-6 touches a game to a 10-14
  touch committee partner if Seattle protects Price on third downs and near the goal
  line. His single-game ceiling is highest in weeks Seattle trails.
- Key upside condition: an injury to Price, or Price failing in pass protection, which
  could expand Holani’s role; Emanuel Wilson has more NFL rushing experience.
- Key downside condition: Price handling all three downs and Wilson taking short
  yardage, leaving Holani a special-teams back; Charbonnet's return in Week 5 or later
  pushes him further down.
- Confidence: medium on a defined passing-down role for Weeks 1-4; low on touches
  beyond that; low on any standalone value after Charbonnet returns.

## Team history

- 2024-present: Seattle Seahawks. Signed as an undrafted free agent out of Boise State
  on 2024-05-03; waived 2024-08-27 and re-signed to the practice squad 2024-08-28;
  signed to the active roster 2024-12-26. Placed on injured reserve 2025-11-29 and
  activated for the 2025 NFC Championship Game. Tendered as an exclusive-rights free
  agent 2026-03-02 and signed 2026-03-16.

## What would change this assessment

- Week 1 route and third-down snaps: if he runs the majority of backfield routes he
  has a PPR role; if Price or Wilson does, he is a depth back.
- Any Price absence or a visible third-down substitution pattern against Price.
- Wilson taking the short-yardage and two-minute work, which would remove Holani's
  second path to touches.
- Charbonnet's announced return-to-practice and activation dates; eligibility alone is not an activation forecast.
- A recurrence of the 2025 hamstring injury.
