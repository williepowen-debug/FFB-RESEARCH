# Malik Nabers

- Position: WR
- Current team: New York Giants
- Season: durable profile (verified 2026-08-26)
- Last verified: 2026-08-26
- Status: active

## Durable player profile

### Traits and role fit

Nabers is a high-volume separator with explosive after-catch ability and alignment flexibility.
His route and target profile supports a primary-receiver role from the perimeter or slot rather
than dependence on one deployment.

### Historical usage and production

New York selected Nabers sixth overall in the 2024 NFL Draft. He started 13 of 15 games as a
rookie and caught 109 passes for 1,204 yards and seven touchdowns. He opened 2025 with 18 catches
for 271 yards and two touchdowns across four starts before a knee injury ended his season.

His first two NFL seasons therefore produced 127 receptions, 1,475 yards, and nine touchdowns in
19 games, establishing target-command upside despite the interrupted second year.

### Strengths, limitations, and relevant context

Nabers' strengths are separation, open-field acceleration, and demonstrated target earning. The
central limitation is medical rather than role-based: the 2025 knee injury creates workload and
availability uncertainty until full-contact, full-route participation is established.

## Current situation

### Team and depth-chart role

Nabers remains the Giants' projected target-share anchor. The team added multiple veteran
receivers, but those additions create competition for secondary routes rather than a demonstrated
replacement for his primary role.

### Coaching/scheme fit

The first-year staff is building around Jaxson Dart with Matt Nagy, Brian Callahan, and Greg Roman
in the offensive structure. A more physical run-game identity can reduce raw pass volume, but
Nabers' ability to win from multiple alignments should keep him central when healthy.

### Health and availability

Nabers shed the red non-contact jersey on August 24. John Harbaugh said Week 1 readiness was a
reasonable expectation barring a setback. That is a meaningful ramp milestone, not confirmation
of full first-team routes or an unrestricted Week 1 workload.
